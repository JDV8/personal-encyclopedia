# Science

**Prerequisites:** None.

The natural and health sciences, and the methods that underpin them. This branch currently holds general research methods; subject areas such as medicine have their own top-level branch.

## Contents

- [Research methods](research-methods/README.md): how research works and how to read it.
  - [Reading scientific research](research-methods/reading-research/README.md)

## References & further reading

- *Philosophy of Science: A Very Short Introduction*, Samir Okasha, 2nd ed. (Oxford University Press, 2016). **Beginner**
- *What Is This Thing Called Science?*, Alan Chalmers, 4th ed. (Open University Press, 2013). **Beginner**
- *Why Trust Science?*, Naomi Oreskes (Princeton University Press, 2019). **Intermediate**
- *The Structure of Scientific Revolutions*, Thomas S. Kuhn (University of Chicago Press, 1962). **Intermediate**
- *The Logic of Scientific Discovery*, Karl Popper (Hutchinson, 1959; first published in German 1934). **Advanced**
