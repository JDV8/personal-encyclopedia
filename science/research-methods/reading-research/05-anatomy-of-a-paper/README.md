# 5. Anatomy of a paper

**Prerequisites:** [Statistics for readers](../02-statistics/README.md) and [Study designs](../03-study-designs/README.md).

A guided tour of a research paper, section by section: title and abstract, introduction, methods, results and Table 1, graphs, forest plots, Kaplan-Meier curves, funnel plots, the discussion and spin, supplementary material, and reporting checklists such as CONSORT, STROBE and PRISMA.

## Contents

1. [The shape of a paper](01-the-shape-of-a-paper.md): Most research papers follow IMRaD: Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion.
2. [Title and abstract](02-title-and-abstract.md): Titles and abstracts are the most-read and most-spun parts of a paper.
3. [Introduction and background](03-introduction-and-background.md): The introduction states the problem, what is known, the gap and the objective.
4. [Methods](04-methods.md): The methods section is where most of the judgement in reading research happens.
5. [Results and Table 1](05-results-and-table-one.md): Start with the participant flow: who was randomised, lost and analysed.
6. [Reading graphs](06-reading-graphs.md): Read the axes, scales and legend before interpreting any graph.
7. [Forest plots](07-forest-plots.md): Each row shows one study's estimate (square, sized by weight) and confidence interval (line).
8. [Kaplan-Meier curves](08-kaplan-meier-curves.md): Kaplan-Meier curves estimate the probability of remaining event-free over time, handling censored data.
9. [Funnel plots](09-funnel-plots.md): A funnel plot graphs effect size against precision; without bias it looks like a symmetric inverted funnel.
10. [Discussion, limitations and spin](10-discussion-limitations-and-spin.md): The discussion is interpretation; form your own view before reading it.
11. [Supplementary material and data](11-supplementary-material-and-data.md): Supplements often contain the protocol, analysis plan, sensitivity analyses and full harms data.
12. [Reporting checklists](12-reporting-checklists.md): Reporting guidelines specify what papers of each type should include.

## Suggested reading order

Read 01–05 and 10 in order for the main structure; 06–09 cover specific figures; 11–12 cover what lies beyond the main text.
