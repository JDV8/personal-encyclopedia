# 11. Practice

**Prerequisites:** Sections 01–10, or at least [Statistics](../02-statistics/README.md), [Study designs](../03-study-designs/README.md) and [Causation](../04-causation/README.md).

Putting it all together: how to find and access papers, a step-by-step checklist, and five worked examples applying it to real papers, a randomised trial (PREDIMED), a cohort study (MMR and autism), a meta-analysis (aspirin for primary prevention), a news story (chocolate and Nobel prizes) and a case-control study (Doll and Hill).

## Contents

1. [Finding and accessing papers](01-finding-and-accessing-papers.md): Use press releases, DOIs, PubMed and Google Scholar to find the original study.
2. [A step-by-step checklist](02-a-step-by-step-checklist.md): A routine checklist catches common problems in claims and studies.
3. [Worked example: a randomised trial (PREDIMED)](03-worked-example-a-randomised-trial.md): PREDIMED tested Mediterranean diets on clinical outcomes in 7,447 high-risk Spaniards.
4. [Worked example: a cohort study (MMR and autism)](04-worked-example-a-cohort-study.md): Nationwide registries allow very large cohorts with little selection bias.
5. [Worked example: a meta-analysis (aspirin for primary prevention)](05-worked-example-a-meta-analysis.md): Individual participant data meta-analyses are the strongest form of evidence synthesis.
6. [Worked example: a news story (chocolate and Nobel prizes)](06-worked-example-a-news-story.md): Messerli's 2012 *NEJM* note correlated national chocolate consumption with Nobel laureates (r = 0.79, 23 countries).
7. [Worked example: a case-control study (Doll and Hill, 1950)](07-worked-example-a-case-control-study.md): Doll and Hill compared lung cancer patients with matched controls on smoking.

## Suggested reading order
