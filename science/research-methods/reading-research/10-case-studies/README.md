# 10. Case studies

**Prerequisites:** Sections 01–09, especially [Funding and conflicts](../08-funding-and-conflicts/README.md) and [From lab to headline](../09-from-lab-to-headline/README.md).

Ten real episodes that show the course's ideas in action: industry-manufactured doubt (tobacco, sugar), surrogate endpoints (CAST), observational studies overturned by trials (HRT), hidden harms and data (Vioxx, Study 329, Tamiflu), fraud with undisclosed litigation interests (Wakefield), the replication crisis (power posing) and unverifiable data (Surgisphere).

## Contents

1. [Case study: tobacco industry research](01-tobacco-industry-research.md): Doll and Hill's 1950 case-control study and later cohort studies established smoking as a cause of lung cancer.
2. [Case study: the Sugar Research Foundation](02-sugar-research-foundation.md): The SRF funded a 1965–67 Harvard review that discounted sugar and emphasised fat; the funding was not disclosed.
3. [Case study: the Cardiac Arrhythmia Suppression Trial](03-the-cardiac-arrhythmia-suppression-trial.md): Antiarrhythmic drugs were used after heart attacks because they suppressed PVCs, a predictor of sudden death.
4. [Case study: hormone replacement therapy and the WHI](04-hormone-replacement-therapy-and-the-whi.md): Observational studies suggested HRT prevented heart disease; HERS and the WHI did not support this.
5. [Case study: Vioxx](05-vioxx.md): Vioxx was a COX-2 inhibitor marketed for fewer gastrointestinal side effects.
6. [Case study: paroxetine Study 329](06-paroxetine-study-329.md): Study 329's 2001 paper called paroxetine effective in adolescents, although neither protocol-specified primary outcome was significant.
7. [Case study: Wakefield 1998](07-wakefield-1998.md): Wakefield's 1998 paper was a 12-child case series that suggested an MMR-autism link.
8. [Case study: Tamiflu and hidden trial data](08-tamiflu-and-hidden-trial-data.md): A 2003 meta-analysis claiming fewer complications relied mostly on unpublished trials.
9. [Case study: power posing](09-power-posing.md): Carney, Cuddy and Yap's 2010 study (n=42) reported effects of power poses on hormones and risk-taking.
10. [Case study: Surgisphere](10-surgisphere.md): Surgisphere supplied data for high-profile COVID-19 papers in *The Lancet* and *NEJM* in 2020.
