# 7. Journals, peer review and publishing

**Prerequisites:** [Anatomy of a paper](../05-anatomy-of-a-paper/README.md).

How research gets published and how far that process can be trusted: how journals work, peer review, impact factors and other metrics, preprints, predatory journals, and retractions and corrections.

## Contents

1. [How journals work](01-how-journals-work.md): Journals began in 1665 and create a public, dated record of research.
2. [Peer review](02-peer-review.md): Peer review is expert evaluation before publication, in single-blind, double-blind or open forms.
3. [Impact factors and metrics](03-impact-factors-and-metrics.md): The journal impact factor is average recent citations per article, created for librarians.
4. [Preprints](04-preprints.md): Preprints are papers shared before peer review, on servers such as arXiv, bioRxiv and medRxiv.
5. [Predatory journals](05-predatory-journals.md): Predatory journals charge fees while providing little or no real review.
6. [Retractions and corrections](06-retractions-and-corrections.md): Corrections fix minor errors; expressions of concern flag serious unresolved doubts; retractions withdraw unreliable papers.

## Key studies & sources

- Schroter S, Black N, Evans S, et al. What errors do peer reviewers detect, and does training improve their ability to detect them? *Journal of the Royal Society of Medicine* 2008;101:507–514. [doi](https://doi.org/10.1258/jrsm.2008.080062). Reviewers spotted on average about 3 of 9 major deliberate errors.
- Bohannon J. Who's afraid of peer review? *Science* 2013;342:60–65. [doi](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.342.6154.60). A fake paper was accepted by 157 of 304 open-access journals; the sting did not include subscription journals.
- Fang FC, Steen RG, Casadevall A. Misconduct accounts for the majority of retracted scientific publications. *PNAS* 2012;109:17028–17033. [doi](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1212247109). 67.4% of 2,047 retractions due to misconduct.
- Brierley L, Nanni F, Polka JK, et al. Tracking changes between preprint posting and journal publication during a pandemic. *PLoS Biology* 2022;20:e3001285. [doi](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3001285). Most COVID-19 preprints changed little on publication.
- Retraction Watch Database, now maintained openly by Crossref: [retractiondatabase.org](https://retractiondatabase.org/).
