# Cohort study

An observational study that follows a group of people over time, measuring exposures first and then recording outcomes. Prospective cohorts collect data going forward; retrospective cohorts use existing records. Cohort studies establish the sequence of exposure and outcome but remain vulnerable to confounding and loss to follow-up.
