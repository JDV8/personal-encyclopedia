# Industry bias

The tendency, documented in systematic reviews such as the 2017 Cochrane review by Lundh and colleagues, for studies sponsored by companies to report results and conclusions more favourable to the sponsor's product than studies with other sponsors. It operates through question, design, outcome, publication and framing choices rather than obviously poor methods.
