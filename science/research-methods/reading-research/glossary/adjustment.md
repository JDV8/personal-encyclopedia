# Adjustment

Statistical techniques, such as regression, stratification or propensity scores, used to account for differences between groups in measured variables, to reduce confounding. Adjustment cannot handle unmeasured confounders, and adjusting for the wrong variables, such as colliders or mediators, can introduce bias.
