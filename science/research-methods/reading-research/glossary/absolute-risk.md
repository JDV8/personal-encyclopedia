# Absolute risk

The probability that an event happens to a person in a defined group over a stated period, such as 2 in 100 over five years. Absolute risks let you judge how much a treatment or exposure matters to an individual, which relative risks alone cannot show. An absolute risk reduction is the difference between two absolute risks.
