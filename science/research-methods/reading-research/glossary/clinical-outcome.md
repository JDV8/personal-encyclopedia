# Clinical outcome

An outcome that directly reflects how patients feel, function or survive, such as death, heart attack, stroke, hospitalisation, pain or quality of life. Also called patient-important outcomes. Treatments ideally should be judged on clinical outcomes rather than surrogate endpoints.
