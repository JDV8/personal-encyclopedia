# Statistical significance

A label applied when a p-value falls below a chosen threshold, conventionally 0.05. It indicates that the data would be unusual if the null hypothesis were true, not that an effect is large, important or certain. Many statisticians have called for abandoning the binary 'significant or not' classification.
