# Base rate

The underlying frequency of a condition or event in a population before considering any specific evidence, such as the prevalence of a disease. Ignoring base rates, the base rate fallacy, leads people to overestimate how likely a positive test result is to be correct when the condition is rare. Bayes' theorem combines base rates with new evidence.
