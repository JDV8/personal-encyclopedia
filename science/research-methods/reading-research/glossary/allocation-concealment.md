# Allocation concealment

Keeping the upcoming treatment assignment hidden from the people enrolling participants into a trial, so they cannot influence who goes into which group. It is different from blinding, which hides the assignment after allocation. Inadequate concealment lets recruiters steer sicker or healthier patients into particular arms and is associated with exaggerated treatment effects.
