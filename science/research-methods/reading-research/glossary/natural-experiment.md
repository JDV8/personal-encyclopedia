# Natural experiment

A situation in which an exposure is assigned by circumstances outside researchers' control in a way that resembles random assignment, such as a policy change, a lottery or a geographical boundary. Natural experiments can support causal inference when randomised trials are impossible, though their assumptions need careful scrutiny.
