# Censoring

In survival analysis, the situation in which a participant's event time is not observed because follow-up ended, they withdrew or the study closed before an event occurred. Censored participants contribute information up to their last follow-up. Methods assume censoring is unrelated to outcome risk; if not, results can be biased.
