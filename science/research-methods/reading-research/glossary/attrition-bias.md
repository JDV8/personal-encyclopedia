# Attrition bias

Bias caused by systematic differences between groups in who withdraws or is lost from a study. For example, if participants with side effects leave a treatment group, the remaining participants may make the treatment look better tolerated than it is. Intention-to-treat analysis and careful handling of missing data reduce it.
