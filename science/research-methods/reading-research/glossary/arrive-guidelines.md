# ARRIVE guidelines

Animal Research: Reporting of In Vivo Experiments, a checklist first published in 2010 and updated as ARRIVE 2.0 in 2020. It sets out what animal studies should report, including sample size calculation, randomisation, blinding, inclusion and exclusion criteria and statistical methods. Many journals endorse it, though compliance has often been incomplete in practice.
