# Replication crisis

The recognition, from about the early 2010s, that many published findings in psychology, medicine and other fields could not be replicated. It was highlighted by the Open Science Collaboration's 2015 project, which found significant results in 36% of replications of 100 psychology studies, and led to reforms such as preregistration.
