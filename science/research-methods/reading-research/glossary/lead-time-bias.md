# Lead-time bias

The apparent increase in survival time after diagnosis that occurs when screening detects a disease earlier, even if death happens at the same time as it would have without screening. Comparing survival from diagnosis between screened and unscreened groups is therefore misleading; mortality comparisons are needed.
