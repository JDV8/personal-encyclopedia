# Blinding

Keeping trial participants, clinicians, outcome assessors or analysts unaware of which treatment each participant received, also called masking. Blinding reduces bias from expectations and from differences in care or assessment. 'Double-blind' is used inconsistently, so good reports say exactly who was blinded. Unblinded trials tend to show larger effects on subjective outcomes.
