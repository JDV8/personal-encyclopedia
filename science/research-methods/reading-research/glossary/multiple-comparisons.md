# Multiple comparisons

The problem that the more statistical tests are performed, the more likely some will be significant by chance. With 20 independent tests at the 0.05 level and no true effects, about one false positive is expected. Corrections such as Bonferroni and false discovery rate control, and prespecification, address it.
