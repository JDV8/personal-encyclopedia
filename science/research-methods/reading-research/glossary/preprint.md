# Preprint

A manuscript shared publicly, usually on a server such as arXiv, bioRxiv or medRxiv, before peer review. Preprints speed up communication, as during COVID-19, but have not been formally vetted. Most are later published, often with modest changes, but some contain errors or are never published.
