# Ad hominem

Rejecting an argument because of who makes it, rather than on its merits. In research, dismissing a study only because of its funder or its authors' affiliations is an ad hominem move; conflicts of interest are better used to direct scrutiny toward where bias could enter, and should be applied symmetrically.
