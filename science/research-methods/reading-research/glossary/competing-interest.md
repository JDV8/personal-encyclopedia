# Competing interest

Another term for conflict of interest, used by many journals. It covers financial interests such as employment, grants, fees, shares and patents, and non-financial interests such as personal, political, ideological or academic commitments. Declaring a competing interest does not mean wrongdoing; it lets readers judge the risk of bias.
