# P-hacking

Trying multiple analyses, outcomes, subgroups, exclusions or stopping points and reporting those that yield p-values below 0.05. Often unintentional, it greatly inflates false-positive rates. Signs include many p-values just below 0.05. Preregistration and analysis plans limit it.
