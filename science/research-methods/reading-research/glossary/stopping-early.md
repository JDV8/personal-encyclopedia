# Stopping early

Ending a trial before its planned completion, usually after an interim analysis shows clear benefit, harm or futility. Trials stopped early for benefit tend to overestimate effects, because they stop when chance fluctuations favour the treatment; this was a concern for PREDIMED. Stopping for harm, as in CAST, protects participants.
