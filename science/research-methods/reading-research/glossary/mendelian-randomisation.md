# Mendelian randomisation

A method that uses genetic variants associated with an exposure, such as cholesterol levels, as instrumental variables to estimate the causal effect of the exposure on an outcome. Because genes are allocated at conception, it reduces confounding and reverse causation, but pleiotropy can violate its assumptions.
