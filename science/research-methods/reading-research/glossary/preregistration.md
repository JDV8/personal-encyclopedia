# Preregistration

Recording a study's hypotheses, design, primary outcomes and analysis plan in a time-stamped public registry before collecting or analysing data. It distinguishes confirmatory from exploratory analyses and makes outcome switching and HARKing detectable. Registration is required for clinical trials and increasingly used elsewhere.
