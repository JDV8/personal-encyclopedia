# ICMJE

The International Committee of Medical Journal Editors, a group of editors that sets widely adopted recommendations on authorship, conflicts of interest and trial registration. Its 2004 policy required registration of trials before enrolment as a condition of publication for trials starting from July 2005, and it maintains a standard disclosure form.
