# Ascertainment bias

A systematic difference in how outcomes or exposures are detected or recorded between groups. For example, people who visit doctors more often will have more conditions diagnosed, which can make an exposure that brings people to doctors look harmful. It overlaps with detection bias and with information bias generally.
