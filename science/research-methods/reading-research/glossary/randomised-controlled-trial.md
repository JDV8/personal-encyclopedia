# Randomised controlled trial

A study in which participants are randomly assigned to receive an intervention or a comparator, such as placebo or standard care, and outcomes are compared. Well-conducted trials give the most reliable evidence about treatment effects but can be expensive, short, unrepresentative, or impossible for some questions.
