# Normal distribution

A symmetrical, bell-shaped probability distribution defined by its mean and standard deviation; about 68% of values lie within one standard deviation of the mean and about 95% within two. Many statistical methods assume normality, and the central limit theorem explains why sample means tend to be normally distributed.
