# Residual confounding

Confounding that remains after adjustment, because confounders were not measured, were measured imprecisely, or were modelled poorly. It is the main reason observational associations, especially weak ones, should be interpreted cautiously. Sensitivity analyses can estimate how strong an unmeasured confounder would need to be.
