# Questionable research practices

Practices short of outright fraud that increase the chance of false positives or misleading reports, such as selective reporting of outcomes, optional stopping, excluding data after seeing results, rounding p-values down and HARKing. Surveys suggest they have been common across fields.

**Learn more:** [Fraud and misconduct](../06-research-integrity/08-fraud-and-misconduct.md)
