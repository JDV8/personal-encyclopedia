# Disclosure

The declaration by authors, reviewers or experts of relationships that might be perceived as conflicts of interest, usually on a standard form such as the ICMJE form. Disclosure is necessary but has limits: declarations are often incomplete, and experiments suggest disclosure can sometimes increase biased advice.
