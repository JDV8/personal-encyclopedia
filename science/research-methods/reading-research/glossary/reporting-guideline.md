# Reporting guideline

A checklist, often with a flow diagram, specifying the minimum information a study report should contain, such as CONSORT for trials, STROBE for observational studies, PRISMA for systematic reviews and ARRIVE for animal studies. The EQUATOR Network catalogues them. They improve reporting, not the underlying study.
