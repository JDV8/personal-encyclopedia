# GRADE

Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation, a widely used system for rating the certainty of a body of evidence as high, moderate, low or very low, and for making recommendations. Randomised evidence starts high and observational evidence low; ratings move down for risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, imprecision and publication bias.
