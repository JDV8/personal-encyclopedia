# Retraction

The formal withdrawal of a published paper by a journal, because of serious error, misconduct, plagiarism, duplicate publication or unverifiable data. Retracted papers remain visible but marked. Fang and colleagues found in 2012 that most retractions in biomedicine were due to misconduct. Retracted papers often continue to be cited.
