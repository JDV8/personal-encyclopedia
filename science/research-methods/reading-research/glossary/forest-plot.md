# Forest plot

A graph showing the effect estimate and confidence interval from each study in a meta-analysis, or each subgroup in a trial, as squares with horizontal lines, along with a pooled estimate shown as a diamond. A vertical line marks no effect. Forest plots show at a glance how consistent results are.
