# Counterfactual

What would have happened to the same people under a different exposure or treatment. Causal effects are defined as the difference between actual and counterfactual outcomes, which cannot both be observed for one person. Randomised trials estimate counterfactuals by comparing similar groups; observational methods try to approximate them.
