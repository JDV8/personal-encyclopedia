# Researcher degrees of freedom

The many choices researchers make in collecting and analysing data, such as which participants to exclude, which covariates to include, which outcomes to report and when to stop collecting data. Simmons, Nelson and Simonsohn showed in 2011 that exploiting these choices can make false positives very likely.
