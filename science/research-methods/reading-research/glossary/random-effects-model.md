# Random-effects model

A meta-analysis model that assumes true effects vary between studies and estimates their average, giving relatively more weight to smaller studies than a fixed-effect model does. It produces wider confidence intervals when heterogeneity is present. A prediction interval shows the range of effects expected in a new study.
