# Garden of forking paths

A phrase used by statisticians Andrew Gelman and Eric Loken for the way analysis choices can depend on the data even without deliberate fishing: researchers make one reasonable-looking analysis, but would have made different choices with different data. The multiplicity is hidden, so p-values are misleading.
