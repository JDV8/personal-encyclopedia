# Publication bias

The tendency for studies with positive or statistically significant results to be published more often, faster or more prominently than studies with null or negative results. It distorts the literature and inflates meta-analyses. Turner and colleagues found that published antidepressant trials greatly overstated the proportion of positive trials.
