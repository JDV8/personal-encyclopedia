# Evidence hierarchy

A ranking of study designs by their usual susceptibility to bias, often drawn as a pyramid with systematic reviews and randomised trials at the top and case reports and expert opinion at the bottom. It is a useful rough guide, but quality within a design and the question asked matter as much as the design label.
