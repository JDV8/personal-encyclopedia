# Confidence interval

A range of values, calculated from sample data, that indicates the precision of an estimate. A 95% confidence interval is produced by a method that would contain the true value in 95% of repeated samples. Narrow intervals indicate precise estimates; intervals that include no effect indicate results compatible with no difference.
