# Statistical power

The probability that a study will detect an effect of a given size if it really exists, usually set at 80% or 90% when planning. Power depends on sample size, effect size, variability and the significance level. Underpowered studies miss real effects and, when they do reach significance, tend to exaggerate effects. Button and colleagues estimated median power in neuroscience at about 21% in 2013.
