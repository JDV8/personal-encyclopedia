# STROBE

Strengthening the Reporting of Observational Studies in Epidemiology, a reporting guideline published in 2007 for cohort, case-control and cross-sectional studies. Its checklist covers items such as study design, setting, participants, variables, bias, sample size and statistical methods, helping readers assess observational research.
