# Standardised mean difference

The difference between group means divided by a measure of standard deviation, such as Cohen's d. It allows effects measured on different scales to be compared or pooled. By Cohen's rough conventions 0.2 is small, 0.5 medium and 0.8 large, but importance depends on context.
