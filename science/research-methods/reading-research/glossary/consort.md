# CONSORT

Consolidated Standards of Reporting Trials, a reporting guideline for randomised trials, first published in 1996 and most recently updated in 2025. It includes a checklist of items and a flow diagram showing how participants moved through the trial. Using CONSORT helps readers judge validity, and many journals require it.
