# Dose-response relationship

A pattern in which larger amounts or longer durations of an exposure are associated with larger effects, also called a biological gradient. It is one of the Bradford Hill considerations and supports causation, though confounding can also produce gradients. Doll and Hill found lung cancer risk rose with the amount smoked.
