# Hypothesis

A proposed explanation or prediction that can be tested by observation or experiment. Good hypotheses are specific enough to be wrong. In statistics, the research hypothesis is often contrasted with the null hypothesis. Stating hypotheses before data collection distinguishes confirmatory from exploratory research.
