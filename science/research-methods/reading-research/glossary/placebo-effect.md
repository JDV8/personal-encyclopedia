# Placebo effect

An improvement in symptoms that results from receiving a treatment, independent of its specific pharmacological action, through expectation and conditioning. Much of the improvement in placebo groups is actually due to natural recovery and regression to the mean. Placebo effects are largest for subjective outcomes such as pain.
