# Surveillance bias

A form of ascertainment bias in which people who are monitored more closely, for example because they take a drug or attend clinics more often, have more conditions detected. It can make an exposure appear to cause a condition that is simply being found more often.

**Learn more:** [Measurement and recall bias](../04-causation/07-measurement-and-recall-bias.md)
