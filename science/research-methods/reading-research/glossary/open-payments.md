# Open Payments

A US federal database, created under the Physician Payments Sunshine Act of 2010 and first published in 2014, that lists payments and other transfers of value from drug and device manufacturers to physicians and teaching hospitals. Researchers have used it to check whether authors fully disclosed industry payments.
