# Expression of concern

A notice published by a journal to alert readers to serious doubts about a paper while an investigation is ongoing or when the issues cannot be resolved. The New England Journal of Medicine issued one about the VIGOR trial of Vioxx in 2005. It may later be followed by a retraction or correction.
