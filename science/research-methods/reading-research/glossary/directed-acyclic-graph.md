# Directed acyclic graph

A diagram of assumed causal relationships, with arrows from causes to effects and no loops. It makes assumptions explicit and helps identify confounders to adjust for and colliders or mediators not to adjust for. Its conclusions are only as good as the assumptions it encodes.
