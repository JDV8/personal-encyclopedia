# Heterogeneity

Variation in results between studies in a meta-analysis beyond what chance would produce, often measured with the I-squared statistic. Heterogeneity can reflect differences in populations, interventions, outcomes or methods. When it is high, a single pooled estimate may be misleading and reasons should be explored.
