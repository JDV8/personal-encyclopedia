# Confirmation bias

The tendency to seek, interpret and remember information in ways that confirm existing beliefs. In research, it can affect hypothesis choice, analysis, interpretation and peer review. Preregistration, blinded analysis and adversarial collaboration are among the methods used to reduce it.
