# Impact factor

A journal-level metric, published by Clarivate, equal to the average number of citations in a year to items the journal published in the previous two years. It measures citation rates of journals, not the quality of individual papers, and can be skewed by a few highly cited articles. The DORA declaration urges against using it to assess researchers.
