# Ghostwriting

Writing of a paper by an undisclosed person, often a medical writer paid by a company, with named academic authors who may have contributed little. Documents from litigation over Vioxx and paroxetine revealed ghostwriting practices. Authorship rules require writers to be acknowledged and authors to take responsibility for content.
