# Instrumental variable

A variable that affects the exposure but affects the outcome only through the exposure and is not related to confounders. Instrumental variable analysis uses it to estimate causal effects in observational data. Mendelian randomisation uses genetic variants as instruments. The assumptions are strong and often untestable.
