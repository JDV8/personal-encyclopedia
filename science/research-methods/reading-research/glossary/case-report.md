# Case report

A detailed description of a single patient, often with an unusual presentation, reaction or outcome. Case reports can generate hypotheses and signal rare harms or new diseases, but cannot establish causes or treatment effects because there is no comparison group. A case series describes several similar patients and has the same basic limitation.
