# Per-protocol analysis

An analysis that includes only trial participants who adhered to the assigned treatment as specified in the protocol. It estimates the effect of actually receiving treatment but can be biased because adherence is related to prognosis. It is often reported alongside intention-to-treat analysis, especially in non-inferiority trials.
