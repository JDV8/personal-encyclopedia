# Surrogate endpoint

A laboratory measure or physical sign, such as blood pressure, cholesterol, tumour shrinkage or antibody level, used as a substitute for a clinical outcome that matters to patients. Surrogates speed up trials but can mislead when treatments affect them without improving outcomes, as in the Cardiac Arrhythmia Suppression Trial.
