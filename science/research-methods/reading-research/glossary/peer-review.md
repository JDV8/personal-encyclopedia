# Peer review

The evaluation of a manuscript by independent experts before publication, coordinated by an editor. Peer review can improve papers and filter obvious errors but is slow, inconsistent, and poor at detecting fraud or subtle flaws; reviewers rarely see raw data. Publication after review is not a guarantee of correctness.
