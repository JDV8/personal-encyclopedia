## References & further reading

- *A Dictionary of Epidemiology*, ed. Miquel Porta, 6th ed. (Oxford University Press, 2014). The standard reference for epidemiological terms. **Intermediate**
- *The Cartoon Guide to Statistics*, Larry Gonick and Woollcott Smith (HarperPerennial, 1993). **Beginner**
- *Testing Treatments: Better Research for Better Healthcare*, Imogen Evans, Hazel Thornton, Iain Chalmers and Paul Glasziou, 2nd ed. (Pinter & Martin, 2011), free online at [testingtreatments.org](https://www.testingtreatments.org/). **Beginner**
- *Catalogue of Bias*, Centre for Evidence-Based Medicine, University of Oxford: [catalogofbias.org](https://catalogofbias.org/). Definitions and examples of named biases. **Intermediate**
- *Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions*, ed. Julian Higgins and colleagues, online at [training.cochrane.org/handbook](https://training.cochrane.org/handbook). **Advanced**
