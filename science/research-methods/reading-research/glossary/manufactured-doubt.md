# Manufactured doubt

The deliberate creation or exaggeration of scientific uncertainty to delay regulation or protect products, famously by the tobacco industry, whose 1969 internal memo said 'Doubt is our product'. Historians Naomi Oreskes and Erik Conway documented similar tactics on other issues in 'Merchants of Doubt'.
