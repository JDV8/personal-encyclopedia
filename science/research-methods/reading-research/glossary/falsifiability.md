# Falsifiability

Karl Popper's criterion that a scientific theory must make claims that could in principle be shown false by observation. A theory that can accommodate any possible outcome tells us nothing about the world. Falsifiability is a widely used heuristic, though philosophers debate whether it fully demarcates science.
