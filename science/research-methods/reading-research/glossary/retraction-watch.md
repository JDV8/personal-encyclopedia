# Retraction Watch

A blog founded in 2010 by journalists Ivan Oransky and Adam Marcus that reports on retractions and research misconduct. Its database of retractions was acquired by Crossref in 2023 and made openly available, and is used by reference managers and publishers to flag retracted papers.
