# Research misconduct

Fabrication, falsification or plagiarism in proposing, performing, reviewing or reporting research, as defined by US federal policy. Broader questionable research practices, such as p-hacking or selective reporting, are not usually classed as misconduct but are far more common. In a 2009 meta-analysis Fanelli found about 2% of scientists admitted fabrication or falsification.
