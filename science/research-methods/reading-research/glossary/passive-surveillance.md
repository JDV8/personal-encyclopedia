# Passive surveillance

Monitoring that relies on voluntary reports from clinicians, patients or manufacturers, such as VAERS or Yellow Card. It is good for detecting unexpected signals quickly and cheaply, but reports are incomplete, unverified and lack a denominator, so they cannot by themselves establish how often an event occurs or whether a product caused it.
