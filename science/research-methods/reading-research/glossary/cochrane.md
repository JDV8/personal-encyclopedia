# Cochrane

An international network, founded in 1993 as the Cochrane Collaboration, that produces systematic reviews of health interventions using standardised methods. Its reviews are published in the Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. Cochrane reviews are widely respected, though like all reviews they depend on available data and judgement.
