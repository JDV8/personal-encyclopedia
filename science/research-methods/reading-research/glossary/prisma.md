# PRISMA

Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses, a reporting guideline first published in 2009 and updated in 2020. It includes a checklist and a flow diagram showing how records were identified, screened and included. Following PRISMA makes it easier to judge whether a review was comprehensive.
