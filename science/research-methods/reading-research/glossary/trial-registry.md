# Trial registry

A public database, such as ClinicalTrials.gov (launched 2000) or the EU Clinical Trials Register, where trials are recorded before they start, with their design, outcomes and later results. Registries let readers detect unpublished trials and outcome switching. The WHO ICTRP portal searches many registries at once.
