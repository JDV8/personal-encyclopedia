# Individual participant data

The raw data for each participant in a study, as opposed to summary results. Meta-analyses that pool individual participant data can standardise definitions, run consistent analyses and examine subgroups more reliably, and are considered the gold standard of evidence synthesis, though they take much more time and cooperation.
