# PubPeer

A website for post-publication discussion of research papers, launched in 2012, where commenters, often anonymously, point out problems such as duplicated images, statistical errors or inconsistencies. Several retractions have followed issues raised there. Browser extensions show PubPeer comments on journal pages.
