# HARKing

Hypothesising After the Results are Known: presenting a hypothesis developed after seeing the data as if it had been specified in advance. The term was coined by Norbert Kerr in 1998. HARKing makes exploratory findings look confirmatory and inflates false positives. Preregistration makes it detectable.
