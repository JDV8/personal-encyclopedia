# Standard error

The standard deviation of an estimate, such as a mean or difference, across hypothetical repeated samples. It measures precision and shrinks as sample size increases, roughly in proportion to one over the square root of the sample size. Confidence intervals are typically the estimate plus or minus about two standard errors.
