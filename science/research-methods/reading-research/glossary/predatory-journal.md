# Predatory journal

A publication that charges authors fees while providing little or no genuine editorial or peer review, often imitating legitimate journals and soliciting submissions aggressively. Papers in predatory journals have not been meaningfully vetted. Tools such as the Directory of Open Access Journals help readers identify legitimate open-access titles.
