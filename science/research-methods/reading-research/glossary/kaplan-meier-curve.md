# Kaplan-Meier curve

A graph showing the estimated proportion of participants who have not yet had an event, such as death, over time, falling in steps as events occur. Separate curves for each group show when differences emerge. Tick marks show censored participants, and numbers at risk below the graph show how reliable later portions are.
