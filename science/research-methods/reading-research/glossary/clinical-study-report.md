# Clinical study report

A detailed document, often thousands of pages long, describing the methods, results and individual adverse events of a clinical trial, prepared by a sponsor for regulators. Clinical study reports contain much more information than journal articles. Access to them changed conclusions in the Tamiflu and Study 329 reanalyses.
