# Adverse event

Any unfavourable medical occurrence in a trial participant or patient, whether or not it is caused by the treatment. Trials record adverse events in all groups so that rates can be compared. A serious adverse event is one that results in death, is life-threatening, requires hospitalisation or causes lasting disability. How events are coded can hide harms, as in Study 329.
