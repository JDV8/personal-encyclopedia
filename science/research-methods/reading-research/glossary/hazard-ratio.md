# Hazard ratio

The ratio of the instantaneous rates of an event in two groups over time, usually estimated with a Cox proportional hazards model. A hazard ratio of 0.7 means the event rate in one group is about 30% lower at any given time. It is a relative measure and should be read alongside absolute risks.
