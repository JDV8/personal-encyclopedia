# Positive predictive value

The proportion of people with a positive test result who actually have the condition. It depends heavily on prevalence: the same test has a much lower positive predictive value in a low-risk population than in a high-risk one. Its counterpart, negative predictive value, is the proportion of negatives who are truly negative.
