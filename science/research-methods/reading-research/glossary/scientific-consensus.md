# Scientific consensus

The collective judgement of the relevant expert community based on the totality of evidence, usually expressed through systematic reviews, academy reports and guidelines. A strong consensus rests on converging evidence from independent methods. It is the best current judgement, not a certainty, and has sometimes been overturned by better evidence.
