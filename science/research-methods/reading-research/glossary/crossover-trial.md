# Crossover trial

A trial in which each participant receives two or more treatments in sequence, often with a washout period in between, so each person serves as their own control. Crossover designs need fewer participants but only suit stable, chronic conditions and treatments whose effects wear off, and are vulnerable to carryover effects.
