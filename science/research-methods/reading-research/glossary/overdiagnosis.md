# Overdiagnosis

Diagnosis of a condition that would never have caused symptoms or death during a person's lifetime, often through screening that detects slow-growing or non-progressive disease. Overdiagnosed patients can be harmed by unnecessary treatment, and their inclusion makes survival statistics look better.
