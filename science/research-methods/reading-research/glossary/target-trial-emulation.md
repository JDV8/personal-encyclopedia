# Target trial emulation

An approach to observational research, championed by Miguel Hernán and James Robins, in which researchers specify the randomised trial they would ideally run and then design the observational analysis to mimic it, including eligibility, time zero and treatment strategies. It helps avoid time-related biases, though not unmeasured confounding.
