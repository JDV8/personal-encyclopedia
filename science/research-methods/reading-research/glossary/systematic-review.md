# Systematic review

A review that answers a defined question by searching for all relevant studies using explicit, reproducible methods, assessing their quality and synthesising their results, often with meta-analysis. Systematic reviews are less biased than narrative reviews, but depend on the studies available and on reviewers' judgements.
