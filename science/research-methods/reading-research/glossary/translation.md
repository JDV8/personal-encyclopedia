# Translation

The process of turning findings from basic or preclinical research into treatments and practice in humans, sometimes called the 'bench to bedside' pathway. Many promising laboratory and animal findings fail to translate; Hackam and Redelmeier found only about a third of highly cited animal studies were replicated in human trials.
