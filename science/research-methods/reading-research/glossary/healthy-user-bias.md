# Healthy-user bias

Bias arising because people who choose a preventive treatment or healthy behaviour also tend to be healthier, wealthier and more health-conscious in other ways. It can make treatments look beneficial in observational studies when they are not, as with hormone replacement therapy and heart disease.

**Learn more:** [The healthy user effect](../04-causation/06-healthy-user-bias.md)
