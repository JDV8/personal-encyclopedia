# Incidence

The number of new cases of a condition occurring in a population during a specified period, often expressed per 1,000 or 100,000 person-years. Cohort studies measure incidence directly. Incidence and prevalence are related through disease duration: long-lasting conditions have high prevalence relative to incidence.
