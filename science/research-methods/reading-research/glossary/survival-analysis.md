# Survival analysis

Statistical methods for time-to-event data, such as time until death, relapse or diagnosis, that handle participants followed for different lengths of time and censoring. Common tools include Kaplan-Meier curves, log-rank tests and Cox proportional hazards regression, which estimates hazard ratios.
