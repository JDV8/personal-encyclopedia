# Risk of bias

An assessment of how likely the design and conduct of a study are to have produced systematic error. Cochrane's RoB 2 tool for trials assesses randomisation, deviations from intended interventions, missing data, outcome measurement and selection of reported results; ROBINS-I does similar for non-randomised studies.
