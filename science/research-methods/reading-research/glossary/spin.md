# Spin

Reporting that makes results look more favourable than they are, for example by emphasising secondary outcomes or subgroups when the primary outcome was negative, using causal language for associations, or omitting harms. Studies have found spin in a substantial share of abstracts of trials with non-significant primary outcomes.
