# Conflict of interest

A situation in which a person's judgement about a primary interest, such as the validity of research, risks being unduly influenced by a secondary interest, such as money, career or beliefs. A conflict is a risk, not proof of bias. Evidence shows that industry funding is associated with more favourable results on average.
