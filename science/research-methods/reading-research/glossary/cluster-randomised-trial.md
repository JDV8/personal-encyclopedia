# Cluster randomised trial

A trial in which groups, such as schools, villages or hospital wards, rather than individuals are randomised. Clustering is useful when an intervention works at group level or when contamination between individuals is likely. Analyses must account for the fact that people in the same cluster are more alike than people in different clusters.
