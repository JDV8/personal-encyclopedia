# Ecological fallacy

The error of inferring relationships for individuals from relationships observed between groups such as countries or regions. A correlation between national chocolate consumption and Nobel prizes, for instance, says nothing about whether individuals who eat chocolate win prizes. Group-level associations can differ in size or even direction from individual-level ones.
