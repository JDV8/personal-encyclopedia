# Anecdote

An individual story or personal experience offered as evidence. Anecdotes can be vivid and can suggest hypotheses, but they are selected, unrepresentative and lack comparison groups, so they cannot show how often something happens or whether a treatment caused an outcome. Case reports are formal anecdotes.
