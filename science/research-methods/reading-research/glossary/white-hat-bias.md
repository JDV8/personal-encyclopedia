# White hat bias

A term coined by Mark Cope and David Allison in 2010 for bias leading to the distortion of research information in the service of what may be perceived as righteous ends, such as public health goals. They illustrated it with research on sugar-sweetened beverages and breastfeeding in relation to obesity.
