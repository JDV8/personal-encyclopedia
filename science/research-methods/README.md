# Research methods

**Prerequisites:** None.

How knowledge is produced, tested and communicated: the methods scientists use to study the world and the skills readers need to evaluate what they find.

## Contents

- [Reading scientific research](reading-research/README.md): a full course on reading, questioning and weighing research, from statistics and study design to conflicts of interest, case studies and worked examples.

- *How to Read a Paper: The Basics of Evidence-Based Medicine and Healthcare*, Trisha Greenhalgh, 6th ed. (Wiley-Blackwell, 2019). **Beginner**
- *The Art of Statistics: Learning from Data*, David Spiegelhalter (Pelican, 2019). **Beginner**
- *What Is This Thing Called Science?*, Alan Chalmers, 4th ed. (Open University Press, 2013). **Beginner**
- *Experimental and Quasi-Experimental Designs for Generalized Causal Inference*, William Shadish, Thomas Cook and Donald Campbell (Houghton Mifflin, 2002). **Advanced**
