# Old Testament books

This folder is the book-by-book path through the Protestant Old Testament's thirty-nine books, arranged in the usual Christian order and grouped by section: law (the Torah / Pentateuch), history, wisdom and poetry, the major prophets, and the minor prophets. Each book has its own overview: name, authorship (traditional and scholarly), context, outline, key passages, themes, and reception. The [narrative lessons](../README.md) in the parent folder retell the major stories; these files are the place to study each book as a book.

The Hebrew Bible (Tanakh) contains the same books as this Protestant list, in a different order and under three headings: Torah, Nevi'im (Prophets), and Ketuvim (Writings). Daniel, for example, stands among the Writings in the Tanakh and among the Prophets in the Christian Old Testament. That difference is explained in [canon](../../02-canon-and-hebrew-bible-vs-old-testament.md). Books and additions found in Catholic and Orthodox Bibles but not in this list are treated in [deuterocanon](../../deuterocanon/README.md).

## Suggested reading order

Read the [foundation lessons](../../README.md) first, then either follow the [Old Testament story path](../README.md) and use these overviews as companions, or read section by section starting with the [Torah](law-torah/README.md). Within each section, file order is canonical order.

## References & further reading

- *The Jewish Study Bible*, 2nd ed. (Beginner). The same books in a Tanakh frame, with introductions to each.
- John J. Collins, *Introduction to the Hebrew Bible* (Intermediate). A standard historical-critical map of every book in this folder.
- Robert Alter, *The Hebrew Bible: A Translation with Commentary* (Intermediate). Literary close reading, especially strong on narrative and poetry.
- Brevard S. Childs, *Introduction to the Old Testament as Scripture* (Advanced). Canonical-shaped introductions that still know the critical issues.
