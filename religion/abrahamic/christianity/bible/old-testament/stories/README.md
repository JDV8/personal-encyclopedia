# Old Testament stories

The numbered lessons in the parent folder retell the main narrative arcs of the Old Testament. This folder slows down for a few episodes that are easy to compress in a survey and that repay a closer look: the binding of Isaac, the long prophetic careers of Elijah and Elisha, and the court tales of Daniel. They are companions to the [book overviews](../books/README.md) and to the survey path, not replacements for them.

## Suggested reading order

Read the relevant survey lesson first ([Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob](../03-abraham-isaac-jacob.md); [the prophets](../08-prophets.md) and [Saul, David, and Solomon](../07-saul-david-solomon.md) as background for the Omride period; [Exile and return](../10-exile-and-return.md) for Daniel), then the story file.

## References & further reading

- Jon D. Levenson, *The Death and Resurrection of the Beloved Son* (Advanced). A searching study of the Isaac story and its afterlife in Jewish and Christian tradition.
- Robert Alter, *The Art of Biblical Narrative* (Intermediate). Literary attention that is especially helpful for Genesis 22 and for the Joseph and court tales.
- John J. Collins, *Daniel* (Advanced). For the court tales in their Second Temple setting as well as for the visions.
