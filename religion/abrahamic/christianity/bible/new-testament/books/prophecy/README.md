# Prophecy (New Testament)

The New Testament closes with one prophetic-apocalyptic book: [Revelation](revelation.md). It is a letter to seven churches, a series of visions, and a worship book, not a modern calendar of events. The survey lesson [Revelation](../../06-revelation.md) is the narrative companion; [Daniel](../../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/daniel.md) is the principal Old Testament companion.

## Suggested reading order

Read the survey lesson, then this overview, then the text of Revelation itself, preferably in a version that sets out the allusions to the Old Testament. Start with chapters 1–5 before entering the seals and beasts.

## References & further reading

- Richard Bauckham, *The Theology of the Book of Revelation* (Intermediate). The best short theological guide.
- G. K. Beale, *The Book of Revelation* (Advanced). A large commentary especially attentive to Old Testament allusions.
- Adela Yarbro Collins, *Crisis and Catharsis* (Advanced). A historical-critical reading of the book in its first-century setting.
