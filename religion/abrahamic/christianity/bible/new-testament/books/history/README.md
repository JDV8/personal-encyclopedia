# History (New Testament)

The New Testament's one narrative book after the Gospels is [Acts](acts.md), the second volume of Luke–Acts. It tells how the witness to the risen Jesus moved from Jerusalem to Rome, and how a Jewish messianic community became a mission that included the Gentiles. The survey lesson [Acts](../../04-acts.md) and the story lessons [early church](../../stories/08-early-church.md) and [Paul's journeys](../../stories/09-pauls-journeys.md) are companions.

## Suggested reading order

Read [Luke](../gospels/luke.md) first, then [Acts](acts.md) straight through, then the [Pauline letters](../pauline-epistles/README.md).

## References & further reading

- Joseph A. Fitzmyer, *The Acts of the Apostles* (Advanced). A major historical-critical commentary.
- F. F. Bruce, *The Book of the Acts* (Intermediate). A learned conservative commentary, strong on historical setting.
- James D. G. Dunn, *Beginning from Jerusalem* (Advanced). A historical account of the earliest church that lives in conversation with Acts.
