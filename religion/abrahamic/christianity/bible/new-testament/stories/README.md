# New Testament stories

The survey lesson [Key stories and parables](../03-key-stories-and-parables.md) is a map of the main episodes. This folder slows down and gives each major cluster its own lesson: the birth narratives, the baptism and temptation, the Sermon on the Mount, miracles, parables, Passion week, the resurrection appearances, the early church in Acts, and Paul's journeys. The four [Gospels](../books/gospels/README.md) and [Acts](../books/history/acts.md) remain the primary texts; these lessons are companions, not replacements, and they keep differences among the sources in view rather than blending them into a single harmonized film.

## Suggested reading order

Read [The four Gospels](../02-the-four-gospels.md) first, then follow the numbered files below. After the resurrection lesson, go to the early church and to Paul's journeys, with [Acts](../books/history/acts.md) open. The [book overviews](../books/README.md) can be used at any point as companions.

## References & further reading

- Raymond E. Brown, *The Birth of the Messiah* and *The Death of the Messiah* (Advanced). The standard large-scale studies of the infancy and Passion narratives.
- Joachim Jeremias, *The Parables of Jesus* (Intermediate). A classic, still worth reading with later caveats.
- N. T. Wright, *The Resurrection of the Son of God* (Advanced). A full historical and theological treatment of the appearance traditions.
- F. F. Bruce, *Paul: Apostle of the Heart Set Free* (Intermediate). A readable companion to the journeys in Acts and the letters.
