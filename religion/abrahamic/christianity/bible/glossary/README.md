# Bible glossary

A glossary for the biblical branch: short, cross-linked entries on people, places, and terms, plus a handful of longer [thematic files](themes/README.md). There are 228 entries in all (101 people, 31 places, 87 terms, 9 themes). Each article is a real paragraph (the themes are short essays) with links into the [book overviews](../old-testament/books/README.md) and [story lessons](../new-testament/stories/README.md). It is a companion, not a substitute for those paths.

Names and terms that appear often in the lessons — prophets, apostles, disciples, [chapter](terms/chapter.md) and [verse](terms/verse.md), [canon](terms/canon.md), [covenant](themes/covenant.md), [Messiah](themes/messiah.md), [Pharisees](terms/pharisees.md) — are here on purpose. Romanization and overlap of names (James, Mary, Herod, John) are distinguished in the titles.

## Suggested reading order

Use this glossary from the lessons and book files as you go. If you want a first tour, read the theme [covenant](themes/covenant.md), then [Messiah](themes/messiah.md), then the people [Abraham](people/abraham.md), [Moses](people/moses.md), [David](people/david.md), and [Jesus](people/jesus.md), then the terms [canon](terms/canon.md) and [Torah](terms/torah.md). After that, follow links.

## References & further reading

- *The Jewish Study Bible* and *The New Oxford Annotated Bible* (Beginner). Best first companions for names and terms in context.
- *The Anchor Yale Bible Dictionary* (Advanced). The standard large reference when an entry here is only a start.
- The process and the rest of this branch are mapped in the [Bible README](../README.md).
