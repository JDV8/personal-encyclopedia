# Nicodemus

Nicodemus, a Pharisee and a "leader of the Jews," comes to Jesus by night in [John](../../new-testament/books/gospels/john.md) 3, hears the word about new birth, later objects to judging Jesus without a hearing, and at the end brings spices for the burial. He is a study in coming toward faith in stages, and a warning against treating "the Pharisees" as a single cartoon.
