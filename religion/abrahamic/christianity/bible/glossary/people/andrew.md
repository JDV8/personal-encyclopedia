# Andrew

Andrew, Peter's brother, is one of the first called in the Synoptics, a fisherman of Galilee, and, in [John](../../new-testament/books/gospels/john.md), a disciple of the Baptist who brings his brother to Jesus. He appears at the feeding and when Greeks ask to see Jesus. Later tradition, outside the New Testament, associates him with wide travels and with a particular martyrdom; those are reception, not Gospel report.
