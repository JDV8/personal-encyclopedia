# Stephen

Stephen, one of the seven appointed in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) 6, is the first Christian martyr in that narrative: a man of wisdom and Spirit, a speaker of a long rereading of Israel's story, stoned after a vision of the Son of Man standing at God's right hand. Saul stands by. His death is the hinge toward a scattering and toward the Gentile mission. See [the early church](../../new-testament/stories/08-early-church.md).
