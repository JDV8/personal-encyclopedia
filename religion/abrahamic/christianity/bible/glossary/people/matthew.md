# Matthew (Levi)

Matthew, also identified with Levi the tax collector, is one of the Twelve and, in early church tradition, the author of the First Gospel. The Gospel itself is anonymous; modern study often dates it later and treats it as the work of a Greek-writing Jewish Christian who used Mark. The call of the tax collector remains, either way, a signature story of unexpected discipleship. See [Matthew](../../new-testament/books/gospels/matthew.md).
