# Micah

Micah of Moresheth, an eighth-century Judahite prophet, announces that Zion shall be plowed as a field, that a ruler shall come from Bethlehem, and that the Lord requires justice, hesed, and humility. Jeremiah's generation later remembered his temple-judgment word. See [Micah](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/micah.md).
