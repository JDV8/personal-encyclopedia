# Herod Antipas

Herod Antipas, tetrarch of Galilee and Perea, is the Herod of Jesus' adult ministry: the killer of [John the Baptist](john-the-baptist.md), a curious and dangerous figure in the Gospels, and, in Luke's Passion, a ruler to whom [Pilate](pontius-pilate.md) sends Jesus. His marriage to Herodias is the occasion of John's rebuke. He is a son of [Herod the Great](herod-the-great.md), not the same man.
