# Timothy

Timothy, a coworker of mixed Jewish-Gentile parentage, is Paul's close delegate in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) and in the undisputed letters, and the named addressee of [1–2 Timothy](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/1-timothy.md), whose Pauline authorship is debated. He is a first-principles figure of a second-generation leader, trusted to carry letters and to stay in difficult churches.

**See also:** [Paul](paul.md), [2 Timothy](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/2-timothy.md)
