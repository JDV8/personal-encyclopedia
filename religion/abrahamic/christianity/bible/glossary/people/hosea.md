# Hosea

Hosea son of Beeri is the eighth-century northern prophet whose book uses a marriage and the naming of children to speak of Israel's unfaithfulness and the Lord's [hesed](../terms/hesed.md). "Not my people" becoming "my people" is later taken up in the New Testament. See [Hosea](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/hosea.md).
