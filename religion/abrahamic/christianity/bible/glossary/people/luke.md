# Luke

Luke, in tradition the beloved physician and companion of Paul, is the attributed author of the Gospel of [Luke](../../new-testament/books/gospels/luke.md) and of [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md). Both books are formally anonymous and addressed to Theophilus. Modern study debates the identification and often dates the two-volume work to the 80s or 90s. The "we" passages in Acts are the main internal argument for a companion-author.
