# John the Baptist

John the Baptist is the wilderness prophet who preaches repentance, baptizes in the Jordan, and, in the Gospels, identifies one greater than himself, in the lesson [baptism and temptation](../../new-testament/stories/02-baptism-and-temptation.md). Josephus also mentions him. Jesus' first public act, in the Synoptics, is to be baptized by him. Herod Antipas has him killed. The evangelists reread him as [Malachi](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/malachi.md)'s messenger and, in some texts, as Elijah.
