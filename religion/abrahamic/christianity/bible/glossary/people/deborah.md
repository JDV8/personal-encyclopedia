# Deborah

Deborah is a prophetess and judge in [Judges](../../old-testament/books/history/judges.md) 4–5 who, with Barak, leads Israel against Sisera; Jael finishes the victory in her tent. The story is told in prose and again in the Song of Deborah, one of the oldest-sounding poems in the Bible. She is a first-principles reminder that "judge" in this book is not only a male military office. See [Joshua and Judges](../../old-testament/06-joshua-and-judges.md).
