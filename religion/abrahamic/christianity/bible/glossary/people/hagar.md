# Hagar

Hagar is Sarah's Egyptian slave, the mother of Ishmael by Abraham, and a figure of the God who hears affliction (the well *Beer-lahai-roi*, the God who sees). Genesis 16 and 21 tell of her flight, an angel's command to return, her expulsion with the boy, and a promise that Ishmael too will be a great nation. Paul allegorizes her in [Galatians](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/galatians.md) 4 in a way that later readers have sometimes used carelessly against Jews or against outsiders; the Genesis story itself is more interested in her survival and in God's attention to a slave.
