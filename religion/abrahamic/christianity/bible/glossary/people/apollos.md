# Apollos

Apollos, a learned Jew of Alexandria, appears in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) 18 as an eloquent preacher who knows only John's baptism until Priscilla and Aquila instruct him, and then in [1 Corinthians](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/1-corinthians.md) as a figure around whom a faction forms. Paul insists that he and Apollos are servants, not rivals. Some have guessed he wrote Hebrews; that remains only a guess.
