# Isaiah

Isaiah son of Amoz is the eighth-century Judahite prophet whose name stands over the long scroll of [Isaiah](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/isaiah.md). Traditional reading takes the whole book as his, including later chapters as prediction; critical study commonly hears also an exilic "Second Isaiah" and a post-exilic "Third." The temple vision of chapter 6, the Immanuel sign, the servant poems, and the word of comfort in chapter 40 have had enormous afterlives in Jewish and Christian reading.
