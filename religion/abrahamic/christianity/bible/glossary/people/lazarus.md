# Lazarus of Bethany

Lazarus, brother of Mary and Martha, is raised from the dead in [John](../../new-testament/books/gospels/john.md) 11, the last and most developed of that Gospel's signs, and a direct occasion, in John's plot, of the decision to kill Jesus. He should not be confused with the Lazarus of Luke's parable. The story is about glory and about a death that still smells, not about a general theory of immortality.
