# Caiaphas

Joseph Caiaphas, high priest (approximately 18–36 CE), presides over the priestly hearing of Jesus in the Gospel Passion narratives and, in John, is remembered for the sentence that it is better for one man to die for the people. He is a historical official under Roman appointment, not a free agent, and teaching the Passion requires that colonial fact.
