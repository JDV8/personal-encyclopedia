# Bathsheba

Bathsheba, wife of Uriah the Hittite and later of David, is the woman at the center of [2 Samuel](../../old-testament/books/history/2-samuel.md) 11–12: seen from the roof, taken, widowed by David's arranged killing of her husband, and then the mother of a child who dies and of Solomon. Nathan's parable forces David to judge himself. Later tradition sometimes blamed her; the Samuel narrative places the crime with the king. In [1 Kings](../../old-testament/books/history/1-kings.md) 1 she is a court actor in Solomon's succession.
