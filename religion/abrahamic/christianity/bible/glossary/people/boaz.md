# Boaz

Boaz is the Bethlehem landowner in [Ruth](../../old-testament/books/history/ruth.md) who protects a Moabite gleaner, accepts the role of [kinsman-redeemer](../terms/goel.md) when a nearer relative declines, and becomes the great-grandfather of David. His courtesy, his public legal care at the gate, and his blessing by the elders are the book's picture of hesed from the other side of the field.
