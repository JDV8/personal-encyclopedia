# Joseph of Nazareth

Joseph, of the line of David, is Mary's husband in the [birth narratives](../../new-testament/stories/01-birth-narratives.md): in Matthew he is the dreamer who takes the child to Egypt; in Luke he travels to Bethlehem for a census and is a quieter presence. He is called a righteous man and a carpenter (or craftsman). He is absent from the adult ministry narratives, which later tradition has often explained by an earlier death, though the text does not say.
