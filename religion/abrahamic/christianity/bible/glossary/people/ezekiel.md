# Ezekiel

Ezekiel son of Buzi is a priest-prophet among the exiles in Babylon, called in a vision of a mobile throne, in the book of [Ezekiel](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/ezekiel.md). He enacts the siege, sees the glory leave the temple and later return, walks a valley of dry bones, and is shown a future house and land. His book is dated with unusual precision and is a primary source for exilic theology of presence.
