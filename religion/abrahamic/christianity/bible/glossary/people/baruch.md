# Baruch

Baruch son of Neriah is Jeremiah's scribe, who writes the scroll that King Jehoiakim burns, and writes it again, in [Jeremiah](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/jeremiah.md) 36. A later book in his name, [Baruch](../../deuterocanon/baruch.md) in the deuterocanon, is a Second Temple work of confession and consolation using that authoritative name. He is a first-principles figure of the written prophetic word, not only the spoken one.
