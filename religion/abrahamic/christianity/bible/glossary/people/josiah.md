# Josiah

Josiah, king of Judah in the late seventh century, is the great reformer of [2 Kings](../../old-testament/books/history/2-kings.md) 22–23: a law book is found in the temple, the covenant is renewed, and high places are pulled down. Historical-critical study often connects that book with [Deuteronomy](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/deuteronomy.md). His death at Megiddo is a shock the tradition never fully explains. The reform, the narrator says, still cannot cancel the judgment already announced.
