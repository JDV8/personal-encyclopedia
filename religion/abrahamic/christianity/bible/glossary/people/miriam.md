# Miriam

Miriam, sister of Moses and Aaron, watches the basket on the Nile, leads a victory song after the sea in [Exodus](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/exodus.md) 15, and is struck with a skin disease when she and Aaron speak against Moses in [Numbers](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/numbers.md) 12, then healed at Moses' prayer. She is called a prophetess. Her death is recorded at Kadesh. The song at the sea is one of the oldest-sounding poems in the Bible, and her name became a standard Jewish name, including for [Mary](mary.md) the mother of Jesus (the same Hebrew name, *Miryam*).
