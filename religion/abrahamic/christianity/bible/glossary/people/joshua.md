# Joshua (son of Nun)

Joshua, Moses' assistant and successor, is one of the two faithful spies and the leader of the entry into the land in the book of [Joshua](../../old-testament/books/history/joshua.md). His name (*Yehoshua*) is the same name that, in later form, becomes Jesus. The book presents him as a model of obedience and as the one who reads the law at Shechem. Historical-critical study debates the conquest model itself; the figure of Joshua remains the narrative hinge between Torah and life in the land. See [Joshua and Judges](../../old-testament/06-joshua-and-judges.md).
