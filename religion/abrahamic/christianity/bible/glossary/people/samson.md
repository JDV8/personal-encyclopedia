# Samson

Samson is a Nazirite judge of huge strength, whose story in [Judges](../../old-testament/books/history/judges.md) 13–16 is a cycle of riddles, Philistine conflict, desire, and a death that pulls down a temple of Dagon. Delilah extracts the secret of his hair. The narrator does not tidy him into a simple hero; the Spirit of the Lord rushes on him, and his life is a string of broken bounds. He is the last major judge before the book's closing chaos.
