# Amos

Amos, a shepherd and dresser of sycamores from Tekoa in Judah, prophesies to the northern kingdom in the eighth century and announces justice and a day of the Lord that is darkness, not light. His "let justice roll down like waters" is among the most quoted prophetic sentences. See [Amos](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/amos.md).
