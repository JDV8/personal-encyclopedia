# Cornelius

Cornelius, a Roman centurion at Caesarea, is the Gentile whose household receives the Spirit while Peter is still speaking, in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) 10–11. The episode, with the vision of the sheet of animals, is the theological center of the book's Gentile threshold: God has already acted, and the church must catch up. See [the early church](../../new-testament/stories/08-early-church.md).
