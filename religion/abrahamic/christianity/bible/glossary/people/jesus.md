# Jesus

Jesus of Nazareth, called the Messiah (Christ) by his followers, is the central figure of the New Testament: a first-century Jew, baptized by John, a preacher of the [kingdom of God](../themes/kingdom-of-god.md), crucified under [Pontius Pilate](pontius-pilate.md), and proclaimed risen by his disciples. The four [Gospels](../../new-testament/books/gospels/README.md) are the primary narrative witnesses; the letters interpret his death and resurrection; [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) tells the movement that followed. Traditional Christian confession adds that he is the incarnate Son; historical study works at the overlap of those claims with a Jewish life under Rome. This encyclopedia's path is to read the sources, not to replace them with a single later formula.
