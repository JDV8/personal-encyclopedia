# Jezebel

Jezebel, a Phoenician princess and Ahab's wife, is portrayed in [1–2 Kings](../../old-testament/books/history/1-kings.md) as the patron of Baal, the enemy of the Lord's prophets, the engineer of Naboth's death, and, at the end, a queen who paints her eyes and is thrown down at Jehu's coming. Later use of her name as a slur, including in [Revelation](../../new-testament/books/prophecy/revelation.md) 2, has often outrun the already harsh biblical portrait. In the Kings narrative she is a political and religious actor, not a free-floating symbol of "wicked women."
