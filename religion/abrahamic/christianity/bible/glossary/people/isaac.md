# Isaac

Isaac is the promised son of Abraham and Sarah, the almost-offered child of [Genesis 22](../../old-testament/stories/01-the-binding-of-isaac.md), the husband of Rebekah, and the father of Esau and Jacob. He is the quietest of the three patriarchs: more acted upon than acting, a digger of wells, a man of a narrower itinerary than his father or his son. The promise nevertheless passes through him. His blessing of Jacob, obtained by a deception he does not fully see, is one of Genesis's most psychologically sharp scenes.
