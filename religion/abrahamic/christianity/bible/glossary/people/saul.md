# Saul

Saul, of Benjamin, is Israel's first king, anointed by Samuel, victorious at Jabesh-gilead, and then slowly unmade by fear, jealousy, and rejection, in [1 Samuel](../../old-testament/books/history/1-samuel.md). His rivalry with David and his death on Mount Gilboa are tragic in a nearly classical sense. The book will not let him be only a villain, or only a hero of the early days. See [Saul, David, and Solomon](../../old-testament/07-saul-david-solomon.md).
