# Barnabas

Barnabas ("son of encouragement"), a Levite of Cyprus, sells a field for the Jerusalem community, vouches for Paul, and is Paul's companion on the first journey in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md), until they separate over Mark. He is a "pillar" of the Gentile mission and a reminder that Paul did not work alone. Tradition later connected him with a letter that modern study does not treat as his.
