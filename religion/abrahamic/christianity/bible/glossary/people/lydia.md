# Lydia

Lydia, a dealer in purple of Thyatira, is the first named convert in Macedonia in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) 16: she hears Paul at a place of prayer by the river in Philippi, is baptized with her household, and offers her house. She is a first-principles figure of a female patron of a European church, at the start of the [Philippian](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/philippians.md) story.
