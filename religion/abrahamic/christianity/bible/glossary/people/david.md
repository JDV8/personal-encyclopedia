# David

David, son of Jesse of Bethlehem, is the shepherd-king, psalmist by later tradition, and the one who receives the dynastic promise of [2 Samuel](../../old-testament/books/history/2-samuel.md) 7, a text that will fund later [messianic](../themes/messiah.md) hope. His rise, the Bathsheba crime, the revolt of Absalom, and the purchase of the temple site are told with unusual candor in Samuel, and more liturgically in [Chronicles](../../old-testament/books/history/1-chronicles.md). The New Testament's "son of David" language is unintelligible without him. See [Saul, David, and Solomon](../../old-testament/07-saul-david-solomon.md).
