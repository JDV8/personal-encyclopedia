# James (the Lord's brother)

James, "the Lord's brother," becomes the leading figure of the Jerusalem church in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) 15 and 21 and in [Galatians](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/galatians.md). Tradition identifies him with the author of the letter of [James](../../new-testament/books/general-epistles/james.md); many scholars debate that ascription. Josephus records his death in the early 60s. He should not be confused with James son of Zebedee.
