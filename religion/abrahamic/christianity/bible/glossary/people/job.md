# Job

Job is the blameless sufferer of the land of Uz in the book of [Job](../../old-testament/books/wisdom-poetry/job.md), whose prose frame and poetic speeches together form the Bible's deepest argument about innocent suffering. He is not an Israelite in the narrative, which already widens the question. Later tradition, including [James](../../new-testament/books/general-epistles/james.md), remembers his endurance; the poems themselves remember his protest as well.
