# Jonathan

Jonathan, Saul's son, is David's covenant friend, a warrior who takes a Philistine garrison, and a man caught between his father and the one he knows is the Lord's future king, in [1 Samuel](../../old-testament/books/history/1-samuel.md). He dies with Saul on Gilboa; David's lament ("how the mighty have fallen") is in part a lament for him. Later reading has sometimes made the friendship a modern romance or a purely political pact; the text itself speaks of a covenant of loyal love.
