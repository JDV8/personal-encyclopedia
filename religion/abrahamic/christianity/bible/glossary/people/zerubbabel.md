# Zerubbabel

Zerubbabel, a Davidic descendant, is the governor who, with the high priest Joshua, leads the rebuilding of the Second Temple in [Ezra](../../old-testament/books/history/ezra.md), [Haggai](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/haggai.md), and [Zechariah](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/zechariah.md). Haggai's "signet ring" word quietly restates hope for David's house under Persia. His later fate is historically unclear; the hope spoken over him outlived the biography.
