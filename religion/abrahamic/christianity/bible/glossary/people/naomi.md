# Naomi

Naomi, whose name means "pleasant," asks to be called Mara ("bitter") when she returns from Moab emptied of husband and sons, in the book of [Ruth](../../old-testament/books/history/ruth.md). She then becomes the strategist of Ruth's appeal to Boaz, and at the end she takes the child Obed to her breast. The book is as much her restoration as Ruth's arrival. Bethlehem, "the house of bread," is full again at the harvest.
