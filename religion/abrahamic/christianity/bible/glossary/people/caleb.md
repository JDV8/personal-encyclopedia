# Caleb

Caleb, of the tribe of Judah (and, in some notices, a Kenizzite), is with Joshua one of the two spies who urge Israel to trust the Lord and enter the land in [Numbers](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/numbers.md) 13–14. He is later granted Hebron in [Joshua](../../old-testament/books/history/joshua.md). In the wilderness tradition he is a model of a "different spirit," an adult of the exodus generation who is allowed to enter what the rest of that generation is denied.
