# Goliath

Goliath of Gath is the Philistine champion felled by the young David in [1 Samuel](../../old-testament/books/history/1-samuel.md) 17, a story of an unexpected election as much as of a sling. A later notice in Samuel that Elhanan killed Goliath has generated a long discussion of sources and of how heroic tales attach to a name. In popular memory the story is a type of the weak defeating the strong; in the book it is also the moment David's public rise begins and Saul's jealousy is born.
