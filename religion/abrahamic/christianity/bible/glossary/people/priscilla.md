# Priscilla (Prisca)

Priscilla, usually named with her husband Aquila and often named first, is a coworker of Paul who, in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) 18, explains the way of God more accurately to Apollos, and who is greeted in [Romans](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/romans.md) 16 and elsewhere as a host of a house church. She is one of the clearest New Testament examples of a woman teaching a male teacher.
