# Balaam

Balaam son of Beor is a non-Israelite seer hired by Balak of Moab to curse Israel in [Numbers](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/numbers.md) 22–24, who instead blesses them, with a famous line about a star and a scepter out of Jacob later read messianically. The talking donkey is the popular surface of a serious claim: blessing is not for sale. Later biblical memory of Balaam is often darker (he is blamed in connection with Baal Peor, and in [2 Peter](../../new-testament/books/general-epistles/2-peter.md) and [Jude](../../new-testament/books/general-epistles/jude.md) he becomes a type of the greedy teacher).
