# Mordecai

Mordecai, Esther's cousin and guardian in the book of [Esther](../../old-testament/books/history/esther.md), refuses to bow to Haman, uncovers a plot against the king, and at the end is exalted in the court. His challenge that Esther may have come to royalty "for such a time as this" is the book's theological hinge, spoken without the divine name.
