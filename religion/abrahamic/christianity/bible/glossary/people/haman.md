# Haman

Haman the Agagite is the official in [Esther](../../old-testament/books/history/esther.md) who, insulted by Mordecai, plots the destruction of the Jews and is hanged on the gallows he had built. His name is blotted out with noise in the later Purim reading of the Megillah. "Agagite" links him, in the story's memory, to Israel's old Amalekite enemy.
