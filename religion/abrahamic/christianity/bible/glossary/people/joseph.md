# Joseph (son of Jacob)

Joseph, son of Rachel, is sold into Egypt by his brothers, rises in Pharaoh's court, and becomes the means by which the family survives a famine, in the closing novella of [Genesis](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/genesis.md). The survey lesson is [Joseph](../../old-testament/04-joseph.md). His claim that "you meant it for harm; God meant it for good" is a theological summary, not a denial of the harm. In later tradition he is a model of wisdom and, in some Christian typological reading, of a rejected one who saves; that typology should not replace the Genesis tale.
