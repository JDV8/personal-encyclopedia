# Nebuchadnezzar

Nebuchadnezzar II of Babylon is the emperor who destroyed Jerusalem in 586 BCE, in [2 Kings](../../old-testament/books/history/2-kings.md) and Jeremiah, and who appears as a dream-troubled king, humbled and then blessing the Most High, in the [court tales of Daniel](../../old-testament/stories/03-the-court-tales-of-daniel.md). [Judith](../../deuterocanon/judith.md) later uses his name in a stylized, historically mixed tale. He is the archetypal destroyer in biblical memory, and also, in Daniel, a king who can be taught.
