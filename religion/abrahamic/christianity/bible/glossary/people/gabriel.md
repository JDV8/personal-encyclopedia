# Gabriel

Gabriel is an angel who interprets visions in [Daniel](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/daniel.md) 8–9 and who, in [Luke](../../new-testament/books/gospels/luke.md) 1, announces the births of John and of Jesus. The name means "God is my strength" (or a similar construction). He is one of the few named angels in the Protestant canon; [Raphael](raphael.md) is named in [Tobit](../../deuterocanon/tobit.md).
