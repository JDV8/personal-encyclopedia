# Phoebe

Phoebe, a deacon (or minister) of the church at Cenchreae and a "patron" (or benefactor) of many, including Paul, is commended in [Romans](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/romans.md) 16:1–2 and is widely thought to have carried the letter to Rome. She is a first-principles reminder that the Pauline mission already included women in public, practical, and probably financial leadership, whatever later debates have made of the verse.
