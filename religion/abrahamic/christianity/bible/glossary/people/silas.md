# Silas (Silvanus)

Silas, also called Silvanus, is a leader in the Jerusalem church who accompanies Paul on the second journey in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md), is imprisoned with him at Philippi, and is named as co-sender in [1 Thessalonians](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/1-thessalonians.md) and as a possible scribe in [1 Peter](../../new-testament/books/general-epistles/1-peter.md) 5:12. He is a bridge figure between Jerusalem and the Pauline mission.
