# Jonah

Jonah son of Amittai, a prophet named in 2 Kings 14, is the reluctant missionary of the book of [Jonah](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/jonah.md): he flees, is swallowed by a great fish, preaches to Nineveh, and resents the city's repentance. Jesus refers to the "sign of Jonah." The book ends with a question about the Lord's pity, not with the prophet's conversion.
