# Ahab

Ahab son of Omri, king of Israel, is the royal antagonist of Elijah's cycle in [1 Kings](../../old-testament/books/history/1-kings.md): married to Jezebel, builder of a Baal temple in Samaria, taker of Naboth's vineyard, and a figure the Deuteronomistic narrator judges more severely than almost any other northern king. Extra-biblical sources (the Mesha Stele, Assyrian records) confirm an Omride king of this period as a real political power. The biblical portrait is theological history, not a modern biography, and it is not therefore useless as history.
