# Sarah

Sarah (first Sarai) is Abraham's wife and, in Genesis, the mother of Isaac, the child of the promise. She laughs at the announcement of a birth in old age, denies the laugh, and later names the child Isaac ("he laughs"). The Hagar and Ishmael stories show her in a harsher light, and later tradition has had to read those chapters with moral care. In [1 Peter](../../new-testament/books/general-epistles/1-peter.md) she is remembered as a model of hope in God. The promise depends, in the narrative, on her body as much as on Abraham's call.
