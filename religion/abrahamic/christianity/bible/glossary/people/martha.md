# Martha

Martha of Bethany, with her sister Mary and her brother Lazarus, hosts Jesus in Luke 10 (the "busy" sister) and in [John](../../new-testament/books/gospels/john.md) 11–12, where she makes a sharp confession of Jesus as the Messiah and the one who is the resurrection and the life, before Lazarus is called out. She is a first-principles reminder that a "practical" disciple in one scene can be a theologian in another.
