# Esther

Esther (Hadassah) is the Jewish queen of a Persian king in the book of [Esther](../../old-testament/books/history/esther.md), who risks an uninvited approach in order to reverse a decree of destruction and whose story underwrites the festival of [Purim](../terms/purim.md). The Hebrew book never names God; the [Greek additions](../../deuterocanon/additions-to-esther.md) do. She is a diaspora heroine, not a returnee to Jerusalem.
