# The beloved disciple

The "disciple whom Jesus loved" is an unnamed figure in the Gospel of [John](../../new-testament/books/gospels/john.md): at the last supper, at the cross, at the empty tomb, and in the epilogue of chapter 21, where he is identified as a witness standing behind the book. Tradition identifies him with [John son of Zebedee](john-the-apostle.md); modern proposals have also included Lazarus or an idealized typological disciple. The Gospel itself will not quite close the question.
