# Thomas

Thomas, one of the Twelve, is best known from [John](../../new-testament/books/gospels/john.md) 20, where he refuses to believe without touching the risen Jesus and then confesses "my Lord and my God." He also appears in John 11 and 14. Later tradition, outside the New Testament, associates him with a mission to the East; the "doubting" label is a later shorthand that underplays the book's interest in a confession after honest resistance.
