# Samuel

Samuel is the prophet, priestly figure, and judge who marks the turn from the period of the judges to the monarchy in [1 Samuel](../../old-testament/books/history/1-samuel.md). Dedicated by Hannah, called as a boy at Shiloh, he anoints first Saul and then David. He is the Lord's word in a time when that word is said to be rare, and he remains a severe critic of kingship even as he administers it. See [Saul, David, and Solomon](../../old-testament/07-saul-david-solomon.md).
