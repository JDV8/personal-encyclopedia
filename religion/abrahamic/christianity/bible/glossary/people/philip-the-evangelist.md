# Philip (the evangelist)

Philip, one of the seven of [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) 6, preaches in Samaria and to the Ethiopian official on the road, and later hosts Paul at Caesarea. He should not be automatically identified with Philip the apostle, one of the Twelve. His story is a first threshold of the witness beyond Judea. See [the early church](../../new-testament/stories/08-early-church.md).
