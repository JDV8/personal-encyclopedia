# Elisha

Elisha, Elijah's successor, receives the dropped mantle and a "double share," and then fills [2 Kings](../../old-testament/books/history/2-kings.md) 2–13 with a long cycle of signs: oil for a widow, a raised child, Naaman's healing, an army struck with blindness, and the political anointing of Jehu. He is more embedded in daily and royal life than Elijah, and no less a bearer of the word. Jesus cites the Naaman story in [Luke](../../new-testament/books/gospels/luke.md) 4. See [Elijah and Elisha](../../old-testament/stories/02-elijah-and-elisha.md).
