# Paul (Saul of Tarsus)

Paul, also called Saul, is a Jewish Pharisee, a persecutor of the early movement, then the most influential missionary and letter-writer of the New Testament. The undisputed letters are the earliest Christian documents; [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) narrates his [journeys](../../new-testament/stories/09-pauls-journeys.md) and his path to Rome. His gospel of a crucified and risen Messiah for Jews and Gentiles, apart from works of the law as a requirement of membership, has shaped — and divided — later Christianity. See [Paul's letters](../../new-testament/05-pauls-letters.md) and the [Pauline epistles](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/README.md).
