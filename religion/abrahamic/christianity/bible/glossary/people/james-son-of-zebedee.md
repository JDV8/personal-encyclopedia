# James (son of Zebedee)

James son of Zebedee, with his brother John, is one of the inner three disciples (the transfiguration, Gethsemane) and is killed by Herod Agrippa I in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) 12, the first of the Twelve whose death is narrated. Jesus names the pair "sons of thunder." He should not be confused with [James the Lord's brother](james-the-brother.md).
