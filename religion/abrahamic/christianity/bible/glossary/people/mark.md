# Mark (John Mark)

John Mark, a companion of Barnabas and, at times, of Paul and, in tradition, of Peter, is the traditional author of the Gospel of [Mark](../../new-testament/books/gospels/mark.md). The Gospel is anonymous; Papias already links it to Peter's preaching. He is also the occasion of Paul and Barnabas's split in Acts 15, and is later called useful in [2 Timothy](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/2-timothy.md).
