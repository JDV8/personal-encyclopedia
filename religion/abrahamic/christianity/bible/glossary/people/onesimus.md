# Onesimus

Onesimus, whose name means "useful," is the enslaved man at the center of [Philemon](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/philemon.md), whom Paul sends back as "more than a slave, a beloved brother," and who is also commended in [Colossians](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/colossians.md) 4:9. Later tradition made him a bishop; that is reception. The letter is a primary and difficult text for early Christian ethics of slavery.
