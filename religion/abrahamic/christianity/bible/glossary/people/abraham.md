# Abraham

Abraham (first called Abram) is the patriarch to whom the Lord promises a land, a people, and a blessing for the families of the earth, in the central cycle of [Genesis](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/genesis.md) 12–25. Traditional Jewish, Christian, and Muslim memory honors him as a father of faith; the New Testament rereads him especially in [Paul](paul.md) and in [James](james-the-brother.md)'s letter. The covenant of circumcision, the birth of Isaac in old age, and the [binding of Isaac](../../old-testament/stories/01-the-binding-of-isaac.md) are the load-bearing scenes. His name-change and the promise are treated more fully in the survey [Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob](../../old-testament/03-abraham-isaac-jacob.md).
