# Raphael

Raphael ("God heals") is the angel who travels in disguise with Tobias, binds a demon, and reveals himself as one of the seven who stand ready, in the deuterocanonical book of [Tobit](../../deuterocanon/tobit.md). He is a primary Second Temple text for named angels and for a cosmology in which healing and exorcism belong together.
