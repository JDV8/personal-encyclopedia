# Herod the Great

Herod the Great, client king of Judea under Rome (d. 4 BCE), is the builder of the expanded Second Temple and of palaces and fortresses, and, in [Matthew](../../new-testament/books/gospels/matthew.md) 2, the king who seeks the child's life in Bethlehem. Josephus confirms a ruthless and impressive reign; the massacre of the innocents is not attested outside Matthew. He should not be confused with his son [Herod Antipas](herod-antipas.md).
