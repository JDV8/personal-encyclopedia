# Antiochus IV Epiphanes

Antiochus IV Epiphanes, a Seleucid king of the 170s–160s BCE, is the persecutor of [1–2 Maccabees](../../deuterocanon/1-maccabees.md) and, on the usual critical reading, the "little horn" of [Daniel](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/daniel.md) 8 and 11. His desecration of the temple and ban on Jewish law provoked the Maccabean revolt and the festival of [Hanukkah](../terms/hanukkah.md). He is a first-principles figure for later Christian and Jewish images of a blasphemous ruler.
