# Delilah

Delilah, in [Judges](../../old-testament/books/history/judges.md) 16, is the woman in the Sorek valley who, paid by the Philistine lords, presses Samson until he tells her the secret of his hair and is taken. The narrator does not give her inner life; later reception has filled that gap with a range of portraits, from villain to victim. In the book she is the last and successful test of a Nazirite who has already crossed many borders.
