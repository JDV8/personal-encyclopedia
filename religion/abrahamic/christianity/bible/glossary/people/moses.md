# Moses

Moses is the central human figure of the Torah: drawn out of the Nile, called at the bush, sent to Pharaoh, leader of the [exodus](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/exodus.md), mediator of the covenant at Sinai, and the one who dies in sight of the land in [Deuteronomy](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/deuteronomy.md). Traditional authorship of the Pentateuch is ascribed to him; scholarly study describes a long compositional history behind the books that bear his teaching. Later Jewish memory calls him the greatest of the prophets; the New Testament compares and contrasts him with Jesus, especially in [Matthew](../../new-testament/books/gospels/matthew.md) and [Hebrews](../../new-testament/books/general-epistles/hebrews.md).
