# Peter (Simon Peter)

Simon, called Peter (Cephas, "rock"), is the most prominent of the Twelve: first among the disciples in many lists, the confessor in [Matthew](../../new-testament/books/gospels/matthew.md) 16, the denier in the Passion, a primary witness in the appearance traditions and in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) 1–12, and the addressee (or attributed author) of [1–2 Peter](../../new-testament/books/general-epistles/1-peter.md). Paul names him a "pillar" in [Galatians](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/galatians.md). Later claims about the papacy are a long reception of Matthew 16, not a first-century office described in full in the New Testament.
