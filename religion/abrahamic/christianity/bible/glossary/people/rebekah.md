# Rebekah

Rebekah, fetched from Abraham's kin in [Genesis](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/genesis.md) 24, is Isaac's wife and the mother of Esau and Jacob. She receives an oracle that the older will serve the younger, favors Jacob, and engineers the stolen blessing. The book does not moralize her in a simple way; it shows a woman acting inside a promise she has heard, at a cost to the household. She is buried in the cave of Machpelah with the other ancestors.
