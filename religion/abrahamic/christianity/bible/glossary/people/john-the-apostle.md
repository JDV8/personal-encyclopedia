# John (son of Zebedee)

John son of Zebedee is, in the Synoptics, one of the inner three, and, in church tradition, the author of the Fourth Gospel, the Johannine letters, and sometimes Revelation. Modern scholarship often distinguishes some or all of those authors and speaks of a Johannine tradition or school. The "beloved disciple" of [John](../../new-testament/books/gospels/john.md) is traditionally identified with him; the Gospel itself leaves the figure unnamed. See [beloved disciple](beloved-disciple.md).
