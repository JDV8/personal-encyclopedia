# Elijah

Elijah the Tishbite is the ninth-century prophet of the drought, Carmel, Horeb, and the whirlwind, in [1–2 Kings](../../old-testament/books/history/1-kings.md). The closer look is [Elijah and Elisha](../../old-testament/stories/02-elijah-and-elisha.md). Because he does not die in the ordinary way, [Malachi](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/malachi.md) can promise his return, and the [Gospels](../../new-testament/books/gospels/README.md) can set him in debates about [John the Baptist](john-the-baptist.md) and on the mountain of transfiguration. He is the type, in later memory, of the confronting prophet.
