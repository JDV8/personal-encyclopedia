# Manasseh

Manasseh, Hezekiah's son, reigns long and, in [2 Kings](../../old-testament/books/history/2-kings.md), is blamed more than any other Judahite king for the coming exile. [2 Chronicles](../../old-testament/books/history/2-chronicles.md) 33 adds a captivity in Babylon and a repentance, and the later [Prayer of Manasseh](../../deuterocanon/prayer-of-manasseh.md) supplies words for that repentance. The two portraits are a first-principles lesson in how tradition can both harden a verdict and imagine mercy for the worst case.
