# Cyrus

Cyrus II of Persia, who took Babylon in 539 BCE, is named in [Isaiah](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/isaiah.md) 45 as the Lord's "anointed" and in [Ezra](../../old-testament/books/history/ezra.md) and [2 Chronicles](../../old-testament/books/history/2-chronicles.md) as the king who decrees that exiles may return and rebuild the temple. He is a first-principles case of a foreign emperor used, in Israel's theology, as an instrument, without becoming a worshiper of Israel's God in the narrative.
