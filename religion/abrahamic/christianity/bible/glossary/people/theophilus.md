# Theophilus

Theophilus ("friend of God," or "loved by God") is the addressee of [Luke](../../new-testament/books/gospels/luke.md) and [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md), called "most excellent" in the Gospel's preface, a title that may indicate a real patron of some rank, or a symbolic reader, or both. Either way, the two-volume work presents itself as an orderly account for someone already instructed.

**See also:** [Luke](luke.md), [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md)
