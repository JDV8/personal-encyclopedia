# Jude (brother of James)

Jude (Judas), who calls himself a brother of James in the letter of [Jude](../../new-testament/books/general-epistles/jude.md), is traditionally identified with a brother of Jesus. The short letter warns against infiltrating teachers and quotes 1 Enoch. The name is also that of another of the Twelve (not Iscariot); the letter's self-description is the first thing to notice.
