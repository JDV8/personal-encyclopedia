# Mary Magdalene

Mary of Magdala is a witness of the [crucifixion](../terms/crucifixion.md) and, in all four Gospels, of the empty tomb, and, in John and Matthew, of a first appearance of the risen Jesus. Luke 8 notes that seven demons had gone out of her. Later Western tradition often identified her, without Gospel warrant, with the sinful woman of Luke 7 or with Mary of Bethany; that conflation has been widely corrected in modern study. She is, in the narratives, an apostle to the apostles in all but the later official title.
