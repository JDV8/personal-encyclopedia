# Nehemiah

Nehemiah, cupbearer to a Persian king and later governor, rebuilds Jerusalem's wall, confronts debt-slavery, and, with Ezra, leads a covenant renewal, in the book of [Nehemiah](../../old-testament/books/history/nehemiah.md). His first-person "memoir" is one of the most distinctive voices in the Bible: pragmatic, prayerful, and concerned to be remembered.
