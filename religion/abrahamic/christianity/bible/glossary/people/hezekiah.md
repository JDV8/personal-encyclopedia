# Hezekiah

Hezekiah, king of Judah, is remembered in [2 Kings](../../old-testament/books/history/2-kings.md) 18–20, [Isaiah](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/isaiah.md) 36–39, and [2 Chronicles](../../old-testament/books/history/2-chronicles.md) as a reformer and as the king who trusts the Lord in the Assyrian crisis of 701 BCE, when Sennacherib invades but does not take Jerusalem. The Chronicler expands his Passover. His last years (the Babylonian envoys, the promised exile) already look toward 586. He is one of the few kings the Deuteronomistic narrator almost fully praises.
