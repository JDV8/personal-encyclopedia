# Judas Maccabeus

Judas, called Maccabeus ("the Hammer"), son of Mattathias, is the principal military leader of the revolt in [1 Maccabees](../../deuterocanon/1-maccabees.md) and [2 Maccabees](../../deuterocanon/2-maccabees.md). He rededicates the temple in 164 BCE, makes a treaty with Rome, and dies in battle. Later Hasmonean rule descends from his family. He is not in the Hebrew or Protestant canons as a biblical hero, and he is indispensable background to the [Second Temple](../terms/second-temple.md) world of the New Testament.
