# Judas Iscariot

Judas Iscariot is the disciple who betrays Jesus to the authorities, in all four Gospels, for a payment (thirty pieces of silver in Matthew), with a kiss in the Synoptics, and who then dies, in two different accounts (Matthew's hanging; Acts' fall in a field). The traditions about his motives (money, Satan's entry, disappointment) should not be blended without notice. His name became a byword; teaching the story requires care not to slide from one man into a slur on a people.
