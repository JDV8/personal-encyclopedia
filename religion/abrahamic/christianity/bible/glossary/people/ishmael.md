# Ishmael

Ishmael is Abraham's first son, born to Hagar, circumcised, and later sent away into the wilderness, where God hears him and promises him a future as the father of twelve princes. Genesis is careful both to distinguish him from the Isaac-promise and to refuse to discard him. Later Islamic tradition honors him in connection with Abraham and the Kaaba, a reception this encyclopedia notes without narrating here, because that story belongs in a still-unwritten Islam branch.
