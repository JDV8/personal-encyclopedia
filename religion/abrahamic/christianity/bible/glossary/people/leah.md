# Leah

Leah is Laban's elder daughter, given to Jacob by a deception that mirrors Jacob's own, and the mother of six of the twelve tribes, including Judah and Levi, as well as of Dinah. The narrative of her unloved status and of the naming of her sons ("the Lord has seen my affliction") is one of Genesis's most unsparing household portraits. Later Israel nevertheless comes, in large part, from her, not only from the favored Rachel.
