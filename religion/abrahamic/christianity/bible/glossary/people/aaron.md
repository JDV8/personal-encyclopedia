# Aaron

Aaron, Moses' brother of the tribe of Levi, becomes the first high priest in the Exodus–Leviticus narrative: his mouth is given to help Moses, he shares in the signs before Pharaoh, he fails in the golden calf episode, and he is ordained in [Leviticus](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/leviticus.md) 8–9. The death of his sons Nadab and Abihu, and the later budding of his staff, belong to the same priestly drama. Later Israelite priesthood traces itself to him. See also [priest](../terms/priest.md) and [high priest](../terms/high-priest.md).
