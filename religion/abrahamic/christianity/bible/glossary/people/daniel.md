# Daniel

Daniel is the Judean courtier and seer of the book of [Daniel](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/daniel.md): interpreter of dreams in Babylon and Persia in the tales, and recipient of visions of beasts, weeks, and resurrection in the second half. Traditional reading takes him as a sixth-century figure; many scholars date the book's final form to the 160s BCE. The [court tales](../../old-testament/stories/03-the-court-tales-of-daniel.md) and the [Greek additions](../../deuterocanon/additions-to-daniel.md) belong to the same cycle.
