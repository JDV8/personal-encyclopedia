# Ezra

Ezra the priest and scribe, in the book of [Ezra](../../old-testament/books/history/ezra.md) and in [Nehemiah](../../old-testament/books/history/nehemiah.md) 8, is commissioned under Persia to teach the Torah in the restored community, and is remembered as a second Moses, a restorer of the law. The public reading in Nehemiah 8 is a foundation scene for later Judaism as a people of a book. His policies on intermarriage are among the harder pages of the restoration.
