# Ruth

Ruth the Moabite is the central figure of the book of [Ruth](../../old-testament/books/history/ruth.md): a widow who clings to Naomi, gleans in Boaz's field, and becomes, by a kinsman-redeemer marriage, an ancestor of David. Her "your people shall be my people, and your God my God" is one of the Bible's most quoted pledges. The book is a quiet argument about belonging and [hesed](../terms/hesed.md) in the days of the judges.
