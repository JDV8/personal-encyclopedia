# Nathan

Nathan is the prophet at David's court who brings the promise of a "house" in [2 Samuel](../../old-testament/books/history/2-samuel.md) 7 and later the parable of the poor man's lamb after the Bathsheba affair. He is also involved in Solomon's accession in 1 Kings 1. As a court prophet he is not a chaplain; he is the one who can say "you are the man." The Davidic [covenant](../themes/covenant.md) is spoken through him.
