# Satan (the satan, the devil)

"The satan" in [Job](../../old-testament/books/wisdom-poetry/job.md) is an accuser in the heavenly court, not yet the later fully dualistic devil. In the New Testament, Satan (or the devil, the tempter, the evil one) is the opponent of Jesus in the [wilderness](../../new-testament/stories/02-baptism-and-temptation.md), a personal power of evil, and, in some texts, the dragon of [Revelation](../../new-testament/books/prophecy/revelation.md). Later Christian and Jewish demonology should not be read back unchanged into every Old Testament use of the word.
