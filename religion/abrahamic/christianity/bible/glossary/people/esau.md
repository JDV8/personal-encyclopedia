# Esau

Esau, the elder twin, a hunter, sells his birthright for a meal and is deprived of the blessing, later meeting Jacob in a tense reconciliation. He is the ancestor of [Edom](../places/edom.md), a kinship that [Obadiah](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/obadiah.md) and other prophets will remember as a moral charge when Edom stands against Judah. Genesis itself is more intimate than later oracle: Esau weeps, prospers in Seir, and is not simply a villain.
