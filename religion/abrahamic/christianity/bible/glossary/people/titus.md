# Titus

Titus, a Gentile coworker whom Paul refused to have circumcised (Galatians 2), is the agent of reconciliation in [2 Corinthians](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/2-corinthians.md) and the addressee of the Pastoral letter to [Titus](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/titus.md). He represents, in the undisputed letters, the freedom of the Gentile mission in person.

**See also:** [Paul](paul.md), [Galatians](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/galatians.md)
