# Rachel

Rachel is Jacob's favored wife, the mother of Joseph and, at the cost of her life, of Benjamin. She steals her father's household gods, is long barren, and dies on the road and is buried near Bethlehem, a grave Jeremiah later hears weeping. Matthew's [birth narrative](../../new-testament/stories/01-birth-narratives.md) will quote that weeping over the children of Bethlehem. In the structure of Genesis she is the beloved, and her first son is the one through whom the family will be kept alive in Egypt.
