# Hannah

Hannah, long barren, prays at Shiloh for a son, is mistaken by Eli for a drunkard, and bears Samuel, whom she lends to the Lord. Her song in [1 Samuel](../../old-testament/books/history/1-samuel.md) 2, about the poor raised up and the mighty brought down, is a model for Mary's Magnificat in [Luke](../../new-testament/books/gospels/luke.md). She is a first-principles figure of prayer that changes a household and, in the book's structure, a kingdom.
