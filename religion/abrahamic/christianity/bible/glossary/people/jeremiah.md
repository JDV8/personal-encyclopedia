# Jeremiah

Jeremiah son of Hilkiah is the prophet of Judah's last decades, of the temple sermon, of a burned scroll, of a [new covenant](../terms/new-covenant.md), and of a letter to the exiles, in the book of [Jeremiah](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/jeremiah.md). His "confessions" show a prophet at war with his own calling. Tradition also links him, less securely, with [Lamentations](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/lamentations.md) and with the later [Baruch](baruch.md) literature.
