# Mary (mother of Jesus)

Mary of Nazareth is the mother of Jesus, central to the [birth narratives](../../new-testament/stories/01-birth-narratives.md) of Matthew and especially Luke (the annunciation, the Magnificat, the temple scenes). She appears at Cana and at the cross in [John](../../new-testament/books/gospels/john.md), and in prayer in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) 1. Later doctrines (perpetual virginity, immaculate conception, assumption) are receptions that go beyond the New Testament text and should be taught as such, without erasing her actual role in the Gospels as a hearer and a witness.
