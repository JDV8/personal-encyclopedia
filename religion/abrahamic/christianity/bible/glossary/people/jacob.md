# Jacob

Jacob, later named Israel, is the younger twin who buys a birthright and steals a blessing, flees to Laban, becomes the husband of Leah and Rachel, fathers twelve sons (and a daughter, Dinah), wrestles at the Jabbok, and goes down to Egypt in the [Joseph](joseph.md) story. The change of name at the Jabbok is the origin of the people's name. His life is the longest character study in Genesis: deceiver, beloved, wounded, and the one who blesses Pharaoh and his own sons before he dies. See [Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob](../../old-testament/03-abraham-isaac-jacob.md).
