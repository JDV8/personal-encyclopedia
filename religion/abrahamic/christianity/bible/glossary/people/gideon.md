# Gideon

Gideon, also called Jerubbaal, is a judge in [Judges](../../old-testament/books/history/judges.md) 6–8 who is called from a winepress, reduces his army at the Lord's command, defeats Midian, refuses a crown, and then makes an ephod that becomes a snare. His story is a study in reluctant faith, unexpected victory, and the failure that can follow success. Abimelech, his son, is a darker sequel in chapter 9.
