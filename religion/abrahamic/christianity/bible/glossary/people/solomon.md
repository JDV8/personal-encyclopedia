# Solomon

Solomon, son of David and Bathsheba, is the king of wisdom, temple-building, and later excess in [1 Kings](../../old-testament/books/history/1-kings.md) 1–11. He asks for an understanding mind, judges the disputed child, builds the [temple](../themes/temple.md), and then multiplies horses, gold, and foreign wives, with high places that the book treats as the seed of the split. Wisdom literature is traditionally ascribed to him ([Proverbs](../../old-testament/books/wisdom-poetry/proverbs.md), [Ecclesiastes](../../old-testament/books/wisdom-poetry/ecclesiastes.md), the [Song](../../old-testament/books/wisdom-poetry/song-of-songs.md), and, in the deuterocanon, the [Wisdom of Solomon](../../deuterocanon/wisdom-of-solomon.md)); scholarly study treats much of that as later tradition in his name.
