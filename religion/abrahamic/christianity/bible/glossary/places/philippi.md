# Philippi

Philippi, a Roman colony in Macedonia, is the first European church in the Acts narrative (the river, Lydia, a prison, a jailer) and the addressee of [Philippians](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/philippians.md), a warm prison letter of joy and of the Christ hymn. See [Paul's journeys](../../new-testament/stories/09-pauls-journeys.md).
