# Alexandria

Alexandria in Egypt was the great Hellenistic Jewish city of the Septuagint's legend, of a large diaspora, and, probably, of the [Wisdom of Solomon](../../deuterocanon/wisdom-of-solomon.md) and of Apollos's formation. It is not a New Testament narrative setting of length, but it is part of the world that made a Greek Bible and a philosophically voiced Judaism possible.
