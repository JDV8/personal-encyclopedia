# Sinai (Horeb)

Sinai, also called Horeb especially in Deuteronomy and in the Elijah story, is the mountain of the covenant, the Decalogue, and the making of a people, in [Exodus](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/exodus.md). The exact historical peak is debated; the theological geography is not: this is where the Lord speaks and Israel is bound. Elijah returns to Horeb in [1 Kings](../../old-testament/books/history/1-kings.md) 19.
