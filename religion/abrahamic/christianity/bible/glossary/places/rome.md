# Rome

Rome is the imperial power of the New Testament world: the census of Luke, the prefect [Pilate](../people/pontius-pilate.md), Paul's appeal, and the destination of [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md). [Revelation](../../new-testament/books/prophecy/revelation.md)'s Babylon is, in the first instance, Rome. The New Testament's attitude is not single: there is a call to honor the emperor in 1 Peter, and a visionary unmasking of the beast.
