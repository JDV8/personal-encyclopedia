# Shiloh

Shiloh is the sanctuary of the ark in the period of the judges and of young Samuel, later ruined (Jeremiah uses it as a warning to those who trust the Jerusalem temple). It is a first-principles fact that the Lord's house in Israel's story is not, at first, on Zion, and that a sanctuary can fall.
