# Galilee

Galilee, the northern region of the land, is the main field of Jesus' ministry in the Synoptic Gospels: Capernaum, the lake, the villages, and a mixed Jewish population under Herod Antipas. Isaiah 9's "Galilee of the nations" is quoted in Matthew. Mark's earliest ending sends the disciples back there to meet the risen one.
