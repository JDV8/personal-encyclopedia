# Shechem

Shechem is a covenant place: Abraham's first altar in the land, the terrible story of Dinah, Joshua's last assembly, and, later, the site of the northern kingdom's break in 1 Kings 12. It is a first-principles reminder that "the land" has particular towns with memories, not only a vague holy geography.
