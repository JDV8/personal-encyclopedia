# Nineveh

Nineveh, capital of [Assyria](assyria.md), is the great city of the book of [Jonah](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/jonah.md), which receives mercy, and of [Nahum](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/nahum.md), which announces its fall (612 BCE). The two books belong together as a canon-wide argument about empire and pity. Tobit's tale is also set there in the diaspora.
