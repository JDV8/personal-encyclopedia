# Zion

Zion is, strictly, a hill in Jerusalem and, in poetry and prophecy, a name for the city, the temple mount, and the people. Daughter Zion weeps in [Lamentations](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/lamentations.md) and is comforted in [Isaiah](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/isaiah.md) and [Baruch](../../deuterocanon/baruch.md). The word should not be used as a modern political slogan without first hearing its biblical range.
