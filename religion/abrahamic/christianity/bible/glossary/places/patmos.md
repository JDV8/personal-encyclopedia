# Patmos

Patmos is the Aegean island where "John" receives the visions of [Revelation](../../new-testament/books/prophecy/revelation.md), "because of the word of God and the testimony of Jesus," usually taken as a place of exile or confinement. The book's letters are addressed not to Patmos but to seven churches of the mainland.
