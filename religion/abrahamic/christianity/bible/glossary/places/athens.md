# Athens

Athens is the setting of Paul's Areopagus speech in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) 17, a first-principles scene of the gospel in a philosophical city: an altar to an unknown god, a quotation of a poet, and a split in the audience at the mention of resurrection. The speech should be read as Luke's portrayal of mission, not as a stenograph.
