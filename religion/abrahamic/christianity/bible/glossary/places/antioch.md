# Antioch (on the Orontes)

Antioch in Syria is, in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md), the first great mixed church, the place where disciples were first called Christians, and the sending base of Paul's journeys. Galatians 2 also records a sharp conflict there over table fellowship. It is a first-principles city of the Gentile mission, not only of later patriarchates.
