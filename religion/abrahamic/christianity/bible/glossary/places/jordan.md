# The Jordan

The Jordan River is the border of the land in the Joshua story (Israel crosses on dry ground, as at the sea) and the water of [John's baptism](../../new-testament/stories/02-baptism-and-temptation.md) and of Naaman's washing in 2 Kings. It is a first-principles boundary and a first-principles font: entry and repentance share a geography.
