# Jerusalem

Jerusalem, also called Zion, is David's capital, the site of the [temple](../themes/temple.md), the city destroyed in 586 BCE and again in 70 CE, and the narrative center of the [Passion](../../new-testament/stories/06-passion-week.md) and of the beginning of [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md). In the Psalms and the prophets it is both a place and a theological name. Later Christian and Jewish imaginations of a heavenly or new Jerusalem (see [Revelation](../../new-testament/books/prophecy/revelation.md) 21) depend on the earthly city first.
