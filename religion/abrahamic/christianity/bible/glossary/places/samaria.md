# Samaria

Samaria is the capital of the northern kingdom, then the name of the region and of a people whom later Judahite sources often treat as mixed and rival (2 Kings 17; Ezra–Nehemiah). In the New Testament, Jesus' conversation in John 4 and the mission in Acts 8 make Samaria a theological threshold. The rivalry is old; the New Testament does not invent it.
