# Edom

Edom, the land of Esau southeast of Judah, is a brother-people and often a rival, judged in [Obadiah](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/obadiah.md) for violence in the day of Jerusalem's fall. The kinship is the moral point: this is not a generic enemy. Later typological uses (Edom as Rome, or as empire) should be labeled as reception.
