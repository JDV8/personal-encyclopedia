# Gethsemane

Gethsemane, an olive grove or garden on the Mount of Olives, is the place of Jesus' prayer and arrest in the Synoptic [Passion](../../new-testament/stories/06-passion-week.md) (John has a garden, without the name). The failure of the disciples to watch, and the prayer that a cup might pass, are among the most human scenes in the Gospels.
