# The Mount of Olives

The Mount of Olives, east of Jerusalem, is the ridge of a royal flight in 2 Samuel, of a coming in Zechariah 14, of Jesus' eschatological discourse and entry, of Gethsemane, and, in Acts 1, of the ascension. It is a first-principles example of a place that accumulates meaning by reuse.
