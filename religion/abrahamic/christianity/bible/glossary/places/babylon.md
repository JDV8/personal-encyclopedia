# Babylon

Babylon is the empire that destroys Jerusalem in 586 BCE, the site of the exile, and, later, a symbolic name for a proud, persecuting city — already in some prophetic oracles, and then in [1 Peter](../../new-testament/books/general-epistles/1-peter.md) 5:13 and especially [Revelation](../../new-testament/books/prophecy/revelation.md) 17–18, where it almost certainly codes for Rome. The historical city and the typological city should both be kept in view.
