# Jericho

Jericho is the first city taken in the book of [Joshua](../../old-testament/books/history/joshua.md), with Rahab's household spared, and, in the Gospels, a setting of healings and of Zacchaeus in Luke. Archaeology and the conquest narrative have a famous, unfinished conversation. The city's later life in the Gospels is simply that of a road town on the way to Jerusalem.
