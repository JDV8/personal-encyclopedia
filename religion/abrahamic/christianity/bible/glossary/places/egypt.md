# Egypt

Egypt is, in the Bible, the land of Joseph's rise, of later bondage, of the [exodus](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/exodus.md), and, repeatedly, a political temptation (alliances the prophets condemn). Matthew's infancy flight "out of Egypt" rereads Hosea. It is not only a symbol of "the world"; it is a real empire with a long, particular relationship to Israel.
