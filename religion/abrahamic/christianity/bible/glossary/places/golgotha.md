# Golgotha (Calvary)

Golgotha, "the place of a skull," called Calvary in Latin-derived English, is the site of the crucifixion, outside the city according to the Gospels and to ordinary Roman practice. The exact modern spot is debated (Church of the Holy Sepulchre versus other proposals). The theological geography is clearer: a public, shameful death at a place of execution, not a hidden shrine.
