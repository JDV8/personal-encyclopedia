# Capernaum

Capernaum, on the sea of Galilee, is Jesus' base of ministry in the Synoptics: a synagogue, a house, a door crowded with the sick, and a town later warned in woes. Many of the [miracle](../../new-testament/stories/04-miracles.md) stories are set here. It is ordinary geography made famous by a ministry, not a capital.

**See also:** [Galilee](galilee.md), [Miracles](../../new-testament/stories/04-miracles.md)
