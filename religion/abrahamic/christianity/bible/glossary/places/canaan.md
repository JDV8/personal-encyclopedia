# Canaan

Canaan is the land promised to the ancestors and entered in [Joshua](../../old-testament/books/history/joshua.md), already inhabited, in the narrative, by peoples Israel is told to displace. The moral and historical problems of that command are real and should not be preached away. In later usage "Canaanite" can also mean a cultural or religious foil. The first task is to hear the land as a gift with a cost, in the book's own terms, and then to read the prophets' later critiques of Israel's own violence and idolatry.
