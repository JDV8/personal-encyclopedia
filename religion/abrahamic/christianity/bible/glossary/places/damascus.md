# Damascus

Damascus is the city of Paul's call or conversion in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) 9 (and in his own brief notices in Galatians and 2 Corinthians): a light, a voice, a blindness, and a baptism. The "road to Damascus" has become proverbial; the primary texts remain shorter and stranger than the proverb.
