# Corinth

Corinth, a Roman colony and port, is the church of the long, messy correspondence of [1–2 Corinthians](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/1-corinthians.md), founded in Acts 18. Status, rhetoric, sex, gifts, and the Lord's table are all local issues there. Paul probably writes [Romans](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/romans.md) from this city or its vicinity.
