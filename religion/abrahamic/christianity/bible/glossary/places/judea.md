# Judea

Judea (Judah in older periods) is the southern region around Jerusalem, the surviving kingdom after the north falls, the Persian province of Yehud, and, in the New Testament, the Roman-period district of the temple and of the Passion. "Jew" and "Judean" are historically related words. See [Judah](../terms/judah.md).
