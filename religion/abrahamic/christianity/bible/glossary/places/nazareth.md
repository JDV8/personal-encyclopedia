# Nazareth

Nazareth of Galilee is the town of Jesus' upbringing, so much so that he is called "Jesus of Nazareth" and, in early hostile or descriptive use, a "Nazorean." It is obscure in earlier biblical geography, which is part of the point of John 1:46. The [birth narratives](../../new-testament/stories/01-birth-narratives.md) get the family there by different routes in Matthew and Luke.
