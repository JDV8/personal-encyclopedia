# Bethlehem

Bethlehem of Judah, "house of bread," is the town of [Ruth](../../old-testament/books/history/ruth.md), the city of David's origin, the promised place of a ruler in [Micah](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/micah.md) 5, and, in Matthew and Luke, the birthplace of Jesus; see [birth narratives](../../new-testament/stories/01-birth-narratives.md). A small town in the literature, it carries a large memory.

**See also:** [David](../people/david.md), [Micah](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/micah.md)
