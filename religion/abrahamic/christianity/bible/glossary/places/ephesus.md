# Ephesus

Ephesus, a major city of Asia, is the site of a long Pauline stay in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) 19, a likely setting or addressee of several letters (1 Corinthians is written from there; Ephesians may be a circular letter; the Pastorals mention it; Revelation addresses its church first). It is a first-principles urban stage for the early movement: temple of Artemis, magic books, and a riot of silversmiths.
