# Assyria

Assyria is the empire that destroys the northern kingdom in 722/721 BCE and nearly takes Jerusalem in 701, in [2 Kings](../../old-testament/books/history/2-kings.md) and [Isaiah](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/isaiah.md). Its inscriptions and the Bible sometimes overlap (Sennacherib's campaign). Nahum is the oracle of its end.
