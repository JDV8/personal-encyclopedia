# Justice (mishpat)

Justice (*mishpat*) and righteousness (*tsedaqah*) are central prophetic demands, especially in Amos, Micah, and Isaiah: courts, weights, treatment of the poor, and worship that is empty without them. The words are legal and social, not only "fairness" as a mood. New Testament "justification" language is related and not identical.
