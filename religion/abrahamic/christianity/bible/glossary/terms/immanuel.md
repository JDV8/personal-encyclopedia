# Immanuel

Immanuel, "God with us," is the child-sign of [Isaiah](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/isaiah.md) 7, given to Ahaz in an eighth-century crisis, and reread in [Matthew](../../new-testament/books/gospels/matthew.md) 1 of Jesus' birth. Both contexts belong in view. The name is a promise of presence, in Isaiah a sign with a time-limit, in Matthew a claim about who the child is.
