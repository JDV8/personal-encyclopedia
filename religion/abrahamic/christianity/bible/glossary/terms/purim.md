# Purim

Purim is the festival whose origin is told in [Esther](../../old-testament/books/history/esther.md): lots, reversal, feasting, gifts to the poor, and the public reading of the Megillah. The Hebrew book does not name God; the feast is the community's interpretive decision that the story is to be remembered as deliverance.
