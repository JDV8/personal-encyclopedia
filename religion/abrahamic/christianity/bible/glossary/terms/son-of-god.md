# Son of God

"Son of God" in the Bible can name Israel, the king, and, in the New Testament, Jesus in a unique sense (Mark's opening, the baptismal voice, John's theology, Paul's gospel). Later trinitarian doctrine is a way of rereading that language; the first-century uses already vary in intensity from royal messiahship to a more explicit identification with God's own life.
