# The Shema

The Shema is the confession of [Deuteronomy](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/deuteronomy.md) 6:4–5, "Hear, O Israel: the Lord is our God, the Lord is one," with the command to love the Lord with all the heart, soul, and might. It is the daily creed of Jewish life and, in the Gospels, Jesus' first great commandment, paired with love of neighbor from Leviticus 19.
