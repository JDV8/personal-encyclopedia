# Remnant

The remnant is the portion of Israel that remains after judgment, a prophetic hope especially in Isaiah, Micah, and Zephaniah, and a Pauline theme in Romans 9–11. It is not a boast of the pure; it is often a severe mercy. Later churches have sometimes claimed to be "the remnant" too quickly.
