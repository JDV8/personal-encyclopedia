# Glory

Glory (*kavod*, *doxa*) is the weight and radiance of the Lord's presence, filling the tabernacle and temple, departing and returning in [Ezekiel](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/ezekiel.md), and, in John, becoming flesh. It is not only fame. Paul's "weight of glory" and the Synoptic transfiguration belong to the same family of language.
