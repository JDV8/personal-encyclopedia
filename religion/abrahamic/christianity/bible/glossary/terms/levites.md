# Levites

Levites are members of the tribe of Levi, set apart for sanctuary service. In some texts priests are a subset (Aaron's line) and Levites a wider serving class; in others the language overlaps. Chronicles is especially interested in their organized work of music and gates. See [1 Chronicles](../../old-testament/books/history/1-chronicles.md).
