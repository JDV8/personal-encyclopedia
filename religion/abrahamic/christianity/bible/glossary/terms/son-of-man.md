# Son of Man

"Son of Man" is Jesus' characteristic self-designation in the Gospels, with a background in ordinary Hebrew/Aramaic "human being" and in the heavenly figure of [Daniel](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/daniel.md) 7. Whether Jesus used it as a title, a modest idiom, or both is debated. In the Gospels it is often tied to authority, suffering, and coming.
