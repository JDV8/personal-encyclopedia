# The Servant of the Lord

The Servant poems of [Isaiah](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/isaiah.md) 40–55 portray a figure (or a people) who brings justice, suffers, and, in 52:13–53:12, is wounded and bears the sins of many. Jewish interpretation has often heard Israel, or a remnant, or a particular righteous one; Christian interpretation has heard Jesus' Passion. The poem itself does not name the servant. Honest teaching presents the disagreement.
