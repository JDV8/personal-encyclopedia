# Second Temple

The Second Temple is the house rebuilt after the exile (dedicated 516/515 BCE) and later greatly enlarged by Herod, destroyed by Rome in 70 CE. Most of the New Testament happens in its shadow. "Second Temple Judaism" is the modern name for the period and its parties, scriptures, and hopes. See [Ezra](../../old-testament/books/history/ezra.md) and [Second Temple Judaism and Rome](../../new-testament/01-second-temple-judaism-and-rome.md).
