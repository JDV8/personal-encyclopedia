# Lament

Lament is a biblical speech-form: complaint, protest, plea, and often a turn to trust, in the [Psalms](../../old-testament/books/wisdom-poetry/psalms.md) and in [Lamentations](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/lamentations.md). It is a first-principles fact that scripture includes the refusal to praise too soon. Christian liturgy has sometimes under-used it; Jewish liturgy has not.

**See also:** [Psalms](../../old-testament/books/wisdom-poetry/psalms.md), [Lamentations](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/lamentations.md)
