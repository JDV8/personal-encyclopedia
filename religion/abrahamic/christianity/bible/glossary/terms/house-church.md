# House church

A house church is an assembly that meets in a household, the ordinary setting of much early Christian life (Romans 16, Philemon, Prisca and Aquila, the elect lady of 2 John). Patronage, hospitality, and conflict over tables all belong to that setting. Later basilicas should not be read back into the 50s.
