# Resurrection

Resurrection is, in [Daniel](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/daniel.md) 12 and in 2 Maccabees, the hope that the dead will be raised; in the New Testament, first of all the claim that God raised Jesus, and then the hope of a general raising. It is not the same as the immortality of the soul, though the two have been combined in later theology. See [resurrection appearances](../../new-testament/stories/07-resurrection-appearances.md).
