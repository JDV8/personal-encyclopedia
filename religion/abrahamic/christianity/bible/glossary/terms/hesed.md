# Hesed

Hesed is a Hebrew word of loyal kindness, covenant love, and stubborn mercy, often translated "steadfast love" or "loving-kindness." It is central to Hosea, to the Psalms, and to the book of Ruth. English "love" is sometimes too thin for it; "obligation" is sometimes too cold. The word sits between affection and pledged loyalty.
