# Disciple

A disciple is a learner and follower. In the Gospels the word is wider than the Twelve: it includes a larger company, and, in some texts, women who travel and provide. Acts uses it of the whole community. Later Christian "discipleship" language should keep this ordinary, costly, first-century sense.
