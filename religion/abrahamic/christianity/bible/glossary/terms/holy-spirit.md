# Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit (Spirit of the Lord, Spirit of God) is, in the Hebrew Bible, the Lord's power for creation, leadership, prophecy, and life, and, in the New Testament, the promised presence poured out at [Pentecost](pentecost.md), the one by whom believers live, pray, and are gifted, and, in later doctrine, the third person of the Trinity. Luke–Acts is especially a narrative of the Spirit. Later doctrine should not flatten the word's history.

**See also:** [Pentecost](pentecost.md), [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md)
