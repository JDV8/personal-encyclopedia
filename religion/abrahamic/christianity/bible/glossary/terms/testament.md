# Testament

"Testament" in "Old" and "New Testament" is a rendering of "covenant." The names are a Christian framing: they imply a structure of promise and fulfillment. Jewish readers have a Bible without a "New Testament" and without calling their scriptures old. See [covenant](../themes/covenant.md) and [canon](../../02-canon-and-hebrew-bible-vs-old-testament.md).
