# Psalm

A psalm is a sacred poem, and the [Psalms](../../old-testament/books/wisdom-poetry/psalms.md) are the Bible's prayer book: lament, praise, royal songs, wisdom, and curse. [Psalm 151](../../deuterocanon/psalm-151.md) stands outside the 150 in the Septuagint. New Testament writers quote the Psalter constantly, especially in the Passion.
