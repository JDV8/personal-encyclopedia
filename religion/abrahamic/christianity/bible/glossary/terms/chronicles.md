# Chronicles (as a tradition)

"Chronicles" names both the biblical books of [1–2 Chronicles](../../old-testament/books/history/1-chronicles.md) and, more loosely, a later way of retelling Israel's past for a temple community. The Chronicler's differences from Samuel–Kings (a repentant Manasseh, a liturgical David) are the point, not a defect. The [Prayer of Manasseh](../../deuterocanon/prayer-of-manasseh.md) is a further step in that tradition.
