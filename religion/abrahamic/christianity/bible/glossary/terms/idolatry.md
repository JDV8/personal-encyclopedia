# Idolatry

Idolatry is the worship of images or of other gods, the central covenant transgression of the Torah and the prophets, satirized in Jeremiah, Isaiah 44, the [Letter of Jeremiah](../../deuterocanon/letter-of-jeremiah.md), and Wisdom 13–15. The New Testament can also use idol-language of greed or of emperor cult. The first sense is cultic and concrete.

**See also:** [Letter of Jeremiah](../../deuterocanon/letter-of-jeremiah.md), [Wisdom of Solomon](../../deuterocanon/wisdom-of-solomon.md)
