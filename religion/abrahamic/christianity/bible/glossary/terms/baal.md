# Baal

Baal ("lord" or "master") is a Canaanite storm and fertility god, the typical rival deity in the Former Prophets, especially in the Elijah cycle and in Hosea. The name can also be a common noun. The prophetic polemic is that the Lord, not Baal, gives rain and loyalty. Later readers should not treat Canaanite religion only through its biblical enemies' insults, but they should also hear what the biblical writers thought was at stake.
