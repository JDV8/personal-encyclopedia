# Priest

A priest, in Israel's scriptures, is a descendant of Aaron (or, more loosely, of Levi) who serves at the altar, teaches, and deals with purity and sacrifice, especially in [Leviticus](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/leviticus.md). The high priest is the chief of that house. [Hebrews](../../new-testament/books/general-epistles/hebrews.md) rereads priesthood typologically in Jesus. "Priesthood of believers" in later Christian talk should not erase the Old Testament office.
