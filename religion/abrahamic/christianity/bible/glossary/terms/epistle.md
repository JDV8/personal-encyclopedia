# Epistle (letter)

An epistle is a letter. Much of the New Testament is actual correspondence (Paul, 2–3 John, Philemon), and some is more like a sermon or tract in letter form (Hebrews, 1 John, perhaps Ephesians). Ancient letters had conventions (greeting, thanksgiving, body, close) that help a reader see where a writer is doing something usual and where something unusual.
