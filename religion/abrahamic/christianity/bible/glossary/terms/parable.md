# Parable

A parable is a short comparison or story that does more than decorate a known point. Jesus' parables of the kingdom can reveal and conceal; see the lesson [parables](../../new-testament/stories/05-parables.md). Later allegory sometimes over-reads them; a purely moral reading sometimes under-reads them.
