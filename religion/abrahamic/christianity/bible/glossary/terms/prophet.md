# Prophet

A prophet, in the Bible, is not primarily a predictor but one who speaks the Lord's word into a present crisis — often to kings, often at a cost. The Former and Latter Prophets, and the New Testament's prophetic figures (John, Jesus, Agabus, the book of Revelation), belong to this family. See [the prophets](../../old-testament/08-prophets.md) and the theme of [prophecy](../themes/prophets-and-prophecy.md).
