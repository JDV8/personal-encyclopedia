# Parousia (the coming)

Parousia means "presence" or "arrival" and, in the New Testament, often the coming of the Lord (1 Thessalonians, the Synoptic apocalypses, 2 Peter). Delay is already a pastoral problem in the first century. Later timetables have a poor record. The first texts are pastoral: stay awake, do not grieve as those without hope, do not be quickly shaken.
