# Justification

Justification is a central Pauline term for being set right with God, by grace, through faith (or through the faithfulness of Jesus Christ), apart from "works of the law." [Romans](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/romans.md) and [Galatians](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/galatians.md) are the primary texts; [James](../../new-testament/books/general-epistles/james.md) 2 uses the same family of words differently. Reformation and "New Perspective" debates are later attempts to say what Paul meant.
