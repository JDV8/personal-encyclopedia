# Canon

A canon is a recognized list of authoritative books. The Bible has more than one: the Jewish Tanakh, the Protestant Old Testament (same books, different order), Catholic and Orthodox lists that include the [deuterocanon](deuterocanon.md), and a nearly settled twenty-seven-book New Testament after a long process. See the lesson [canon](../../02-canon-and-hebrew-bible-vs-old-testament.md). "The Bible" is therefore a family of related collections, not a single identical table of contents.
