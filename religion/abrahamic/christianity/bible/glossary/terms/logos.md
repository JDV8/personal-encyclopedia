# Logos (Word)

Logos in [John](../../new-testament/books/gospels/john.md) 1 is the Word who was with God and was God, and who became flesh. The term already had a life in Greek philosophy (see the [philosophy glossary](../../../../../../philosophy/glossary/greek/logos.md)) and in Jewish wisdom theology. John does not simply baptize Heraclitus or Philo; he makes a claim about Jesus. The overlap is still part of a first-principles reading.
