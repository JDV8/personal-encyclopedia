# YHWH (the Lord)

YHWH is the four-letter name of the God of Israel, revealed in the bush narrative of [Exodus](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/exodus.md) 3, traditionally not pronounced in Jewish reading and often represented as "the Lord" (Adonai) in translation. Scholarly hypotheses about pronunciation (including "Yahweh") remain hypotheses. The name is a claim about who this God is for a people, not a magic word.
