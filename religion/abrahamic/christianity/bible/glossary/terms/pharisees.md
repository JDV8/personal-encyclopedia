# Pharisees

The Pharisees were a Jewish movement of the late Second Temple period, devoted to the law and to ancestral traditions, believing in resurrection, and often in tension with the Sadducees. In the Gospels they are Jesus' most frequent sparring partners, in an inner-Jewish debate later misused as a general verdict on Jews. Paul was a Pharisee. Fair teaching keeps their seriousness in view and refuses the cartoon.
