# Faith

Faith (*emunah*, *pistis*) is trust, fidelity, and, in the New Testament, reliance on God and on Jesus Christ. Habakkuk 2:4, "the righteous shall live by his faithfulness," is reread in Romans, Galatians, and Hebrews. James insists that a faith without works is dead. The word is bigger than "agreeing with a doctrine."
