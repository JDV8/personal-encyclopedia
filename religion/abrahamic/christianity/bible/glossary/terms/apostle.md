# Apostle

An apostle is "one sent." In the New Testament the word names, first, the Twelve, then also others (Paul, Barnabas, and, in one debated verse, possibly Junia). The title is about commission and witness, especially of the resurrection, not about a later ecclesiastical rank in the first instance. See [the Twelve](the-twelve.md).
