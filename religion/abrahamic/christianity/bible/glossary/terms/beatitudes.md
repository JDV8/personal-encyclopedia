# Beatitudes

The Beatitudes are the blessings that open the [Sermon on the Mount](../../new-testament/stories/03-sermon-on-the-mount.md) (Matthew 5) and, in a sharper economic form with woes, Luke's sermon on the plain. They announce the kingdom's reversal, not a personality inventory. "Blessed" is a declaration, not a suggestion.
