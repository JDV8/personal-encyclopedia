# The Lord's Supper

The Lord's Supper (Eucharist, Mass, Communion, in later names) is the meal of bread and cup in which, according to Paul and the Synoptics, Jesus identifies his body and blood of the covenant, to be done "in remembrance." The earliest written form is [1 Corinthians](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/1-corinthians.md) 11. John's last supper emphasizes foot-washing and a long farewell. Later theories of presence are interpretations of these texts, not replacements for them.
