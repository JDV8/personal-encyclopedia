# Crucifixion

Crucifixion was a Roman method of public, shameful execution, used on Jesus under Pilate — one of the most secure facts of Christian origins. The [Passion](../../new-testament/stories/06-passion-week.md) narratives, Paul, and later theology all try to say what this death meant. The physical and political facts (a cross outside the city, a charge of kingship) should not be dissolved into metaphor.
