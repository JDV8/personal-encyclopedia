# Philistines

The Philistines are a people of the coastal plain, Israel's frequent rivals in Judges and Samuel (the ark, Goliath, Samson, Saul's death). They are not "Philistines" in the modern casual sense of the word. Historically they belong to the so-called Sea Peoples and to the Iron Age cities of Gaza, Ashkelon, Ashdod, Ekron, and Gath.
