# Sabbath

The Sabbath is the seventh day of rest, grounded in creation (Genesis 2) and in deliverance (Deuteronomy 5), a sign of the covenant and a command about time, work, and the neighbor (including servants and animals). Gospel controversies about sabbath healing are inner-Jewish debates about how to keep it, not a simple cancellation. Later Christian Sunday practice is a further reception.
