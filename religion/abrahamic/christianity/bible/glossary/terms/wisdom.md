# Wisdom

Wisdom (*hokhmah*, *sophia*) is skilled knowledge of how to live, beginning with the fear of the Lord, and, in some texts, a figure who was with God in creation ([Proverbs](../../old-testament/books/wisdom-poetry/proverbs.md) 8, [Sirach](../../deuterocanon/sirach.md) 24, [Wisdom of Solomon](../../deuterocanon/wisdom-of-solomon.md)). Later Christian theology will relate this figure to the Word and to Christ; Jewish tradition can identify her with Torah. Hear the Old Testament and deuterocanonical texts first.
