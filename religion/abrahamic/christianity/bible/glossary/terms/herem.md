# Herem (the ban)

Herem is the "ban" or devoted destruction of a city or its spoil, commanded in some conquest texts, especially in [Deuteronomy](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/deuteronomy.md) and [Joshua](../../old-testament/books/history/joshua.md). It is one of the hardest institutions in the Bible. Later readers have typologized it, historicized it, or wrestled with it as a moral problem; a first-principles reading should first state what the texts actually say.

**See also:** [Joshua](../../old-testament/books/history/joshua.md), [Canaan](../places/canaan.md)
