# Inspiration

Inspiration, in 2 Timothy 3:16, is the claim that sacred writings are "God-breathed" and useful for teaching and training. Later theories (dictation, verbal inerrancy, a more dynamic cooperation) are attempts to spell that out. The verse itself is practical and, in context, about the Jewish scriptures Timothy knew as a child.
