# Slavery

Slavery is a given of the ancient world that the Bible both regulates (Exodus, Deuteronomy, the household codes) and, in [Philemon](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/philemon.md), presses from inside a particular relationship. It does not contain a modern abolitionist tract, and it has been used to defend slavery. Later abolitionist readings also used the Bible. A first-principles account must report both the texts and that history of use.

**See also:** [Philemon](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/philemon.md), [Onesimus](../people/onesimus.md)
