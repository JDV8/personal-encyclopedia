# Septuagint

The Septuagint (LXX) is the Greek translation of Israel's scriptures, and of some additional books, produced in the Hellenistic period, with a legend of seventy (or seventy-two) translators in Alexandria. New Testament writers often cite scripture in Greek forms close to it. It is a first-principles fact that "the Old Testament" of the first churches was, in many cases, a Greek Bible.
