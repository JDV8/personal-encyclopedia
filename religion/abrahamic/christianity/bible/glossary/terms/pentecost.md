# Pentecost (Weeks)

Pentecost, the Feast of Weeks (Shavuot), is, in later Jewish tradition, also a commemoration of the giving of the Torah, and, in [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) 2, the day of the Spirit and of the church's public birth, with a reading of [Joel](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/joel.md). The Christian meaning depends on the Jewish festival, not the other way around. See [the early church](../../new-testament/stories/08-early-church.md).
