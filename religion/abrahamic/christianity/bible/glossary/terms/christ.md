# Christ

Christ is the Greek for [Messiah](../themes/messiah.md), "anointed one." In the New Testament it quickly becomes a title and then, in effect, part of Jesus' name. To say "Jesus Christ" is already to make a claim about Israel's hope, not only to use a surname. The theme file traces the hope's history.

**See also:** [Messiah](../themes/messiah.md), [Jesus](../people/jesus.md)
