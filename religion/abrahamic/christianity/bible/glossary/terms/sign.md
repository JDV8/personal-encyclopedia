# Sign

A sign, especially in [John](../../new-testament/books/gospels/john.md), is a miracle meant to be read, not only gaped at: water to wine, a feeding, a man born blind, Lazarus. The book says these are written so that readers may believe. A sign can also be refused, or demanded in a way Jesus will not meet, in the Synoptics.
