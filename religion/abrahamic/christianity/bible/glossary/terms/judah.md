# Judah

Judah is a son of Jacob, a tribe, the southern kingdom after the split, and the name from which "Jew" and "Judea" derive. The Davidic promise is a Judahite promise (Genesis 49; 2 Samuel 7). After the exile, "Judah" and "Israel" are sometimes used with overlapping reference for the restored community.
