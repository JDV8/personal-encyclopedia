# Martyrdom

A martyr is a witness who dies for the confession. In the Bible the great narrative cluster is [2 Maccabees](../../deuterocanon/2-maccabees.md) 6–7 (and [4 Maccabees](../../deuterocanon/4-maccabees.md)), then Stephen in Acts, and the blood of the witnesses in Revelation. [Hebrews](../../new-testament/books/general-epistles/hebrews.md) 11 looks back to those who would not accept release. Later martyrology grows from this soil.
