# Scripture

Scripture is writing recognized as holy and authoritative. For the New Testament authors it usually means the writings of Israel (in Hebrew or in Greek). 2 Peter can already class Pauline letters with "the other scriptures." The later closed canon is a community's decision about which writings belong; see [canon](canon.md).
