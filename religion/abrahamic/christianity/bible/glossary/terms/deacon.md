# Deacon

A deacon (*diakonos*, "servant" or "minister") appears as an office in Philippians and in 1 Timothy 3, and as a description of [Phoebe](../people/phoebe.md) in Romans 16. The seven of Acts 6 are often later called deacons; the text itself does not use the title. The word's range, from table service to recognized ministry, should be kept in view.
