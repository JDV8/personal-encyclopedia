# Circumcision

Circumcision is the sign of the covenant with Abraham in Genesis 17, a physical mark of belonging in Israel. In the Pauline letters, especially [Galatians](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/galatians.md) and Romans, the question whether Gentile believers must be circumcised becomes the test of the gospel. The debate is not about hygiene; it is about how the promise to Abraham reaches the nations.
