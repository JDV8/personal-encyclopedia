# Torah

Torah means "instruction" or "law" and, narrowly, the five books of Moses ([Genesis](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/genesis.md) through [Deuteronomy](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/deuteronomy.md)); more broadly, the whole teaching of Israel. Christian "law" is a narrower English word and has sometimes distorted Paul and the Gospels. Traditional ascription is Mosaic; critical study describes a long compositional history. See also the theme of [covenant](../themes/covenant.md).
