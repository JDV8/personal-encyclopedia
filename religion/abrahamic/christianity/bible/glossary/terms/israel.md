# Israel

Israel is the name given to Jacob at the Jabbok and then to the people of the twelve tribes, later to the northern kingdom as distinct from Judah, and, in a theological sense, to the people of the promise in all periods. Paul wrestles with the name in Romans 9–11. Later Christian claims to be "the true Israel" have a dangerous history and must be read against those chapters' warning not to boast over the branches.
