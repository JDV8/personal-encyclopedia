# Judge (shophet)

A judge, in the book of [Judges](../../old-testament/books/history/judges.md), is a deliverer and sometimes a legal figure, not primarily a modern courtroom official. The office is occasional, charismatic, and, in the book's frame, a sign that "there was no king in Israel." Deborah, Gideon, and Samson are the most developed portraits.

**See also:** [Deborah](../people/deborah.md), [Judges](../../old-testament/books/history/judges.md)
