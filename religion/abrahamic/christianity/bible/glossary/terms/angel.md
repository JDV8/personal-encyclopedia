# Angel

An angel is a "messenger." In the Bible angels appear as the Lord's agents, sometimes barely distinguished from the Lord's own appearing (the "angel of the Lord" in Genesis and Exodus), and later as named figures ([Gabriel](../people/gabriel.md), [Raphael](../people/raphael.md), Michael). Popular later angelology is often more crowded and more sentimental than the biblical texts.
