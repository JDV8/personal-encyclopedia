# The day of the Lord

The day of the Lord is a prophetic phrase for a coming crisis in which the Lord acts in judgment and, for some, in deliverance. Amos turns a popular hope into a warning; Joel, Zephaniah, and later texts develop it; the New Testament can use similar language of the Lord's coming. It is not automatically comfort.
