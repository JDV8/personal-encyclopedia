# Diaspora (Dispersion)

The diaspora is Jewish life outside the land, after the exiles and by later migration: Babylon, Egypt, and the cities of the Greek and Roman world. Esther, Tobit, and much of the New Testament assume it. "Dispersion" in James and 1 Peter can also be used theologically of a Christian minority. The first sense is a Jewish historical fact.
