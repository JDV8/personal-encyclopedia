# Overseer (bishop)

An overseer (*episkopos*, later "bishop") appears in the qualification lists of the Pastorals and in Philippians' greeting, as a local leader of character, not yet, in those texts, a monarchical diocesan official. Later episcopacy is a development from such language, not a photograph of the year 50. See [1 Timothy](../../new-testament/books/pauline-epistles/1-timothy.md).
