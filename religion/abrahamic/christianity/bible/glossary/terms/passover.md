# Passover

Passover (*Pesach*) is the festival of Israel's deliverance from Egypt, instituted in [Exodus](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/exodus.md) 12: a lamb, unleavened bread, a night of watching, a story told to children. The [Passion](../../new-testament/stories/06-passion-week.md) is timed and interpreted in relation to it, though John and the Synoptics do not count the days identically. It remains a living Jewish festival, not only a Christian symbol.
