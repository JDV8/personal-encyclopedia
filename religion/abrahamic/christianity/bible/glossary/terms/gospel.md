# Gospel

Gospel (Greek *euangelion*, "good news") is, first, the announcement of God's reign and of Jesus' death and resurrection, and then the name of a book-form: the four canonical [Gospels](../../new-testament/books/gospels/README.md). Paul can speak of "my gospel" without meaning a narrative book. Later, "the Gospel" can become a slogan; the first use is a message about an event.
