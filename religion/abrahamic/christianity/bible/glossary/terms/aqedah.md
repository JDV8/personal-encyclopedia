# Aqedah (the binding)

The Aqedah is the Jewish name for the binding of Isaac in Genesis 22; see the story lesson [The binding of Isaac](../../old-testament/stories/01-the-binding-of-isaac.md). The word itself means "binding." Jewish liturgy, Christian typology, and later philosophy have all worked the scene hard. The chapter remains short, severe, and unfinished as a theory.
