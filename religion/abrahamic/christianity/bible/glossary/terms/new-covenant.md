# New covenant

The new covenant of [Jeremiah](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/jeremiah.md) 31 is a promise of a law written on the heart and of sins remembered no more, after judgment. The New Testament takes it up at the last supper and in [Hebrews](../../new-testament/books/general-epistles/hebrews.md). In Jeremiah it is a hope for Israel and Judah; later Christian uses should not erase that first address.
