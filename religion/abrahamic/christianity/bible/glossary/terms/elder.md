# Elder (presbyter)

Elders (*presbyteroi*) are local leaders in Acts, in the Pastorals, in James, and in 1 Peter: they pray, teach, and shepherd. The later word "priest" in some Christian traditions comes from this Greek term, which is not, in the New Testament, a sacrificial office in the Levitical sense. The overlap and difference of elder and overseer is already a historical question in these texts.
