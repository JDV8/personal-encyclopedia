# Sadducees

The Sadducees were a priestly and often aristocratic grouping in late Second Temple Judaism, associated with the temple, tending not to accept resurrection or some ancestral traditions the Pharisees held, and appearing in the Gospels mainly in controversy (especially about the resurrection) and in the Passion. They fade after the temple's destruction in 70 CE.
