# The Twelve

The Twelve are the inner circle of Jesus' disciples, numbered to suggest a restoration of Israel's tribes, listed with small variations in the Gospels and in Acts 1 (Matthias replacing Judas). They are not the only disciples, and not all later "apostles" are of the Twelve. The number is theological as well as historical.
