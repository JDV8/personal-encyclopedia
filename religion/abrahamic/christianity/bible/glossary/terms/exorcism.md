# Exorcism

Exorcism in the Gospels is Jesus' (and then the disciples') expulsion of "unclean spirits" or demons, a regular feature of the Synoptic ministry and a sign of the kingdom's arrival. Modern readers disagree about how to describe the phenomena; the texts themselves treat the confrontations as real and as contested. They belong with the lesson on [miracles](../../new-testament/stories/04-miracles.md).
