# Verses (and their history)

Verse numbers in the Old Testament rest on older Jewish traditions of lining out the text; the New Testament verse system is early modern (Robert Estienne, sixteenth century, is the usual name). Verses are invaluable for reference and dangerous when they turn the Bible into a quarry of isolated sayings. Read paragraphs and books, not only verses.
