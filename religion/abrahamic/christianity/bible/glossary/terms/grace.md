# Grace

Grace (*hen*, *charis*) is the Lord's undeserved favor, a gift, and, in Paul, the sphere in which one stands. In the Pastorals, "the grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation to all." The word can become a slogan that no longer costs anything; in the letters it is tied to a cross and to a new way of life.
