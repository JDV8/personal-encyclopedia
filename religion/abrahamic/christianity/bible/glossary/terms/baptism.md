# Baptism

Baptism is, in the New Testament, a water rite of repentance (John) and of initiation into Christ (Paul, Acts), a dying and rising, a washing, a naming in the triune formula of Matthew 28. It has a Jewish background in purifications and in John's movement. Later disagreements about infant baptism, mode, and effect are receptions of a first-century practice the New Testament assumes more than it theorizes.
