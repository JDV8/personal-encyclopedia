# Almsgiving

Almsgiving is the giving of money or goods to the poor, a central duty in [Tobit](../../deuterocanon/tobit.md) and in Sirach, in the Torah's harvest laws, and in Jesus' Sermon on the Mount (do it in secret). It is not an optional extra to "spiritual" religion in these texts. The later Christian virtue is a continuation, not a novelty.
