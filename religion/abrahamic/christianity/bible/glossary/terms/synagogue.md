# Synagogue

A synagogue is a gathering (and, later, a building) for prayer, reading of scripture, and community, especially important after the exile and in the diaspora, and a regular first stop of Paul's mission in Acts. Jesus teaches in synagogues in the Gospels. It is a Jewish institution the church did not invent; later separation was a process, not a given of the year 30.
