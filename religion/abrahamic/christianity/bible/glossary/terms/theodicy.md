# Theodicy

Theodicy is the problem of how God can be just, given suffering and evil. The word is modern; the problem is biblical: [Job](../../old-testament/books/wisdom-poetry/job.md), [Habakkuk](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/habakkuk.md), [Lamentations](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/lamentations.md), [2 Esdras](../../deuterocanon/2-esdras.md), and the psalms of lament. The Bible more often prays the question than solves it in a system.

**See also:** [Job](../people/job.md), [Habakkuk](../../old-testament/books/minor-prophets/habakkuk.md)
