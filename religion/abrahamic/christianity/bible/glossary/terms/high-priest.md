# High priest

The high priest is the head of the Aaronic priesthood, the one who enters the inner sanctuary on the Day of Atonement in [Leviticus](../../old-testament/books/law-torah/leviticus.md) 16. In the first century the office was entangled with Roman appointment; [Caiaphas](../people/caiaphas.md) is the Passion's high priest. Hebrews argues for a different, Melchizedek-like high priest.
