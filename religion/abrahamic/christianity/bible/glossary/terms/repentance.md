# Repentance

Repentance is a turning: in Hebrew often *shuv*, a return; in the New Testament *metanoia*, a change of mind and life. John the Baptist and Jesus both announce it; Jonah's Nineveh is a parable of it; the Prayer of Manasseh is a later liturgical form of it. It is not only feeling sorry.
