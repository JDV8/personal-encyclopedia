# Gentile

A Gentile is, in biblical usage, one of the nations (*goyim*, *ethne*), a non-Jew. The dramatic question of the New Testament is how Gentiles can belong to the God of Israel without, in Paul's gospel, becoming Jews by circumcision and full law-observance. Acts and Galatians are the narrative and argumentative centers of that question. The word should not be used as a synonym for "Christian."
