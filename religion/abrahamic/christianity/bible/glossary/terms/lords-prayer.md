# The Lord's Prayer

The Lord's Prayer is the prayer Jesus teaches in Matthew 6 (and, shorter, in Luke 11): Father, name, kingdom, bread, forgiveness, testing, and evil. It sits in a warning against performative piety. It is a first-principles prayer of a people who have a Father and also have hunger and enemies.
