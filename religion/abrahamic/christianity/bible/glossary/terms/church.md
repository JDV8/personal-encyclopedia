# Church

Church (*ekklesia*, "assembly") in the New Testament names a local gathering and, in some texts (especially Ephesians and Matthew 16), a more-than-local people. It is not, at first, a building. [Acts](../../new-testament/books/history/acts.md) and the letters show messy, gifted assemblies, not a finished constitution. Later polity is a long argument about those beginnings.
