# Deuterocanon (Apocrypha)

Deuterocanonical books are those in the Septuagint and in Catholic and Orthodox Bibles but not in the Hebrew Bible or in most Protestant Old Testaments; Protestants often say "Apocrypha." This encyclopedia treats them in [deuterocanon](../../deuterocanon/README.md). The word means "second canon," not "second-rate," and the lists still vary among churches that include them.
