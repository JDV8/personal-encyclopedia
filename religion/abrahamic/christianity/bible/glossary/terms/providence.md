# Providence

Providence is later theology's word for God's hidden governing of events. The Hebrew book of [Esther](../../old-testament/books/history/esther.md) is a classic narrative of it without the divine name; Genesis 50:20 is a classic sentence of it ("you meant it for harm"). The Bible more often shows this than defines it.
