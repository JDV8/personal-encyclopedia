# Incarnation

Incarnation names the Christian claim that the Word became flesh, classically from [John](../../new-testament/books/gospels/john.md) 1:14. The later doctrinal word is not in the New Testament; the claim is. [1 John](../../new-testament/books/general-epistles/1-john.md) makes the confession that Jesus Christ has come in the flesh a test of the community.
