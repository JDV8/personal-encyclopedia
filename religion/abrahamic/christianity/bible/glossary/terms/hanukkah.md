# Hanukkah

Hanukkah, the festival of the temple's rededication after the Maccabean revolt, is narrated in [1–2 Maccabees](../../deuterocanon/1-maccabees.md). The later rabbinic oil miracle is not in those books. John 10 mentions the "festival of Dedication" in Jerusalem. For many Jewish readers this is a living feast; for a first-principles Bible student it is also essential Second Temple history.
