# Apocalyptic

Apocalyptic is a genre and a way of seeing: visions, symbols, heavenly interpreters, a coming crisis, hope that does not rest on ordinary politics alone. [Daniel](../../old-testament/books/major-prophets/daniel.md) 7–12, [2 Esdras](../../deuterocanon/2-esdras.md), and [Revelation](../../new-testament/books/prophecy/revelation.md) are primary biblical examples. It is not a modern synonym for "disaster," and it is not a newspaper code.

**See also:** [Daniel](../people/daniel.md), [Revelation](../../new-testament/books/prophecy/revelation.md)
