# Messianic secret

The "messianic secret" is a modern name (after William Wrede) for Mark's pattern of silence: Jesus silences demons, sometimes the healed, and the disciples after Peter's confession, until the cross. Whether this is historical memory, Markan theology, or both is debated. It is a first-principles warning against a Messiah defined only by power.
