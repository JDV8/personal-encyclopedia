# Chapters (and their history)

The chapter divisions familiar in modern Bibles are medieval (Stephen Langton, in the early thirteenth century, is the name usually attached to the Western system). They are helps for reading and citation, not original to the authors. Sometimes they cut in the wrong place. A first-principles reader should not treat a chapter break as a revealed structure.
