# Miracle (sign)

A miracle, in the Gospels, is an act of power (*dynamis*) or a sign (*semeion*) of the kingdom: healings, exorcisms, nature wonders, raisings of the dead. See the lesson [miracles](../../new-testament/stories/04-miracles.md). The first task is to hear what the stories claim, before deciding a modern philosophical policy about whether "miracles happen."
