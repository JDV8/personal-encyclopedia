# Goel (kinsman-redeemer)

A goel is a kinsman who redeems land or a name, the legal role Boaz accepts in [Ruth](../../old-testament/books/history/ruth.md). The word's wider use includes God as Israel's redeemer in the prophets, and, in some Christian rereading, a background for later atonement language. In Ruth it is first of all a public, costly legal act at the town gate.
