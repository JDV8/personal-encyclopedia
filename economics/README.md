# Economics

Economics studies how people, firms and governments allocate scarce resources and how the resulting choices add up to prices, markets and the wealth of nations. This branch contains two courses. [Microeconomics](microeconomics/README.md) covers how individuals and firms choose under scarcity and how markets, prices, competition, market failures and welfare work. [Finance](finance/README.md) covers how money, credit, securities and financial markets work. Finance is applied economics under uncertainty: it takes the microeconomic ideas of scarcity, incentives and prices and asks what a claim on future money is worth and how it should be traded and held.

**Prerequisites:** none. This is the entry point for the economics branch.

## Suggested reading order

Start with [microeconomics](microeconomics/README.md), which builds the toolkit (opportunity cost, supply and demand, elasticity, surplus, game theory, externalities) that the [finance course](finance/README.md) relies on, then read finance. Natural future neighbours, not yet written, are macroeconomics (growth, inflation, unemployment) and the economics of banking and money.

## Contents

- [Microeconomics](microeconomics/README.md) — 105 lessons in 10 sections, 16 thinker profiles and a 345-entry glossary: foundations, supply and demand, consumer and producer theory, market structures and game theory, factor markets, market failure, welfare, behavioural economics and the history of thought.
- [Finance](finance/README.md) — 102 lessons and a 583-entry glossary: time value, securities, markets, regulation, portfolios, behaviour, history, and personal investing in Canada.

## References & further reading

- N. Gregory Mankiw, *Principles of Economics* (Beginner). A standard first textbook covering micro and macro.
- Paul Krugman and Robin Wells, *Economics* (Beginner). A widely used alternative introduction.
- Frederic S. Mishkin, *The Economics of Money, Banking and Financial Markets* (Intermediate). The bridge from economics to finance.
- Hal R. Varian, *Intermediate Microeconomics* (Intermediate). Microeconomic foundations used in portfolio and option theory.
- Robert L. Heilbroner, *The Worldly Philosophers* (Beginner). The history of economic ideas through the lives of the great economists.
- Thomas Sowell, *Basic Economics* (Beginner). A non-mathematical introduction to prices and markets.
