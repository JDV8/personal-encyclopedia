# Thinkers

Profiles of sixteen economists whose ideas shaped microeconomics, in chronological order of birth: the classical economists Smith, Ricardo, Mill and Marx; the marginalists Jevons, Menger and Walras; Marshall, Pareto and Pigou, who built neoclassical and welfare economics; and twentieth-century figures Hayek, Coase, Arrow, Nash, Ostrom and Kahneman. Each profile covers life, main ideas with a worked example, criticisms and legacy.

**Prerequisites:** [History of economic thought](../README.md) lessons 01 to 05 give the context.

## References & further reading

- Robert L. Heilbroner, *The Worldly Philosophers* (Beginner). Smith, Ricardo, Malthus, Mill, Marx, Marshall and others.
- Nicholas Phillipson, *Adam Smith: An Enlightened Life* (Intermediate). A biography placing Smith in the Scottish Enlightenment.
- Gareth Stedman Jones, *Karl Marx: Greatness and Illusion* (Intermediate). A biography of Marx in his historical context.
- Sylvia Nasar, *A Beautiful Mind* (Beginner). The biography of John Nash.
- Bruce Caldwell, *Hayek's Challenge* (Intermediate). An intellectual biography of Hayek.
- Michael Lewis, *The Undoing Project* (Beginner). Kahneman and Tversky.
- William Breit and Barry T. Hirsch (eds.), *Lives of the Laureates* (Beginner). Autobiographical essays by Nobel economists, including Arrow and Coase.
