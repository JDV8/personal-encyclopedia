# Externality

A cost or benefit of an activity that falls on people who are not party to the transaction and is not reflected in the price. Pollution is a negative externality; vaccination and research generate positive externalities. Because decision-makers ignore external effects, markets produce too much of goods with negative externalities and too little of those with positive ones.
