# Marxian economics

The school of economics based on Karl Marx's analysis of capitalism. It holds that labour is the source of value, that capitalists extract surplus value from workers, and that capitalism is driven by accumulation, mechanisation and class conflict, producing crises. It is influential in sociology and history; mainstream economists reject its labour theory of value.
