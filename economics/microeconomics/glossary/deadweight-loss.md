# Deadweight loss

The reduction in total surplus that results when a market produces a quantity other than the efficient one. Taxes, price controls, monopoly pricing and externalities all create deadweight losses, mutually beneficial trades that do not happen (or harmful ones that do). On a supply-and-demand diagram it is usually a triangle between the curves. Its size grows with the square of the distortion, so doubling a tax roughly quadruples the loss.
