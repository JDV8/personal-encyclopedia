# Economies of scope

Cost savings from producing several products together rather than separately, because they share inputs, facilities, know-how or distribution. A bakery making both bread and pastries, or an airline carrying passengers and cargo on the same flights, enjoys economies of scope. They help explain why firms diversify into related products.
