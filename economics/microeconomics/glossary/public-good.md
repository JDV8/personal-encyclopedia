# Public good

A good that is both non-rival and non-excludable, such as national defence, basic research or a flood barrier. Because non-payers cannot be excluded, people have an incentive to free-ride, and markets tend to underprovide public goods. Paul Samuelson showed in 1954 that the efficient quantity sets the sum of individuals' marginal benefits equal to marginal cost.
