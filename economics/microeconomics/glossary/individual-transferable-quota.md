# Individual transferable quota

A share of a total allowable catch in a fishery, assigned to an individual fisher or company, that can be bought, sold or leased. ITQs give fishers a stake in the long-run health of the stock and allow fishing to be done by the most efficient boats. Iceland and New Zealand adopted them in the 1980s; research suggests ITQ fisheries are less prone to collapse.
