# Choice architecture

The design of the context in which people make decisions: how options are presented, ordered, framed and defaulted. Thaler and Sunstein argued that there is no neutral design; whoever arranges a cafeteria or a pension enrolment form inevitably influences choices. Good choice architecture uses this influence to help people achieve their own goals, through defaults, simplification, reminders and feedback.
