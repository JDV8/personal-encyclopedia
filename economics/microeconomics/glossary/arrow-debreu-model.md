# Arrow–Debreu model

The canonical model of general equilibrium developed by Kenneth Arrow and Gérard Debreu, who proved in 1954 that a competitive equilibrium exists under conditions such as convex preferences and technologies. Goods are distinguished by physical characteristics, date and state of the world, so the model implicitly assumes complete markets for every contingency. It is the framework within which the welfare theorems are proved and the benchmark against which market incompleteness is measured.

In this course the term is introduced in [Kenneth Arrow (1921–2017)](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/13-kenneth-arrow.md), part of the section [Thinkers](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/README.md).
