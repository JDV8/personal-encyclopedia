# Replication crisis

The discovery, especially in the 2010s, that many published findings in psychology and some in economics could not be reproduced. A 2015 project replicated only about 36 percent of 100 psychology studies. Responses include pre-registration of hypotheses, larger samples and data sharing. Core behavioural results such as loss aversion have generally held up, though often with smaller effects.
