# Prospect theory

A descriptive theory of decisions under risk developed by Kahneman and Tversky (1979, 1992). People evaluate outcomes as gains or losses relative to a reference point, weigh losses more heavily than gains, show diminishing sensitivity in both directions, and weight probabilities non-linearly. It explains many departures from expected utility theory.
