# Game theory

The study of strategic interaction: situations where each decision-maker's outcome depends on the choices of others. Founded by von Neumann and Morgenstern (1944) and developed by Nash, Selten and Harsanyi, it analyses games using concepts such as dominant strategies, Nash equilibrium and backward induction. It is used in oligopoly theory, auctions, bargaining, politics and biology.
