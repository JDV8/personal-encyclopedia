# Social discount rate

The rate used to discount future costs and benefits in public cost-benefit analysis. One view bases it on the market return on capital, the opportunity cost of public investment; another on ethical judgements about weighing future generations. The choice can decide the outcome for long-lived projects such as climate policy, as the Stern–Nordhaus debate showed.
