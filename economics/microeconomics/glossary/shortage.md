# Shortage

A situation in which quantity demanded exceeds quantity supplied at the current price, also called excess demand. In a free market, shortages push prices up until the market clears. When prices are held below equilibrium by a price ceiling, shortages persist and goods are rationed by queues, lotteries, favouritism or black markets.
