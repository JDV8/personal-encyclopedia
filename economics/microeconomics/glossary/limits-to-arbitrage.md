# Limits to arbitrage

Constraints that prevent rational traders from fully correcting mispricing, including capital limits, risk that mispricing worsens before it corrects, transaction costs and short-selling restrictions. Shleifer and Vishny (1997) showed that arbitrage can be weak precisely when it is most needed. Limits to arbitrage allow behavioural biases to affect market prices.
