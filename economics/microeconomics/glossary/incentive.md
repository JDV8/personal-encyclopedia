# Incentive

Something that motivates a person to act, such as a reward, price, penalty or social approval. Economics assumes people respond to incentives: higher prices encourage supply and discourage demand; taxes discourage the taxed activity. Poorly designed incentives can produce unintended consequences, and non-monetary motives can sometimes be crowded out by monetary ones.
