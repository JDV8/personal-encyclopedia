# Production possibilities frontier

A curve showing the maximum combinations of two goods an economy can produce with its resources and technology. Points on the frontier are productively efficient; points inside are wasteful; points outside are unattainable. Its slope is the opportunity cost of one good in terms of the other, and it is usually bowed outward because resources are not equally suited to all uses.
