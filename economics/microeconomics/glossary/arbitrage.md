# Arbitrage

Buying something where it is cheap and selling it where it is dear to profit from the price difference. Arbitrage tends to equalise prices across markets. It is why price discrimination requires that customers cannot easily resell: if students could buy cheap tickets and resell them to adults, the price difference would collapse. In finance, arbitrage is the basis of many pricing theories, though in practice it has limits.
