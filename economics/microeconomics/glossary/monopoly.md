# Monopoly

A market with a single seller of a product without close substitutes, protected by barriers to entry. A monopolist chooses output where marginal revenue equals marginal cost and charges a price above marginal cost, producing less than the efficient quantity and creating deadweight loss. Sources include patents, control of resources, economies of scale and government licences.
