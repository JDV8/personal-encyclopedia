# Substitute

A good that can be used in place of another, so that a rise in the price of one increases demand for the other: tea and coffee, butter and margarine, buses and trains. Substitutes have positive cross-price elasticity. The availability of close substitutes makes demand more elastic.

In this course the term is introduced in [Shifts in demand](../02-supply-and-demand/02-shifts-in-demand.md), part of the section [Supply and demand](../02-supply-and-demand/README.md).
