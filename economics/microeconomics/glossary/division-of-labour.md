# Division of labour

The splitting of production into separate tasks performed by different workers or firms. Adam Smith opened the Wealth of Nations with a pin factory in which ten specialised workers produced about 48,000 pins a day. Specialisation raises productivity through skill, saved time and invention of tools, but is limited by the extent of the market.
