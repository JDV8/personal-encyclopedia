# Club good

A good that is excludable but non-rival, at least up to the point of congestion: streaming services, private parks, cinemas, uncongested toll roads, software. Because non-payers can be excluded, club goods can be provided by markets or voluntary associations through membership fees. James Buchanan developed the economic theory of clubs in 1965.
