# Property rights

Legally and socially recognised rights to use, earn income from and transfer resources. Clear, secure property rights give people incentives to invest, conserve and trade. Missing or poorly defined property rights, as with the atmosphere or open-access fisheries, are a root cause of many externality and commons problems, a point emphasised by Coase.
