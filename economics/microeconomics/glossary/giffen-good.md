# Giffen good

A good for which quantity demanded rises when its price rises, violating the law of demand. It must be strongly inferior and form a large part of the budget, so that the income effect of a price rise overwhelms the substitution effect. Jensen and Miller (2008) found evidence of Giffen behaviour for rice among very poor households in China. Giffen goods are very rare.

In this course the term is introduced in [Inferior and Giffen goods](../03-consumer-theory/09-inferior-and-giffen-goods.md), part of the section [Consumer theory](../03-consumer-theory/README.md).
