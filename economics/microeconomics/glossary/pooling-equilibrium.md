# Pooling equilibrium

In signalling and screening models, an outcome in which different types of people make the same choice, so their types are not revealed. If a signal is cheap enough for low-quality types to send too, everyone sends it, and employers or insurers must treat everyone as average. It contrasts with a separating equilibrium.

In this course the term is introduced in [Signalling and screening](../07-market-failure/14-signalling-and-screening.md), part of the section [Market failure](../07-market-failure/README.md).
