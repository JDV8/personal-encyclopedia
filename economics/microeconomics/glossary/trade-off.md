# Trade-off

A situation in which getting more of one thing means getting less of another. Because resources are scarce, people, firms and societies face trade-offs: between work and leisure, consumption and saving, efficiency and equity. The production possibilities frontier depicts the trade-off between two goods.

In this course the term is introduced in [Choice and trade-offs](../01-foundations/02-choice-and-trade-offs.md), part of the section [Foundations: scarcity, choice and trade](../01-foundations/README.md).
