# Dictator game

An experiment in which one player decides how to split a sum of money with another, who must accept whatever is offered. Purely self-interested dictators would give nothing, yet many give something, often around 20 to 30 percent on average, though a large share give nothing. It measures altruism separately from the fear of rejection that shapes ultimatum-game offers.
