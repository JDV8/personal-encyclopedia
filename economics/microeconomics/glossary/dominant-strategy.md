# Dominant strategy

A strategy that gives a player a higher payoff than any alternative, whatever the other players do. In the prisoners' dilemma, confessing is a dominant strategy for each prisoner. If every player has a dominant strategy, the outcome is predictable, though, as in the prisoners' dilemma, not necessarily good for the players collectively.
