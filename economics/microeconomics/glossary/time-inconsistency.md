# Time inconsistency

A situation in which a plan that seems optimal today will not seem optimal when the time comes to carry it out, so preferences change merely because time passes. Present bias makes individual plans time-inconsistent, as with procrastination. In policy, governments face similar problems with commitments such as low inflation.
