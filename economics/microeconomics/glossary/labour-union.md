# Labour union

An organisation of workers that bargains collectively with employers over wages, hours and conditions. Unions can raise members' wages above competitive levels, which may reduce employment, but in monopsonistic labour markets they can raise both wages and employment. They also provide a voice for grievances. In Canada roughly 30 percent of employees are covered by collective agreements.
