# Peak-load pricing

Charging higher prices when demand is highest and capacity is strained, and lower prices at off-peak times, as with electricity tariffs, rush-hour transit fares and holiday airfares. It spreads demand more evenly, makes better use of capacity and makes peak users bear the cost of the capacity they require.
