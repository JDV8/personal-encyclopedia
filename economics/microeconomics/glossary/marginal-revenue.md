# Marginal revenue

The additional revenue from selling one more unit. For a competitive firm, which can sell any quantity at the market price, marginal revenue equals price. For a monopolist, which must lower its price to sell more, marginal revenue is less than price. Profit is maximised where marginal revenue equals marginal cost.
