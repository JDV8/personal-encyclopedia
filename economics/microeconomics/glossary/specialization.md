# Specialisation

Concentrating on a narrow range of tasks or products rather than producing everything for oneself. Specialisation raises productivity through skill, focus and economies of scale, and makes trade necessary. Adam Smith's division of labour and Ricardo's comparative advantage explain why it creates gains.
