# Marginal social cost

The total cost to society of producing one more unit: the marginal private cost borne by the producer plus any marginal external cost borne by others. When marginal social cost exceeds marginal private cost, markets overproduce. Efficiency requires producing where marginal social benefit equals marginal social cost.
