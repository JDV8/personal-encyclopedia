# Goodhart's law

The principle, named after economist Charles Goodhart and often paraphrased as "when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure". When people are rewarded for hitting a metric, they find ways to improve the metric without improving the underlying goal, such as teaching to the test. It is a key warning in designing incentive schemes.
