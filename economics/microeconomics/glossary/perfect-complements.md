# Perfect complements

Goods that are consumed in fixed proportions, such as left and right shoes. Extra units of one without the other add no utility, so indifference curves are L-shaped. The elasticity of substitution is zero. Price changes have no substitution effect, only income effects.

In this course the term is introduced in [Indifference curves](../03-consumer-theory/03-indifference-curves.md), part of the section [Consumer theory](../03-consumer-theory/README.md).
