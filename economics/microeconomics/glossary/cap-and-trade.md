# Cap and trade

A pollution-control system in which a regulator sets a total limit (cap) on emissions, issues permits adding up to that cap, and allows firms to buy and sell them. Firms that can cut emissions cheaply sell permits to firms for which cutting is expensive, so the cap is met at the lowest total cost. The US Acid Rain Program and the EU Emissions Trading System are major examples. Cap and trade fixes the quantity of pollution and lets the price adjust.
