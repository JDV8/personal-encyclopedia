# Minimum efficient scale

The smallest output at which a firm achieves the lowest long-run average cost. If minimum efficient scale is large relative to market demand, only a few firms can operate efficiently, leading to oligopoly or natural monopoly. Industries such as cement or aircraft have large minimum efficient scales.
