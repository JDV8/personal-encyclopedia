# Subjective value

The principle that the value of a good is not intrinsic but depends on the importance people attach to it for satisfying their wants. Carl Menger and the marginalists made it the foundation of value theory, replacing the labour theory of value. Subjective value explains why both sides gain from voluntary exchange.

In this course the term is introduced in [Carl Menger (1840–1921)](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/06-carl-menger.md), part of the section [Thinkers](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/README.md). Related terms from the same lesson: [Spontaneous order](spontaneous-order.md).
