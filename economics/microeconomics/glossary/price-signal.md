# Price signal

The information conveyed by a price about the scarcity of a good and the value people place on it. A rising price signals that a good has become scarcer, prompting buyers to economise and producers to supply more. Friedrich Hayek emphasised that prices summarise dispersed knowledge that no central planner could gather.
