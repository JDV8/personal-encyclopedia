# Scarcity

The condition that resources are limited relative to the wants they could satisfy, so choices must be made. Scarcity is the fundamental problem of economics: because time, money and resources are finite, every choice involves an opportunity cost. Even abundant societies face scarcity of time and attention.

In this course the term is introduced in [Scarcity and the economic problem](../01-foundations/01-scarcity.md), part of the section [Foundations: scarcity, choice and trade](../01-foundations/README.md).
