# Price leadership

A pattern in oligopoly where one firm, often the largest or lowest-cost, sets prices and others follow. It can be a form of tacit collusion that keeps prices high without explicit agreement, or simply a convenient way to coordinate in response to cost changes. It is generally legal unless based on an agreement.
