# Hedonic pricing

A method that estimates the value of individual characteristics of a good from the prices of goods with different combinations of characteristics. Comparing house prices near and far from a park, airport or polluted site reveals how much people value those features. It is a revealed-preference technique used in cost-benefit analysis and in adjusting price indices for quality.
