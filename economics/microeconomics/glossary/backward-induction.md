# Backward induction

A method for solving sequential games by starting at the last decision and working back to the first. At each step, a player chooses the best action, anticipating how later players will respond. It yields subgame perfect equilibria, ruling out threats that would not be carried out. Applied to the ultimatum game it predicts a minimal offer, a prediction that experiments contradict.
