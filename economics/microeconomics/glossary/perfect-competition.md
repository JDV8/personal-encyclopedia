# Perfect competition

A market structure with many buyers and sellers, identical products, full information and free entry and exit, so that every participant is a price taker. In long-run equilibrium, price equals marginal cost and minimum average total cost, and economic profit is zero. It is an idealised benchmark against which real markets are compared.
