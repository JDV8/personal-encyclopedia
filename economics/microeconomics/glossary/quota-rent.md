# Quota rent

The extra profit earned by whoever holds the right to sell under a quota, equal to the difference between the higher domestic price and the world or competitive price, times the quantity allowed. If quota licences are given away, the rent goes to their holders; if auctioned, to the government. Quota rents can encourage lobbying to obtain them.

In this course the term is introduced in [Subsidies, quotas and tariffs](../02-supply-and-demand/15-subsidies-quotas-and-tariffs.md), part of the section [Supply and demand](../02-supply-and-demand/README.md). Related terms from the same lesson: [Quota](quota.md), [Tariff](tariff.md).
