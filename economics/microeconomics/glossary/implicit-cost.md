# Implicit cost

An opportunity cost that does not involve an explicit payment, such as the salary an owner forgoes by running their own business or the interest their invested capital could have earned elsewhere. Implicit costs are included in economic cost but not in accounting cost, which is why economic profit is smaller than accounting profit.
