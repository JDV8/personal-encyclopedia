# Partial equilibrium

Analysis of a single market in isolation, holding prices and conditions in other markets constant. Alfred Marshall's supply-and-demand framework is the standard example. It is simple and often adequate, but can miss important feedback effects when a market is large or closely linked to others, where general equilibrium analysis is needed.

In this course the term is introduced in [General equilibrium](../08-welfare-economics/02-general-equilibrium.md), part of the section [Welfare economics](../08-welfare-economics/README.md).
