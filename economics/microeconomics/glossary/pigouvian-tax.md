# Pigouvian tax

A tax on an activity that generates negative externalities, set equal to the marginal external cost, so that private decision-makers bear the full social cost. Named after Arthur Cecil Pigou. Unlike most taxes, it reduces deadweight loss while raising revenue. Carbon taxes, congestion charges and tobacco taxes are often justified in Pigouvian terms.
