# Present bias

The tendency to give disproportionate weight to immediate costs and rewards compared with future ones. A present-biased person may plan to exercise tomorrow but skip it when tomorrow arrives. It explains procrastination, under-saving and difficulty with healthy habits, and is modelled by quasi-hyperbolic discounting.
