# Credible commitment

A promise or threat that others believe will be carried out because the party making it would actually want to follow through when the time comes, or has made it costly not to. Building excess capacity to deter entry, or signing binding contracts, are ways to make commitments credible. In game theory, non-credible threats are eliminated by requiring subgame perfection.
