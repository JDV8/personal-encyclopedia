# Independence of irrelevant alternatives

One of Arrow's conditions for a social choice rule: the social ranking of two options should depend only on how individuals rank those two options, not on their views about some third option. Plurality voting violates it when a "spoiler" candidate changes the winner between two others. Arrow showed it cannot be combined with his other conditions.
