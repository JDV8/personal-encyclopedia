# Elastic

Describes demand or supply that responds strongly to price changes: the absolute value of the elasticity is greater than one, so quantity changes by a larger percentage than price. Demand tends to be elastic when close substitutes are available, the good is a luxury, or the time period is long. When demand is elastic, raising price reduces total revenue.
