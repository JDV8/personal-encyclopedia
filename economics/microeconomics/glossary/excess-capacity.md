# Excess capacity

Producing at an output below the level that minimises average total cost. In long-run monopolistically competitive equilibrium, each firm faces a downward-sloping demand curve tangent to its average cost curve to the left of the minimum, so it has excess capacity. Some see this as the cost of product variety that consumers value.
