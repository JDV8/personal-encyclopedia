# Reciprocity

The tendency to respond to kindness with kindness and to unkindness with punishment, even at a personal cost. Matthew Rabin's 1993 model made intentions central: people reward actions perceived as fair and punish those perceived as hostile. Reciprocity helps explain gift exchange in labour markets and cooperation in repeated interactions.
