# Ultimatum game

An experiment in which a proposer offers a share of a sum of money to a responder, who can accept (both get the proposed split) or reject (both get nothing). Self-interest predicts a tiny offer that is accepted; in practice most offers are 40 to 50 percent and low offers are often rejected. It reveals fairness concerns and willingness to punish.
