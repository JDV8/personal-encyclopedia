# Utilitarianism

The ethical theory, developed by Jeremy Bentham and John Stuart Mill, that the right action is the one producing the greatest total happiness or utility. In economics, utilitarian welfare functions add up individual utilities; combined with diminishing marginal utility, they imply some redistribution is desirable, balanced against incentive costs.
