# Social cost of carbon

An estimate of the monetary value of the damage caused by emitting one additional tonne of carbon dioxide, including effects on agriculture, health, property and ecosystems, discounted to the present. It is used to evaluate climate policies and set carbon prices. Estimates vary widely, depending especially on the discount rate and assumptions about damages.

In this course the term is introduced in [Negative externalities and pollution](../07-market-failure/03-negative-externalities-and-pollution.md), part of the section [Market failure](../07-market-failure/README.md).
