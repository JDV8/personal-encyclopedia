# Principal–agent problem

The difficulty that arises when one party (the agent) acts on behalf of another (the principal) whose interests differ and who cannot fully observe the agent's actions. Shareholders and managers, patients and doctors, and voters and politicians are examples. Incentive contracts, monitoring and reputation are partial solutions, and agency costs are the result.

In this course the term is introduced in [Incentives](../01-foundations/04-incentives.md), part of the section [Foundations: scarcity, choice and trade](../01-foundations/README.md).
