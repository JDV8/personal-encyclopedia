# Economies of scale

Falling long-run average cost as output increases. Sources include specialisation, indivisible equipment, bulk purchasing, and spreading fixed costs such as research or design over more units. Strong economies of scale can lead to natural monopoly or oligopoly, since a few large firms can produce more cheaply than many small ones.
