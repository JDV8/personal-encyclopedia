# Socialist calculation debate

A debate in the 1920s to 1940s over whether a socialist economy could allocate resources rationally. Ludwig von Mises and Friedrich Hayek argued that without market prices for capital goods, planners lacked the information needed; Oskar Lange and Abba Lerner proposed market socialism with planners adjusting prices by trial and error. Hayek's emphasis on dispersed knowledge became the most influential argument.
