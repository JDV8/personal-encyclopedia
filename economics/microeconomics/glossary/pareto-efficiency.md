# Pareto efficiency

An allocation in which no one can be made better off without making someone else worse off. Named after Vilfredo Pareto, it is the main efficiency standard in welfare economics because it requires no comparison of utilities across people. It says nothing about fairness: giving everything to one person is Pareto efficient.
