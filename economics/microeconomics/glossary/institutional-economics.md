# Institutional economics

The study of how institutions, the formal rules and informal norms that structure social interaction, shape economic behaviour and outcomes. The "old" institutionalists, such as Veblen and Commons, criticised abstract theory and emphasised habits and power; the "new" institutional economics of Coase, Williamson, North and Ostrom uses mainstream tools to analyse property rights, contracts and governance.
