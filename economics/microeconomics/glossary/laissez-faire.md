# Laissez-faire

French for "let do" or "let it be": the doctrine that governments should leave markets and economic activity largely alone. Associated with the 18th-century Physiocrats, the phrase was later linked to classical economics, though Smith and Mill accepted important roles for government. It is now used mostly to describe a strong free-market position.
