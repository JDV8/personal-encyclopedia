# Moral hazard

The tendency of a party to take more risk or less care after a deal because it does not bear the full consequences and the other party cannot observe its behaviour. Insurance can make policyholders careless; expectations of bailouts can make banks reckless. Deductibles, co-payments, monitoring and incentive pay are responses, each trading insurance against incentives.

In this course the term is introduced in [Asymmetric information](../07-market-failure/10-asymmetric-information.md), part of the section [Market failure](../07-market-failure/README.md).
