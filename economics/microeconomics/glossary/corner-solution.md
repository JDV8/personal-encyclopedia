# Corner solution

A consumer optimum at which the consumer buys none of one of the goods. It occurs when, even at zero consumption of a good, its marginal utility per dollar is lower than that of the alternative, as when goods are perfect substitutes and one is cheaper. At a corner solution, the tangency condition does not hold.
