# Two-part tariff

A pricing scheme with a fixed fee plus a per-unit price, such as a gym membership plus a charge per class, or a phone plan with a monthly fee plus usage charges. A firm with market power can set the per-unit price near marginal cost to encourage efficient use and capture surplus through the fixed fee. Regulators sometimes use it for natural monopolies.

In this course the term is introduced in [Natural monopoly and regulation](../05-market-structures/07-natural-monopoly-and-regulation.md), part of the section [Market structures and game theory](../05-market-structures/README.md).
