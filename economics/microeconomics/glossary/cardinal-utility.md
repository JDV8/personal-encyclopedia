# Cardinal utility

The idea that utility can be measured on a scale where differences have meaning, so that one could say a person gets twice as much satisfaction from one bundle as another. Early marginalists such as Jevons thought of utility this way. Modern consumer theory mostly uses ordinal utility instead, though cardinal measures reappear in expected utility theory under risk and in utilitarian welfare analysis.
