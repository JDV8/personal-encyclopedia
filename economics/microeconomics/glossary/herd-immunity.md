# Herd immunity

Protection of a population from an infectious disease when enough people are immune that the disease cannot easily spread, which also protects those who cannot be vaccinated. For highly contagious diseases such as measles, roughly 95 percent coverage is needed. Herd immunity makes vaccination a classic positive externality and explains subsidies and requirements.
