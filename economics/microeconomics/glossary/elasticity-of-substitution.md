# Elasticity of substitution

A measure of how easily one input (or good) can be substituted for another: the percentage change in the ratio of inputs used divided by the percentage change in their relative price (or the marginal rate of technical substitution). Cobb–Douglas functions have an elasticity of one; perfect substitutes have an infinite elasticity and perfect complements zero.
