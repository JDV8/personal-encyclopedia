# Capital

In economics, produced goods used to produce other goods: machines, buildings, tools, infrastructure and software. It is one of the factors of production, alongside labour and land (and sometimes entrepreneurship). Financial capital, money raised to buy such goods, is a related but distinct idea. Capital earns a return because it is productive and because using resources for investment means postponing consumption.
