# Jevons paradox

William Stanley Jevons's observation in The Coal Question (1865) that more efficient use of a resource can increase, rather than decrease, its total consumption, because efficiency lowers the cost of using it and expands its uses. Modern economists call the general phenomenon the rebound effect; full "backfire" occurs only in some cases, but rebound reduces the savings from efficiency.
