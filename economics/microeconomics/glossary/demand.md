# Demand

The relationship between the price of a good and the quantity buyers are willing and able to purchase, holding other factors constant. Demand is the whole schedule or curve, while quantity demanded is the amount at one price. Demand shifts when income, tastes, prices of related goods, expectations or the number of buyers change.

In this course the term is introduced in [Demand](../02-supply-and-demand/01-demand.md), part of the section [Supply and demand](../02-supply-and-demand/README.md).
