# Economic model

A simplified representation of how part of the economy works, used to understand relationships and make predictions. Models may be diagrams, equations or computer simulations. They deliberately leave out detail, like a map: the supply-and-demand model ignores many real features but captures how prices respond to changes. Models are judged by their usefulness and predictive power.

In this course the term is introduced in [Models and economic method](../01-foundations/11-models-and-economic-method.md), part of the section [Foundations: scarcity, choice and trade](../01-foundations/README.md).
