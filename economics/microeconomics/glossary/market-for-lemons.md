# Market for lemons

George Akerlof's 1970 model of a used-car market in which sellers know whether their car is good (a peach) or bad (a lemon) and buyers do not. Buyers will pay only for average quality, which drives owners of good cars out of the market, lowering average quality further. The market can unravel until only lemons are traded.
