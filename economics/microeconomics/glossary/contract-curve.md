# Contract curve

In an Edgeworth box, the set of Pareto-efficient allocations between two traders: the points where their indifference curves are tangent, so their marginal rates of substitution are equal. From any allocation off the curve, both traders can gain by trading toward it. Which point on the curve they reach depends on the starting endowments and bargaining.
