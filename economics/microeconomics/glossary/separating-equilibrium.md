# Separating equilibrium

In signalling and screening models, an outcome in which different types of people make different choices, revealing their hidden types. In Spence's education model, high-ability workers get degrees and low-ability workers do not, because the degree is too costly for the latter. It contrasts with a pooling equilibrium.

In this course the term is introduced in [Signalling and screening](../07-market-failure/14-signalling-and-screening.md), part of the section [Market failure](../07-market-failure/README.md).
