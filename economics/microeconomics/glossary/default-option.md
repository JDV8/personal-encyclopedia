# Default option

The outcome a person receives if they make no active choice. Because of inertia, procrastination, implied endorsement and loss aversion, defaults have powerful effects on behaviour: switching to automatic enrolment raised pension participation at one US firm from about 37 to 86 percent among new employees. Setting defaults is a central tool of choice architecture.
