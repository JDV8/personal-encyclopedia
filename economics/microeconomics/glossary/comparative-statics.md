# Comparative statics

The method of comparing two equilibria, before and after a change in some external factor, without analysing the path between them. For example, an increase in income shifts demand for a normal good to the right, raising the equilibrium price and quantity. Comparative statics is the workhorse of supply-and-demand analysis and of much of economic theory.

In this course the term is introduced in [Comparative statics: when supply and demand shift](../02-supply-and-demand/06-comparative-statics.md), part of the section [Supply and demand](../02-supply-and-demand/README.md).
