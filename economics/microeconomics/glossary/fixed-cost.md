# Fixed cost

A cost that does not vary with the quantity produced in the short run, such as rent, insurance or the cost of a machine already installed. Fixed costs must be paid even if output is zero. In the brewery example, fixed cost is 100. Because they are sunk in the short run, fixed costs do not affect the decision about how much to produce, only whether to stay in business in the long run.

In this course the term is introduced in [Short-run costs](../04-producer-theory/05-short-run-costs.md), part of the section [Producer theory](../04-producer-theory/README.md). Related terms from the same lesson: [Average total cost](average-total-cost.md), [Variable cost](variable-cost.md).
