# Condorcet paradox

The possibility, noticed by the Marquis de Condorcet in 1785, that majority voting among three or more options produces a cycle: A beats B, B beats C, and C beats A, even though every voter has consistent preferences. When cycles exist there is no option that beats all others, and whoever controls the order of votes can determine the outcome.
