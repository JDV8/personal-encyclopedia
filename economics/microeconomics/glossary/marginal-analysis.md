# Marginal analysis

Decision-making by comparing the additional benefit and the additional cost of a small change. A rational decision-maker continues an activity as long as marginal benefit exceeds marginal cost, and stops where they are equal. Firms produce where marginal revenue equals marginal cost; consumers equalise marginal utility per dollar. It is the core method of microeconomics.
