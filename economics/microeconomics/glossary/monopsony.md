# Monopsony

A market with a single buyer, or a few buyers with power over the price they pay. In labour markets, a monopsonistic employer pays less than the marginal revenue product of labour and hires fewer workers than a competitive market would. Joan Robinson coined the term in 1933. Modern research finds some degree of monopsony power is common, especially where employers are few or job switching is costly.
