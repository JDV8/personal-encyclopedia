# Human capital

The knowledge, skills, health and experience that make workers productive, acquired through education, training and experience. Like physical capital, it requires investment (tuition, forgone earnings) and yields returns (higher wages). Theodore Schultz and Gary Becker developed the theory in the 1960s. It is distinct from, though compatible with, the signalling view of education.
