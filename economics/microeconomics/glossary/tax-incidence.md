# Tax incidence

Who actually bears the burden of a tax, as opposed to who legally pays it. The burden falls more heavily on the side of the market that is less elastic: if demand is inelastic, buyers bear most of a tax even if sellers remit it. Statutory incidence is irrelevant to the economic outcome in competitive markets.
