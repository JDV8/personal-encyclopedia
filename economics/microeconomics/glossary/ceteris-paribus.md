# Ceteris paribus

Latin for "other things being equal". Economists use it to isolate the effect of one variable by holding everything else constant: the law of demand says that quantity demanded falls when price rises, ceteris paribus. Alfred Marshall made the method central to partial equilibrium analysis. Its weakness is that in reality other things rarely stay equal, which is why general equilibrium analysis is sometimes needed.
