# St Petersburg paradox

A puzzle about a coin-flipping game whose expected monetary payoff is infinite, yet which few people would pay more than a small sum to play. Daniel Bernoulli resolved it in 1738 by proposing that people maximise expected utility, with diminishing marginal utility of wealth, rather than expected money. It is the origin of expected utility theory.
