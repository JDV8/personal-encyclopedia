# Neoclassical economics

The dominant approach to economics since the late 19th century, built on the marginal revolution. It models individuals as maximising utility and firms as maximising profit subject to constraints, and analyses outcomes as equilibria. Marshall, Walras, Pareto, Hicks and Samuelson shaped it. Modern economics has extended it with information, institutions and behavioural insights.
