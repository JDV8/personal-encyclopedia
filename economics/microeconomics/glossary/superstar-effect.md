# Superstar effect

The phenomenon, analysed by Sherwin Rosen in 1981, in which small differences in talent lead to enormous differences in earnings, because technology allows the best performers to serve huge markets. Top musicians, athletes, authors and executives can reach global audiences, so the market rewards being slightly better far more than proportionally.
