# Government failure

A situation in which government intervention produces a less efficient outcome than the market would have, or corrects a market failure at excessive cost. Sources include limited information, rational ignorance of voters, lobbying and rent-seeking, regulatory capture, bureaucratic incentives and short time horizons. Public choice theory studies these problems.
