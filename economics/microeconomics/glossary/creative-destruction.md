# Creative destruction

Joseph Schumpeter's term, from Capitalism, Socialism and Democracy (1942), for the process by which innovation creates new products, firms and industries while destroying old ones. Schumpeter saw it as the essential fact about capitalism and argued that temporary monopoly profits from innovation drive growth, which is why static efficiency can be a misleading guide to policy.

In this course the term is introduced in [Perfect competition](../05-market-structures/01-perfect-competition.md), part of the section [Market structures and game theory](../05-market-structures/README.md).
