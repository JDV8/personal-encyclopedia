# Okun's leaky bucket

Arthur Okun's metaphor, from Equality and Efficiency: The Big Tradeoff (1975), for the costs of redistribution. Transferring money from rich to poor is like carrying water in a leaky bucket: some is lost through administrative costs and reduced incentives to work and save. How much leakage society should accept is a value judgement; how big the leak is, an empirical question.
