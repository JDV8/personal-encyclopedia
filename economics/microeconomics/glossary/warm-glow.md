# Warm glow

The satisfaction people get from the act of giving itself, apart from its effect on recipients. James Andreoni's 1990 model of warm-glow giving explains why private donations are not fully crowded out by government spending on the same causes.

In this course the term is introduced in [Social preferences and fairness](../09-behavioural-economics/06-social-preferences-and-fairness.md), part of the section [Behavioural economics](../09-behavioural-economics/README.md).
