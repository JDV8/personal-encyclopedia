# Returns to education

The increase in earnings associated with additional schooling, often expressed as the percentage wage gain per extra year. Estimates using natural experiments, such as changes in compulsory schooling laws, commonly find returns of roughly 7 to 10 percent per year in rich countries. Part of the return may reflect signalling rather than increased productivity.
