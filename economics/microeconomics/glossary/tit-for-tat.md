# Tit for tat

A strategy for repeated games in which a player cooperates in the first round and then copies the other player's previous move. In Robert Axelrod's computer tournaments around 1980, submitted by Anatol Rapoport, it won by being nice, retaliatory, forgiving and clear. It illustrates how cooperation can emerge among self-interested players.
