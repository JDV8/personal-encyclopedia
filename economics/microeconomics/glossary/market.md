# Market

Any arrangement that brings buyers and sellers together to exchange goods, services or assets, whether a physical marketplace, an online platform or a dispersed network of transactions. Markets coordinate economic activity through prices, which signal scarcity and guide resources to their most valued uses.

In this course the term is introduced in [Markets, prices and property rights](../01-foundations/10-markets-prices-and-property-rights.md), part of the section [Foundations: scarcity, choice and trade](../01-foundations/README.md).
