# Ricardian equivalence

The proposition that, under certain conditions, it makes no difference whether a government finances spending by taxes or by borrowing, because rational households anticipate the future taxes needed to repay the debt and save accordingly. Ricardo discussed it but doubted it held in practice; Robert Barro revived it in 1974. Evidence suggests it holds only partially.
