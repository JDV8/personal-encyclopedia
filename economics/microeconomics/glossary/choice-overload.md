# Choice overload

The finding that offering more options can sometimes reduce the likelihood of choosing at all or lower satisfaction with the choice made. Iyengar and Lepper's jam study found that a display of 24 jams attracted more shoppers but led to far fewer purchases than a display of 6. Later meta-analyses show the effect varies widely and is often small, depending on complexity and how clear preferences are.
