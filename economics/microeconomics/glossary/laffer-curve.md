# Laffer curve

A curve showing tax revenue as a function of the tax rate. Revenue is zero at a rate of 0 percent and, plausibly, at 100 percent, so there is some revenue-maximising rate in between; above it, cutting rates would raise revenue. Associated with Arthur Laffer in the 1970s, the idea is old. Most economists think most taxes in rich countries are below the revenue-maximising rate.
