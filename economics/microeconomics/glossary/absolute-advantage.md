# Absolute advantage

A producer has an absolute advantage in a good if it can make more of it than another producer with the same resources, or the same amount with fewer resources. Adam Smith framed the case for trade in these terms. Absolute advantage is not what determines who should specialise: a producer can be better at everything and still gain by trading, because what matters is the comparison of opportunity costs, which is comparative advantage.
