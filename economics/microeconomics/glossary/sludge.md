# Sludge

Richard Thaler's term for friction in choice architecture that makes it harder for people to do things in their own interest, such as complicated cancellation processes, mail-in rebates, long forms for benefits or hidden opt-outs. Sludge is the opposite of a helpful nudge. Some governments now conduct "sludge audits" to reduce administrative burdens.

In this course the term is introduced in [Nudges and choice architecture](../09-behavioural-economics/07-nudges-and-choice-architecture.md), part of the section [Behavioural economics](../09-behavioural-economics/README.md).
