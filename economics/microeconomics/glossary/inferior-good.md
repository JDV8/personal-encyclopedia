# Inferior good

A good for which demand falls as income rises, such as instant noodles or long-distance bus travel for some consumers. People switch to preferred alternatives as they become richer. Inferior goods have negative income elasticity. A Giffen good is an extreme inferior good.

In this course the term is introduced in [Shifts in demand](../02-supply-and-demand/02-shifts-in-demand.md), part of the section [Supply and demand](../02-supply-and-demand/README.md).
