# Cost-benefit analysis

A method for evaluating projects and policies by listing all their costs and benefits, expressing them in money, discounting future values, and comparing the totals. A project passes if the present value of benefits exceeds that of costs. It applies the Kaldor–Hicks criterion. Valuing non-market effects, choosing a discount rate and handling distribution are its main challenges.
