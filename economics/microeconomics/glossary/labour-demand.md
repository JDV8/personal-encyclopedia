# Labour demand

The relationship between the wage and the quantity of labour firms want to hire. In competitive markets, a firm hires workers until the wage equals the marginal revenue product of labour, so the labour demand curve is the MRP curve. It shifts with product demand, technology and the prices of other inputs.
