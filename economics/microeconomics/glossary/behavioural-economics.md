# Behavioural economics

The field that incorporates evidence from psychology into economic models, studying systematic departures from the standard assumptions of rationality, consistent preferences and pure self-interest. Its main topics include heuristics and biases, prospect theory and loss aversion, present bias and self-control, and social preferences. Pioneered by Kahneman, Tversky and Thaler, it has influenced finance, public policy and the design of choices through nudges.
