# Substitution effect

The part of the change in quantity demanded caused by a price change that results from the change in relative prices, holding real income or utility constant. When a good becomes cheaper relative to others, consumers substitute toward it. The substitution effect always moves quantity opposite to price.

In this course the term is introduced in [Income and substitution effects](../03-consumer-theory/08-income-and-substitution-effects.md), part of the section [Consumer theory](../03-consumer-theory/README.md).
