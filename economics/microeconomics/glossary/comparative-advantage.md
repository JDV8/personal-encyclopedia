# Comparative advantage

The ability to produce a good at a lower opportunity cost than someone else. David Ricardo showed in 1817 that trade benefits both parties when each specialises according to comparative advantage, even if one is better at producing everything. It is among the most important and least intuitive ideas in economics, and underlies the case for specialisation and international trade.
