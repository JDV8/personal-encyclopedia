# Barriers to entry

Anything that prevents new firms from entering a market and competing away profits. Examples include economies of scale, control of key resources, patents and licences, network effects, high sunk costs, brand loyalty and regulation. Barriers to entry are what allow monopoly and oligopoly profits to persist in the long run; without them, entry would push profits toward normal levels.
