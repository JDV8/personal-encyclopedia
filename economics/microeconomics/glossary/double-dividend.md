# Double dividend

The hypothesis that environmental taxes yield two benefits: a cleaner environment and, by using the revenue to cut distorting taxes such as income taxes, a more efficient tax system. Research in the 1990s showed that the second dividend is not guaranteed, because environmental taxes interact with existing taxes on labour. The first dividend, correcting the externality, stands regardless.
