# Tariff

A tax on imported goods. A tariff raises the domestic price, increasing domestic production and reducing imports and consumption. Domestic producers and the government gain, but consumers lose more, creating deadweight loss. Tariffs may also provoke retaliation. Economists generally favour free trade, while recognising adjustment costs for affected workers.
