# Edgeworth box

A diagram for analysing exchange between two people and two goods. The box's dimensions equal the total amounts of each good; one person's quantities are measured from the bottom-left corner and the other's from the top-right. Points where their indifference curves are tangent form the contract curve of Pareto-efficient allocations. Named after Francis Ysidro Edgeworth (1881), it was refined by Pareto.

In this course the term is introduced in [Efficiency and Pareto optimality](../08-welfare-economics/01-efficiency-and-pareto-optimality.md), part of the section [Welfare economics](../08-welfare-economics/README.md).
