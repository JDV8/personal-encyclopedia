# Contingent valuation

A survey method for estimating how much people value goods not traded in markets, such as clean air, wilderness or endangered species, by asking them directly how much they would be willing to pay. It was used to estimate damages from the 1989 Exxon Valdez oil spill. Critics argue that hypothetical answers can be unreliable and insensitive to the scale of what is being valued.
