# Free-rider problem

The incentive for people to enjoy the benefits of a good without paying for it, when they cannot be excluded. If everyone free-rides, a good that all value may not be provided at all. The problem affects public goods such as national defence, as well as group efforts like union bargaining or lobbying. Taxation, exclusion, social norms and assurance contracts are responses.
