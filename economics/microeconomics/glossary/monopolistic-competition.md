# Monopolistic competition

A market structure with many firms selling differentiated products and free entry and exit, as with restaurants, hair salons and clothing brands. Each firm has some control over its price because its product is distinct, but entry drives economic profits to zero in the long run. Developed by Edward Chamberlin and Joan Robinson in 1933. Firms produce with excess capacity and charge prices above marginal cost.
