# Antitrust

The body of law and policy that promotes competition by prohibiting cartels, abuse of dominant market positions and anticompetitive mergers. The term comes from the US Sherman Act of 1890, aimed at the "trusts" of the Gilded Age; elsewhere it is often called competition law, as in Canada's Competition Act. Debates centre on whether enforcement should focus narrowly on consumer welfare or more broadly on market structure and power.
