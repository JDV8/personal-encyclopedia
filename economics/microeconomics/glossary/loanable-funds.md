# Loanable funds

A model of the market in which savers supply funds and borrowers, mainly firms investing in capital, demand them, with the real interest rate as the price that balances the two. Higher interest rates encourage saving and discourage investment. Government borrowing increases demand for loanable funds and can raise interest rates.

In this course the term is introduced in [Capital and interest](../06-factor-markets/07-capital-and-interest.md), part of the section [Factor markets and the distribution of income](../06-factor-markets/README.md).
