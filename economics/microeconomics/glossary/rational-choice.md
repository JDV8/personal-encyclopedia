# Rational choice

The assumption that individuals choose the option that best satisfies their consistent preferences given their constraints. It requires complete and transitive preferences and maximising behaviour, but says nothing about what people should want. Rational choice theory is the foundation of standard economic models and is also used in political science and sociology.
