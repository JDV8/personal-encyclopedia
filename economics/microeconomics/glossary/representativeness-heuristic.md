# Representativeness heuristic

Judging the probability that something belongs to a category by how closely it resembles the category's stereotype, while neglecting base rates and statistical logic. It explains the conjunction fallacy in the Linda problem, base-rate neglect in medical testing, and the gambler's fallacy.

In this course the term is introduced in [Heuristics and biases](../09-behavioural-economics/03-heuristics-and-biases.md), part of the section [Behavioural economics](../09-behavioural-economics/README.md).
