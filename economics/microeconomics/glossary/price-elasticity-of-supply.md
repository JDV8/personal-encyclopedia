# Price elasticity of supply

The percentage change in quantity supplied divided by the percentage change in price. Supply is more elastic when production can be expanded easily, spare capacity exists and the time period is long. The supply of land is perfectly inelastic; the supply of many manufactured goods is elastic in the long run.
