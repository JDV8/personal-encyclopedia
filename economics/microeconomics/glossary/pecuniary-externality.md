# Pecuniary externality

An effect of one party's actions on others that operates through prices, such as a new café lowering the prices rival cafés can charge. Unlike technological externalities, pecuniary externalities do not cause inefficiency in competitive markets, because one group's loss is another's gain. They can matter when markets are incomplete or credit is constrained.
