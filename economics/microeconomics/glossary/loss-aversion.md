# Loss aversion

The tendency for losses to feel worse than equivalent gains feel good. Tversky and Kahneman (1992) estimated that losses weigh about 2.25 times as much as gains. Loss aversion explains why people reject favourable small bets, the endowment effect, status quo bias and the disposition effect in investing. It is a core element of prospect theory.
