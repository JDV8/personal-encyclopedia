# Just price

A medieval scholastic concept of the morally fair price for a good. Thomas Aquinas held that selling a thing for more than it is worth is unjust; many scholastics interpreted the just price as the common market price in the absence of fraud, monopoly or coercion. Debates over price gouging in emergencies echo the concept.
