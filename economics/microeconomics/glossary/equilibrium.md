# Equilibrium

A situation in which no participant has an incentive to change behaviour, given what others are doing. In a market, equilibrium is the price at which quantity demanded equals quantity supplied, so there is neither shortage nor surplus. In the coffee example, equilibrium is P = 3, Q = 500. Equilibrium concepts are central to economics, from market clearing to Nash equilibrium in games.
