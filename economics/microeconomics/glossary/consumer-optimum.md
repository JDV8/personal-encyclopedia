# Consumer optimum

The bundle that maximises a consumer's utility given the budget constraint. With smooth preferences and an interior solution, it occurs where an indifference curve is tangent to the budget line, so the marginal rate of substitution equals the price ratio; equivalently, the marginal utility per dollar is equal across goods. Changes in prices or income move the optimum and trace out demand and Engel curves.
