# Subsidy

A payment by government to producers or consumers that lowers the effective cost of a good, shifting supply or demand. Subsidies increase output and can correct positive externalities, as with vaccines or research, but where no externality exists they create deadweight loss by encouraging production whose cost exceeds its value.
