# Physiocracy

The doctrine of the 18th-century French Physiocrats, led by François Quesnay, that only agriculture produces a net surplus and that the economy should be left free of trade restrictions. Quesnay's Tableau économique (1758) was the first model of the circular flow of the economy. The Physiocrats influenced Adam Smith and advocated a single tax on land.
