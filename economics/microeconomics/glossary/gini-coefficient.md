# Gini coefficient

A measure of inequality ranging from 0 (everyone has the same income) to 1 (one person has everything), equal to the area between the line of equality and the Lorenz curve divided by the total area under the line of equality. Developed by Corrado Gini in 1912. Disposable-income Ginis in rich countries range from the high 0.2s in Nordic countries to around 0.39 in the United States.
