# Excise tax

A tax levied on a specific good, usually per unit, such as taxes on petrol, tobacco and alcohol. An excise tax drives a wedge between the price buyers pay and the price sellers receive; the burden is shared according to the relative elasticities of supply and demand, regardless of who legally pays. Excise taxes are often used both to raise revenue and to discourage consumption.
