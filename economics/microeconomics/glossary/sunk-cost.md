# Sunk cost

A cost that has already been incurred and cannot be recovered, whatever is decided now. Rational decisions should ignore sunk costs and look only at future costs and benefits. People often fall prey to the sunk-cost fallacy, continuing a failing project or finishing a bad film because they have already paid for it.
