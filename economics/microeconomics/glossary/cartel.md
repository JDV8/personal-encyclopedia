# Cartel

A group of firms that agree to coordinate prices, output or market shares to act like a monopoly and raise joint profits. OPEC is a well-known cartel of oil-producing countries. Cartels are unstable because each member has an incentive to cheat by producing more, a prisoners' dilemma. Most countries make private cartels illegal; price-fixing is a criminal offence in Canada and the United States.
