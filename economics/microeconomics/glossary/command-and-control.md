# Command and control

Environmental or other regulation that directly specifies what firms must do, such as installing particular technology, meeting emission limits per unit of output, or banning a substance. It is simple to understand and gives certainty about each source's behaviour, which matters for toxic pollutants. But because it ignores differences in abatement costs among firms, it usually achieves a given environmental goal at higher cost than taxes or tradable permits.
