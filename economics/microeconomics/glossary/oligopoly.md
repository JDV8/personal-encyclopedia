# Oligopoly

A market dominated by a few interdependent firms, each of which must consider rivals' reactions when setting price or output. Examples include airlines, telecommunications and banking in Canada. Oligopoly outcomes range from near-competitive to near-monopoly depending on whether firms compete on price or quantity and whether they can sustain tacit collusion. Game theory is the main tool of analysis.
