# Supply curve

A graph of the quantity of a good sellers would offer at each price, holding other factors constant. It usually slopes upward, reflecting rising marginal cost and the law of supply. In a competitive industry, the supply curve is the horizontal sum of firms' marginal cost curves above minimum average variable cost.
