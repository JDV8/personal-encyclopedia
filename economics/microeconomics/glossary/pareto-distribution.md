# Pareto distribution

A probability distribution in which the number of observations above a value x falls in proportion to a power of x, discovered by Vilfredo Pareto in income data in the 1890s. On logarithmic scales it is a straight line. It describes top incomes, wealth, city sizes and firm sizes, and inspired the popular 80/20 rule.
