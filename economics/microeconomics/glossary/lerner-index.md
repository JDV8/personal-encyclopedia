# Lerner index

A measure of market power, defined as (P − MC) / P, the share of the price that is markup over marginal cost. It is zero under perfect competition and rises with market power. For a profit-maximising firm, it equals the inverse of the absolute price elasticity of demand. Abba Lerner introduced it in 1934.
