# Too big to fail

The belief that some financial institutions are so large and interconnected that governments will rescue them rather than let them fail, to avoid wider economic damage. This expectation creates moral hazard, encouraging risk-taking and giving large banks cheaper funding. Post-2008 reforms such as higher capital requirements and resolution plans aim to reduce it.
