# Tragedy of the commons

The overuse of a shared resource that occurs when each user gains the full benefit of extra use but bears only part of the cost. Garrett Hardin popularised the idea in 1968 with the example of herders on a common pasture. Elinor Ostrom showed that communities can often avoid the tragedy through well-designed local rules.
