# Long-run average cost

The lowest average cost at which each level of output can be produced when all inputs can be varied. The long-run average cost curve is the envelope of short-run average cost curves for different plant sizes. Its shape reflects economies and diseconomies of scale.

In this course the term is introduced in [Long-run costs](../04-producer-theory/07-long-run-costs.md), part of the section [Producer theory](../04-producer-theory/README.md).
