# Effective marginal tax rate

The share of an extra dollar of earnings lost to income taxes, payroll taxes and the withdrawal of means-tested benefits combined. Because benefits often shrink as income rises, low-income families can face effective marginal rates above 50 percent, sometimes higher than top earners'. High rates can discourage work and are a key consideration in designing income support.
