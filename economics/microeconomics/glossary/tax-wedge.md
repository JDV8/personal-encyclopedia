# Tax wedge

The gap between the price buyers pay and the price sellers receive created by a tax. In labour markets, it is the difference between the total cost of employing a worker and the worker's take-home pay, including income and payroll taxes. A larger wedge reduces the quantity traded and increases deadweight loss.
