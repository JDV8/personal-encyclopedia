# Polycentric governance

A system with many overlapping centres of decision-making at different scales, rather than a single central authority. Elinor and Vincent Ostrom argued that polycentric arrangements can be more adaptive and better informed about local conditions, and applied the idea to policing, commons management and climate policy.

In this course the term is introduced in [Elinor Ostrom (1933–2012)](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/15-elinor-ostrom.md), part of the section [Thinkers](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/README.md).
