# Transaction costs

The costs of making an exchange, beyond the price itself: searching for trading partners, negotiating, writing contracts, monitoring and enforcing them. Ronald Coase showed that transaction costs explain why firms exist and why legal rules matter for efficiency. High transaction costs can prevent mutually beneficial trades.
