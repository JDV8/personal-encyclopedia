# Carbon tax

A tax per tonne of carbon dioxide (or equivalent greenhouse gas) emitted, usually levied on fossil fuels according to their carbon content. It is a Pigouvian tax designed to make emitters pay for the climate damage of their emissions. British Columbia introduced one in 2008 and Sweden in 1991. Supporters value its efficiency and revenue; critics point to political difficulty, distributional effects and leakage to untaxed countries.
