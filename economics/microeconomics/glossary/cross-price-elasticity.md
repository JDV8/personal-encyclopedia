# Cross-price elasticity of demand

The percentage change in the quantity demanded of one good divided by the percentage change in the price of another. A positive value means the goods are substitutes (a higher price of tea raises demand for coffee); a negative value means they are complements. Competition authorities use cross-price elasticities to define markets in merger cases.
