# Spontaneous order

Order that emerges from the independent actions of many individuals without central design, such as language, common law, money and market prices. Hayek developed the concept, drawing on Adam Ferguson's phrase that such institutions are "the result of human action, but not the execution of any human design". It contrasts with deliberately planned organisation.

In this course the term is introduced in [Carl Menger (1840–1921)](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/06-carl-menger.md), part of the section [Thinkers](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/README.md). Related terms from the same lesson: [Subjective value](subjective-value.md).
