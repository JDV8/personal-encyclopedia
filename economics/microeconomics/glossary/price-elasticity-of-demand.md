# Price elasticity of demand

The percentage change in quantity demanded divided by the percentage change in price. It measures how responsive buyers are to price. Demand is elastic if the absolute value exceeds one and inelastic if it is below one. Elasticity determines how total revenue responds to price changes and how the burden of a tax is shared.
