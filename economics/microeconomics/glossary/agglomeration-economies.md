# Agglomeration economies

Productivity benefits that firms and workers gain from locating near each other: shared pools of skilled labour, specialised suppliers, and the easy exchange of ideas. Alfred Marshall described them in 1890, saying that in industrial districts the "mysteries of the trade" are "in the air". They are a positive production externality and help explain why cities and clusters such as Silicon Valley are so productive.
