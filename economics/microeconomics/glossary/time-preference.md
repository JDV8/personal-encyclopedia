# Time preference

The degree to which people prefer present consumption to future consumption. A positive time preference means a reward now is worth more than the same reward later. It is one reason interest rates are positive and is captured by the discount factor in economic models.

In this course the term is introduced in [Intertemporal choice](../03-consumer-theory/12-intertemporal-choice.md), part of the section [Consumer theory](../03-consumer-theory/README.md).
