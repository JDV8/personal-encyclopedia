# Discount factor

The weight placed on future utility or money relative to the present. In economics, δ (delta) is often the factor applied to utility one period ahead; with exponential discounting, utility t periods ahead is weighted by δ^t. In finance, the discount factor for a cash flow t years away at interest rate r is 1/(1+r)^t. A lower discount factor means greater impatience.
