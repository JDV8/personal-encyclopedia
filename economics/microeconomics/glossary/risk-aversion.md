# Risk aversion

A preference for a certain outcome over a gamble with the same expected value. With a concave utility function, the pain of losing a dollar exceeds the pleasure of gaining one at the margin, so risk-averse people buy insurance and demand a premium to bear risk. Arrow and Pratt developed standard measures of risk aversion.
