# Producer surplus

The difference between the price sellers receive and the minimum they would accept (their marginal cost), summed over all units sold. On a supply-and-demand diagram it is the area above the supply curve and below the price. In the coffee example, producer surplus at the equilibrium price of 3 is 625.
