# Arrow's impossibility theorem

Kenneth Arrow's 1951 result that no rule for combining individual rankings of three or more options into a social ranking can satisfy all of: unrestricted domain, the Pareto principle, independence of irrelevant alternatives, non-dictatorship and transitivity. Every voting system therefore has some flaw. The theorem founded modern social choice theory and shows that a "social preference" cannot be derived from individual preferences without additional value judgements.
