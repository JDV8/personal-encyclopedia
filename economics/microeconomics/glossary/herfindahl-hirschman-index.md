# Herfindahl–Hirschman index

A measure of market concentration calculated by summing the squares of each firm's market share in percent. A monopoly scores 10,000; a market of 100 equal firms scores 100. Competition authorities use the index and its change after a proposed merger to screen for mergers that might reduce competition substantially.
