# Law of demand

The principle that, other things equal, the quantity demanded of a good falls when its price rises. It reflects substitution toward cheaper alternatives and the reduced purchasing power of a higher price. Exceptions such as Giffen goods are extremely rare. The law of demand is one of the most robust empirical regularities in economics.
