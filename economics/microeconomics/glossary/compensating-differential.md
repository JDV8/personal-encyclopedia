# Compensating differential

A wage difference that offsets differences in the non-wage characteristics of jobs. Dangerous, unpleasant, night-shift or remote jobs must pay more to attract workers, while pleasant or prestigious jobs can pay less. Adam Smith described the idea in 1776. Estimates of the wage premium for risky jobs are used to calculate the value of a statistical life.
