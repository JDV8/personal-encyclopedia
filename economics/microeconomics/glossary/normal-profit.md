# Normal profit

The return just sufficient to keep resources in their current use, equal to the opportunity cost of the owner's capital and effort. When a firm earns zero economic profit, it is earning normal profit. In long-run competitive equilibrium, firms earn exactly normal profit.

In this course the term is introduced in [The firm](../04-producer-theory/01-the-firm.md), part of the section [Producer theory](../04-producer-theory/README.md).
