# Marginal rate of substitution

The rate at which a consumer is willing to give up one good for another while remaining equally satisfied: the absolute slope of an indifference curve. It equals the ratio of the goods' marginal utilities and usually falls as the consumer gets more of a good. At the consumer optimum, it equals the price ratio.
