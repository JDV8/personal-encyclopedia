# Marginal utility

The additional satisfaction from consuming one more unit of a good. It usually diminishes as consumption increases. Marginal utility explains why prices reflect the value of the last unit (the diamond–water paradox), why demand curves slope down, and why consumers allocate spending to equalise marginal utility per dollar.
