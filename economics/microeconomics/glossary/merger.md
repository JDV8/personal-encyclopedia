# Merger

The combination of two firms into one. Horizontal mergers join competitors, vertical mergers join firms at different stages of production, and conglomerate mergers join unrelated businesses. Mergers can create efficiencies but may also reduce competition and raise prices, so competition authorities review large mergers.

In this course the term is introduced in [Competition policy](../05-market-structures/15-competition-policy.md), part of the section [Market structures and game theory](../05-market-structures/README.md).
