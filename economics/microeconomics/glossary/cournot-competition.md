# Cournot competition

A model of oligopoly, from Antoine Augustin Cournot (1838), in which firms simultaneously choose quantities and the market price adjusts to clear the market. In the Nash equilibrium, total output lies between the monopoly and competitive levels, and approaches the competitive level as the number of firms grows. It is a standard model of industries with capacity decisions.

In this course the term is introduced in [Cournot, Bertrand and Stackelberg](../05-market-structures/13-cournot-bertrand-and-stackelberg.md), part of the section [Market structures and game theory](../05-market-structures/README.md).
