# Diminishing marginal utility

The principle that each additional unit of a good adds less satisfaction than the one before. The first slice of pizza is more satisfying than the fifth. It helps explain downward-sloping demand, the diamond–water paradox, risk aversion and the utilitarian case for redistribution. Hermann Gossen stated it as his first law in 1854.
