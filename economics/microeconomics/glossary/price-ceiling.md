# Price ceiling

A legal maximum price, such as rent control or caps on fuel prices. A ceiling set below the equilibrium price creates a shortage, because quantity demanded exceeds quantity supplied. Goods are then rationed by queues, connections or black markets, quality may decline, and deadweight loss arises from trades that no longer happen.
