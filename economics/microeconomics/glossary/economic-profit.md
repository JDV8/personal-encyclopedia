# Economic profit

Total revenue minus total cost, where cost includes both explicit costs and implicit costs such as the opportunity cost of the owner's time and capital. Zero economic profit means the firm earns a normal return, as much as its resources could earn elsewhere. In long-run competitive equilibrium, entry and exit drive economic profit to zero.
