# Isocost line

A line showing all combinations of two inputs, such as labour and capital, that cost the same total amount at given input prices. Its slope is the negative of the ratio of input prices. A cost-minimising firm chooses the input combination where an isoquant is tangent to the lowest possible isocost line.

In this course the term is introduced in [Isoquants and input choice](../04-producer-theory/04-isoquants-and-input-choice.md), part of the section [Producer theory](../04-producer-theory/README.md).
