# Nash bargaining solution

John Nash's 1950 solution to the problem of how two parties split the gains from cooperation. Based on a few axioms, it selects the outcome that maximises the product of each party's gain over its disagreement payoff. A party with a better outside option gets a larger share. It is widely used in models of wage bargaining and trade negotiations.

In this course the term is introduced in [John Nash (1928–2015)](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/14-john-nash.md), part of the section [Thinkers](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/README.md).
