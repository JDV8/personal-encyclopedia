# Endowment effect

The tendency to value something more highly once one owns it. In the classic mug experiments by Kahneman, Knetsch and Thaler (1990), owners demanded roughly two to three times as much to sell a mug as non-owners would pay to buy one. It is usually explained by loss aversion. Its size depends on experimental procedures and experience.
