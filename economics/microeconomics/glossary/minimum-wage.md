# Minimum wage

A legal floor on the hourly wage employers may pay. In a competitive labour market, a binding minimum wage raises pay for those employed but reduces employment; in a monopsonistic market, a moderate minimum wage can raise both wages and employment. Empirical research since Card and Krueger (1994) has found smaller employment effects than simple models predict, though debate continues.
