# Budget constraint

The set of consumption bundles a consumer can afford given income and prices. With two goods, it is a straight line whose slope equals the negative of the price ratio and whose intercepts are income divided by each price. A rise in income shifts it outward in parallel; a change in one price rotates it. Consumers choose the point on the budget line that reaches the highest indifference curve.

In this course the term is introduced in [Choice and trade-offs](../01-foundations/02-choice-and-trade-offs.md), part of the section [Foundations: scarcity, choice and trade](../01-foundations/README.md).
