# Coase theorem

The proposition, drawn from Ronald Coase's 1960 article, that if property rights are well defined and transaction costs are zero, private bargaining will lead to an efficient outcome regardless of who initially holds the rights. The assignment affects who pays whom but not the allocation. Coase's own emphasis was the converse: because transaction costs are usually positive, legal rules and institutions matter for efficiency.
