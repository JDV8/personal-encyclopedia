# Price taker

A buyer or seller too small relative to the market to influence the price, who therefore takes it as given. In perfect competition all participants are price takers, so each firm faces a horizontal demand curve and its marginal revenue equals the market price.

In this course the term is introduced in [Perfect competition](../05-market-structures/01-perfect-competition.md), part of the section [Market structures and game theory](../05-market-structures/README.md).
