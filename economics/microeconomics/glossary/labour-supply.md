# Labour supply

The relationship between the wage and the quantity of labour people are willing to offer. Individuals choose between work and leisure; a higher wage has a substitution effect (leisure becomes costlier) and an income effect (people can afford more leisure), so individual supply may even bend backward. Market labour supply also depends on participation decisions, population and migration.
