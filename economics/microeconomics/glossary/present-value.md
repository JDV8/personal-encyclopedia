# Present value

The value today of a future amount of money, found by discounting it at an interest rate: a payment of X in t years is worth X/(1+r)^t today. Present value allows comparison of costs and benefits occurring at different times and underlies investment decisions, asset prices and cost-benefit analysis.

In this course the term is introduced in [Intertemporal choice](../03-consumer-theory/12-intertemporal-choice.md), part of the section [Consumer theory](../03-consumer-theory/README.md).
