# Average total cost

Total cost divided by output: cost per unit. It is the sum of average fixed cost and average variable cost, and in the short run is typically U-shaped, falling at first as fixed costs are spread over more units and rising later as diminishing returns push up variable costs. Marginal cost cuts average total cost at its minimum, the efficient scale. A firm makes economic profit when price exceeds average total cost.
