# X-inefficiency

Harvey Leibenstein's 1966 term for the failure of firms to produce at the lowest possible cost because of weak competitive pressure, slack management or poor motivation. Monopolies and protected firms may have higher costs than necessary, an inefficiency in addition to the deadweight loss of monopoly pricing.

In this course the term is introduced in [Monopoly pricing and deadweight loss](../05-market-structures/05-monopoly-pricing-and-deadweight-loss.md), part of the section [Market structures and game theory](../05-market-structures/README.md).
