# Credibility revolution

The shift in applied economics since the 1990s toward research designs that credibly identify cause and effect, such as randomised experiments, natural experiments, regression discontinuity and difference-in-differences. The term was popularised by Angrist and Pischke in 2010. Card, Angrist and Imbens shared the 2021 Nobel prize for contributions to these methods.

In this course the term is introduced in [Twentieth-century microeconomics](../10-history-of-thought/04-twentieth-century-microeconomics.md), part of the section [History of economic thought](../10-history-of-thought/README.md).
