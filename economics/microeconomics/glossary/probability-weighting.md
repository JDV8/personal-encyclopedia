# Probability weighting

The tendency to treat probabilities non-linearly when making decisions, overweighting small probabilities and underweighting moderate and large ones. It is a feature of prospect theory and helps explain why people buy both lottery tickets and insurance, and why the certainty of an outcome has special appeal.
