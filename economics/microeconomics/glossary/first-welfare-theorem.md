# First welfare theorem

The result that every competitive equilibrium is Pareto efficient, provided there are no externalities, public goods or market power, information is complete, markets exist for all goods, and preferences are locally non-satiated. It is the formal version of Adam Smith's invisible hand, and its assumptions also catalogue the reasons markets can fail.
