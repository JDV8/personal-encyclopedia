# Veil of ignorance

John Rawls's thought experiment, from A Theory of Justice (1971), in which people choose principles of justice without knowing their own position in society: their wealth, talents, race or gender. Rawls argued they would choose equal basic liberties and arrange inequalities to benefit the least advantaged, the difference principle.
