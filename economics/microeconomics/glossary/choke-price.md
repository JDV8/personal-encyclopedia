# Choke price

The price at which quantity demanded falls to zero: the vertical intercept of a linear demand curve. In the coffee example, demand Qd = 1100 − 200P has a choke price of 5.50. The choke price is needed to compute consumer surplus as the triangle between the demand curve and the market price.
