# Libertarian paternalism

Thaler and Sunstein's term for policies that steer people toward choices that make them better off, as judged by themselves, while preserving their freedom to choose otherwise. Nudges such as defaults are its main tool. Critics question whether such steering respects autonomy and how to know what people would judge to be in their interest.

In this course the term is introduced in [Nudges and choice architecture](../09-behavioural-economics/07-nudges-and-choice-architecture.md), part of the section [Behavioural economics](../09-behavioural-economics/README.md).
