# Deductible

The amount an insured person must pay out of pocket before insurance covers a loss. Deductibles reduce moral hazard by leaving the insured with some stake in avoiding losses, lower premiums, and screen customers by risk: low-risk people tend to choose high-deductible plans. The trade-off is that the insured bears more risk.
