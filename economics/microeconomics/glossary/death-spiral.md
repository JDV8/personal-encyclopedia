# Death spiral

An unravelling of an insurance market through adverse selection. When premiums reflect average risk, low-risk customers drop out; the remaining pool is riskier, premiums rise, more people leave, and so on, until only the highest-risk people, or no one, remain insured. Mandates, subsidies and group plans that pool people for reasons unrelated to risk are common defences.
