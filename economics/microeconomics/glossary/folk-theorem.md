# Folk theorem

A family of results in game theory showing that, in infinitely repeated games where players are sufficiently patient, almost any outcome that gives each player at least their minimum guaranteed payoff can be sustained as an equilibrium, including cooperation in the prisoners' dilemma. The name reflects that the result was widely known before anyone published it. It explains how cartels and cooperation can persist.
