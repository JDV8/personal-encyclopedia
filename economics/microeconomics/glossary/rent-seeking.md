# Rent-seeking

Spending resources to capture a transfer of existing wealth, such as a tariff, licence, subsidy or monopoly, rather than to create new wealth. The term was coined by Anne Krueger in 1974, building on Gordon Tullock's 1967 analysis. Resources spent on lobbying and competing for such privileges are a social cost additional to the deadweight loss of the policy itself.
