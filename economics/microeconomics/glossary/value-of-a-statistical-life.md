# Value of a statistical life

The amount a group of people is collectively willing to pay for small reductions in risk that together save one expected life. If 100,000 people would each pay 100 dollars to reduce their annual risk of death by 1 in 100,000, the value of a statistical life is 10 million dollars. It is used in cost-benefit analysis of safety regulations, not to value any particular person.
