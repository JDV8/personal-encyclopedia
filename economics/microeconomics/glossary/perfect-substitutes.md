# Perfect substitutes

Goods that a consumer regards as interchangeable at a constant rate, such as two brands of identical bottled water. Indifference curves are straight lines. The consumer buys only the cheaper good (adjusted for the rate of substitution), usually giving a corner solution.

In this course the term is introduced in [Indifference curves](../03-consumer-theory/03-indifference-curves.md), part of the section [Consumer theory](../03-consumer-theory/README.md).
