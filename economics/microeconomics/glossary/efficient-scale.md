# Efficient scale

The quantity of output at which a firm's average total cost is minimised. In long-run competitive equilibrium, firms produce at their efficient scale and price equals minimum average total cost. In the brewery example, the efficient scale is 10 units, where average total cost is 20. Monopolistically competitive firms typically produce below efficient scale, with excess capacity.
