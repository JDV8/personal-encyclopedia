# Price–specie flow mechanism

David Hume's 1752 argument that trade imbalances under a gold standard correct themselves. A country with a trade surplus gains gold, which raises its money supply and prices, making its exports dearer and imports cheaper until the surplus disappears. It undermined the mercantilist goal of accumulating gold through permanent surpluses.
