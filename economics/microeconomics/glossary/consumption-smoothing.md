# Consumption smoothing

The tendency of people to spread consumption evenly over time rather than letting it rise and fall with income. Because of diminishing marginal utility, a steady path gives more total utility than a feast-and-famine one. People smooth consumption by saving in good times, borrowing in bad times and buying insurance. It underlies the life-cycle and permanent-income theories of saving.
