# Accounting profit

Revenue minus explicit costs: the money actually paid out for wages, materials, rent, interest and so on. It is the profit figure reported in financial statements. Because it ignores implicit costs, such as the owner's time or the return the owner's capital could earn elsewhere, accounting profit is usually larger than economic profit. A business can show an accounting profit while making an economic loss.
