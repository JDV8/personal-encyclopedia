# Economic rent

A payment to a factor of production above the minimum needed to keep it in its current use. Land earns economic rent because its supply does not depend on its price; so do star performers, holders of scarce licences and protected monopolies. David Ricardo developed the theory for land. Taxing pure economic rent does not change behaviour and so causes no deadweight loss.
