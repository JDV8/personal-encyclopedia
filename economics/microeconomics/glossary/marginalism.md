# Marginalism

The approach, emerging from the marginal revolution, of explaining economic decisions and prices by changes at the margin: marginal utility, marginal cost, marginal product and marginal revenue. It is the methodological foundation of neoclassical economics.

In this course the term is introduced in [The marginal revolution](../10-history-of-thought/03-the-marginal-revolution.md), part of the section [History of economic thought](../10-history-of-thought/README.md).
