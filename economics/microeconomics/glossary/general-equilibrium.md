# General equilibrium

A state in which all markets in an economy clear simultaneously, with prices determined together. General equilibrium analysis, pioneered by Léon Walras, captures the interdependence of markets that partial equilibrium analysis ignores. Arrow and Debreu proved existence of competitive general equilibrium in 1954, and computable general equilibrium models are used to analyse trade and tax policies.
