# Patent

A legal right granting an inventor exclusive use of an invention for a limited period, typically 20 years from filing. Patents create temporary monopolies to reward innovation, trading higher prices and deadweight loss for stronger incentives to invent. In return, inventors must publicly disclose how the invention works.
