# Transfer earnings

The minimum payment needed to keep a factor of production in its current use, equal to what it could earn in its next best use. Any payment above transfer earnings is economic rent. A footballer who would play for 200,000 but is paid 10 million has transfer earnings of 200,000.

In this course the term is introduced in [Land and rent](../06-factor-markets/08-land-and-rent.md), part of the section [Factor markets and the distribution of income](../06-factor-markets/README.md). Related terms from the same lesson: [Land value tax](land-value-tax.md).
