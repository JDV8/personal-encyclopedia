# Diamond–water paradox

The puzzle, discussed by Adam Smith, of why water, essential to life, is cheap while diamonds, inessential, are expensive. The marginal revolution resolved it: prices reflect marginal utility, the value of one more unit, not total utility. Water is plentiful, so an extra litre adds little; diamonds are scarce, so an extra one is highly valued.
