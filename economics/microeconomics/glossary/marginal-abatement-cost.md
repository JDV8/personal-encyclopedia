# Marginal abatement cost

The cost of reducing pollution by one more unit. Marginal abatement costs usually rise as more is abated, since cheap reductions are made first. Efficient pollution control sets marginal abatement cost equal to marginal damage, and least-cost control requires equal marginal abatement costs across all sources, which a uniform tax or permit price achieves.
