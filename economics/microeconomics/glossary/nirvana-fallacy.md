# Nirvana fallacy

Harold Demsetz's 1969 term for comparing a real, imperfect institution with an idealised alternative, and concluding that the real one should be replaced. Showing that a market fails does not prove that government intervention would do better, and vice versa. The proper comparison is between realistic alternatives, each with its own imperfections.
