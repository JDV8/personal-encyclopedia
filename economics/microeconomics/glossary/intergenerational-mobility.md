# Intergenerational mobility

The extent to which people's economic position differs from that of their parents. High mobility means children of poor parents have good chances of rising. It is often measured by the correlation between parents' and children's earnings. Research by Miles Corak and others finds mobility higher in Canada and the Nordic countries than in the United States.

In this course the term is introduced in [Income inequality](../06-factor-markets/09-income-inequality.md), part of the section [Factor markets and the distribution of income](../06-factor-markets/README.md).
