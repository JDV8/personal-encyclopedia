# Unintended consequences

Outcomes of an action or policy that were not foreseen or intended, often because people respond to changed incentives. Rent control can reduce housing supply; fuel-efficiency rules can encourage more driving. Economists stress tracing through incentive effects to anticipate them.

In this course the term is introduced in [Incentives](../01-foundations/04-incentives.md), part of the section [Foundations: scarcity, choice and trade](../01-foundations/README.md).
