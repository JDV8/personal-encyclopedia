# Derived demand

Demand for a factor of production that arises from the demand for the goods it helps produce. Firms hire bakers because people want bread; demand for steel depends on demand for cars and buildings. Carl Menger described the value of production goods as derived from consumer goods. Changes in product demand therefore ripple through to wages and other factor prices.
