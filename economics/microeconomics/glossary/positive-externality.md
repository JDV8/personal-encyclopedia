# Positive externality

A benefit that an activity confers on third parties who do not pay for it, such as the reduced infection risk others enjoy when someone is vaccinated, or the knowledge spillovers from research. Because the decision-maker ignores these benefits, markets underprovide such goods. Subsidies, public provision and intellectual property rights are common remedies.
