# Invisible hand

Adam Smith's metaphor for the way individuals pursuing their own interests in competitive markets can promote the general good without intending to. Smith used the phrase only once in the Wealth of Nations. The first welfare theorem gives it precise form, along with the conditions, such as no externalities, under which it holds.

In this course the term is introduced in [Markets, prices and property rights](../01-foundations/10-markets-prices-and-property-rights.md), part of the section [Foundations: scarcity, choice and trade](../01-foundations/README.md).
