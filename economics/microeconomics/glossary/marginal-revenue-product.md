# Marginal revenue product

The additional revenue a firm earns by employing one more unit of an input: the input's marginal product times marginal revenue (or price, for a competitive firm). A profit-maximising firm hires an input until its marginal revenue product equals its price, so the MRP curve is the firm's demand curve for the input.
