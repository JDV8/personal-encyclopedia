# Engel curve

A graph showing how the quantity of a good purchased changes with income, holding prices constant. Upward-sloping Engel curves indicate normal goods; downward-sloping ones indicate inferior goods. Named after the 19th-century German statistician Ernst Engel, who studied household budgets.

In this course the term is introduced in [Income changes and Engel curves](../03-consumer-theory/07-income-changes-and-engel-curves.md), part of the section [Consumer theory](../03-consumer-theory/README.md). Related terms from the same lesson: [Luxury good](luxury-good.md).
