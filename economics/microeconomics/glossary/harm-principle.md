# Harm principle

John Stuart Mill's principle, stated in On Liberty (1859), that "the only purpose for which power can be rightfully exercised over any member of a civilized community, against his will, is to prevent harm to others." It supports intervention to correct externalities but is in tension with paternalistic policies aimed at protecting people from themselves.

In this course the term is introduced in [John Stuart Mill (1806–1873)](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/03-john-stuart-mill.md), part of the section [Thinkers](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/README.md).
