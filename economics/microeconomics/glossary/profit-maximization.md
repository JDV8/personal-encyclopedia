# Profit maximisation

The assumption that firms choose output and inputs to make the largest possible economic profit. The rule is to produce where marginal revenue equals marginal cost, provided price covers average variable cost in the short run. Alternative theories suggest managers may pursue sales, growth or satisficing goals, but profit maximisation predicts behaviour well in many settings.
