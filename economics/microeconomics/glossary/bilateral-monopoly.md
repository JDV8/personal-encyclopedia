# Bilateral monopoly

A market with a single seller facing a single buyer, such as a union bargaining with the only large employer in a town. Price and quantity are not determined by supply and demand alone; the outcome depends on bargaining power and negotiation, within a range bounded by what each side could get without agreement. Bilateral monopoly can, in some cases, produce an outcome closer to the competitive level than either monopoly or monopsony alone.
