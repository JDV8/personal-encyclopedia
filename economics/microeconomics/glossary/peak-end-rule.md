# Peak–end rule

Daniel Kahneman's finding that people judge past experiences mainly by their most intense moment and how they ended, largely neglecting duration. In the cold-hand experiments, people preferred to repeat a longer painful episode that ended less painfully. It shows that remembered utility can differ from experienced utility.

In this course the term is introduced in [Daniel Kahneman (1934–2024)](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/16-daniel-kahneman.md), part of the section [Thinkers](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/README.md). Related terms from the same lesson: [Planning fallacy](planning-fallacy.md).
