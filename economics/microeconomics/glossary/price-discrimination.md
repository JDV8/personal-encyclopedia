# Price discrimination

Charging different prices to different customers for the same good, for reasons not related to cost. Pigou distinguished first-degree (each customer pays their maximum), second-degree (prices vary with quantity or version) and third-degree (different prices for different groups, such as student discounts). It requires market power and the ability to prevent resale.
