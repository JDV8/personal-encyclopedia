# Revealed preference

The approach, developed by Paul Samuelson in 1938, of inferring preferences from actual choices rather than from introspection or surveys. If a consumer chooses bundle A when B was also affordable, A is revealed preferred to B. It grounds consumer theory in observable behaviour, and revealed-preference methods are used to value non-market goods.
