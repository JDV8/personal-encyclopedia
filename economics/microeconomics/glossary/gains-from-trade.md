# Gains from trade

The increase in well-being that results when people exchange goods they value less for goods they value more, or specialise according to comparative advantage and trade. Voluntary trade benefits both sides, otherwise it would not occur. Gains from trade are measured by consumer and producer surplus and underlie the case for markets and international trade.
