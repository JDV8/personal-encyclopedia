# Second welfare theorem

The result that any Pareto-efficient allocation can be achieved as a competitive equilibrium, given appropriate lump-sum redistribution of initial endowments and assumptions including convexity. It suggests that efficiency and equity can in principle be separated, with markets delivering efficiency and transfers delivering equity, though real taxes are rarely lump-sum.
