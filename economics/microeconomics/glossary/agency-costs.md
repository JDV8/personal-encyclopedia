# Agency costs

The costs that arise when an agent acts on behalf of a principal whose interests differ. Jensen and Meckling (1976) divided them into monitoring costs borne by the principal (audits, boards), bonding costs borne by the agent to reassure the principal, and the residual loss from decisions that still diverge from what the principal would want. Agency costs are central to corporate governance and executive pay design.
