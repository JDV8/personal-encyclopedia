# Ecological rationality

Gerd Gigerenzer's view that a decision strategy is rational to the extent that it fits the structure of the environment in which it is used. Simple "fast and frugal" heuristics, such as relying on recognition or a single good cue, can perform as well as complex calculations when information is scarce or noisy. It contrasts with the heuristics-and-biases emphasis on errors.
