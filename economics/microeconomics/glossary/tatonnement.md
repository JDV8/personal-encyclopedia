# Tâtonnement

French for "groping": Léon Walras's description of how an imaginary auctioneer finds general equilibrium by calling out prices, raising those of goods in excess demand and lowering those in excess supply, with no trade until all markets clear. It is a thought experiment; later research showed it need not always converge.

In this course the term is introduced in [Léon Walras (1834–1910)](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/07-leon-walras.md), part of the section [Thinkers](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/README.md).
