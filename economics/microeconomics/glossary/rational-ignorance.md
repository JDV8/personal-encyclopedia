# Rational ignorance

Anthony Downs's term for voters' decision not to become well informed about policy, because the cost of acquiring information exceeds the tiny expected benefit of casting a better-informed vote. It allows policies with concentrated benefits and diffuse costs to pass unnoticed, a source of government failure.

In this course the term is introduced in [Government failure](../07-market-failure/15-government-failure.md), part of the section [Market failure](../07-market-failure/README.md). Related terms from the same lesson: [Government failure](government-failure.md), [Public choice theory](public-choice-theory.md).
