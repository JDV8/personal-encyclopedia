# Representative agent

A modelling simplification in which the behaviour of many individuals is summarised by a single typical decision-maker. It makes models tractable, but can mislead when individuals differ in ways that matter, since the Sonnenschein–Mantel–Debreu results show that aggregate behaviour need not resemble individual behaviour.

In this course the term is introduced in [From individual to market demand](../03-consumer-theory/10-from-individual-to-market-demand.md), part of the section [Consumer theory](../03-consumer-theory/README.md).
