# Inequity aversion

A preference for fairer outcomes: people dislike earning less than others and, to a lesser degree, earning more. Models by Fehr and Schmidt (1999) and Bolton and Ockenfels (2000) use it to explain why people reject low ultimatum offers and why cooperation can be sustained by willingness to punish free riders.
