# Free entry and exit

The condition that firms can enter or leave a market without significant barriers or costs. It is a key assumption of perfect competition and monopolistic competition: in the long run, economic profits attract entrants and losses cause exit, driving profits to zero. Where entry is blocked, profits can persist.
