# Positive economics

Economic statements about what is, was or will be, which can in principle be tested against evidence, such as "a minimum wage increase reduces teen employment by X percent". Milton Friedman's 1953 essay argued positive economics should be judged by its predictions. It contrasts with normative economics.

In this course the term is introduced in [Models and economic method](../01-foundations/11-models-and-economic-method.md), part of the section [Foundations: scarcity, choice and trade](../01-foundations/README.md).
