# Technology

The knowledge and methods used to turn inputs into outputs. Improvements in technology allow more output from the same inputs, shifting supply curves to the right and the production possibilities frontier outward. Technological progress is the main source of long-run growth in living standards.

In this course the term is introduced in [Shifts in supply](../02-supply-and-demand/04-shifts-in-supply.md), part of the section [Supply and demand](../02-supply-and-demand/README.md). Related terms from the same lesson: [Subsidy](subsidy.md).
