# Ordinal utility

The view that utility indicates only the order of preferences, not their intensity: a consumer can say they prefer bundle A to B, but not by how much. Any utility function that preserves the ranking represents the same preferences. Developed by Pareto, Slutsky, Hicks and Allen, it is the basis of modern consumer theory and indifference curves.
