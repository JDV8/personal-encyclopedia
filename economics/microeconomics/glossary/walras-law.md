# Walras's law

The principle that the total value of excess demands across all markets is zero, because each agent's planned spending equals their income. It implies that if all markets but one are in equilibrium, the last must be too, so only relative prices are determined. Named after Léon Walras.

In this course the term is introduced in [General equilibrium](../08-welfare-economics/02-general-equilibrium.md), part of the section [Welfare economics](../08-welfare-economics/README.md).
