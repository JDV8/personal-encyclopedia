# Homo economicus

"Economic man": the idealised decision-maker of standard economic models, who has consistent preferences, processes information perfectly, maximises utility and is usually self-interested. John Stuart Mill described studying people as beings who desire wealth as a deliberate abstraction. Behavioural economics studies where real people depart from this model.

In this course the term is introduced in [The rationality assumption](../09-behavioural-economics/01-the-rationality-assumption.md), part of the section [Behavioural economics](../09-behavioural-economics/README.md).
