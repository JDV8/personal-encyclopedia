# Mercantilism

The set of economic ideas and policies dominant in Europe from the 16th to 18th centuries, holding that national wealth consists of gold and silver and that a country should export more than it imports. Mercantilists favoured tariffs, colonies and support for domestic manufacturing. Hume and Smith criticised it, and its emphasis on trade surpluses still appears in political debate.
