# Lorenz curve

A graph of the cumulative share of income (or wealth) received by the poorest x percent of households, introduced by Max Lorenz in 1905. A perfectly equal distribution is the 45-degree line; the further the curve sags below it, the more unequal the distribution. The Gini coefficient is derived from the area between the curve and the line of equality.

In this course the term is introduced in [Income inequality](../06-factor-markets/09-income-inequality.md), part of the section [Factor markets and the distribution of income](../06-factor-markets/README.md).
