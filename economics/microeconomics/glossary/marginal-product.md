# Marginal product

The additional output produced by one more unit of an input, holding other inputs constant. The marginal product of labour typically falls as more workers are added to a fixed amount of capital, reflecting diminishing marginal returns. Multiplied by marginal revenue, it gives the marginal revenue product, which determines factor demand.
