# Firm

An organisation that combines inputs to produce goods or services for sale. In standard theory, firms maximise profit. Ronald Coase asked why firms exist at all if markets coordinate production so well, and answered that firms economise on the transaction costs of using markets. Firms range from sole proprietors to multinational corporations.
