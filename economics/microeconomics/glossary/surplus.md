# Surplus

Two meanings in this course. In market analysis, a surplus or excess supply is a situation where quantity supplied exceeds quantity demanded at the current price, which pushes prices down or persists under a price floor. In welfare analysis, surplus means the gains from trade: consumer surplus, producer surplus and their sum, total surplus.

In this course the term is introduced in [Market equilibrium](../02-supply-and-demand/05-market-equilibrium.md), part of the section [Supply and demand](../02-supply-and-demand/README.md). Related terms from the same lesson: [Equilibrium](equilibrium.md), [Shortage](shortage.md).
