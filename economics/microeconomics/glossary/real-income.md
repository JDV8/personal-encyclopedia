# Real income

Income adjusted for prices, measuring the quantity of goods and services it can buy. If prices fall while money income is unchanged, real income rises. In consumer theory, a fall in the price of a good raises real income, producing the income effect.

In this course the term is introduced in [The budget constraint](../03-consumer-theory/05-budget-constraint.md), part of the section [Consumer theory](../03-consumer-theory/README.md). Related terms from the same lesson: [Relative price](relative-price.md).
