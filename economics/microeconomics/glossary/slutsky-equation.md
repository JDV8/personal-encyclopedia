# Slutsky equation

The equation, due to Eugen Slutsky (1915), that decomposes the total effect of a price change on quantity demanded into a substitution effect (holding real income constant) and an income effect (the change in purchasing power). It shows why demand curves usually slope downward and when they might not, as with Giffen goods.

In this course the term is introduced in [Income and substitution effects](../03-consumer-theory/08-income-and-substitution-effects.md), part of the section [Consumer theory](../03-consumer-theory/README.md).
