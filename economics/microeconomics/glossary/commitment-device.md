# Commitment device

An arrangement that restricts one's own future choices to help stick to a plan, especially in the face of present bias. Odysseus tying himself to the mast is the classic image. Modern examples include Save More Tomorrow pension plans, savings accounts that restrict withdrawals, and pledges with financial penalties. Demand for commitment devices suggests people are at least partly aware of their self-control problems.
