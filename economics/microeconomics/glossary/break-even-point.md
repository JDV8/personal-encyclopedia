# Break-even point

The price or output at which revenue just covers all costs, so economic profit is zero. For a competitive firm, the break-even price is the minimum of average total cost. Above it, the firm makes economic profit and new firms are attracted; below it, firms make losses and, in the long run, exit. In the brewery example, the break-even price is 20, where average total cost is minimised at 10 units.
