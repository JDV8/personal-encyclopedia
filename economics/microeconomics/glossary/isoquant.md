# Isoquant

A curve showing all combinations of inputs that produce the same quantity of output, the producer's counterpart of an indifference curve. Isoquants are usually downward sloping and convex. Their slope is the marginal rate of technical substitution, which measures how much of one input can replace another while keeping output constant.
