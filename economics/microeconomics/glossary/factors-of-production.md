# Factors of production

The resources used to produce goods and services, traditionally classified as land (natural resources), labour (human effort) and capital (produced means of production), with entrepreneurship sometimes added as a fourth. Their payments are rent, wages, interest and profit. The distribution of income among these factors was the central question of classical economics.

In this course the term is introduced in [Scarcity and the economic problem](../01-foundations/01-scarcity.md), part of the section [Foundations: scarcity, choice and trade](../01-foundations/README.md).
