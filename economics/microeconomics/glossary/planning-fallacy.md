# Planning fallacy

The tendency to underestimate the time, cost and risk of future projects while overestimating their benefits, identified by Kahneman and Tversky in 1979. It explains chronic overruns in construction, software and public infrastructure projects. Taking an "outside view", comparing with similar past projects, helps correct it.

In this course the term is introduced in [Daniel Kahneman (1934–2024)](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/16-daniel-kahneman.md), part of the section [Thinkers](../10-history-of-thought/thinkers/README.md). Related terms from the same lesson: [Peak–end rule](peak-end-rule.md).
