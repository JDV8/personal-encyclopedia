# Variable cost

A cost that changes with the quantity produced, such as raw materials, energy and hourly labour. Variable cost is zero at zero output. In the short run, a firm should keep producing as long as revenue covers variable cost.

In this course the term is introduced in [Short-run costs](../04-producer-theory/05-short-run-costs.md), part of the section [Producer theory](../04-producer-theory/README.md). Related terms from the same lesson: [Average total cost](average-total-cost.md), [Fixed cost](fixed-cost.md).
