# Adverse selection

A problem of hidden information before a deal: the people most eager to trade are disproportionately the ones the other side least wants to trade with. Sick people are keener to buy health insurance; owners of bad used cars are keener to sell. If the uninformed side prices for the average, good types withdraw, average quality falls, and the market can shrink or collapse. Responses include mandates, risk classification, warranties, screening and reputation.
