# Certainty equivalent

The sure amount of money a person regards as equally good as a risky gamble. A risk-averse person's certainty equivalent is less than the gamble's expected value: someone might accept 40 dollars for certain instead of a 50-50 chance of 0 or 100. The difference between the expected value and the certainty equivalent is the risk premium, the amount the person would pay to avoid the risk.
