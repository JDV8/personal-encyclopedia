# Signalling

An action taken by an informed party to reveal hidden information to an uninformed party, credible because it is more costly for low-quality types to take. Michael Spence's 1973 model of education as a signal of ability is the classic example; warranties, advertising and dividends can also be signals. Signalling can be socially wasteful even when privately rational.

In this course the term is introduced in [Human capital](../06-factor-markets/05-human-capital.md), part of the section [Factor markets and the distribution of income](../06-factor-markets/README.md).
