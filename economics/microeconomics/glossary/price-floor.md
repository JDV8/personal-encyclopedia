# Price floor

A legal minimum price, such as a minimum wage or agricultural price support. A floor set above the equilibrium price creates a surplus, because quantity supplied exceeds quantity demanded. Governments may buy the surplus, as with farm price supports, or the excess may show up as unemployment in labour markets.
