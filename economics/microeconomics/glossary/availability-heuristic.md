# Availability heuristic

Judging how frequent or likely something is by how easily examples come to mind. Vivid, recent or heavily reported events, such as plane crashes or shark attacks, seem more common than they are, while undramatic risks are underestimated. Availability helps explain why flood insurance purchases rise after floods and fade afterwards, and why media coverage can distort perceptions of risk.
