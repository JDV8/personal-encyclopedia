# Quota

A legal limit on the quantity of a good that may be produced, sold or imported. Import quotas raise domestic prices like tariffs, but the extra revenue goes to holders of quota licences rather than the government. Production quotas, such as Canada's supply management of dairy and poultry, restrict output to support prices.
