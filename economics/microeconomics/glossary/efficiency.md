# Efficiency

The property of an allocation in which resources are used to create the greatest possible value, so that no one can be made better off without making someone else worse off (Pareto efficiency), or total surplus is maximised. Economists distinguish productive efficiency (producing at lowest cost) from allocative efficiency (producing the right mix). Efficiency is distinct from fairness.
