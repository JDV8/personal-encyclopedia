# Mixed strategy

A strategy in which a player chooses among actions randomly, with specified probabilities. In matching pennies, each player shows heads or tails with probability one half. John Nash proved that every finite game has an equilibrium if players may use mixed strategies. Penalty kicks in football are a well-studied real example.
