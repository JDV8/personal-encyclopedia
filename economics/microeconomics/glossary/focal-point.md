# Focal point

A solution that people tend to choose in a coordination game because it seems natural, salient or special, even without communication. Thomas Schelling, who introduced the idea in The Strategy of Conflict (1960), found that people asked to meet a stranger in New York often chose noon at Grand Central Terminal. Focal points help explain how people coordinate when there are many possible equilibria.
