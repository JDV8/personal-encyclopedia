# Non-rival

Describes a good whose use by one person does not reduce the amount available to others, such as a radio broadcast, a mathematical theorem or a lighthouse beam. For non-rival goods, the marginal cost of an additional user is zero, so excluding anyone is inefficient. Public goods and club goods are non-rival.

In this course the term is introduced in [Public goods](../07-market-failure/08-public-goods.md), part of the section [Market failure](../07-market-failure/README.md).
