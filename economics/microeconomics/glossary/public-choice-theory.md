# Public choice theory

The application of economic methods to political behaviour, treating voters, politicians and bureaucrats as self-interested actors. Founded by James Buchanan and Gordon Tullock, it analyses voting, lobbying, rent-seeking and bureaucracy, and identifies sources of government failure. Buchanan won the 1986 Nobel prize.

In this course the term is introduced in [Government failure](../07-market-failure/15-government-failure.md), part of the section [Market failure](../07-market-failure/README.md).
