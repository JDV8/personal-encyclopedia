# Willingness to pay

The maximum amount a buyer would pay for a good, measuring its value to them. A demand curve can be read as the willingness to pay of the marginal buyer at each quantity. Consumer surplus is the difference between willingness to pay and the price paid.

In this course the term is introduced in [Demand](../02-supply-and-demand/01-demand.md), part of the section [Supply and demand](../02-supply-and-demand/README.md). Related terms from the same lesson: [Demand](demand.md), [Demand curve](demand-curve.md), [Law of demand](law-of-demand.md).
