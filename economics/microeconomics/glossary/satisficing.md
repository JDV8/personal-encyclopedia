# Satisficing

Herbert Simon's term, combining "satisfy" and "suffice", for choosing the first option that meets an aspiration level rather than searching for the best. Satisficing can be rational when search and computation are costly. It is a central idea of bounded rationality.

In this course the term is introduced in [Bounded rationality](../09-behavioural-economics/02-bounded-rationality.md), part of the section [Behavioural economics](../09-behavioural-economics/README.md).
