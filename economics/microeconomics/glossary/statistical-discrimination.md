# Statistical discrimination

Treating individuals differently according to the average characteristics of a group they belong to, when individual information is costly to obtain, for example employers using race, gender or age as a proxy for productivity. Edmund Phelps and Kenneth Arrow developed the theory in the 1970s. Even when based on accurate averages, it is unfair to individuals and often illegal.
