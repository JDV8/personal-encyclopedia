# Black market

Illegal trade in goods or services, often arising when prices are capped below equilibrium, goods are banned, or taxes are very high. Under a binding price ceiling, buyers who cannot get the good at the legal price may pay more to illegal sellers. Black markets transfer some of the surplus to sellers who are willing to break the law, and often involve poor quality, fraud and violence because contracts cannot be legally enforced.
