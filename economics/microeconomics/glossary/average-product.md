# Average product

Total output divided by the quantity of an input, usually labour: output per worker. Average product rises when marginal product is above it and falls when marginal product is below it, so marginal product crosses average product at its maximum. Average product of labour is the micro counterpart of the macroeconomic measure of labour productivity.
