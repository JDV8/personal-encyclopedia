# Inelastic

Describes demand or supply that responds weakly to price: the absolute value of the elasticity is less than one, so quantity changes by a smaller percentage than price. Demand tends to be inelastic for necessities with few substitutes, such as insulin or petrol in the short run. When demand is inelastic, raising price increases total revenue.
