# Experimental economics

The use of controlled experiments, in the laboratory or field, with real monetary incentives, to test economic theories. Vernon Smith showed that double-auction markets converge quickly to competitive equilibrium; other experiments revealed social preferences, loss aversion and bargaining behaviour. Smith shared the 2002 Nobel prize with Kahneman.
