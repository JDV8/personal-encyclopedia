# Equal marginal principle

The rule that a consumer maximises utility by allocating spending so that the marginal utility per dollar is the same for every good purchased. If a dollar spent on one good yields more extra utility than a dollar spent on another, shifting spending raises total utility. The same logic applies to allocating any scarce resource across uses. Gossen stated it as his second law in 1854.
