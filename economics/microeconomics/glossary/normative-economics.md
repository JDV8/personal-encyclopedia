# Normative economics

Economic statements about what ought to be, involving value judgements, such as "the minimum wage should be raised" or "inequality is too high". Normative claims cannot be settled by evidence alone, though evidence about consequences informs them. They are contrasted with positive economics.

In this course the term is introduced in [Models and economic method](../01-foundations/11-models-and-economic-method.md), part of the section [Foundations: scarcity, choice and trade](../01-foundations/README.md).
