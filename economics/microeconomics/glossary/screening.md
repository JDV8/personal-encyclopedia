# Screening

An action taken by an uninformed party to sort informed parties by their hidden characteristics, typically by offering a menu of options that different types will choose differently. Insurers offer plans with different deductibles; airlines offer cheap tickets with restrictions business travellers will not accept. Rothschild and Stiglitz (1976) analysed screening in insurance markets.

In this course the term is introduced in [Signalling and screening](../07-market-failure/14-signalling-and-screening.md), part of the section [Market failure](../07-market-failure/README.md).
