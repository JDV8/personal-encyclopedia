# Product differentiation

Making a product distinct from competitors' products in quality, design, features, location, branding or service. Differentiation gives a firm some market power, a downward-sloping demand curve, even with many competitors. It is the defining feature of monopolistic competition.

In this course the term is introduced in [Monopolistic competition](../05-market-structures/08-monopolistic-competition.md), part of the section [Market structures and game theory](../05-market-structures/README.md).
