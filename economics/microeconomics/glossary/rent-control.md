# Rent control

A legal limit on the rent landlords may charge, a form of price ceiling. In the short run it benefits sitting tenants, but economists generally find that over time it reduces the supply and quality of rental housing, discourages mobility and creates shortages. Modern "rent stabilisation" with exemptions for new buildings tries to limit these effects.
