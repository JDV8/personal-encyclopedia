# Income elasticity of demand

The percentage change in quantity demanded divided by the percentage change in income. Normal goods have positive income elasticity, inferior goods negative. Goods with income elasticity above one are luxuries, whose budget share rises with income; necessities, such as food, have elasticities between zero and one.
