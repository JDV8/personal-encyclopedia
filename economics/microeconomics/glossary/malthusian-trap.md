# Malthusian trap

The situation described by Thomas Malthus (1798) in which any improvement in productivity leads to population growth that eventually returns living standards to subsistence. It describes much of pre-industrial history reasonably well, but industrialising economies escaped it from the 19th century through sustained productivity growth and falling birth rates.
