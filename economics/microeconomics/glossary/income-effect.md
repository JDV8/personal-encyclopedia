# Income effect

The part of the change in quantity demanded caused by a price change that results from the change in the consumer's purchasing power. A fall in price makes the consumer effectively richer; for a normal good this raises demand, for an inferior good it lowers it. Together with the substitution effect, it makes up the total effect of a price change.
