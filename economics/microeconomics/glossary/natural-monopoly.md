# Natural monopoly

An industry in which a single firm can supply the whole market at lower cost than two or more firms, because of large fixed costs and economies of scale over the relevant range of output. Water pipes, electricity grids and railways are classic examples. Natural monopolies are often regulated or publicly owned, raising the question of how to price when marginal cost is below average cost.
