# Nudge

In Thaler and Sunstein's definition, any aspect of the choice architecture that alters people's behaviour in a predictable way without forbidding any options or significantly changing their economic incentives. Examples include default enrolment in pensions, reminders and social comparisons. Nudges are usually cheap but their effects are often modest.
