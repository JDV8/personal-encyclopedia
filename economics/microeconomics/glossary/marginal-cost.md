# Marginal cost

The additional cost of producing one more unit of output. In the short run, marginal cost typically falls at first and then rises because of diminishing marginal returns. It cuts average variable and average total cost at their minimums. A competitive firm's supply curve is its marginal cost curve above minimum average variable cost.
