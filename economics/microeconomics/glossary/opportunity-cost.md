# Opportunity cost

The value of the best alternative given up when making a choice. The opportunity cost of attending university includes forgone earnings as well as tuition. Because resources are scarce, every choice has an opportunity cost. It is the economic concept of cost and the basis of comparative advantage.

In this course the term is introduced in [Scarcity and the economic problem](../01-foundations/01-scarcity.md), part of the section [Foundations: scarcity, choice and trade](../01-foundations/README.md).
