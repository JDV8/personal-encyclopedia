# Equity

In welfare economics, fairness in the distribution of economic well-being. Unlike efficiency, which concerns the size of the pie, equity concerns how it is divided. There are many conceptions: utilitarian, Rawlsian, libertarian, equality of opportunity and capabilities. (In finance, equity has a different meaning: ownership of a company.)
