# Stackelberg competition

A model of oligopoly, from Heinrich von Stackelberg (1934), in which a leader firm chooses its output first and a follower firm responds. Because the leader anticipates the follower's reaction, it produces more than in Cournot competition and earns higher profit, a first-mover advantage. Total output is higher than in the Cournot model.

In this course the term is introduced in [Cournot, Bertrand and Stackelberg](../05-market-structures/13-cournot-bertrand-and-stackelberg.md), part of the section [Market structures and game theory](../05-market-structures/README.md).
