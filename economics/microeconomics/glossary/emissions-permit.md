# Emissions permit

A legal right to emit a specified amount of a pollutant, such as one tonne of carbon dioxide or sulphur dioxide, within a cap-and-trade system. Permits can be given away or auctioned, and are tradable, so their market price reveals the marginal cost of abatement. The total number of permits determines the total amount of pollution allowed.

In this course the term is introduced in [Cap and trade and regulation](../07-market-failure/07-cap-and-trade-and-regulation.md), part of the section [Market failure](../07-market-failure/README.md).
