# Median voter theorem

The result, from Duncan Black (1948) and Anthony Downs (1957), that if voters' preferences are single-peaked along one dimension, majority voting selects the option preferred by the median voter. It predicts that two competing parties will converge toward the centre. It fails when there are several issues or when turnout varies.
