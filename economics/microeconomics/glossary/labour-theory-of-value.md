# Labour theory of value

The theory that the value of a good is determined by the amount of labour required to produce it. Developed in different forms by Smith, Ricardo and Marx, it was central to classical and Marxian economics. Marginalists replaced it with the view that value depends on marginal utility and scarcity, since it struggles to explain prices of rare goods and the role of capital and time.
