# Anchoring

The tendency to rely too heavily on an initial number when making an estimate, adjusting away from it insufficiently. In a famous Tversky–Kahneman experiment, a rigged wheel of fortune stopping at 10 or 65 shifted people's estimates of the share of African countries in the UN. Anchors matter in negotiations, list prices, salary offers, legal damages and real-estate appraisals, even when the anchor is obviously irrelevant.
