# Luxury good

A good with an income elasticity of demand greater than one, so that spending on it rises more than proportionally with income and its share of the budget grows. Examples often include foreign travel, fine dining and jewellery. The term is technical: whether something is a luxury depends on observed behaviour, not on its being expensive.
