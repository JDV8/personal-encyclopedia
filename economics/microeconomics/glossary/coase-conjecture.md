# Coase conjecture

Ronald Coase's 1972 argument that a monopolist selling a durable good may be unable to exercise its monopoly power. Buyers know that once the monopolist has sold to high-value customers it will want to cut prices to sell to the rest, so they wait, forcing the price down toward marginal cost. Monopolists respond by leasing rather than selling, committing not to cut prices, or making products obsolete.
