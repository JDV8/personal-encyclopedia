# Predatory pricing

Setting prices below cost to drive rivals out of a market or deter entry, with the aim of raising prices later. It is illegal under competition law in many countries but hard to prove, because low prices also benefit consumers and recouping losses later requires lasting barriers to entry. Chicago school economists argued it is rarely rational.
