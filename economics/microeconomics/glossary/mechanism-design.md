# Mechanism design

The study of how to design rules, such as auctions, voting systems, contracts and matching procedures, to achieve desired outcomes when participants have private information and act in their own interest. Sometimes called "reverse game theory". Hurwicz, Maskin and Myerson shared the 2007 Nobel prize for its foundations; it underlies spectrum auctions and kidney exchanges.

In this course the term is introduced in [Twentieth-century microeconomics](../10-history-of-thought/04-twentieth-century-microeconomics.md), part of the section [History of economic thought](../10-history-of-thought/README.md).
