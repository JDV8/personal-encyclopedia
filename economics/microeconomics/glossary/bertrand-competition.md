# Bertrand competition

A model of oligopoly, named after Joseph Bertrand (1883), in which firms choose prices rather than quantities. With identical products and constant marginal cost, the Nash equilibrium is for each firm to price at marginal cost, so even two firms produce the competitive outcome, the Bertrand paradox. Product differentiation, capacity limits or repeated interaction allow prices above cost.
