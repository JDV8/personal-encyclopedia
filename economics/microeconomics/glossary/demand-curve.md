# Demand curve

A graph of the quantity of a good buyers would purchase at each price, holding other factors constant. It usually slopes downward, reflecting the law of demand. By Alfred Marshall's convention, price is on the vertical axis. The height of the demand curve at any quantity measures the marginal buyer's willingness to pay.
