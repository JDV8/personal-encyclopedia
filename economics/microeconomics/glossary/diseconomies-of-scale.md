# Diseconomies of scale

Rising long-run average cost as a firm grows beyond some size. Causes include coordination and communication problems, bureaucracy, weakened incentives and monitoring difficulties in large organisations. Diseconomies of scale limit the size of firms and explain the upward-sloping part of a U-shaped long-run average cost curve.
