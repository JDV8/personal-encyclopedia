# Relative price

The price of one good in terms of another, the ratio of their money prices. It measures the opportunity cost of one good in terms of the other and is the slope of the budget line. Consumer and producer decisions depend on relative prices, not absolute ones.

In this course the term is introduced in [The budget constraint](../03-consumer-theory/05-budget-constraint.md), part of the section [Consumer theory](../03-consumer-theory/README.md). Related terms from the same lesson: [Real income](real-income.md).
