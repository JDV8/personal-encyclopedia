# Disposition effect

The tendency of investors to sell assets that have risen in value too soon and hold on to assets that have fallen too long. Documented by Shefrin and Statman (1985) and Odean (1998), it is consistent with prospect theory: people are risk-averse over gains and risk-seeking over losses, and dislike realising a loss. It can reduce returns and increase taxes.
