# Productive efficiency

Producing output at the lowest possible cost, or equivalently, being on the production possibilities frontier so that more of one good cannot be produced without less of another. In long-run competitive equilibrium, firms produce at minimum average total cost. It differs from allocative efficiency, which concerns producing the right goods.
