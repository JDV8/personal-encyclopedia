# Finance

This course teaches how securities and financial markets work, starting from first principles. It does not open with stock tips. It begins with what money is, how interest compounds and why a dollar next year is worth less than a dollar today, then adds risk, then companies, and only then the instruments themselves (stocks, bonds, funds, derivatives), how they trade, how they are regulated, how portfolios are built, why people err, and how it all went wrong in famous crises. It ends with a practical section for a Canadian investor.

Each lesson has a prerequisites line, a short list of key ideas, worked examples and a link onward, and almost every lesson has a Wikimedia Commons image. Terms are defined in the [glossary](glossary/README.md), which has 583 entries.

> **Education, not advice.** This is a study resource, not personalised financial advice. Rules, rates and limits (especially the Canadian ones) change; confirm current figures with official sources before acting.

**Prerequisites:** none. Comfort with percentages helps; every formula is built from an example.

## Suggested reading order

- **Full course:** read the sections in numerical order. Each builds on the previous one: time and risk (01), firms (02), instruments (03), markets (04), rules (05), portfolios (06), psychology (07), history (08) and practice (09).
- **Fast path to investing in Canada:** 01 lessons 03 to 05 and 10 to 11, [what is a security](02-firms-and-capital/07-what-is-a-security.md), the [fund lessons](03-securities/03-funds/README.md), then [section 09](09-personal-investing-canada/README.md) and its glossary terms as needed.
- **Why markets break:** [behavioural finance](07-behavioural-finance/README.md) and [history](08-history/README.md) can be read after section 01 as stories, then reread after the technical sections.

## References & further reading

- Zvi Bodie, Alex Kane and Alan J. Marcus, *Investments* (Intermediate). The standard textbook across sections 03 to 06.
- Richard A. Brealey, Stewart C. Myers and Franklin Allen, *Principles of Corporate Finance* (Intermediate). Present value, capital structure and valuation.
- Burton G. Malkiel, *A Random Walk Down Wall Street* (Beginner). A readable survey of markets and investing.
- Benjamin Graham, *The Intelligent Investor* (Beginner to Intermediate). The classic on value investing and temperament.
- Charles P. Kindleberger and Robert Z. Aliber, *Manias, Panics, and Crashes* (Intermediate). The history of financial crises.
- Niall Ferguson, *The Ascent of Money* (Beginner). A narrative history of finance.
- John C. Bogle, *The Little Book of Common Sense Investing* (Beginner). The case for low-cost indexing.
- Canada Revenue Agency, canada.ca/en/revenue-agency (Beginner). Official TFSA, RRSP and FHSA rules.
- Ontario Securities Commission, GetSmarterAboutMoney.ca (Beginner). Canadian investor education.
