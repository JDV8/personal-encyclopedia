# Currency crisis

A sudden collapse in a currency's value, often when a peg breaks, as in Asia in 1997. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [1987, LTCM and the 1990s crises](../../08-history/09-1987-ltcm-and-the-1990s-crises.md). That lesson also covers [LTCM](ltcm.md), [Black Monday](black-monday.md), [Portfolio insurance](portfolio-insurance.md) and [Relative-value arbitrage](relative-value-arbitrage.md).
