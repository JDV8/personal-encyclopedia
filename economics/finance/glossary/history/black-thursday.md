# Black Thursday

24 October 1929, the first day of the panic that led to the 1929 crash. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The crash of 1929 and the Great Depression](../../08-history/07-the-crash-of-1929-and-the-great-depression.md). That lesson also covers [Great Depression](great-depression.md), [Debt deflation](debt-deflation.md), [Glass–Steagall Act](glass-steagall.md) and [Smoot–Hawley Tariff](smoot-hawley.md).
