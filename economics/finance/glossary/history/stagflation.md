# Stagflation

Slow growth together with high inflation, as in the 1970s. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bretton Woods and the postwar order](../../08-history/08-bretton-woods-and-the-postwar-order.md). That lesson also covers [Gold standard](gold-standard.md), [Bretton Woods](bretton-woods.md), [Nixon shock](nixon-shock.md) and [Triffin dilemma](triffin-dilemma.md).
