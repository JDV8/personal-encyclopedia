# FDIC

The US Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, created in 1933 to insure bank deposits. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The crash of 1929 and the Great Depression](../../08-history/07-the-crash-of-1929-and-the-great-depression.md). That lesson also covers [Deflation](deflation.md), [Black Thursday](black-thursday.md), [Great Depression](great-depression.md) and [Debt deflation](debt-deflation.md).
