# Tulip mania

The speculative frenzy in tulip bulb contracts in the Netherlands, peaking in early 1637. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Tulip mania](../../08-history/03-tulip-mania.md). That lesson also covers [Fundamental value](fundamental-value.md) and [Windhandel](windhandel.md).
