# Bill of exchange

A written order to pay a sum on a set date, used in medieval and early modern trade finance. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Lending and banking before stock markets](../../08-history/01-lending-and-banking-before-stock-markets.md). That lesson also covers [Maritime loan](maritime-loan.md), [Usury](usury.md) and [Double-entry bookkeeping](double-entry-bookkeeping.md).
