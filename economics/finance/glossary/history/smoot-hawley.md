# Smoot–Hawley Tariff

The 1930 US tariff law that raised duties on many imports and invited retaliation. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The crash of 1929 and the Great Depression](../../08-history/07-the-crash-of-1929-and-the-great-depression.md). That lesson also covers [FDIC](fdic.md), [Deflation](deflation.md), [Black Thursday](black-thursday.md) and [Great Depression](great-depression.md).
