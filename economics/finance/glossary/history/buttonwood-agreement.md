# Buttonwood Agreement

The 1792 agreement among 24 brokers in New York that is regarded as the start of the NYSE. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The Buttonwood Agreement and the NYSE](../../08-history/05-the-buttonwood-agreement-and-the-nyse.md). That lesson also covers [Robber barons](robber-barons.md) and [NYSE](nyse.md).
