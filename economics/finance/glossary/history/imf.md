# IMF

The International Monetary Fund, created at Bretton Woods to support exchange-rate stability and lend to countries in crisis. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bretton Woods and the postwar order](../../08-history/08-bretton-woods-and-the-postwar-order.md). That lesson also covers [World Bank](world-bank.md), [Reserve currency](reserve-currency.md), [Impossible trinity](impossible-trinity.md) and [Peg](peg.md).
