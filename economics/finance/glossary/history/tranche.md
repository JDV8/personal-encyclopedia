# Tranche

A slice of a securitised pool with a defined priority for receiving payments and absorbing losses. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The 2008 financial crisis](../../08-history/11-the-2008-financial-crisis.md). That lesson also covers [Shadow banking](shadow-banking.md), [Subprime](subprime.md), [Too big to fail](too-big-to-fail.md) and [TARP](tarp.md).
