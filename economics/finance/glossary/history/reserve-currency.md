# Reserve currency

A currency held by central banks and governments, such as the US dollar. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bretton Woods and the postwar order](../../08-history/08-bretton-woods-and-the-postwar-order.md). That lesson also covers [Impossible trinity](impossible-trinity.md), [Peg](peg.md), [Stagflation](stagflation.md) and [Gold standard](gold-standard.md).
