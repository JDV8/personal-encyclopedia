# COVID crash

The fall of about 34% in the S&P 500 between 19 February and 23 March 2020, followed by a fast recovery. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The COVID crash and the rate shock](../../08-history/12-covid-crash-and-the-rate-shock.md). That lesson also covers [Inflation shock](inflation-shock.md).
