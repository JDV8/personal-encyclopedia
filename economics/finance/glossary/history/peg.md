# Peg

A fixed exchange rate against another currency or gold. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bretton Woods and the postwar order](../../08-history/08-bretton-woods-and-the-postwar-order.md). That lesson also covers [Stagflation](stagflation.md), [Gold standard](gold-standard.md), [Bretton Woods](bretton-woods.md) and [Nixon shock](nixon-shock.md).
