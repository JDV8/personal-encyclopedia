# Windhandel

"Wind trade", the Dutch term for trading in things not owned, a feature of tulip mania. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Tulip mania](../../08-history/03-tulip-mania.md). That lesson also covers [Tulip mania](tulip-mania.md) and [Fundamental value](fundamental-value.md).
