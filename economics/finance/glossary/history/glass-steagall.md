# Glass–Steagall Act

The 1933 US law separating commercial and investment banking; its core provisions were repealed in 1999. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The crash of 1929 and the Great Depression](../../08-history/07-the-crash-of-1929-and-the-great-depression.md). That lesson also covers [Smoot–Hawley Tariff](smoot-hawley.md), [FDIC](fdic.md), [Deflation](deflation.md) and [Black Thursday](black-thursday.md).
