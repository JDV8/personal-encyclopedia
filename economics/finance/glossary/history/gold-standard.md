# Gold standard

A monetary system fixing the currency's value to a quantity of gold. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bretton Woods and the postwar order](../../08-history/08-bretton-woods-and-the-postwar-order.md). That lesson also covers [Bretton Woods](bretton-woods.md), [Nixon shock](nixon-shock.md), [Triffin dilemma](triffin-dilemma.md) and [IMF](imf.md).
