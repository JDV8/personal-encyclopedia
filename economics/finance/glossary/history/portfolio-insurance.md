# Portfolio insurance

A dynamic strategy that sells stocks as they fall to mimic a put; widely used before the 1987 crash. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [1987, LTCM and the 1990s crises](../../08-history/09-1987-ltcm-and-the-1990s-crises.md). That lesson also covers [Relative-value arbitrage](relative-value-arbitrage.md), [Contagion](contagion.md), [Flight to quality](flight-to-quality.md) and [Currency crisis](currency-crisis.md).
