# Triffin dilemma

The tension that a reserve-currency country must run deficits to supply the world with currency, undermining confidence in it. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bretton Woods and the postwar order](../../08-history/08-bretton-woods-and-the-postwar-order.md). That lesson also covers [IMF](imf.md), [World Bank](world-bank.md), [Reserve currency](reserve-currency.md) and [Impossible trinity](impossible-trinity.md).
