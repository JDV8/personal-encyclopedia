# Charter

A grant of rights from a ruler or government to form a company. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The Dutch East India Company and the first stock exchange](../../08-history/02-the-dutch-east-india-company-and-the-first-stock-exchange.md). That lesson also covers [Bear raid](bear-raid.md), [Permanent capital](permanent-capital.md), [Beurs](beurs.md) and [Joint-stock company](joint-stock-company.md).
