# World Bank

An institution created at Bretton Woods to finance reconstruction and development. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bretton Woods and the postwar order](../../08-history/08-bretton-woods-and-the-postwar-order.md). That lesson also covers [Reserve currency](reserve-currency.md), [Impossible trinity](impossible-trinity.md), [Peg](peg.md) and [Stagflation](stagflation.md).
