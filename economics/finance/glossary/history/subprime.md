# Subprime

Lending to borrowers with weak credit histories at higher rates; subprime mortgages were central to 2008. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The 2008 financial crisis](../../08-history/11-the-2008-financial-crisis.md). That lesson also covers [Too big to fail](too-big-to-fail.md), [TARP](tarp.md), [Mortgage-backed security (MBS)](mbs.md) and [Tranche](tranche.md).
