# TARP

The US Troubled Asset Relief Program, authorised in October 2008 to stabilise banks. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The 2008 financial crisis](../../08-history/11-the-2008-financial-crisis.md). That lesson also covers [Mortgage-backed security (MBS)](mbs.md), [Tranche](tranche.md), [Shadow banking](shadow-banking.md) and [Subprime](subprime.md).
