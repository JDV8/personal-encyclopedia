# Great Depression

The severe worldwide economic downturn of the 1930s. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The crash of 1929 and the Great Depression](../../08-history/07-the-crash-of-1929-and-the-great-depression.md). That lesson also covers [Debt deflation](debt-deflation.md), [Glass–Steagall Act](glass-steagall.md), [Smoot–Hawley Tariff](smoot-hawley.md) and [FDIC](fdic.md).
