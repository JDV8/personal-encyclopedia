# Impossible trinity

The principle that a country cannot have a fixed exchange rate, free capital movement and independent monetary policy together. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bretton Woods and the postwar order](../../08-history/08-bretton-woods-and-the-postwar-order.md). That lesson also covers [Peg](peg.md), [Stagflation](stagflation.md), [Gold standard](gold-standard.md) and [Bretton Woods](bretton-woods.md).
