# South Sea Bubble

The 1720 rise and collapse of the South Sea Company's shares in Britain. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The Mississippi and South Sea bubbles](../../08-history/04-the-mississippi-and-south-sea-bubbles.md). That lesson also covers [Bubble Act](bubble-act.md) and [John Law](john-law.md).
