# Flight to quality

Investors shifting money from risky assets to safe ones such as government bonds during stress. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [1987, LTCM and the 1990s crises](../../08-history/09-1987-ltcm-and-the-1990s-crises.md). That lesson also covers [Currency crisis](currency-crisis.md), [LTCM](ltcm.md), [Black Monday](black-monday.md) and [Portfolio insurance](portfolio-insurance.md).
