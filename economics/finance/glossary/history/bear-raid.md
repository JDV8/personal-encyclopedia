# Bear raid

Selling a stock short and spreading negative rumours to push its price down. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The Dutch East India Company and the first stock exchange](../../08-history/02-the-dutch-east-india-company-and-the-first-stock-exchange.md). That lesson also covers [Permanent capital](permanent-capital.md), [Beurs](beurs.md), [Joint-stock company](joint-stock-company.md) and [VOC](voc.md).
