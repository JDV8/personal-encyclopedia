# Beurs

The Dutch word for exchange; the Amsterdam Beurs opened in 1611. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The Dutch East India Company and the first stock exchange](../../08-history/02-the-dutch-east-india-company-and-the-first-stock-exchange.md). That lesson also covers [Joint-stock company](joint-stock-company.md), [VOC](voc.md), [Charter](charter.md) and [Bear raid](bear-raid.md).
