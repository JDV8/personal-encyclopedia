# LTCM

Long-Term Capital Management, a hedge fund that nearly collapsed in 1998 and was rescued by a bank-organised consortium. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [1987, LTCM and the 1990s crises](../../08-history/09-1987-ltcm-and-the-1990s-crises.md). That lesson also covers [Black Monday](black-monday.md), [Portfolio insurance](portfolio-insurance.md), [Relative-value arbitrage](relative-value-arbitrage.md) and [Contagion](contagion.md).
