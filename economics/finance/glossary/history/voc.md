# VOC

The Dutch East India Company, founded in 1602, which issued tradable shares in Amsterdam. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The Dutch East India Company and the first stock exchange](../../08-history/02-the-dutch-east-india-company-and-the-first-stock-exchange.md). That lesson also covers [Charter](charter.md), [Bear raid](bear-raid.md), [Permanent capital](permanent-capital.md) and [Beurs](beurs.md).
