# NYSE

The New York Stock Exchange, tracing its origin to the 1792 Buttonwood Agreement. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The Buttonwood Agreement and the NYSE](../../08-history/05-the-buttonwood-agreement-and-the-nyse.md). That lesson also covers [Buttonwood Agreement](buttonwood-agreement.md) and [Robber barons](robber-barons.md).
