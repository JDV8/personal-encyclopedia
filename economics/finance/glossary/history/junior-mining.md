# Junior mining

Small exploration companies that search for mineral deposits, a speculative part of Canadian markets. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Canadian markets history](../../08-history/06-canadian-markets-history.md). That lesson also covers [Hudson's Bay Company](hudsons-bay-company.md), [Bank Act](bank-act.md), [Victory Bonds](victory-bonds.md) and [TMX Group](tmx-group.md).
