# Permanent capital

Money that stays in a company because shareholders cannot demand repayment, only sell shares to others. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The Dutch East India Company and the first stock exchange](../../08-history/02-the-dutch-east-india-company-and-the-first-stock-exchange.md). That lesson also covers [Beurs](beurs.md), [Joint-stock company](joint-stock-company.md), [VOC](voc.md) and [Charter](charter.md).
