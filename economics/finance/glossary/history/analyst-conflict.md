# Analyst conflict of interest

The pressure on research analysts, whose employers earn banking fees, to be optimistic; a theme of the dot-com boom. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The dot-com bubble](../../08-history/10-the-dot-com-bubble.md). That lesson also covers [Nasdaq](nasdaq.md), [Dot-com bubble](dot-com-bubble.md) and [New economy](new-economy.md).
