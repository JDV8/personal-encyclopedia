# Usury

Lending at interest; historically charging excessive or any interest, which many religions condemned. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Lending and banking before stock markets](../../08-history/01-lending-and-banking-before-stock-markets.md). That lesson also covers [Double-entry bookkeeping](double-entry-bookkeeping.md), [Bill of exchange](bill-of-exchange.md) and [Maritime loan](maritime-loan.md).
