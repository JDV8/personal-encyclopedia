# John Law

The Scottish financier who ran the Mississippi Company and a French bank in 1716 to 1720. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The Mississippi and South Sea bubbles](../../08-history/04-the-mississippi-and-south-sea-bubbles.md). That lesson also covers [South Sea Bubble](south-sea-bubble.md) and [Bubble Act](bubble-act.md).
