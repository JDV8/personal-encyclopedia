# Relative-value arbitrage

A trade betting that the price gap between two similar assets will narrow, as run by LTCM. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [1987, LTCM and the 1990s crises](../../08-history/09-1987-ltcm-and-the-1990s-crises.md). That lesson also covers [Contagion](contagion.md), [Flight to quality](flight-to-quality.md), [Currency crisis](currency-crisis.md) and [LTCM](ltcm.md).
