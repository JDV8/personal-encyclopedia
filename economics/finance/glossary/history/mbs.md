# Mortgage-backed security (MBS)

A security whose cash flows come from a pool of mortgages. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The 2008 financial crisis](../../08-history/11-the-2008-financial-crisis.md). That lesson also covers [Tranche](tranche.md), [Shadow banking](shadow-banking.md), [Subprime](subprime.md) and [Too big to fail](too-big-to-fail.md).
