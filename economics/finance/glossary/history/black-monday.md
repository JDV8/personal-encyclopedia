# Black Monday

19 October 1987, when the Dow fell about 22% in one day. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [1987, LTCM and the 1990s crises](../../08-history/09-1987-ltcm-and-the-1990s-crises.md). That lesson also covers [Portfolio insurance](portfolio-insurance.md), [Relative-value arbitrage](relative-value-arbitrage.md), [Contagion](contagion.md) and [Flight to quality](flight-to-quality.md).
