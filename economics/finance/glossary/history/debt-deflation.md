# Debt deflation

Irving Fisher's theory that falling prices raise the real burden of debt, deepening a downturn. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The crash of 1929 and the Great Depression](../../08-history/07-the-crash-of-1929-and-the-great-depression.md). That lesson also covers [Glass–Steagall Act](glass-steagall.md), [Smoot–Hawley Tariff](smoot-hawley.md), [FDIC](fdic.md) and [Deflation](deflation.md).
