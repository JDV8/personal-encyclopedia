# Double-entry bookkeeping

Recording every transaction as equal debits and credits; formalised in 15th-century Italy and basis of modern accounts. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Lending and banking before stock markets](../../08-history/01-lending-and-banking-before-stock-markets.md). That lesson also covers [Bill of exchange](bill-of-exchange.md), [Maritime loan](maritime-loan.md) and [Usury](usury.md).
