# Maritime loan

An ancient loan repayable only if the ship's voyage succeeded, combining credit and insurance. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Lending and banking before stock markets](../../08-history/01-lending-and-banking-before-stock-markets.md). That lesson also covers [Usury](usury.md), [Double-entry bookkeeping](double-entry-bookkeeping.md) and [Bill of exchange](bill-of-exchange.md).
