# Nixon shock

The 1971 US suspension of dollar convertibility into gold that ended Bretton Woods. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bretton Woods and the postwar order](../../08-history/08-bretton-woods-and-the-postwar-order.md). That lesson also covers [Triffin dilemma](triffin-dilemma.md), [IMF](imf.md), [World Bank](world-bank.md) and [Reserve currency](reserve-currency.md).
