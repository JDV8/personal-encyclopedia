# Too big to fail

The idea that some firms are so large and connected that governments will rescue them. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The 2008 financial crisis](../../08-history/11-the-2008-financial-crisis.md). That lesson also covers [TARP](tarp.md), [Mortgage-backed security (MBS)](mbs.md), [Tranche](tranche.md) and [Shadow banking](shadow-banking.md).
