# Joint-stock company

A business whose capital is divided into transferable shares owned by many investors, pioneered by chartered trading companies of the 1600s. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The Dutch East India Company and the first stock exchange](../../08-history/02-the-dutch-east-india-company-and-the-first-stock-exchange.md). That lesson also covers [VOC](voc.md), [Charter](charter.md), [Bear raid](bear-raid.md) and [Permanent capital](permanent-capital.md).
