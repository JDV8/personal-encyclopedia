# Bretton Woods

The 1944 conference and the system of fixed exchange rates against the dollar that followed. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bretton Woods and the postwar order](../../08-history/08-bretton-woods-and-the-postwar-order.md). That lesson also covers [Nixon shock](nixon-shock.md), [Triffin dilemma](triffin-dilemma.md), [IMF](imf.md) and [World Bank](world-bank.md).
