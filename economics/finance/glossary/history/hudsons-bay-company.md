# Hudson's Bay Company

Chartered in 1670 for the fur trade; a very early joint-stock-type company in what is now Canada. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Canadian markets history](../../08-history/06-canadian-markets-history.md). That lesson also covers [Bank Act](bank-act.md), [Victory Bonds](victory-bonds.md), [TMX Group](tmx-group.md) and [Junior mining](junior-mining.md).
