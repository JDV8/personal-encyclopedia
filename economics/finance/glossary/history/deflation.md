# Deflation

A sustained fall in the general price level. It raises the real burden of debt and can deepen recessions; see [debt deflation](debt-deflation.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The crash of 1929 and the Great Depression](../../08-history/07-the-crash-of-1929-and-the-great-depression.md). That lesson also covers [Black Thursday](black-thursday.md), [Great Depression](great-depression.md), [Debt deflation](debt-deflation.md) and [Glass–Steagall Act](glass-steagall.md).
