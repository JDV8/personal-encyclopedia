# Shadow banking

Credit intermediation outside the regulated banking system, such as money market funds and securitisation vehicles. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The 2008 financial crisis](../../08-history/11-the-2008-financial-crisis.md). That lesson also covers [Subprime](subprime.md), [Too big to fail](too-big-to-fail.md), [TARP](tarp.md) and [Mortgage-backed security (MBS)](mbs.md).
