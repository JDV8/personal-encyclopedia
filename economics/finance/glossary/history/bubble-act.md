# Bubble Act

A 1720 British law restricting joint-stock companies without a royal charter. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The Mississippi and South Sea bubbles](../../08-history/04-the-mississippi-and-south-sea-bubbles.md). That lesson also covers [John Law](john-law.md) and [South Sea Bubble](south-sea-bubble.md).
