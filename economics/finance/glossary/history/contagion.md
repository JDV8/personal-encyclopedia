# Contagion

The spread of financial distress from one institution, market or country to others. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [1987, LTCM and the 1990s crises](../../08-history/09-1987-ltcm-and-the-1990s-crises.md). That lesson also covers [Flight to quality](flight-to-quality.md), [Currency crisis](currency-crisis.md), [LTCM](ltcm.md) and [Black Monday](black-monday.md).
