# Target-date fund

A fund that gradually shifts from stocks toward bonds as a chosen target year, often retirement, approaches. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Pooled investing and mutual funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/01-pooled-investing-and-mutual-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Segregated fund](segregated-fund.md), [Custodian](custodian.md), [Mutual fund](mutual-fund.md) and [Net asset value (NAV)](net-asset-value.md).
