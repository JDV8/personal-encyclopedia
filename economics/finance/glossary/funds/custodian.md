# Custodian

An institution that holds a fund's or investor's assets separately from the manager or broker for safekeeping. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Pooled investing and mutual funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/01-pooled-investing-and-mutual-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Mutual fund](mutual-fund.md), [Net asset value (NAV)](net-asset-value.md), [Open-end fund](open-end-fund.md) and [Target-date fund](target-date-fund.md).
