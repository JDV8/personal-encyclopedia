# Private equity

Funds that buy companies, often with borrowed money, improve them and sell them, typically after four to seven years. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Hedge funds, private equity and closed-end funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/05-hedge-funds-private-equity-and-closed-end-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Venture capital](venture-capital.md), [Carried interest](carried-interest.md), [Closed-end fund](closed-end-fund.md) and [Hedge fund](hedge-fund.md).
