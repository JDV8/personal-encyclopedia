# Open-end fund

A fund that issues and redeems units on demand at NAV, so its size changes with investor flows. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Pooled investing and mutual funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/01-pooled-investing-and-mutual-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Target-date fund](target-date-fund.md), [Segregated fund](segregated-fund.md), [Custodian](custodian.md) and [Mutual fund](mutual-fund.md).
