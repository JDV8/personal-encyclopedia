# Mutual fund

A pooled investment vehicle that sells units to investors and invests the money in a diversified portfolio run by a manager. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Pooled investing and mutual funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/01-pooled-investing-and-mutual-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Net asset value (NAV)](net-asset-value.md), [Open-end fund](open-end-fund.md), [Target-date fund](target-date-fund.md) and [Segregated fund](segregated-fund.md).
