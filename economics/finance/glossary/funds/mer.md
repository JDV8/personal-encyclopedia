# Management expense ratio (MER)

The annual cost of running a fund, including management fees and taxes, as a percentage of assets; deducted automatically from returns. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Fees and the cost of investing](../../03-securities/03-funds/04-fees-and-the-cost-of-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Trading expense ratio (TER)](trading-expense-ratio.md), [Trailing commission](trailing-commission.md), [Load](load.md) and [Fund Facts](fund-facts.md).
