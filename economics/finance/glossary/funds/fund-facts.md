# Fund Facts

A two-page Canadian document summarising a mutual fund's costs, past performance and risk; ETFs have an equivalent ETF Facts. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Fees and the cost of investing](../../03-securities/03-funds/04-fees-and-the-cost-of-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Management expense ratio (MER)](mer.md), [Trading expense ratio (TER)](trading-expense-ratio.md), [Trailing commission](trailing-commission.md) and [Load](load.md).
