# Leveraged ETF

An ETF aiming for a multiple of an index's daily return; compounding makes long-run results diverge from the multiple. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Exchange-traded funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/02-exchange-traded-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Synthetic ETF](synthetic-etf.md), [Exchange-traded fund (ETF)](etf.md) and [Authorized participant](authorized-participant.md).
