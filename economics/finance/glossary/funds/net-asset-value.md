# Net asset value (NAV)

A fund's assets minus liabilities divided by units outstanding; the per-unit value at which open-end funds trade. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Pooled investing and mutual funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/01-pooled-investing-and-mutual-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Open-end fund](open-end-fund.md), [Target-date fund](target-date-fund.md), [Segregated fund](segregated-fund.md) and [Custodian](custodian.md).
