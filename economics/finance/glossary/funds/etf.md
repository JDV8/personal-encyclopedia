# Exchange-traded fund (ETF)

A pooled fund whose units trade on an exchange throughout the day at a market price close to NAV. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Exchange-traded funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/02-exchange-traded-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Authorized participant](authorized-participant.md), [Leveraged ETF](leveraged-etf.md) and [Synthetic ETF](synthetic-etf.md).
