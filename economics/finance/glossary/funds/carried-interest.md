# Carried interest

The share of profits, typically 20% above a hurdle, paid to the managers of a private equity or hedge fund. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Hedge funds, private equity and closed-end funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/05-hedge-funds-private-equity-and-closed-end-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Closed-end fund](closed-end-fund.md), [Hedge fund](hedge-fund.md), [Private equity](private-equity.md) and [Venture capital](venture-capital.md).
