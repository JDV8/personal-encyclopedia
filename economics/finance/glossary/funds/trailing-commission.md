# Trailing commission

An ongoing payment from a fund company to an adviser or dealer, embedded in the fund's MER. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Fees and the cost of investing](../../03-securities/03-funds/04-fees-and-the-cost-of-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Load](load.md), [Fund Facts](fund-facts.md), [Management expense ratio (MER)](mer.md) and [Trading expense ratio (TER)](trading-expense-ratio.md).
