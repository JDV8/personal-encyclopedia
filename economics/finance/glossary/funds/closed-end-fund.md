# Closed-end fund

A fund with a fixed number of shares that trade on an exchange, often at a discount or premium to NAV. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Hedge funds, private equity and closed-end funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/05-hedge-funds-private-equity-and-closed-end-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Hedge fund](hedge-fund.md), [Private equity](private-equity.md), [Venture capital](venture-capital.md) and [Carried interest](carried-interest.md).
