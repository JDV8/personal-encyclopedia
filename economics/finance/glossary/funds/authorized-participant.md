# Authorized participant

A large institution allowed to create or redeem ETF shares in exchange for baskets of securities, keeping the ETF price near NAV. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Exchange-traded funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/02-exchange-traded-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Leveraged ETF](leveraged-etf.md), [Synthetic ETF](synthetic-etf.md) and [Exchange-traded fund (ETF)](etf.md).
