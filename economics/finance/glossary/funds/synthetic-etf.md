# Synthetic ETF

An ETF that replicates an index with derivatives such as swaps rather than holding the securities, adding counterparty risk. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Exchange-traded funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/02-exchange-traded-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Exchange-traded fund (ETF)](etf.md), [Authorized participant](authorized-participant.md) and [Leveraged ETF](leveraged-etf.md).
