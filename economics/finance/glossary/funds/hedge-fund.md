# Hedge fund

A privately offered partnership that may use short selling, leverage and derivatives, typically charging "two and twenty". In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Hedge funds, private equity and closed-end funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/05-hedge-funds-private-equity-and-closed-end-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Private equity](private-equity.md), [Venture capital](venture-capital.md), [Carried interest](carried-interest.md) and [Closed-end fund](closed-end-fund.md).
