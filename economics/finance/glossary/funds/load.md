# Load

A sales charge on a mutual fund, paid at purchase (front-end) or at sale (deferred). Deferred sales charges are banned in Canada since June 2022. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Fees and the cost of investing](../../03-securities/03-funds/04-fees-and-the-cost-of-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Fund Facts](fund-facts.md), [Management expense ratio (MER)](mer.md), [Trading expense ratio (TER)](trading-expense-ratio.md) and [Trailing commission](trailing-commission.md).
