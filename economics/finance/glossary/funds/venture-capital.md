# Venture capital

Investment in young, high-growth start-ups, accepting that many will fail and a few will succeed greatly. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Hedge funds, private equity and closed-end funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/05-hedge-funds-private-equity-and-closed-end-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Carried interest](carried-interest.md), [Closed-end fund](closed-end-fund.md), [Hedge fund](hedge-fund.md) and [Private equity](private-equity.md).
