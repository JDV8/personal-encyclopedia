# Index fund

A fund that holds the securities of a market index in index weights at minimal cost, aiming to match the market return. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Index funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/03-index-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Tracking error](tracking-error.md).
