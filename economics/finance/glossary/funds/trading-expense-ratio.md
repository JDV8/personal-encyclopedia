# Trading expense ratio (TER)

A fund's trading costs, reported separately from the MER in Canada; MER plus TER is the better measure of total cost. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Fees and the cost of investing](../../03-securities/03-funds/04-fees-and-the-cost-of-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Trailing commission](trailing-commission.md), [Load](load.md), [Fund Facts](fund-facts.md) and [Management expense ratio (MER)](mer.md).
