# Segregated fund

An insurance-company investment fund with guarantees on part of the principal, typically with higher fees than a mutual fund. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Pooled investing and mutual funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/01-pooled-investing-and-mutual-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Custodian](custodian.md), [Mutual fund](mutual-fund.md), [Net asset value (NAV)](net-asset-value.md) and [Open-end fund](open-end-fund.md).
