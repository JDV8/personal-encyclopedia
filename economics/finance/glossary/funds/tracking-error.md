# Tracking error

The volatility of the difference between a fund's return and its benchmark's return; also the shortfall of an index fund versus its index. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Index funds](../../03-securities/03-funds/03-index-funds.md). That lesson also covers [Index fund](index-fund.md).
