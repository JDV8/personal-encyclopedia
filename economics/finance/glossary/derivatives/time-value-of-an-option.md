# Time value of an option

The part of an option's price above intrinsic value, reflecting the chance of further gains before expiry; it decays toward expiry. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Options: calls and puts](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/03-options-calls-and-puts.md). That lesson also covers [Moneyness](moneyness.md), [Put-call parity](put-call-parity.md), [Call option](call-option.md) and [Put option](put-option.md).
