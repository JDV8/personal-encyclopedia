# Speculation

Taking risk in the hope of profiting from price changes, with no offsetting exposure. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What is a derivative?](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/01-what-is-a-derivative.md). That lesson also covers [Arbitrage](arbitrage.md), [Counterparty risk](counterparty-risk.md), [Derivative](derivative.md) and [Underlying](underlying.md).
