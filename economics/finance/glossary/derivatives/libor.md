# LIBOR

The London Interbank Offered Rate, a former benchmark floating rate phased out by mid-2023 in favour of rates such as SOFR and CORRA. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Swaps and credit derivatives](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/06-swaps-and-credit-derivatives.md). That lesson also covers [Notional amount](notional-amount.md), [Swap](swap.md), [Interest-rate swap](interest-rate-swap.md) and [Credit default swap (CDS)](credit-default-swap.md).
