# Implied volatility

The volatility that, put into an option pricing model, reproduces the market price of the option. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Option pricing](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/05-option-pricing.md). That lesson also covers [Risk-neutral valuation](risk-neutral-valuation.md), [Replication](replication.md), [Volatility smile](volatility-smile.md) and [Real option](real-option.md).
