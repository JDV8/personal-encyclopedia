# Option

A contract giving the right, not the obligation, to buy (call) or sell (put) an asset at a set strike price on or before expiry, for a premium. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Options: calls and puts](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/03-options-calls-and-puts.md). That lesson also covers [Strike price](strike-price.md), [Premium](premium.md), [Intrinsic value](intrinsic-value.md) and [Time value of an option](time-value-of-an-option.md).
