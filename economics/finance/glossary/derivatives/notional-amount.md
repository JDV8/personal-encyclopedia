# Notional amount

The reference size of a derivative contract used to compute payments; it is not usually exchanged. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Swaps and credit derivatives](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/06-swaps-and-credit-derivatives.md). That lesson also covers [Swap](swap.md), [Interest-rate swap](interest-rate-swap.md), [Credit default swap (CDS)](credit-default-swap.md) and [ISDA](isda.md).
