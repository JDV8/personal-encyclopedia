# Call option

The right to buy the underlying at the strike price; a bullish contract. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Options: calls and puts](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/03-options-calls-and-puts.md). That lesson also covers [Put option](put-option.md), [Option](option.md), [Strike price](strike-price.md) and [Premium](premium.md).
