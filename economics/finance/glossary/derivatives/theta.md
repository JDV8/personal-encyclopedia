# Theta

The decline in an option's value as time passes. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Option payoffs and strategies](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/04-option-payoffs-and-strategies.md). That lesson also covers [Vega](vega.md), [Protective put](protective-put.md), [Covered call](covered-call.md) and [Straddle](straddle.md).
