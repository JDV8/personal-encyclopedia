# Forward contract

A private agreement to buy or sell an asset at a fixed price on a future date. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Forwards and futures](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/02-forwards-and-futures.md). That lesson also covers [Futures contract](futures-contract.md), [Margin](margin.md), [Mark-to-market](mark-to-market.md) and [Cost of carry](cost-of-carry.md).
