# Hedging

Taking a position that offsets an existing risk, such as a farmer selling futures to lock in a crop price. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What is a derivative?](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/01-what-is-a-derivative.md). That lesson also covers [Speculation](speculation.md), [Arbitrage](arbitrage.md), [Counterparty risk](counterparty-risk.md) and [Derivative](derivative.md).
