# Arbitrage

Exploiting a price difference between identical or related assets to earn a riskless profit; its pressure keeps prices consistent. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What is a derivative?](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/01-what-is-a-derivative.md). That lesson also covers [Counterparty risk](counterparty-risk.md), [Derivative](derivative.md), [Underlying](underlying.md) and [Hedging](hedging.md).
