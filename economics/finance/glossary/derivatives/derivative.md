# Derivative

A contract whose value derives from an underlying asset, rate or index; includes forwards, futures, options and swaps. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What is a derivative?](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/01-what-is-a-derivative.md). That lesson also covers [Underlying](underlying.md), [Hedging](hedging.md), [Speculation](speculation.md) and [Arbitrage](arbitrage.md).
