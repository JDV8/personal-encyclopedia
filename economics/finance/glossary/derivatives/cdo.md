# Collateralised debt obligation (CDO)

A security backed by a pool of debt, split into tranches of differing seniority. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Swaps and credit derivatives](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/06-swaps-and-credit-derivatives.md). That lesson also covers [LIBOR](libor.md), [Notional amount](notional-amount.md), [Swap](swap.md) and [Interest-rate swap](interest-rate-swap.md).
