# Margin

A good-faith deposit required to hold a futures position or a loan against securities; see [margin call](../markets/margin-call.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Forwards and futures](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/02-forwards-and-futures.md). That lesson also covers [Mark-to-market](mark-to-market.md), [Cost of carry](cost-of-carry.md), [Contango](contango.md) and [Backwardation](backwardation.md).
