# Moneyness

Whether an option is in, at or out of the money relative to the underlying's price. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Options: calls and puts](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/03-options-calls-and-puts.md). That lesson also covers [Put-call parity](put-call-parity.md), [Call option](call-option.md), [Put option](put-option.md) and [Option](option.md).
