# Swap

An agreement to exchange streams of cash payments, such as fixed for floating interest. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Swaps and credit derivatives](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/06-swaps-and-credit-derivatives.md). That lesson also covers [Interest-rate swap](interest-rate-swap.md), [Credit default swap (CDS)](credit-default-swap.md), [ISDA](isda.md) and [Collateralised debt obligation (CDO)](cdo.md).
