# Basis risk

The risk that a hedge does not move exactly with the exposure it offsets. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Forwards and futures](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/02-forwards-and-futures.md). That lesson also covers [Clearing house](clearing-house.md), [Forward contract](forward-contract.md), [Futures contract](futures-contract.md) and [Margin](margin.md).
