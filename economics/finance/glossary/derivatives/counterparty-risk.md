# Counterparty risk

The risk that the other party to a contract fails to perform; clearing houses reduce it for exchange-traded derivatives. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What is a derivative?](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/01-what-is-a-derivative.md). That lesson also covers [Derivative](derivative.md), [Underlying](underlying.md), [Hedging](hedging.md) and [Speculation](speculation.md).
