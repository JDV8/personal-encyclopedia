# The Greeks

Sensitivities of an option's price: delta (to the stock price), gamma (to delta), theta (to time), vega (to volatility) and rho (to rates). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Option payoffs and strategies](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/04-option-payoffs-and-strategies.md). That lesson also covers [Delta](delta.md), [Gamma](gamma.md), [Theta](theta.md) and [Vega](vega.md).
