# Protective put

Owning a stock and buying a put on it, creating a floor under the position like insurance. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Option payoffs and strategies](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/04-option-payoffs-and-strategies.md). That lesson also covers [Covered call](covered-call.md), [Straddle](straddle.md), [Option spread](spread-strategy.md) and [The Greeks](greeks.md).
