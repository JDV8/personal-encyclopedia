# Gamma

The rate at which delta changes as the underlying moves. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Option payoffs and strategies](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/04-option-payoffs-and-strategies.md). That lesson also covers [Theta](theta.md), [Vega](vega.md), [Protective put](protective-put.md) and [Covered call](covered-call.md).
