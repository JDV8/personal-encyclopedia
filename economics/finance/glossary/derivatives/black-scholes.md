# Black–Scholes model

The 1973 formula for the price of a European option, built on continuous replication; extended by Merton. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Option pricing](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/05-option-pricing.md). That lesson also covers [Implied volatility](implied-volatility.md), [Risk-neutral valuation](risk-neutral-valuation.md), [Replication](replication.md) and [Volatility smile](volatility-smile.md).
