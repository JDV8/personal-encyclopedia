# Risk-neutral valuation

Pricing a derivative as its expected payoff under risk-neutral probabilities, discounted at the risk-free rate. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Option pricing](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/05-option-pricing.md). That lesson also covers [Replication](replication.md), [Volatility smile](volatility-smile.md), [Real option](real-option.md) and [Black–Scholes model](black-scholes.md).
