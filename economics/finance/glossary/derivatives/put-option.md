# Put option

The right to sell the underlying at the strike price; a bearish or protective contract. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Options: calls and puts](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/03-options-calls-and-puts.md). That lesson also covers [Option](option.md), [Strike price](strike-price.md), [Premium](premium.md) and [Intrinsic value](intrinsic-value.md).
