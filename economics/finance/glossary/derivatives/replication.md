# Replication

Building a derivative's payoff from the underlying and borrowing; its cost fixes the derivative's price. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Option pricing](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/05-option-pricing.md). That lesson also covers [Volatility smile](volatility-smile.md), [Real option](real-option.md), [Black–Scholes model](black-scholes.md) and [Implied volatility](implied-volatility.md).
