# Real option

The flexibility to expand, delay or abandon a real investment, valued with option methods. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Option pricing](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/05-option-pricing.md). That lesson also covers [Black–Scholes model](black-scholes.md), [Implied volatility](implied-volatility.md), [Risk-neutral valuation](risk-neutral-valuation.md) and [Replication](replication.md).
