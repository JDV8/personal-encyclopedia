# Clearing house

An institution that stands between buyer and seller of an exchange-traded contract and guarantees performance. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Forwards and futures](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/02-forwards-and-futures.md). That lesson also covers [Forward contract](forward-contract.md), [Futures contract](futures-contract.md), [Margin](margin.md) and [Mark-to-market](mark-to-market.md).
