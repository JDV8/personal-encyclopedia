# Credit default swap (CDS)

A contract in which a buyer pays a regular spread for protection against a borrower's default. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Swaps and credit derivatives](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/06-swaps-and-credit-derivatives.md). That lesson also covers [ISDA](isda.md), [Collateralised debt obligation (CDO)](cdo.md), [LIBOR](libor.md) and [Notional amount](notional-amount.md).
