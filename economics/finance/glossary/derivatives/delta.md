# Delta

The change in an option's price for a one-dollar move in the underlying, between 0 and 1 for calls. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Option payoffs and strategies](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/04-option-payoffs-and-strategies.md). That lesson also covers [Gamma](gamma.md), [Theta](theta.md), [Vega](vega.md) and [Protective put](protective-put.md).
