# Volatility smile

The pattern of implied volatility differing across strike prices, contradicting the constant-volatility assumption. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Option pricing](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/05-option-pricing.md). That lesson also covers [Real option](real-option.md), [Black–Scholes model](black-scholes.md), [Implied volatility](implied-volatility.md) and [Risk-neutral valuation](risk-neutral-valuation.md).
