# Straddle

Buying a call and a put with the same strike and expiry, profiting from a large move in either direction. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Option payoffs and strategies](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/04-option-payoffs-and-strategies.md). That lesson also covers [Option spread](spread-strategy.md), [The Greeks](greeks.md), [Delta](delta.md) and [Gamma](gamma.md).
