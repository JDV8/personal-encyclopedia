# Backwardation

A market in which futures prices are below the spot price; rolling long positions can earn a gain. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Forwards and futures](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/02-forwards-and-futures.md). That lesson also covers [Basis risk](basis-risk.md), [Clearing house](clearing-house.md), [Forward contract](forward-contract.md) and [Futures contract](futures-contract.md).
