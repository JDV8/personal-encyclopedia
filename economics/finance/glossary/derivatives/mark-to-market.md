# Mark-to-market

Revaluing a position at current market prices, as futures accounts do every day. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Forwards and futures](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/02-forwards-and-futures.md). That lesson also covers [Cost of carry](cost-of-carry.md), [Contango](contango.md), [Backwardation](backwardation.md) and [Basis risk](basis-risk.md).
