# Contango

A market in which futures prices exceed the spot price and rise with maturity; rolling long positions costs money. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Forwards and futures](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/02-forwards-and-futures.md). That lesson also covers [Backwardation](backwardation.md), [Basis risk](basis-risk.md), [Clearing house](clearing-house.md) and [Forward contract](forward-contract.md).
