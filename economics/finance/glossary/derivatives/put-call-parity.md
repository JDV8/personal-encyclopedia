# Put-call parity

The no-arbitrage relation C − P = S − K·e^(−rT) linking European call and put prices. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Options: calls and puts](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/03-options-calls-and-puts.md). That lesson also covers [Call option](call-option.md), [Put option](put-option.md), [Option](option.md) and [Strike price](strike-price.md).
