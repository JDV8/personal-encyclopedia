# Futures contract

A standardised, exchange-traded forward contract with daily settlement of gains and losses. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Forwards and futures](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/02-forwards-and-futures.md). That lesson also covers [Margin](margin.md), [Mark-to-market](mark-to-market.md), [Cost of carry](cost-of-carry.md) and [Contango](contango.md).
