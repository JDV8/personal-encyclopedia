# Interest-rate swap

A swap in which one party pays a fixed rate and the other a floating rate on a notional amount. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Swaps and credit derivatives](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/06-swaps-and-credit-derivatives.md). That lesson also covers [Credit default swap (CDS)](credit-default-swap.md), [ISDA](isda.md), [Collateralised debt obligation (CDO)](cdo.md) and [LIBOR](libor.md).
