# Covered call

Owning a stock and selling a call on it, earning premium but capping the upside. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Option payoffs and strategies](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/04-option-payoffs-and-strategies.md). That lesson also covers [Straddle](straddle.md), [Option spread](spread-strategy.md), [The Greeks](greeks.md) and [Delta](delta.md).
