# Option spread

Combining options of the same type with different strikes or expiries, such as a bull call spread, to limit cost and profit. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Option payoffs and strategies](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/04-option-payoffs-and-strategies.md). That lesson also covers [The Greeks](greeks.md), [Delta](delta.md), [Gamma](gamma.md) and [Theta](theta.md).
