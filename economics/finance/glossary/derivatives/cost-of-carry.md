# Cost of carry

The net cost of holding an asset until a future date: interest and storage costs minus income earned; it determines the forward price. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Forwards and futures](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/02-forwards-and-futures.md). That lesson also covers [Contango](contango.md), [Backwardation](backwardation.md), [Basis risk](basis-risk.md) and [Clearing house](clearing-house.md).
