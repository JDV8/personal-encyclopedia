# ISDA

The International Swaps and Derivatives Association, whose master agreement governs most over-the-counter derivatives. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Swaps and credit derivatives](../../03-securities/04-derivatives/06-swaps-and-credit-derivatives.md). That lesson also covers [Collateralised debt obligation (CDO)](cdo.md), [LIBOR](libor.md), [Notional amount](notional-amount.md) and [Swap](swap.md).
