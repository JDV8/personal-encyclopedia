# Modigliani–Miller theorem

The 1958 result that, without taxes, bankruptcy costs and information problems, a firm's value does not depend on how it mixes debt and equity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Capital structure and cost of capital](../../02-firms-and-capital/08-capital-structure-and-cost-of-capital.md). That lesson also covers [Tax shield](tax-shield.md), [Leverage](leverage.md), [Capital stack](capital-stack.md) and [Debt-for-equity swap](debt-for-equity-swap.md).
