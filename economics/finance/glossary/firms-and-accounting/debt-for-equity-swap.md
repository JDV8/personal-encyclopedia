# Debt-for-equity swap

A restructuring in which lenders exchange debt for shares. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Capital structure and cost of capital](../../02-firms-and-capital/08-capital-structure-and-cost-of-capital.md). That lesson also covers [Weighted average cost of capital (WACC)](wacc.md), [Cost of equity](cost-of-equity.md), [Cost of debt](cost-of-debt.md) and [Modigliani–Miller theorem](modigliani-miller.md).
