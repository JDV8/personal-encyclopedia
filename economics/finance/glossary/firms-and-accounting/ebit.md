# EBIT

Earnings before interest and taxes: operating profit that ignores how the firm is financed. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The income statement](../../02-firms-and-capital/05-the-income-statement.md). That lesson also covers [Net income](net-income.md), [Depreciation](depreciation.md), [Accrual accounting](accrual-accounting.md) and [Income statement](income-statement.md).
