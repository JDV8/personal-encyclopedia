# Current ratio

Current assets divided by current liabilities; a basic measure of short-term liquidity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The balance sheet](../../02-firms-and-capital/04-the-balance-sheet.md). That lesson also covers [Working capital](working-capital.md), [Asset](asset.md), [Liability](liability.md) and [Net worth](net-worth.md).
