# Residual claim

The right of shareholders to whatever is left after all other claims (employees, suppliers, lenders, taxes) have been paid. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Equity and debt](../../02-firms-and-capital/03-equity-and-debt.md). That lesson also covers [Equity](equity.md), [Debt](debt.md), [Default](default.md) and [Seniority](seniority.md).
