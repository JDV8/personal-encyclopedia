# Seniority

The order in which creditors are repaid if a borrower fails: secured and senior debt first, then subordinated debt, preferred shares and finally common equity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Equity and debt](../../02-firms-and-capital/03-equity-and-debt.md). That lesson also covers [Collateral](collateral.md), [Convertible bond](convertible-bond.md), [Residual claim](residual-claim.md) and [Equity](equity.md).
