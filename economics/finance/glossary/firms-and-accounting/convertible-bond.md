# Convertible bond

A bond that its holder can exchange for a fixed number of the issuer's shares; combines a bond with a call option. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Equity and debt](../../02-firms-and-capital/03-equity-and-debt.md). That lesson also covers [Residual claim](residual-claim.md), [Equity](equity.md), [Debt](debt.md) and [Default](default.md).
