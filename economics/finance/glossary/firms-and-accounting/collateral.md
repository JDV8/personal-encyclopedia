# Collateral

Assets pledged to secure a loan; if the borrower defaults, the lender can seize them. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Equity and debt](../../02-firms-and-capital/03-equity-and-debt.md). That lesson also covers [Convertible bond](convertible-bond.md), [Residual claim](residual-claim.md), [Equity](equity.md) and [Debt](debt.md).
