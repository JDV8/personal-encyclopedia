# Cost of debt

The effective interest rate a company pays on its borrowing, before or after the tax deduction on interest. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Capital structure and cost of capital](../../02-firms-and-capital/08-capital-structure-and-cost-of-capital.md). That lesson also covers [Modigliani–Miller theorem](modigliani-miller.md), [Tax shield](tax-shield.md), [Leverage](leverage.md) and [Capital stack](capital-stack.md).
