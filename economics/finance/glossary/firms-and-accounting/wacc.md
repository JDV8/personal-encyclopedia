# Weighted average cost of capital (WACC)

A company's blended cost of financing: the cost of equity and the after-tax cost of debt weighted by their shares of total capital. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Capital structure and cost of capital](../../02-firms-and-capital/08-capital-structure-and-cost-of-capital.md). That lesson also covers [Cost of equity](cost-of-equity.md), [Cost of debt](cost-of-debt.md), [Modigliani–Miller theorem](modigliani-miller.md) and [Tax shield](tax-shield.md).
