# Liquidity

How quickly and cheaply an asset can be turned into cash without moving its price. Cash is perfectly liquid; a building is not. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What a security is](../../02-firms-and-capital/07-what-is-a-security.md). That lesson also covers [Security](security.md) and [Securitization](securitization.md).
