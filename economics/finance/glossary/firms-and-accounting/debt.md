# Debt

Money borrowed that must be repaid with interest under a contract. Lenders have a priority claim over shareholders. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Equity and debt](../../02-firms-and-capital/03-equity-and-debt.md). That lesson also covers [Default](default.md), [Seniority](seniority.md), [Collateral](collateral.md) and [Convertible bond](convertible-bond.md).
