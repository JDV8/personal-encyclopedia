# Income statement

A report of revenues, expenses and profit over a period, showing how revenue becomes net income. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The income statement](../../02-firms-and-capital/05-the-income-statement.md). That lesson also covers [Revenue](revenue.md), [EBIT](ebit.md), [Net income](net-income.md) and [Depreciation](depreciation.md).
