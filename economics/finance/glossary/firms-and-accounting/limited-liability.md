# Limited liability

A legal protection under which shareholders can lose no more than they invested; creditors cannot pursue their personal assets. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Limited liability and the corporation](../../02-firms-and-capital/02-limited-liability-and-the-corporation.md). That lesson also covers [Board of directors](board-of-directors.md) and [Corporation](corporation.md).
