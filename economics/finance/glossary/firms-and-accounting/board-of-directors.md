# Board of directors

The group elected by shareholders to oversee management, approve major decisions and protect shareholders' interests. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Limited liability and the corporation](../../02-firms-and-capital/02-limited-liability-and-the-corporation.md). That lesson also covers [Corporation](corporation.md) and [Limited liability](limited-liability.md).
