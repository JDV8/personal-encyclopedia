# Book value

The accounting value of equity: assets minus liabilities as recorded in the books, which may differ greatly from market value. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The balance sheet](../../02-firms-and-capital/04-the-balance-sheet.md). That lesson also covers [Current ratio](current-ratio.md), [Working capital](working-capital.md), [Asset](asset.md) and [Liability](liability.md).
