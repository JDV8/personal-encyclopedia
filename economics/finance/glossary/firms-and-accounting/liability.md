# Liability

An obligation to pay or deliver value to someone else, such as a loan, bond or account payable. Liabilities appear on the [balance sheet](balance-sheet.md) alongside equity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The balance sheet](../../02-firms-and-capital/04-the-balance-sheet.md). That lesson also covers [Net worth](net-worth.md), [Balance sheet](balance-sheet.md), [Retained earnings](retained-earnings.md) and [Book value](book-value.md).
