# Securitization

Pooling many loans, such as mortgages, and selling claims on the pooled cash flows as tradable securities. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What a security is](../../02-firms-and-capital/07-what-is-a-security.md). That lesson also covers [Liquidity](liquidity.md) and [Security](security.md).
