# Corporation

A company that is a separate legal person from its owners, with [limited liability](limited-liability.md), perpetual existence and transferable shares. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Limited liability and the corporation](../../02-firms-and-capital/02-limited-liability-and-the-corporation.md). That lesson also covers [Limited liability](limited-liability.md) and [Board of directors](board-of-directors.md).
