# Free cash flow

Cash from operations minus capital expenditure: cash available to pay lenders and shareholders. The key input to [DCF valuation](../stocks/dcf.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The cash flow statement](../../02-firms-and-capital/06-the-cash-flow-statement.md). That lesson also covers [Capital expenditure (capex)](capital-expenditure.md) and [Cash flow](cash-flow.md).
