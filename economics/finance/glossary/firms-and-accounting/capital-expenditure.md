# Capital expenditure (capex)

Spending on long-lived assets such as equipment and buildings, as opposed to day-to-day operating expenses. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The cash flow statement](../../02-firms-and-capital/06-the-cash-flow-statement.md). That lesson also covers [Cash flow](cash-flow.md) and [Free cash flow](free-cash-flow.md).
