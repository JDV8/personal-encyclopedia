# Equity

The owners' residual claim on a company's assets after liabilities; also the general term for shares. Assets − liabilities = equity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Equity and debt](../../02-firms-and-capital/03-equity-and-debt.md). That lesson also covers [Debt](debt.md), [Default](default.md), [Seniority](seniority.md) and [Collateral](collateral.md).
