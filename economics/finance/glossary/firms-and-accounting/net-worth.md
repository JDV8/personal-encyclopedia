# Net worth

Assets minus liabilities. For a household it is a snapshot of wealth; for a company it equals shareholders' [equity](equity.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The balance sheet](../../02-firms-and-capital/04-the-balance-sheet.md). That lesson also covers [Balance sheet](balance-sheet.md), [Retained earnings](retained-earnings.md), [Book value](book-value.md) and [Current ratio](current-ratio.md).
