# Cash flow

The actual movement of cash into and out of a business over a period, which can differ from accounting profit. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The cash flow statement](../../02-firms-and-capital/06-the-cash-flow-statement.md). That lesson also covers [Free cash flow](free-cash-flow.md) and [Capital expenditure (capex)](capital-expenditure.md).
