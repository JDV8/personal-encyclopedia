# Retained earnings

Cumulative profits a company has kept and reinvested rather than paid out as dividends. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The balance sheet](../../02-firms-and-capital/04-the-balance-sheet.md). That lesson also covers [Book value](book-value.md), [Current ratio](current-ratio.md), [Working capital](working-capital.md) and [Asset](asset.md).
