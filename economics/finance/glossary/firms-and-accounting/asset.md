# Asset

Anything of value that an individual or firm owns or is owed, such as cash, shares, property or a loan receivable. Assets appear on the left of the [balance sheet](balance-sheet.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The balance sheet](../../02-firms-and-capital/04-the-balance-sheet.md). That lesson also covers [Liability](liability.md), [Net worth](net-worth.md), [Balance sheet](balance-sheet.md) and [Retained earnings](retained-earnings.md).
