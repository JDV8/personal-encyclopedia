# Net income

Profit after all expenses, interest and taxes; the "bottom line" from which [earnings per share](../stocks/earnings-per-share.md) is derived. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The income statement](../../02-firms-and-capital/05-the-income-statement.md). That lesson also covers [Depreciation](depreciation.md), [Accrual accounting](accrual-accounting.md), [Income statement](income-statement.md) and [Revenue](revenue.md).
