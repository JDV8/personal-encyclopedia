# Accrual accounting

Recording revenue when earned and expenses when incurred, regardless of when cash changes hands. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The income statement](../../02-firms-and-capital/05-the-income-statement.md). That lesson also covers [Income statement](income-statement.md), [Revenue](revenue.md), [EBIT](ebit.md) and [Net income](net-income.md).
