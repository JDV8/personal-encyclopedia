# Depreciation

The accounting allocation of an asset's cost over its useful life, reducing reported profit without any cash leaving the firm that year. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The income statement](../../02-firms-and-capital/05-the-income-statement.md). That lesson also covers [Accrual accounting](accrual-accounting.md), [Income statement](income-statement.md), [Revenue](revenue.md) and [EBIT](ebit.md).
