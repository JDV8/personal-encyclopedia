# Balance sheet

A statement of what a firm owns (assets), owes (liabilities) and the owners' residual (equity) at one date. Assets = liabilities + equity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The balance sheet](../../02-firms-and-capital/04-the-balance-sheet.md). That lesson also covers [Retained earnings](retained-earnings.md), [Book value](book-value.md), [Current ratio](current-ratio.md) and [Working capital](working-capital.md).
