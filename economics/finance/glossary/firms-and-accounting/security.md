# Security

A tradable financial instrument representing ownership (a share), a debt claim (a bond) or rights to ownership (an option). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What a security is](../../02-firms-and-capital/07-what-is-a-security.md). That lesson also covers [Securitization](securitization.md) and [Liquidity](liquidity.md).
