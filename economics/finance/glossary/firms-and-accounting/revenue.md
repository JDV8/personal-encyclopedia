# Revenue

The money a company earns from selling goods or services before any expenses are deducted; the "top line". In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The income statement](../../02-firms-and-capital/05-the-income-statement.md). That lesson also covers [EBIT](ebit.md), [Net income](net-income.md), [Depreciation](depreciation.md) and [Accrual accounting](accrual-accounting.md).
