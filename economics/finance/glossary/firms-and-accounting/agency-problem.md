# Agency problem

The conflict that arises when managers (agents) act in their own interest rather than the interest of shareholders (principals). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What a company is](../../02-firms-and-capital/01-what-is-a-company.md). That lesson also covers [Company](company.md).
