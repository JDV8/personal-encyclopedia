# Working capital

Current assets minus current liabilities: the short-term funding needed to run daily operations. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The balance sheet](../../02-firms-and-capital/04-the-balance-sheet.md). That lesson also covers [Asset](asset.md), [Liability](liability.md), [Net worth](net-worth.md) and [Balance sheet](balance-sheet.md).
