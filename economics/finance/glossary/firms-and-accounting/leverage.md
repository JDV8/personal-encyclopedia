# Leverage

The use of borrowed money (or derivatives) to increase exposure; it magnifies both gains and losses. In accounting, the ratio of debt to equity or assets. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Capital structure and cost of capital](../../02-firms-and-capital/08-capital-structure-and-cost-of-capital.md). That lesson also covers [Capital stack](capital-stack.md), [Debt-for-equity swap](debt-for-equity-swap.md), [Weighted average cost of capital (WACC)](wacc.md) and [Cost of equity](cost-of-equity.md).
