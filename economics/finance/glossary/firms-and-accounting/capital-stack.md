# Capital stack

The layers of a company's financing ordered by claim priority, from secured debt through junior debt and preferred shares to common equity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Capital structure and cost of capital](../../02-firms-and-capital/08-capital-structure-and-cost-of-capital.md). That lesson also covers [Debt-for-equity swap](debt-for-equity-swap.md), [Weighted average cost of capital (WACC)](wacc.md), [Cost of equity](cost-of-equity.md) and [Cost of debt](cost-of-debt.md).
