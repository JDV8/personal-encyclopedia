# Tax shield

The tax savings from deducting interest payments, which makes debt financing cheaper than equity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Capital structure and cost of capital](../../02-firms-and-capital/08-capital-structure-and-cost-of-capital.md). That lesson also covers [Leverage](leverage.md), [Capital stack](capital-stack.md), [Debt-for-equity swap](debt-for-equity-swap.md) and [Weighted average cost of capital (WACC)](wacc.md).
