# Company

A legal organisation formed to carry on business, which can own assets, owe debts and enter contracts in its own name. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What a company is](../../02-firms-and-capital/01-what-is-a-company.md). That lesson also covers [Agency problem](agency-problem.md).
