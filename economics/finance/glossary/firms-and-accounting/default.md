# Default

Failure to make a required payment of interest or principal on a debt when due. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Equity and debt](../../02-firms-and-capital/03-equity-and-debt.md). That lesson also covers [Seniority](seniority.md), [Collateral](collateral.md), [Convertible bond](convertible-bond.md) and [Residual claim](residual-claim.md).
