# Cost of equity

The return shareholders require for bearing the risk of owning the company; often estimated with the [CAPM](../portfolio-theory/capm.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Capital structure and cost of capital](../../02-firms-and-capital/08-capital-structure-and-cost-of-capital.md). That lesson also covers [Cost of debt](cost-of-debt.md), [Modigliani–Miller theorem](modigliani-miller.md), [Tax shield](tax-shield.md) and [Leverage](leverage.md).
