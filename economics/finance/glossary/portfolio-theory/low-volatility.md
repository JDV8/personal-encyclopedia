# Low volatility

A factor of stocks with below-average price swings that have not underperformed as theory predicts. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Factor investing](../../06-portfolio-theory/07-factor-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Value investing](value-investing.md), [Smart beta](smart-beta.md), [Factor](factor.md) and [Fama–French model](fama-french.md).
