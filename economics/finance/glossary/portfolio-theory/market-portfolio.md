# Market portfolio

The portfolio of all risky assets held in proportion to their market value; in the CAPM it is the tangency portfolio. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [CAPM and beta](../../06-portfolio-theory/03-capm-and-beta.md). That lesson also covers [Capital asset pricing model (CAPM)](capm.md), [Beta](beta.md), [Market risk premium](market-risk-premium.md) and [Security market line](security-market-line.md).
