# Core-satellite

A portfolio design pairing a low-cost index core with smaller, higher-conviction satellite holdings. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Asset allocation and rebalancing](../../06-portfolio-theory/06-asset-allocation-and-rebalancing.md). That lesson also covers [Human capital](human-capital.md), [Home bias](home-bias.md), [Concentration risk](concentration-risk.md) and [Asset allocation](asset-allocation.md).
