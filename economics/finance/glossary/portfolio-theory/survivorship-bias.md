# Survivorship bias

Distorting performance data by studying only funds or firms that survived, hiding the failures. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Active versus passive investing](../../06-portfolio-theory/05-active-versus-passive-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Persistence](persistence.md), [Active management](active-management.md), [Passive investing](passive-investing.md) and [SPIVA](spiva.md).
