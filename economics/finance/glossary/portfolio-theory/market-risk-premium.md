# Market risk premium

The extra return investors expect from the market portfolio over the risk-free rate. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [CAPM and beta](../../06-portfolio-theory/03-capm-and-beta.md). That lesson also covers [Security market line](security-market-line.md), [Alpha](alpha.md), [Market portfolio](market-portfolio.md) and [Capital asset pricing model (CAPM)](capm.md).
