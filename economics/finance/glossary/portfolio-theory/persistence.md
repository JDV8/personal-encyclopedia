# Persistence

Whether strong past performance by a fund continues; evidence for it is weak. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Active versus passive investing](../../06-portfolio-theory/05-active-versus-passive-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Active management](active-management.md), [Passive investing](passive-investing.md), [SPIVA](spiva.md) and [Survivorship bias](survivorship-bias.md).
