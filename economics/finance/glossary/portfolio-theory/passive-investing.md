# Passive investing

Holding a broad, low-cost portfolio that tracks the market without trying to beat it. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Active versus passive investing](../../06-portfolio-theory/05-active-versus-passive-investing.md). That lesson also covers [SPIVA](spiva.md), [Survivorship bias](survivorship-bias.md), [Persistence](persistence.md) and [Active management](active-management.md).
