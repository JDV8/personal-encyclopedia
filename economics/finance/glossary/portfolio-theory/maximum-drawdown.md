# Maximum drawdown

The largest peak-to-trough decline in a portfolio's value over a period. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Measuring performance](../../06-portfolio-theory/08-measuring-performance.md). That lesson also covers [Geometric mean](geometric-mean.md), [Time-weighted return](time-weighted-return.md), [Money-weighted return](money-weighted-return.md) and [VIX](vix.md).
