# Glide path

A planned shift from growth assets toward safer assets as an investment goal approaches. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Asset allocation and rebalancing](../../06-portfolio-theory/06-asset-allocation-and-rebalancing.md). That lesson also covers [Risk tolerance](risk-tolerance.md), [Time horizon](time-horizon.md), [Investment policy statement](investment-policy-statement.md) and [Core-satellite](core-satellite.md).
