# Size premium

The historical tendency of small-company stocks to earn higher returns than large ones. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Factor investing](../../06-portfolio-theory/07-factor-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Momentum](momentum.md), [Low volatility](low-volatility.md), [Value investing](value-investing.md) and [Smart beta](smart-beta.md).
