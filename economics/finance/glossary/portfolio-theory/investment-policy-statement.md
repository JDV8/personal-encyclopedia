# Investment policy statement

A written plan stating goals, risk limits, asset allocation and rules for rebalancing. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Asset allocation and rebalancing](../../06-portfolio-theory/06-asset-allocation-and-rebalancing.md). That lesson also covers [Core-satellite](core-satellite.md), [Human capital](human-capital.md), [Home bias](home-bias.md) and [Concentration risk](concentration-risk.md).
