# Geometric mean

The compound average return per period; always less than or equal to the arithmetic mean when returns vary. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Measuring performance](../../06-portfolio-theory/08-measuring-performance.md). That lesson also covers [Time-weighted return](time-weighted-return.md), [Money-weighted return](money-weighted-return.md), [VIX](vix.md) and [Benchmark](benchmark.md).
