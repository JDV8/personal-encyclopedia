# Asset allocation

The division of a portfolio among asset classes such as stocks, bonds and cash, which largely drives risk and return. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Asset allocation and rebalancing](../../06-portfolio-theory/06-asset-allocation-and-rebalancing.md). That lesson also covers [Rebalancing](rebalancing.md), [Glide path](glide-path.md), [Risk tolerance](risk-tolerance.md) and [Time horizon](time-horizon.md).
