# Smart beta

Rules-based funds that tilt toward factors instead of weighting by market capitalisation. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Factor investing](../../06-portfolio-theory/07-factor-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Factor](factor.md), [Fama–French model](fama-french.md), [Size premium](size-premium.md) and [Momentum](momentum.md).
