# VIX

An index of expected stock-market volatility derived from S&P 500 option prices, often called the "fear gauge". In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Measuring performance](../../06-portfolio-theory/08-measuring-performance.md). That lesson also covers [Benchmark](benchmark.md), [Sharpe ratio](sharpe-ratio.md), [Maximum drawdown](maximum-drawdown.md) and [Geometric mean](geometric-mean.md).
