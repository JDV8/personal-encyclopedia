# SPIVA

S&P's scorecard comparing active funds with their benchmarks; over long horizons most active funds lag. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Active versus passive investing](../../06-portfolio-theory/05-active-versus-passive-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Survivorship bias](survivorship-bias.md), [Persistence](persistence.md), [Active management](active-management.md) and [Passive investing](passive-investing.md).
