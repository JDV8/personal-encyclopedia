# Covariance

A measure of how two variables move together; correlation is covariance scaled by both standard deviations. Central to portfolio variance. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Portfolio return and risk](../../06-portfolio-theory/01-portfolio-return-and-risk.md). That lesson also covers [Portfolio](portfolio.md).
