# Capital asset pricing model (CAPM)

A model stating that an asset's expected return equals the risk-free rate plus beta times the market risk premium. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [CAPM and beta](../../06-portfolio-theory/03-capm-and-beta.md). That lesson also covers [Beta](beta.md), [Market risk premium](market-risk-premium.md), [Security market line](security-market-line.md) and [Alpha](alpha.md).
