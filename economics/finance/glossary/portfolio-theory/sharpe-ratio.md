# Sharpe ratio

Excess return over the risk-free rate divided by volatility; return per unit of risk. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Measuring performance](../../06-portfolio-theory/08-measuring-performance.md). That lesson also covers [Maximum drawdown](maximum-drawdown.md), [Geometric mean](geometric-mean.md), [Time-weighted return](time-weighted-return.md) and [Money-weighted return](money-weighted-return.md).
