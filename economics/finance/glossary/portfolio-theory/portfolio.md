# Portfolio

The collection of all the assets an investor holds, judged as a whole rather than one holding at a time. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Portfolio return and risk](../../06-portfolio-theory/01-portfolio-return-and-risk.md). That lesson also covers [Covariance](covariance.md).
