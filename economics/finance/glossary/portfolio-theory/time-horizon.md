# Time horizon

The length of time until invested money will be needed; longer horizons allow more risk. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Asset allocation and rebalancing](../../06-portfolio-theory/06-asset-allocation-and-rebalancing.md). That lesson also covers [Investment policy statement](investment-policy-statement.md), [Core-satellite](core-satellite.md), [Human capital](human-capital.md) and [Home bias](home-bias.md).
