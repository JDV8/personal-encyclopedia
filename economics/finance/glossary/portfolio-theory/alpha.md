# Alpha

Return in excess of what a benchmark or risk model predicts for the risk taken; skill, luck or mismeasurement. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [CAPM and beta](../../06-portfolio-theory/03-capm-and-beta.md). That lesson also covers [Market portfolio](market-portfolio.md), [Capital asset pricing model (CAPM)](capm.md), [Beta](beta.md) and [Market risk premium](market-risk-premium.md).
