# Efficient market hypothesis

Eugene Fama's proposition that prices reflect available information, so beating the market consistently is hard; weak, semi-strong and strong forms differ in the information included. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The efficient market hypothesis](../../06-portfolio-theory/04-the-efficient-market-hypothesis.md). That lesson also covers [Random walk](random-walk.md), [Anomaly](anomaly.md), [Technical analysis](technical-analysis.md) and [Fundamental analysis](fundamental-analysis.md).
