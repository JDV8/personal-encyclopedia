# Factor

A broad, persistent driver of returns across many securities, such as value, size, momentum or low volatility. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Factor investing](../../06-portfolio-theory/07-factor-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Fama–French model](fama-french.md), [Size premium](size-premium.md), [Momentum](momentum.md) and [Low volatility](low-volatility.md).
