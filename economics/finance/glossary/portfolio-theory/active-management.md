# Active management

Trying to beat a benchmark by selecting securities or timing markets, usually at higher cost. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Active versus passive investing](../../06-portfolio-theory/05-active-versus-passive-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Passive investing](passive-investing.md), [SPIVA](spiva.md), [Survivorship bias](survivorship-bias.md) and [Persistence](persistence.md).
