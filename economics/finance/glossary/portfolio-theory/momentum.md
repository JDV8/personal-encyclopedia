# Momentum

The tendency of recent winners to keep outperforming recent losers over months. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Factor investing](../../06-portfolio-theory/07-factor-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Low volatility](low-volatility.md), [Value investing](value-investing.md), [Smart beta](smart-beta.md) and [Factor](factor.md).
