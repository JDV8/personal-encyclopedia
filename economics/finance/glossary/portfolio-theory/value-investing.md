# Value investing

Buying securities priced below estimated intrinsic value, associated with Benjamin Graham and Warren Buffett. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Factor investing](../../06-portfolio-theory/07-factor-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Smart beta](smart-beta.md), [Factor](factor.md), [Fama–French model](fama-french.md) and [Size premium](size-premium.md).
