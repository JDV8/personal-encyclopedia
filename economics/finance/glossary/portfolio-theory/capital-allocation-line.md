# Capital allocation line

The line showing risk-return combinations from mixing the risk-free asset with a risky portfolio; its slope is the Sharpe ratio. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The efficient frontier](../../06-portfolio-theory/02-the-efficient-frontier.md). That lesson also covers [Modern portfolio theory](modern-portfolio-theory.md), [Efficient frontier](efficient-frontier.md), [Minimum-variance portfolio](minimum-variance-portfolio.md) and [Tangency portfolio](tangency-portfolio.md).
