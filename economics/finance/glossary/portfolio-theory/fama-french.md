# Fama–French model

A model adding size and value factors to market beta, later extended with profitability and investment. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Factor investing](../../06-portfolio-theory/07-factor-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Size premium](size-premium.md), [Momentum](momentum.md), [Low volatility](low-volatility.md) and [Value investing](value-investing.md).
