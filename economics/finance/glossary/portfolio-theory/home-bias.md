# Home bias

The tendency to hold more domestic securities than diversification would suggest. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Asset allocation and rebalancing](../../06-portfolio-theory/06-asset-allocation-and-rebalancing.md). That lesson also covers [Concentration risk](concentration-risk.md), [Asset allocation](asset-allocation.md), [Rebalancing](rebalancing.md) and [Glide path](glide-path.md).
