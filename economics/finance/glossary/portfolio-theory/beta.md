# Beta

A measure of how much an asset moves with the market: 1 is market-like, above 1 amplifies, below 1 dampens. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [CAPM and beta](../../06-portfolio-theory/03-capm-and-beta.md). That lesson also covers [Market risk premium](market-risk-premium.md), [Security market line](security-market-line.md), [Alpha](alpha.md) and [Market portfolio](market-portfolio.md).
