# Human capital

The present value of future labour income; for young investors it is a large, bond-like asset. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Asset allocation and rebalancing](../../06-portfolio-theory/06-asset-allocation-and-rebalancing.md). That lesson also covers [Home bias](home-bias.md), [Concentration risk](concentration-risk.md), [Asset allocation](asset-allocation.md) and [Rebalancing](rebalancing.md).
