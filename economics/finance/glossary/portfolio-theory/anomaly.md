# Anomaly

An empirical pattern, such as momentum or the size effect, that appears to contradict a prevailing model. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The efficient market hypothesis](../../06-portfolio-theory/04-the-efficient-market-hypothesis.md). That lesson also covers [Technical analysis](technical-analysis.md), [Fundamental analysis](fundamental-analysis.md), [Efficient market hypothesis](efficient-market-hypothesis.md) and [Random walk](random-walk.md).
