# Rebalancing

Restoring a portfolio's target weights by selling what has grown and buying what has lagged. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Asset allocation and rebalancing](../../06-portfolio-theory/06-asset-allocation-and-rebalancing.md). That lesson also covers [Glide path](glide-path.md), [Risk tolerance](risk-tolerance.md), [Time horizon](time-horizon.md) and [Investment policy statement](investment-policy-statement.md).
