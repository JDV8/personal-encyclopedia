# Random walk

A series whose changes are unpredictable from past changes; the model for price movements in an efficient market. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The efficient market hypothesis](../../06-portfolio-theory/04-the-efficient-market-hypothesis.md). That lesson also covers [Anomaly](anomaly.md), [Technical analysis](technical-analysis.md), [Fundamental analysis](fundamental-analysis.md) and [Efficient market hypothesis](efficient-market-hypothesis.md).
