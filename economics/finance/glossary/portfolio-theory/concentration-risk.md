# Concentration risk

The danger of having too much in one stock, sector, country or employer. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Asset allocation and rebalancing](../../06-portfolio-theory/06-asset-allocation-and-rebalancing.md). That lesson also covers [Asset allocation](asset-allocation.md), [Rebalancing](rebalancing.md), [Glide path](glide-path.md) and [Risk tolerance](risk-tolerance.md).
