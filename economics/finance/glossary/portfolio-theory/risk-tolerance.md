# Risk tolerance

How much loss an investor can accept, financially (ability) and emotionally (willingness). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Asset allocation and rebalancing](../../06-portfolio-theory/06-asset-allocation-and-rebalancing.md). That lesson also covers [Time horizon](time-horizon.md), [Investment policy statement](investment-policy-statement.md), [Core-satellite](core-satellite.md) and [Human capital](human-capital.md).
