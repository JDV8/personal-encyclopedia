# Ticker symbol

A short code identifying a listed security, such as RY on the TSX for Royal Bank of Canada. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What a share is](../../03-securities/01-stocks/01-what-is-a-share.md). That lesson also covers [Shares outstanding](shares-outstanding.md), [Shareholder](shareholder.md), [Market capitalization](market-capitalization.md) and [Share](share.md).
