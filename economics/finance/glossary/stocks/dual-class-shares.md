# Dual-class shares

A structure in which different share classes carry different voting rights, letting founders keep control with a minority of the equity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Common and preferred shares](../../03-securities/01-stocks/02-common-and-preferred-shares.md). That lesson also covers [Par value](par-value.md), [Callable](callable.md), [Common share](common-share.md) and [Cumulative preferred share](cumulative-preferred.md).
