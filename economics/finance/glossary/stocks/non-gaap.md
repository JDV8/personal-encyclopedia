# Non-GAAP earnings

Company-defined profit measures that adjust standard accounting results, for example excluding one-off costs. Useful but open to flattering choices. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Earnings and earnings per share](../../03-securities/01-stocks/05-earnings-and-eps.md). That lesson also covers [Diluted EPS](diluted-eps.md) and [Earnings per share (EPS)](earnings-per-share.md).
