# Ex-dividend date

The first day on which a buyer of a share no longer receives the already-declared dividend. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Dividends](../../03-securities/01-stocks/03-dividends.md). That lesson also covers [Return of capital](return-of-capital.md), [Retention ratio](retention-ratio.md), [DRIP](drip.md) and [Dividend](dividend.md).
