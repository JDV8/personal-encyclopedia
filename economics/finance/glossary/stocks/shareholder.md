# Shareholder

An owner of one or more shares of a corporation, with a claim on its residual profits and, usually, voting rights. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What a share is](../../03-securities/01-stocks/01-what-is-a-share.md). That lesson also covers [Market capitalization](market-capitalization.md), [Share](share.md), [Ticker symbol](stock-ticker.md) and [Shares outstanding](shares-outstanding.md).
