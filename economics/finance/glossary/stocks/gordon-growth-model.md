# Gordon growth model

The formula P = D1 / (r − g) for the value of a dividend growing at a constant rate g forever. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The dividend discount model](../../03-securities/01-stocks/08-the-dividend-discount-model.md). That lesson also covers [Dividend discount model (DDM)](dividend-discount-model.md).
