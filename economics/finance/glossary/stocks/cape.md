# CAPE (Shiller P/E)

The cyclically adjusted price-to-earnings ratio: price divided by the ten-year average of inflation-adjusted earnings, used to judge market valuation. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Other valuation ratios](../../03-securities/01-stocks/07-other-valuation-ratios.md). That lesson also covers [Price-to-book ratio](price-to-book.md), [Price-to-sales ratio](price-to-sales.md), [Enterprise value](enterprise-value.md) and [EBITDA](ebitda.md).
