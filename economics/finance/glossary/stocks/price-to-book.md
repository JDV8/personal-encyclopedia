# Price-to-book ratio

Share price divided by book value per share; low values may signal cheapness or troubled assets. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Other valuation ratios](../../03-securities/01-stocks/07-other-valuation-ratios.md). That lesson also covers [Price-to-sales ratio](price-to-sales.md), [Enterprise value](enterprise-value.md), [EBITDA](ebitda.md) and [Return on equity (ROE)](roe.md).
