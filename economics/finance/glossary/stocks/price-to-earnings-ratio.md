# Price-to-earnings ratio (P/E)

Share price divided by earnings per share: how many dollars investors pay for a dollar of annual earnings. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The price-to-earnings ratio](../../03-securities/01-stocks/06-the-price-to-earnings-ratio.md). That lesson also covers [Earnings yield](earnings-yield.md), [PEG ratio](peg-ratio.md), [Trailing earnings](trailing-earnings.md) and [Forward earnings](forward-earnings.md).
