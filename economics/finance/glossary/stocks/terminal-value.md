# Terminal value

In a DCF, the value of all cash flows beyond the explicit forecast period, often a large part of the total. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Discounted cash flow valuation](../../03-securities/01-stocks/09-discounted-cash-flow-valuation.md). That lesson also covers [Discounted cash flow (DCF)](dcf.md), [Net debt](net-debt.md) and [Sensitivity analysis](sensitivity-analysis.md).
