# Reverse split

A consolidation of shares, such as one for ten, that raises the price per share without changing total value. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Buybacks and stock splits](../../03-securities/01-stocks/04-buybacks-and-stock-splits.md). That lesson also covers [Normal course issuer bid](normal-course-issuer-bid.md), [Share buyback](share-buyback.md), [Stock split](stock-split.md) and [Treasury stock](treasury-stock.md).
