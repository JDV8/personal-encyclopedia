# Dividend

A payment from a company's profits to its shareholders, usually in cash and usually quarterly in Canada and the US. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Dividends](../../03-securities/01-stocks/03-dividends.md). That lesson also covers [Dividend yield](dividend-yield.md), [Payout ratio](payout-ratio.md), [Ex-dividend date](ex-dividend-date.md) and [Return of capital](return-of-capital.md).
