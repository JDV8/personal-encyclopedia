# Shares outstanding

The number of a company's shares held by investors, excluding treasury shares. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What a share is](../../03-securities/01-stocks/01-what-is-a-share.md). That lesson also covers [Shareholder](shareholder.md), [Market capitalization](market-capitalization.md), [Share](share.md) and [Ticker symbol](stock-ticker.md).
