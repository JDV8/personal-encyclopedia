# Share

A unit of ownership in a corporation, giving a claim on profits and, usually, a vote. Also called stock or equity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What a share is](../../03-securities/01-stocks/01-what-is-a-share.md). That lesson also covers [Ticker symbol](stock-ticker.md), [Shares outstanding](shares-outstanding.md), [Shareholder](shareholder.md) and [Market capitalization](market-capitalization.md).
