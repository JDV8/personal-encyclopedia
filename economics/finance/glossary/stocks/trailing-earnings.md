# Trailing earnings

Earnings over the last twelve months, used for the trailing P/E. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The price-to-earnings ratio](../../03-securities/01-stocks/06-the-price-to-earnings-ratio.md). That lesson also covers [Forward earnings](forward-earnings.md), [Price-to-earnings ratio (P/E)](price-to-earnings-ratio.md), [Earnings yield](earnings-yield.md) and [PEG ratio](peg-ratio.md).
