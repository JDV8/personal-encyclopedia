# Stock split

Dividing each share into several shares at a proportional lower price, leaving total value unchanged. A reverse split does the opposite. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Buybacks and stock splits](../../03-securities/01-stocks/04-buybacks-and-stock-splits.md). That lesson also covers [Treasury stock](treasury-stock.md), [Reverse split](reverse-split.md), [Normal course issuer bid](normal-course-issuer-bid.md) and [Share buyback](share-buyback.md).
