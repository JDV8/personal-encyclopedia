# Payout ratio

The share of earnings paid out as dividends. The complement, the retention ratio, is the share reinvested in the business. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Dividends](../../03-securities/01-stocks/03-dividends.md). That lesson also covers [Ex-dividend date](ex-dividend-date.md), [Return of capital](return-of-capital.md), [Retention ratio](retention-ratio.md) and [DRIP](drip.md).
