# Enterprise value

Market capitalisation plus net debt: the price of the whole business, independent of how it is financed. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Other valuation ratios](../../03-securities/01-stocks/07-other-valuation-ratios.md). That lesson also covers [EBITDA](ebitda.md), [Return on equity (ROE)](roe.md), [Return on invested capital (ROIC)](roic.md) and [Value trap](value-trap.md).
