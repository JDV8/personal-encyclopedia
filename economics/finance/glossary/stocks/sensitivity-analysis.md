# Sensitivity analysis

Testing how a valuation changes when key assumptions, such as growth or the discount rate, are varied. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Discounted cash flow valuation](../../03-securities/01-stocks/09-discounted-cash-flow-valuation.md). That lesson also covers [Terminal value](terminal-value.md), [Discounted cash flow (DCF)](dcf.md) and [Net debt](net-debt.md).
