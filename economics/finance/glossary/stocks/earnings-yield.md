# Earnings yield

Earnings per share divided by price: the inverse of the P/E. Can be compared with bond yields. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The price-to-earnings ratio](../../03-securities/01-stocks/06-the-price-to-earnings-ratio.md). That lesson also covers [PEG ratio](peg-ratio.md), [Trailing earnings](trailing-earnings.md), [Forward earnings](forward-earnings.md) and [Price-to-earnings ratio (P/E)](price-to-earnings-ratio.md).
