# PEG ratio

The P/E ratio divided by the expected earnings growth rate, an attempt to adjust the P/E for growth. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The price-to-earnings ratio](../../03-securities/01-stocks/06-the-price-to-earnings-ratio.md). That lesson also covers [Trailing earnings](trailing-earnings.md), [Forward earnings](forward-earnings.md), [Price-to-earnings ratio (P/E)](price-to-earnings-ratio.md) and [Earnings yield](earnings-yield.md).
