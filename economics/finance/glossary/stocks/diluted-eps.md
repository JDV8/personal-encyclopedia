# Diluted EPS

Earnings per share assuming all convertible securities, options and warrants are exercised. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Earnings and earnings per share](../../03-securities/01-stocks/05-earnings-and-eps.md). That lesson also covers [Earnings per share (EPS)](earnings-per-share.md) and [Non-GAAP earnings](non-gaap.md).
