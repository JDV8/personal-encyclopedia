# Value trap

A stock that looks cheap on ratios but stays cheap, or falls further, because its business is deteriorating. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Other valuation ratios](../../03-securities/01-stocks/07-other-valuation-ratios.md). That lesson also covers [CAPE (Shiller P/E)](cape.md), [Price-to-book ratio](price-to-book.md), [Price-to-sales ratio](price-to-sales.md) and [Enterprise value](enterprise-value.md).
