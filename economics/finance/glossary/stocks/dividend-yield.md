# Dividend yield

Annual dividends per share divided by the share price; the cash income return on the price paid. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Dividends](../../03-securities/01-stocks/03-dividends.md). That lesson also covers [Payout ratio](payout-ratio.md), [Ex-dividend date](ex-dividend-date.md), [Return of capital](return-of-capital.md) and [Retention ratio](retention-ratio.md).
