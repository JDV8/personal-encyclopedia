# Retention ratio

The share of earnings a company keeps rather than pays out as dividends, equal to one minus the payout ratio. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Dividends](../../03-securities/01-stocks/03-dividends.md). That lesson also covers [DRIP](drip.md), [Dividend](dividend.md), [Dividend yield](dividend-yield.md) and [Payout ratio](payout-ratio.md).
