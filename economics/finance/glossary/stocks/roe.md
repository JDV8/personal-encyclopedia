# Return on equity (ROE)

Net income divided by shareholders' equity: how much profit a company generates on owners' capital. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Other valuation ratios](../../03-securities/01-stocks/07-other-valuation-ratios.md). That lesson also covers [Return on invested capital (ROIC)](roic.md), [Value trap](value-trap.md), [CAPE (Shiller P/E)](cape.md) and [Price-to-book ratio](price-to-book.md).
