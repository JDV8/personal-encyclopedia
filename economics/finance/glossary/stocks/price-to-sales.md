# Price-to-sales ratio

Market capitalisation divided by revenue; used for companies with little or no profit. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Other valuation ratios](../../03-securities/01-stocks/07-other-valuation-ratios.md). That lesson also covers [Enterprise value](enterprise-value.md), [EBITDA](ebitda.md), [Return on equity (ROE)](roe.md) and [Return on invested capital (ROIC)](roic.md).
