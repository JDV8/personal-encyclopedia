# Share buyback

A company repurchasing its own shares, which reduces shares outstanding and returns cash to shareholders. In Canada it is done through a normal course issuer bid. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Buybacks and stock splits](../../03-securities/01-stocks/04-buybacks-and-stock-splits.md). That lesson also covers [Stock split](stock-split.md), [Treasury stock](treasury-stock.md), [Reverse split](reverse-split.md) and [Normal course issuer bid](normal-course-issuer-bid.md).
