# Market capitalization

A company's share price multiplied by its shares outstanding: the market value of its equity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What a share is](../../03-securities/01-stocks/01-what-is-a-share.md). That lesson also covers [Share](share.md), [Ticker symbol](stock-ticker.md), [Shares outstanding](shares-outstanding.md) and [Shareholder](shareholder.md).
