# Preferred share

A share with a fixed dividend and priority over common shares in dividends and in liquidation, usually without voting rights. Cumulative preferreds accumulate unpaid dividends. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Common and preferred shares](../../03-securities/01-stocks/02-common-and-preferred-shares.md). That lesson also covers [Dual-class shares](dual-class-shares.md), [Par value](par-value.md), [Callable](callable.md) and [Common share](common-share.md).
