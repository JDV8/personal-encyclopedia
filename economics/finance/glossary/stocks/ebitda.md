# EBITDA

Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization; a rough proxy for operating cash earnings, used in multiples such as EV/EBITDA. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Other valuation ratios](../../03-securities/01-stocks/07-other-valuation-ratios.md). That lesson also covers [Return on equity (ROE)](roe.md), [Return on invested capital (ROIC)](roic.md), [Value trap](value-trap.md) and [CAPE (Shiller P/E)](cape.md).
