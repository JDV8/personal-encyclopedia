# Earnings per share (EPS)

Net income available to common shareholders divided by shares outstanding; diluted EPS also counts options and convertibles. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Earnings and earnings per share](../../03-securities/01-stocks/05-earnings-and-eps.md). That lesson also covers [Non-GAAP earnings](non-gaap.md) and [Diluted EPS](diluted-eps.md).
