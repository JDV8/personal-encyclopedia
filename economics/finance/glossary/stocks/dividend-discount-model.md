# Dividend discount model (DDM)

A valuation method that values a share as the present value of its expected future dividends; with constant growth g it is D₁ ÷ (r − g), the Gordon growth model. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The dividend discount model](../../03-securities/01-stocks/08-the-dividend-discount-model.md). That lesson also covers [Gordon growth model](gordon-growth-model.md).
