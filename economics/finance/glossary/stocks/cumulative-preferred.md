# Cumulative preferred share

A preferred share whose unpaid dividends accumulate and must be paid before any common dividend. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Common and preferred shares](../../03-securities/01-stocks/02-common-and-preferred-shares.md). That lesson also covers [Preferred share](preferred-share.md), [Dual-class shares](dual-class-shares.md), [Par value](par-value.md) and [Callable](callable.md).
