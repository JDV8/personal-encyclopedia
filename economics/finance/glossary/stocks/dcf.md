# Discounted cash flow (DCF)

A valuation method that discounts a company's expected future free cash flows, plus terminal value, to a present value. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Discounted cash flow valuation](../../03-securities/01-stocks/09-discounted-cash-flow-valuation.md). That lesson also covers [Net debt](net-debt.md), [Sensitivity analysis](sensitivity-analysis.md) and [Terminal value](terminal-value.md).
