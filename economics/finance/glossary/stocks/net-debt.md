# Net debt

Total debt minus cash; used to move between enterprise value and equity value. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Discounted cash flow valuation](../../03-securities/01-stocks/09-discounted-cash-flow-valuation.md). That lesson also covers [Sensitivity analysis](sensitivity-analysis.md), [Terminal value](terminal-value.md) and [Discounted cash flow (DCF)](dcf.md).
