# DRIP

A dividend reinvestment plan, which automatically uses dividends to buy more shares, often without commission. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Dividends](../../03-securities/01-stocks/03-dividends.md). That lesson also covers [Dividend](dividend.md), [Dividend yield](dividend-yield.md), [Payout ratio](payout-ratio.md) and [Ex-dividend date](ex-dividend-date.md).
