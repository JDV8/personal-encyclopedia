# Return of capital

A distribution that returns an investor's own money rather than profits; it lowers the adjusted cost base and is not taxed immediately. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Dividends](../../03-securities/01-stocks/03-dividends.md). That lesson also covers [Retention ratio](retention-ratio.md), [DRIP](drip.md), [Dividend](dividend.md) and [Dividend yield](dividend-yield.md).
