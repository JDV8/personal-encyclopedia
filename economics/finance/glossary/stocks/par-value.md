# Par value

The nominal value assigned to a share or bond. For a bond it is the amount repaid at maturity; for shares it has little economic meaning. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Common and preferred shares](../../03-securities/01-stocks/02-common-and-preferred-shares.md). That lesson also covers [Callable](callable.md), [Common share](common-share.md), [Cumulative preferred share](cumulative-preferred.md) and [Preferred share](preferred-share.md).
