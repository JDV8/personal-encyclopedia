# Normal course issuer bid

The Canadian term for a regulated share buyback programme, typically capped at 5% of shares in 12 months. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Buybacks and stock splits](../../03-securities/01-stocks/04-buybacks-and-stock-splits.md). That lesson also covers [Share buyback](share-buyback.md), [Stock split](stock-split.md), [Treasury stock](treasury-stock.md) and [Reverse split](reverse-split.md).
