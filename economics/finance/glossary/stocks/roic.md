# Return on invested capital (ROIC)

After-tax operating profit divided by capital invested in the business by both lenders and shareholders. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Other valuation ratios](../../03-securities/01-stocks/07-other-valuation-ratios.md). That lesson also covers [Value trap](value-trap.md), [CAPE (Shiller P/E)](cape.md), [Price-to-book ratio](price-to-book.md) and [Price-to-sales ratio](price-to-sales.md).
