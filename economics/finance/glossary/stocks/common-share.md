# Common share

A share carrying voting rights and a residual claim on profits and assets after all other claims are paid. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Common and preferred shares](../../03-securities/01-stocks/02-common-and-preferred-shares.md). That lesson also covers [Cumulative preferred share](cumulative-preferred.md), [Preferred share](preferred-share.md), [Dual-class shares](dual-class-shares.md) and [Par value](par-value.md).
