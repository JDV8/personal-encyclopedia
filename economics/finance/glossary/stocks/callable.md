# Callable

A feature allowing the issuer to redeem a bond or preferred share before maturity at a set price, usually when interest rates fall. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Common and preferred shares](../../03-securities/01-stocks/02-common-and-preferred-shares.md). That lesson also covers [Common share](common-share.md), [Cumulative preferred share](cumulative-preferred.md), [Preferred share](preferred-share.md) and [Dual-class shares](dual-class-shares.md).
