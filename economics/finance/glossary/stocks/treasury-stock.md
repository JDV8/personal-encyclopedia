# Treasury stock

Shares a company has repurchased and holds itself, which carry no votes or dividends. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Buybacks and stock splits](../../03-securities/01-stocks/04-buybacks-and-stock-splits.md). That lesson also covers [Reverse split](reverse-split.md), [Normal course issuer bid](normal-course-issuer-bid.md), [Share buyback](share-buyback.md) and [Stock split](stock-split.md).
