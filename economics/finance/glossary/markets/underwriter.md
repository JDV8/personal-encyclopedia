# Underwriter

An investment bank that prices and sells a new issue, often guaranteeing the sale. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [IPOs and the primary market](../../04-markets/02-ipos-and-the-primary-market.md). That lesson also covers [Prospectus](prospectus.md), [Dilution](dilution.md), [Underpricing](underpricing.md) and [Lock-up](lock-up.md).
