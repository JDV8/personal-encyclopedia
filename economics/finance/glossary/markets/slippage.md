# Slippage

The difference between the expected price and the price actually obtained. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Order types](../../04-markets/04-order-types.md). That lesson also covers [Good-till-cancelled (GTC)](good-till-cancelled.md), [VWAP](vwap.md), [Market order](market-order.md) and [Limit order](limit-order.md).
