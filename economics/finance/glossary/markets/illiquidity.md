# Illiquidity

The difficulty of selling an asset quickly without a large price concession. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What an exchange does](../../04-markets/01-what-an-exchange-does.md). That lesson also covers [Secondary market](secondary-market.md), [Stock exchange](stock-exchange.md), [Limit order book](limit-order-book.md) and [Dark pool](dark-pool.md).
