# Margin account

A brokerage account that lets you borrow against your holdings to buy securities or sell short. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Short selling and margin](../../04-markets/08-short-selling-and-margin.md). That lesson also covers [Maintenance margin](maintenance-margin.md), [Naked short selling](naked-shorting.md), [Margin call](margin-call.md) and [Short selling](short-selling.md).
