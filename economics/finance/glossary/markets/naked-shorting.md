# Naked short selling

Selling shares short without first borrowing them or arranging to borrow them; restricted or prohibited in most markets. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Short selling and margin](../../04-markets/08-short-selling-and-margin.md). That lesson also covers [Margin call](margin-call.md), [Short selling](short-selling.md), [Short squeeze](short-squeeze.md) and [Margin account](margin-account.md).
