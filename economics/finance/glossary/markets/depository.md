# Depository

An institution holding securities in electronic form and recording ownership changes. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Clearing and settlement](../../04-markets/07-clearing-and-settlement.md). That lesson also covers [Clearing](clearing.md), [Settlement](settlement.md), [Central counterparty (CCP)](central-counterparty.md) and [Novation](novation.md).
