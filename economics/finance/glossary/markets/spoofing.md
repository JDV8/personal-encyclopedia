# Spoofing

An illegal practice of placing orders with intent to cancel them to mislead other traders. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Algorithmic and high-frequency trading](../../04-markets/09-algorithmic-and-high-frequency-trading.md). That lesson also covers [Market impact](market-impact.md), [Algorithmic trading](algorithmic-trading.md), [High-frequency trading](high-frequency-trading.md) and [Co-location](co-location.md).
