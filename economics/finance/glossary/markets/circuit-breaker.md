# Circuit breaker

A rule that pauses trading after a large price move to allow time for orders to be reassessed. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Algorithmic and high-frequency trading](../../04-markets/09-algorithmic-and-high-frequency-trading.md). That lesson also covers [Spoofing](spoofing.md), [Market impact](market-impact.md), [Algorithmic trading](algorithmic-trading.md) and [High-frequency trading](high-frequency-trading.md).
