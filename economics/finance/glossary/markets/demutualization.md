# Demutualization

The conversion of an exchange from a member-owned club into a for-profit company. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What an exchange does](../../04-markets/01-what-an-exchange-does.md). That lesson also covers [Over-the-counter (OTC)](over-the-counter.md), [Listing](listing.md), [Price discovery](price-discovery.md) and [TSX](tsx.md).
