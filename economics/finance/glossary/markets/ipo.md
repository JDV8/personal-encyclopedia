# Initial public offering (IPO)

The first sale of a company's shares to the public. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [IPOs and the primary market](../../04-markets/02-ipos-and-the-primary-market.md). That lesson also covers [Underwriter](underwriter.md), [Prospectus](prospectus.md), [Dilution](dilution.md) and [Underpricing](underpricing.md).
