# CDS Clearing

Canada's national clearing and depository for securities, part of TMX Group; unrelated to credit default swaps. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Clearing and settlement](../../04-markets/07-clearing-and-settlement.md). That lesson also covers [Depository](depository.md), [Clearing](clearing.md), [Settlement](settlement.md) and [Central counterparty (CCP)](central-counterparty.md).
