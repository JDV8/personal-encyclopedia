# VWAP

Volume-weighted average price; also an algorithm that executes a large order in line with the day's volume. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Order types](../../04-markets/04-order-types.md). That lesson also covers [Market order](market-order.md), [Limit order](limit-order.md), [Stop order](stop-order.md) and [Slippage](slippage.md).
