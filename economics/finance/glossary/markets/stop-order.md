# Stop order

An order that becomes a market order once a trigger price trades; it can fill well beyond the trigger in a gap. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Order types](../../04-markets/04-order-types.md). That lesson also covers [Slippage](slippage.md), [Good-till-cancelled (GTC)](good-till-cancelled.md), [VWAP](vwap.md) and [Market order](market-order.md).
