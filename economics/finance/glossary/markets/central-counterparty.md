# Central counterparty (CCP)

An entity that becomes the buyer to every seller and the seller to every buyer, guaranteeing performance. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Clearing and settlement](../../04-markets/07-clearing-and-settlement.md). That lesson also covers [Novation](novation.md), [Delivery versus payment](delivery-versus-payment.md), [Street name](street-name.md) and [CDS Clearing](cds-clearing.md).
