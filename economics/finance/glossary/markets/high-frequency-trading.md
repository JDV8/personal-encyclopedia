# High-frequency trading

Algorithmic trading that relies on extreme speed, with positions held for very short times. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Algorithmic and high-frequency trading](../../04-markets/09-algorithmic-and-high-frequency-trading.md). That lesson also covers [Co-location](co-location.md), [Flash crash](flash-crash.md), [Circuit breaker](circuit-breaker.md) and [Spoofing](spoofing.md).
