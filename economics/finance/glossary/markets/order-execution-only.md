# Order-execution-only (OEO)

A Canadian account type in which the client makes all decisions and receives no advice. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Brokers and dealers](../../04-markets/03-brokers-and-dealers.md). That lesson also covers [Payment for order flow](payment-for-order-flow.md), [Broker](broker.md), [Dealer](dealer.md) and [Investment bank](investment-bank.md).
