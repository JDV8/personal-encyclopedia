# SPAC

A special purpose acquisition company: a listed cash shell that later merges with a private company. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [IPOs and the primary market](../../04-markets/02-ipos-and-the-primary-market.md). That lesson also covers [Bought deal](bought-deal.md), [Public company](public-company.md), [Primary market](primary-market.md) and [Initial public offering (IPO)](ipo.md).
