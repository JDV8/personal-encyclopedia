# Market liquidity

In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bid, ask and market makers](../../04-markets/05-bid-ask-and-market-makers.md). That lesson also covers [Ask](ask.md), [Bid](bid.md), [Market maker](market-maker.md) and [Bid-ask spread](bid-ask-spread.md).
