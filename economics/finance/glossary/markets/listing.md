# Listing

Admission of a security to trading on an exchange after meeting its requirements. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What an exchange does](../../04-markets/01-what-an-exchange-does.md). That lesson also covers [Price discovery](price-discovery.md), [TSX](tsx.md), [Illiquidity](illiquidity.md) and [Secondary market](secondary-market.md).
