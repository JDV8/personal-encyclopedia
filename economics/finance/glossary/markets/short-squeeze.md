# Short squeeze

A rapid price rise that forces short sellers to buy back shares, pushing the price higher still. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Short selling and margin](../../04-markets/08-short-selling-and-margin.md). That lesson also covers [Margin account](margin-account.md), [Maintenance margin](maintenance-margin.md), [Naked short selling](naked-shorting.md) and [Margin call](margin-call.md).
