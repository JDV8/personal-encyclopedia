# Market impact

The price movement caused by one's own trading, larger for big orders in thin markets. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Algorithmic and high-frequency trading](../../04-markets/09-algorithmic-and-high-frequency-trading.md). That lesson also covers [Algorithmic trading](algorithmic-trading.md), [High-frequency trading](high-frequency-trading.md), [Co-location](co-location.md) and [Flash crash](flash-crash.md).
