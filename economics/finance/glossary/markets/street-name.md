# Street name

Securities registered in a broker's name on behalf of the investor. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Clearing and settlement](../../04-markets/07-clearing-and-settlement.md). That lesson also covers [CDS Clearing](cds-clearing.md), [Depository](depository.md), [Clearing](clearing.md) and [Settlement](settlement.md).
