# Limit order

An order to trade only at a specified price or better. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Order types](../../04-markets/04-order-types.md). That lesson also covers [Stop order](stop-order.md), [Slippage](slippage.md), [Good-till-cancelled (GTC)](good-till-cancelled.md) and [VWAP](vwap.md).
