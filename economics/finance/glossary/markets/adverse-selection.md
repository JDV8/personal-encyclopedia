# Adverse selection

The risk that a counterparty knows more than you; market makers widen spreads to cover trades with informed traders. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bid, ask and market makers](../../04-markets/05-bid-ask-and-market-makers.md). That lesson also covers [Tick size](tick-size.md), [Market liquidity](market-liquidity.md), [Ask](ask.md) and [Bid](bid.md).
