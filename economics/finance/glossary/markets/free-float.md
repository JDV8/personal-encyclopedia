# Free float

The shares available to public investors, excluding those held by insiders or governments. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Market indices](../../04-markets/06-market-indices.md). That lesson also covers [Total return](total-return.md), [Market index](market-index.md), [Price-weighted index](price-weighted.md) and [Cap-weighted index](cap-weighted.md).
