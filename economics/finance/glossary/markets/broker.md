# Broker

An intermediary who arranges trades for clients as an agent, for a commission or fee. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Brokers and dealers](../../04-markets/03-brokers-and-dealers.md). That lesson also covers [Dealer](dealer.md), [Investment bank](investment-bank.md), [Order-execution-only (OEO)](order-execution-only.md) and [Payment for order flow](payment-for-order-flow.md).
