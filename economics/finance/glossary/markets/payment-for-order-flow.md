# Payment for order flow

A broker receiving payment from a market maker for routing client orders; not generally permitted in Canada. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Brokers and dealers](../../04-markets/03-brokers-and-dealers.md). That lesson also covers [Broker](broker.md), [Dealer](dealer.md), [Investment bank](investment-bank.md) and [Order-execution-only (OEO)](order-execution-only.md).
