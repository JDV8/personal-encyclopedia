# Bid-ask spread

The gap between the highest price buyers will pay (bid) and the lowest price sellers will accept (ask); the cost of immediacy. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bid, ask and market makers](../../04-markets/05-bid-ask-and-market-makers.md). That lesson also covers [Adverse selection](adverse-selection.md), [Tick size](tick-size.md), [Market liquidity](market-liquidity.md) and [Ask](ask.md).
