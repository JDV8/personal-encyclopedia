# Over-the-counter (OTC)

Trading conducted directly between parties or through dealers rather than on an exchange. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What an exchange does](../../04-markets/01-what-an-exchange-does.md). That lesson also covers [Listing](listing.md), [Price discovery](price-discovery.md), [TSX](tsx.md) and [Illiquidity](illiquidity.md).
