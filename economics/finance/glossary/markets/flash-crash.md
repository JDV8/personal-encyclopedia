# Flash crash

A very rapid price fall and recovery, such as 6 May 2010 when the Dow dropped about 9% in minutes. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Algorithmic and high-frequency trading](../../04-markets/09-algorithmic-and-high-frequency-trading.md). That lesson also covers [Circuit breaker](circuit-breaker.md), [Spoofing](spoofing.md), [Market impact](market-impact.md) and [Algorithmic trading](algorithmic-trading.md).
