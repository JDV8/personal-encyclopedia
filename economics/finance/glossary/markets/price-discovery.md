# Price discovery

The process by which the interaction of buyers and sellers reveals a market price. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What an exchange does](../../04-markets/01-what-an-exchange-does.md). That lesson also covers [TSX](tsx.md), [Illiquidity](illiquidity.md), [Secondary market](secondary-market.md) and [Stock exchange](stock-exchange.md).
