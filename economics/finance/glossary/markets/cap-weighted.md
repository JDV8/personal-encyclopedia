# Cap-weighted index

An index in which each stock counts in proportion to its market capitalisation, such as the S&P 500. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Market indices](../../04-markets/06-market-indices.md). That lesson also covers [Free float](free-float.md), [Total return](total-return.md), [Market index](market-index.md) and [Price-weighted index](price-weighted.md).
