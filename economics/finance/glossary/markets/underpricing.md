# Underpricing

Setting an IPO price below the first-day trading price; the gap is "money left on the table". In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [IPOs and the primary market](../../04-markets/02-ipos-and-the-primary-market.md). That lesson also covers [Lock-up](lock-up.md), [SPAC](spac.md), [Bought deal](bought-deal.md) and [Public company](public-company.md).
