# Maintenance margin

The minimum equity a margin account must hold; falling below it triggers a [margin call](margin-call.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Short selling and margin](../../04-markets/08-short-selling-and-margin.md). That lesson also covers [Naked short selling](naked-shorting.md), [Margin call](margin-call.md), [Short selling](short-selling.md) and [Short squeeze](short-squeeze.md).
