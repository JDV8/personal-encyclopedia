# Co-location

Placing trading computers next to an exchange's servers to reduce latency. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Algorithmic and high-frequency trading](../../04-markets/09-algorithmic-and-high-frequency-trading.md). That lesson also covers [Flash crash](flash-crash.md), [Circuit breaker](circuit-breaker.md), [Spoofing](spoofing.md) and [Market impact](market-impact.md).
