# Margin call

A demand to add cash or securities when equity in a margin account falls below the maintenance margin. A margin account is one allowing borrowing against holdings. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Short selling and margin](../../04-markets/08-short-selling-and-margin.md). That lesson also covers [Short selling](short-selling.md), [Short squeeze](short-squeeze.md), [Margin account](margin-account.md) and [Maintenance margin](maintenance-margin.md).
