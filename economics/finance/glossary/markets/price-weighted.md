# Price-weighted index

An index in which each stock counts in proportion to its price, such as the Dow Jones Industrial Average. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Market indices](../../04-markets/06-market-indices.md). That lesson also covers [Cap-weighted index](cap-weighted.md), [Free float](free-float.md), [Total return](total-return.md) and [Market index](market-index.md).
