# Lock-up

An agreement barring insiders from selling shares for a period after an IPO, commonly about 180 days. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [IPOs and the primary market](../../04-markets/02-ipos-and-the-primary-market.md). That lesson also covers [SPAC](spac.md), [Bought deal](bought-deal.md), [Public company](public-company.md) and [Primary market](primary-market.md).
