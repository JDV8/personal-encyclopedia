# Investment bank

A firm that underwrites securities, advises on mergers and trades for clients and itself. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Brokers and dealers](../../04-markets/03-brokers-and-dealers.md). That lesson also covers [Order-execution-only (OEO)](order-execution-only.md), [Payment for order flow](payment-for-order-flow.md), [Broker](broker.md) and [Dealer](dealer.md).
