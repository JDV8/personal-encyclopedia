# Dark pool

A trading venue that does not display orders before execution, used by institutions for large trades. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What an exchange does](../../04-markets/01-what-an-exchange-does.md). That lesson also covers [Demutualization](demutualization.md), [Over-the-counter (OTC)](over-the-counter.md), [Listing](listing.md) and [Price discovery](price-discovery.md).
