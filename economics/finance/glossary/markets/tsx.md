# TSX

The Toronto Stock Exchange, Canada's main exchange, founded in 1861 and part of TMX Group. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What an exchange does](../../04-markets/01-what-an-exchange-does.md). That lesson also covers [Illiquidity](illiquidity.md), [Secondary market](secondary-market.md), [Stock exchange](stock-exchange.md) and [Limit order book](limit-order-book.md).
