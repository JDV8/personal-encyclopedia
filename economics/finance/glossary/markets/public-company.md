# Public company

A company whose shares trade on a stock exchange and which must meet ongoing disclosure rules. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [IPOs and the primary market](../../04-markets/02-ipos-and-the-primary-market.md). That lesson also covers [Primary market](primary-market.md), [Initial public offering (IPO)](ipo.md), [Underwriter](underwriter.md) and [Prospectus](prospectus.md).
