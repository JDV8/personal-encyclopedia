# Market maker

A dealer who continuously quotes both a bid and an ask, providing liquidity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bid, ask and market makers](../../04-markets/05-bid-ask-and-market-makers.md). That lesson also covers [Bid-ask spread](bid-ask-spread.md), [Adverse selection](adverse-selection.md), [Tick size](tick-size.md) and [Market liquidity](market-liquidity.md).
