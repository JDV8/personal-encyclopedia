# Prospectus

The legal document describing an offering's business, risks and finances, required for a public offering. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [IPOs and the primary market](../../04-markets/02-ipos-and-the-primary-market.md). That lesson also covers [Dilution](dilution.md), [Underpricing](underpricing.md), [Lock-up](lock-up.md) and [SPAC](spac.md).
