# Ask

The lowest price a seller will accept for a security; the offer. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bid, ask and market makers](../../04-markets/05-bid-ask-and-market-makers.md). That lesson also covers [Bid](bid.md), [Market maker](market-maker.md), [Bid-ask spread](bid-ask-spread.md) and [Adverse selection](adverse-selection.md).
