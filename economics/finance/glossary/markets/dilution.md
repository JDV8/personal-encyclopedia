# Dilution

The reduction in existing shareholders' percentage ownership when new shares are issued. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [IPOs and the primary market](../../04-markets/02-ipos-and-the-primary-market.md). That lesson also covers [Underpricing](underpricing.md), [Lock-up](lock-up.md), [SPAC](spac.md) and [Bought deal](bought-deal.md).
