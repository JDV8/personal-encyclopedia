# Limit order book

The list of outstanding buy and sell orders sorted by price and time, matched by an exchange's engine. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What an exchange does](../../04-markets/01-what-an-exchange-does.md). That lesson also covers [Dark pool](dark-pool.md), [Demutualization](demutualization.md), [Over-the-counter (OTC)](over-the-counter.md) and [Listing](listing.md).
