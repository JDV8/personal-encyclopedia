# Market index

A number summarising the performance of a basket of securities. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Market indices](../../04-markets/06-market-indices.md). That lesson also covers [Price-weighted index](price-weighted.md), [Cap-weighted index](cap-weighted.md), [Free float](free-float.md) and [Total return](total-return.md).
