# Market order

An order to trade immediately at the best available price. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Order types](../../04-markets/04-order-types.md). That lesson also covers [Limit order](limit-order.md), [Stop order](stop-order.md), [Slippage](slippage.md) and [Good-till-cancelled (GTC)](good-till-cancelled.md).
