# Total return

Return including price change and reinvested dividends or interest. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Market indices](../../04-markets/06-market-indices.md). That lesson also covers [Market index](market-index.md), [Price-weighted index](price-weighted.md), [Cap-weighted index](cap-weighted.md) and [Free float](free-float.md).
