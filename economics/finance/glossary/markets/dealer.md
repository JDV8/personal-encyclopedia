# Dealer

A firm that trades as a principal from its own inventory, earning the bid-ask spread. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Brokers and dealers](../../04-markets/03-brokers-and-dealers.md). That lesson also covers [Investment bank](investment-bank.md), [Order-execution-only (OEO)](order-execution-only.md), [Payment for order flow](payment-for-order-flow.md) and [Broker](broker.md).
