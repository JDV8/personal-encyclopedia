# Secondary market

The market where existing securities are traded among investors, such as a stock exchange; proceeds go to the seller, not the issuer. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What an exchange does](../../04-markets/01-what-an-exchange-does.md). That lesson also covers [Stock exchange](stock-exchange.md), [Limit order book](limit-order-book.md), [Dark pool](dark-pool.md) and [Demutualization](demutualization.md).
