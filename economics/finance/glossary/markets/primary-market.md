# Primary market

The market where new securities are first sold, with the proceeds going to the issuer, for example in an [IPO](ipo.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [IPOs and the primary market](../../04-markets/02-ipos-and-the-primary-market.md). That lesson also covers [Initial public offering (IPO)](ipo.md), [Underwriter](underwriter.md), [Prospectus](prospectus.md) and [Dilution](dilution.md).
