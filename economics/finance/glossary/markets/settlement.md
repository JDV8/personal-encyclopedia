# Settlement

The final exchange of securities for cash; T+1 in Canada and the US since May 2024. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Clearing and settlement](../../04-markets/07-clearing-and-settlement.md). That lesson also covers [Central counterparty (CCP)](central-counterparty.md), [Novation](novation.md), [Delivery versus payment](delivery-versus-payment.md) and [Street name](street-name.md).
