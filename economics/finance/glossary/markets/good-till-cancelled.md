# Good-till-cancelled (GTC)

An order that stays active until it is filled or cancelled. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Order types](../../04-markets/04-order-types.md). That lesson also covers [VWAP](vwap.md), [Market order](market-order.md), [Limit order](limit-order.md) and [Stop order](stop-order.md).
