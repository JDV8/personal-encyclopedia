# Bought deal

An offering in which an underwriter commits to buy the whole issue immediately and resell it; common in Canada. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [IPOs and the primary market](../../04-markets/02-ipos-and-the-primary-market.md). That lesson also covers [Public company](public-company.md), [Primary market](primary-market.md), [Initial public offering (IPO)](ipo.md) and [Underwriter](underwriter.md).
