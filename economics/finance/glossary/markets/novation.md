# Novation

Replacing the original contract between two parties with two contracts, each with the central counterparty. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Clearing and settlement](../../04-markets/07-clearing-and-settlement.md). That lesson also covers [Delivery versus payment](delivery-versus-payment.md), [Street name](street-name.md), [CDS Clearing](cds-clearing.md) and [Depository](depository.md).
