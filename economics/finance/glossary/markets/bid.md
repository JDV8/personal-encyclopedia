# Bid

The highest price a buyer will pay for a security. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Bid, ask and market makers](../../04-markets/05-bid-ask-and-market-makers.md). That lesson also covers [Market maker](market-maker.md), [Bid-ask spread](bid-ask-spread.md), [Adverse selection](adverse-selection.md) and [Tick size](tick-size.md).
