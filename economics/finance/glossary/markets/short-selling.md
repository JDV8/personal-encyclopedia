# Short selling

Borrowing shares, selling them, and buying them back later, profiting if the price falls; losses are unlimited. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Short selling and margin](../../04-markets/08-short-selling-and-margin.md). That lesson also covers [Short squeeze](short-squeeze.md), [Margin account](margin-account.md), [Maintenance margin](maintenance-margin.md) and [Naked short selling](naked-shorting.md).
