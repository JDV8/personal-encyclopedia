# Diversification

Spreading investments across assets that do not move perfectly together, reducing portfolio risk without necessarily reducing expected return. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Correlation and diversification](../../01-foundations/11-correlation-and-diversification.md). That lesson also covers [Systematic risk](systematic-risk.md), [Idiosyncratic risk](idiosyncratic-risk.md) and [Correlation](correlation.md).
