# Perpetuity

A stream of equal payments that continues forever. Its present value is simply payment ÷ rate, and it underlies simple valuation formulas such as the [dividend discount model](../stocks/dividend-discount-model.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Annuities and perpetuities](../../01-foundations/06-annuities-and-perpetuities.md). That lesson also covers [Consol](consol.md), [Amortization](amortization.md) and [Annuity](annuity.md).
