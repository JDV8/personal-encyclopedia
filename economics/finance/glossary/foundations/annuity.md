# Annuity

A series of equal payments at regular intervals for a fixed time. Its present value is found with the annuity formula; mortgages and bonds' coupons are examples. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Annuities and perpetuities](../../01-foundations/06-annuities-and-perpetuities.md). That lesson also covers [Perpetuity](perpetuity.md), [Consol](consol.md) and [Amortization](amortization.md).
