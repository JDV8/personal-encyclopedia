# Interest rate

The price of borrowing money for a period, expressed as a percentage of the [principal](principal.md). It compensates lenders for time, inflation and risk. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Simple and compound interest](../../01-foundations/03-simple-and-compound-interest.md). That lesson also covers [Simple interest](simple-interest.md), [Compound interest](compound-interest.md), [Effective annual rate (EAR)](effective-annual-rate.md) and [Rule of 72](rule-of-72.md).
