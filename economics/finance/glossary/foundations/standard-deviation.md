# Standard deviation

The square root of variance; the usual measure of how widely returns vary around their average, and the common definition of [volatility](volatility.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Variance and standard deviation](../../01-foundations/09-variance-and-standard-deviation.md). That lesson also covers [Volatility](volatility.md), [Normal distribution](normal-distribution.md), [Fat tails](fat-tails.md) and [Variance](variance.md).
