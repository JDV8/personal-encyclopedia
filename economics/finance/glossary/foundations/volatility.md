# Volatility

The degree to which an asset's price or return fluctuates, usually measured as annualised standard deviation of returns. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Variance and standard deviation](../../01-foundations/09-variance-and-standard-deviation.md). That lesson also covers [Normal distribution](normal-distribution.md), [Fat tails](fat-tails.md), [Variance](variance.md) and [Standard deviation](standard-deviation.md).
