# Fiat money

Currency that has value because a government declares it legal tender and people trust it, not because it is backed by a commodity such as gold. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Money and value](../../01-foundations/01-money-and-value.md). That lesson also covers [Store of value](store-of-value.md) and [Money](money.md).
