# Correlation

A measure from −1 to +1 of how closely two returns move together. Low or negative correlation makes [diversification](diversification.md) more effective. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Correlation and diversification](../../01-foundations/11-correlation-and-diversification.md). That lesson also covers [Diversification](diversification.md), [Systematic risk](systematic-risk.md) and [Idiosyncratic risk](idiosyncratic-risk.md).
