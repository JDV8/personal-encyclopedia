# Variance

The average squared deviation of outcomes from their expected value; a measure of spread. Its square root is the [standard deviation](standard-deviation.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Variance and standard deviation](../../01-foundations/09-variance-and-standard-deviation.md). That lesson also covers [Standard deviation](standard-deviation.md), [Volatility](volatility.md), [Normal distribution](normal-distribution.md) and [Fat tails](fat-tails.md).
