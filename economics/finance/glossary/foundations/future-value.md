# Future value

What an amount invested today will be worth at a later date at a given rate of growth: PV × (1 + r)^n. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The time value of money](../../01-foundations/04-time-value-of-money.md). That lesson also covers [Opportunity cost](opportunity-cost.md) and [Time value of money](time-value-of-money.md).
