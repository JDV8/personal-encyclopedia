# Hyperinflation

Extremely rapid inflation, often defined as above 50% a month, which destroys the value of money. Famous cases include Weimar Germany and Zimbabwe. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Inflation and real returns](../../01-foundations/07-inflation-and-real-returns.md). That lesson also covers [Inflation](inflation.md), [Real return](real-return.md), [Nominal return](nominal-return.md) and [Consumer Price Index (CPI)](cpi.md).
