# Effective annual rate (EAR)

The actual yearly return after accounting for compounding within the year; a 12% nominal rate compounded monthly has an EAR of about 12.68%. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Simple and compound interest](../../01-foundations/03-simple-and-compound-interest.md). That lesson also covers [Rule of 72](rule-of-72.md), [Principal](principal.md), [Interest rate](interest-rate.md) and [Simple interest](simple-interest.md).
