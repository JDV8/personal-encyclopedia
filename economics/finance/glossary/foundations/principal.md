# Principal

The original amount of money lent or invested, on which [interest](interest-rate.md) is calculated; for a bond, the face value repaid at [maturity](../bonds/maturity.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Simple and compound interest](../../01-foundations/03-simple-and-compound-interest.md). That lesson also covers [Interest rate](interest-rate.md), [Simple interest](simple-interest.md), [Compound interest](compound-interest.md) and [Effective annual rate (EAR)](effective-annual-rate.md).
