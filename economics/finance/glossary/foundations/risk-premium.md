# Risk premium

The extra expected return investors require for holding a risky asset instead of a risk-free one. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Risk and return](../../01-foundations/10-risk-and-return.md). That lesson also covers [Risk-free rate](risk-free-rate.md), [Equity risk premium](equity-risk-premium.md), [Risk](risk.md) and [Risk aversion](risk-aversion.md).
