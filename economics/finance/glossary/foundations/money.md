# Money

Anything widely accepted as payment that serves as a medium of exchange, a unit of account and a store of value. Modern money is mostly [fiat money](fiat-money.md) and bank deposits. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Money and value](../../01-foundations/01-money-and-value.md). That lesson also covers [Fiat money](fiat-money.md) and [Store of value](store-of-value.md).
