# Idiosyncratic risk

Risk specific to one company or asset, such as a product failure, that diversification can largely eliminate. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Correlation and diversification](../../01-foundations/11-correlation-and-diversification.md). That lesson also covers [Correlation](correlation.md), [Diversification](diversification.md) and [Systematic risk](systematic-risk.md).
