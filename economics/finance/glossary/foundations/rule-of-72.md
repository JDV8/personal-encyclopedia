# Rule of 72

A shortcut: divide 72 by the annual percentage return to estimate the years needed to double money. At 6% it takes roughly 12 years. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Simple and compound interest](../../01-foundations/03-simple-and-compound-interest.md). That lesson also covers [Principal](principal.md), [Interest rate](interest-rate.md), [Simple interest](simple-interest.md) and [Compound interest](compound-interest.md).
