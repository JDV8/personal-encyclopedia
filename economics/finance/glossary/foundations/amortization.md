# Amortization

Paying off a loan through regular payments that cover both interest and part of the principal, so the balance reaches zero at the end. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Annuities and perpetuities](../../01-foundations/06-annuities-and-perpetuities.md). That lesson also covers [Annuity](annuity.md), [Perpetuity](perpetuity.md) and [Consol](consol.md).
