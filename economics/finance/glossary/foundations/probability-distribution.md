# Probability distribution

A description of all possible outcomes of an uncertain variable and how likely each is. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Probability and expected value](../../01-foundations/08-probability-and-expected-value.md). That lesson also covers [Random variable](random-variable.md) and [Expected value](expected-value.md).
