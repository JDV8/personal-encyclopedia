# Discount rate

The rate used to convert future cash flows to [present value](present-value.md). It reflects the time value of money and the risk of the cash flows. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Present value and discounting](../../01-foundations/05-present-value-and-discounting.md). That lesson also covers [Discounting](discounting.md), [Present value (PV)](present-value.md), [Net present value (NPV)](net-present-value.md) and [Internal rate of return (IRR)](internal-rate-of-return.md).
