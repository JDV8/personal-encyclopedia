# Normal distribution

The symmetric bell-shaped distribution fully described by its mean and standard deviation; often assumed for returns, though real returns have [fat tails](fat-tails.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Variance and standard deviation](../../01-foundations/09-variance-and-standard-deviation.md). That lesson also covers [Fat tails](fat-tails.md), [Variance](variance.md), [Standard deviation](standard-deviation.md) and [Volatility](volatility.md).
