# Systematic risk

Risk that affects the whole market and cannot be diversified away, such as recessions or interest rate shifts. Also called market risk. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Correlation and diversification](../../01-foundations/11-correlation-and-diversification.md). That lesson also covers [Idiosyncratic risk](idiosyncratic-risk.md), [Correlation](correlation.md) and [Diversification](diversification.md).
