# Fat tails

A property of return distributions in which extreme outcomes occur much more often than a normal distribution predicts, such as market crashes. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Variance and standard deviation](../../01-foundations/09-variance-and-standard-deviation.md). That lesson also covers [Variance](variance.md), [Standard deviation](standard-deviation.md), [Volatility](volatility.md) and [Normal distribution](normal-distribution.md).
