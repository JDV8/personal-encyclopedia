# Opportunity cost

The value of the best alternative you give up when making a choice. Core to deciding whether an investment is worth its return. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The time value of money](../../01-foundations/04-time-value-of-money.md). That lesson also covers [Time value of money](time-value-of-money.md) and [Future value](future-value.md).
