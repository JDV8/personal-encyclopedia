# Time value of money

The principle that a dollar today is worth more than a dollar later because it can earn interest and because of inflation and risk. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The time value of money](../../01-foundations/04-time-value-of-money.md). That lesson also covers [Future value](future-value.md) and [Opportunity cost](opportunity-cost.md).
