# Compound interest

Interest earned on both the original principal and on previously earned interest. Growth is exponential: P(1 + r)^n. Often called the engine of long-term investing. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Simple and compound interest](../../01-foundations/03-simple-and-compound-interest.md). That lesson also covers [Effective annual rate (EAR)](effective-annual-rate.md), [Rule of 72](rule-of-72.md), [Principal](principal.md) and [Interest rate](interest-rate.md).
