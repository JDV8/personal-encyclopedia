# Risk aversion

The preference for a certain outcome over a gamble with the same expected value. Risk-averse investors demand a [risk premium](risk-premium.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Risk and return](../../01-foundations/10-risk-and-return.md). That lesson also covers [Risk premium](risk-premium.md), [Risk-free rate](risk-free-rate.md), [Equity risk premium](equity-risk-premium.md) and [Risk](risk.md).
