# Discounting

Converting a future amount to its equivalent today by dividing by (1 + r)^n. The reverse of compounding. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Present value and discounting](../../01-foundations/05-present-value-and-discounting.md). That lesson also covers [Present value (PV)](present-value.md), [Net present value (NPV)](net-present-value.md), [Internal rate of return (IRR)](internal-rate-of-return.md) and [Discount rate](discount-rate.md).
