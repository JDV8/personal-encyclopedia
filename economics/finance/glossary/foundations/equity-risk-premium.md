# Equity risk premium

The extra return stocks are expected to earn over the risk-free rate. Historical estimates are a few percentage points but vary by period. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Risk and return](../../01-foundations/10-risk-and-return.md). That lesson also covers [Risk](risk.md), [Risk aversion](risk-aversion.md), [Risk premium](risk-premium.md) and [Risk-free rate](risk-free-rate.md).
