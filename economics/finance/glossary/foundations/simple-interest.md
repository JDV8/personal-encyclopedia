# Simple interest

Interest calculated only on the original principal, so it grows linearly: principal × rate × time. Contrast with [compound interest](compound-interest.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Simple and compound interest](../../01-foundations/03-simple-and-compound-interest.md). That lesson also covers [Compound interest](compound-interest.md), [Effective annual rate (EAR)](effective-annual-rate.md), [Rule of 72](rule-of-72.md) and [Principal](principal.md).
