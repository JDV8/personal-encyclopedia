# Expected value

The probability-weighted average of all possible outcomes. A fair bet has an expected value equal to its price. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Probability and expected value](../../01-foundations/08-probability-and-expected-value.md). That lesson also covers [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md) and [Random variable](random-variable.md).
