# Risk-free rate

The return on an investment with no default risk, usually proxied by short-term government treasury bills. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Risk and return](../../01-foundations/10-risk-and-return.md). That lesson also covers [Equity risk premium](equity-risk-premium.md), [Risk](risk.md), [Risk aversion](risk-aversion.md) and [Risk premium](risk-premium.md).
