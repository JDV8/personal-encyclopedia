# Consol

A perpetual bond that pays interest forever without repaying principal, issued historically by the British government. A real-world perpetuity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Annuities and perpetuities](../../01-foundations/06-annuities-and-perpetuities.md). That lesson also covers [Amortization](amortization.md), [Annuity](annuity.md) and [Perpetuity](perpetuity.md).
