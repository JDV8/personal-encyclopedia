# Consumer Price Index (CPI)

A measure of the average change in prices paid by consumers for a fixed basket of goods and services; the usual gauge of inflation. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Inflation and real returns](../../01-foundations/07-inflation-and-real-returns.md). That lesson also covers [Hyperinflation](hyperinflation.md), [Inflation](inflation.md), [Real return](real-return.md) and [Nominal return](nominal-return.md).
