# Random variable

A quantity whose value is determined by chance, such as next year's stock return. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Probability and expected value](../../01-foundations/08-probability-and-expected-value.md). That lesson also covers [Expected value](expected-value.md) and [Probability distribution](probability-distribution.md).
