# Inflation

A general, sustained rise in prices that reduces the purchasing power of money. In Canada the Bank of Canada targets 2%. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Inflation and real returns](../../01-foundations/07-inflation-and-real-returns.md). That lesson also covers [Real return](real-return.md), [Nominal return](nominal-return.md), [Consumer Price Index (CPI)](cpi.md) and [Hyperinflation](hyperinflation.md).
