# Risk

In finance, the uncertainty of outcomes, especially the possibility that actual returns differ from expected returns and include losses. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Risk and return](../../01-foundations/10-risk-and-return.md). That lesson also covers [Risk aversion](risk-aversion.md), [Risk premium](risk-premium.md), [Risk-free rate](risk-free-rate.md) and [Equity risk premium](equity-risk-premium.md).
