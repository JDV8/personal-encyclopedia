# Sovereign default

A government's failure to pay its debts in full or on time. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Government bonds](../../03-securities/02-bonds/07-government-bonds.md). That lesson also covers [National debt](national-debt.md), [Canada Savings Bond](canada-savings-bond.md), [Treasury bill](treasury-bill.md) and [Government bond](government-bond.md).
