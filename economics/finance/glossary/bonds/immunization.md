# Immunization

Matching the duration of assets and liabilities so that interest rate changes affect both equally. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Duration and convexity](../../03-securities/02-bonds/05-duration-and-convexity.md). That lesson also covers [Duration risk](duration-risk.md), [Macaulay duration](macaulay-duration.md), [Modified duration](modified-duration.md) and [Negative convexity](negative-convexity.md).
