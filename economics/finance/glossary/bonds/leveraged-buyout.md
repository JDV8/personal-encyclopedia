# Leveraged buyout (LBO)

Acquiring a company mostly with borrowed money secured on the target's own assets and cash flows. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Corporate bonds and high yield](../../03-securities/02-bonds/08-corporate-bonds-and-high-yield.md). That lesson also covers [Junk bond](junk-bond.md), [High-yield bond](high-yield-bond.md), [Corporate bond](corporate-bond.md) and [Indenture](indenture.md).
