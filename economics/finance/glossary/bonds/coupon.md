# Coupon

The periodic interest payment on a bond, set as a percentage of face value. A 5% coupon on 1,000 face value pays 50 a year. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What a bond is](../../03-securities/02-bonds/01-what-is-a-bond.md). That lesson also covers [Face value](face-value.md), [Par](par.md), [Maturity](maturity.md) and [Bond](bond.md).
