# Negative convexity

A bond price curve that bends the wrong way, as with callable bonds, so prices rise less than they fall. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Duration and convexity](../../03-securities/02-bonds/05-duration-and-convexity.md). That lesson also covers [Duration](duration.md), [Convexity](convexity.md), [Immunization](immunization.md) and [Duration risk](duration-risk.md).
