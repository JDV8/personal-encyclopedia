# Recovery rate

The share of face value bondholders recover after a default; a 40% recovery means a 60% loss. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Credit risk and ratings](../../03-securities/02-bonds/06-credit-risk-and-ratings.md). That lesson also covers [Credit risk](credit-risk.md), [Credit rating](credit-rating.md), [Investment grade](investment-grade.md) and [Fallen angel](fallen-angel.md).
