# Pull to par

The tendency of a bond's price to move toward face value as maturity approaches, if its yield is unchanged. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The price–yield relationship](../../03-securities/02-bonds/04-the-price-yield-relationship.md). That lesson also covers [Interest rate risk](interest-rate-risk.md) and [Price-yield relationship](price-yield-relationship.md).
