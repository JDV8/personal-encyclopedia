# Corporate bond

A bond issued by a company to raise debt financing, with yield above comparable government bonds to reflect credit risk. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Corporate bonds and high yield](../../03-securities/02-bonds/08-corporate-bonds-and-high-yield.md). That lesson also covers [Indenture](indenture.md), [Debenture](debenture.md), [Covenant](covenant.md) and [Subordinated debt](subordinated-debt.md).
