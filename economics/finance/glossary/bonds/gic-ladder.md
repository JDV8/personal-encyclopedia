# GIC ladder

Splitting money across GICs of different maturities so some mature every year, balancing rate and access. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Inflation-linked bonds, zero-coupon bonds and GICs](../../03-securities/02-bonds/10-inflation-linked-zero-coupon-and-gics.md). That lesson also covers [Inflation-linked bond](inflation-linked-bond.md), [Real Return Bond](real-return-bond.md), [Breakeven inflation](breakeven-inflation.md) and [Zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md).
