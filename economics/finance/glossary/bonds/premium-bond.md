# Premium bond

A bond trading above face value, which happens when its coupon exceeds the current market yield. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Pricing a bond](../../03-securities/02-bonds/02-pricing-a-bond.md). That lesson also covers [Discount bond](discount-bond.md), [Accrued interest](accrued-interest.md) and [Bond price](bond-price.md).
