# Canada Savings Bond

A cashable government savings product sold from 1946 until its discontinuation in 2017. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Government bonds](../../03-securities/02-bonds/07-government-bonds.md). That lesson also covers [Treasury bill](treasury-bill.md), [Government bond](government-bond.md), [Canada Mortgage Bond](canada-mortgage-bond.md) and [Sovereign debt](sovereign-debt.md).
