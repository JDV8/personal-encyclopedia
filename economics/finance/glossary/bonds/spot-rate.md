# Spot rate

The yield on a zero-coupon bond for a given maturity, used to discount single cash flows. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The yield curve](../../03-securities/02-bonds/09-the-yield-curve.md). That lesson also covers [Term structure of interest rates](term-structure.md), [Yield curve](yield-curve.md), [Inverted yield curve](inverted-yield-curve.md) and [Term premium](term-premium.md).
