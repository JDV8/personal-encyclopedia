# Discount bond

A bond trading below face value, which happens when its coupon is below the current market yield. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Pricing a bond](../../03-securities/02-bonds/02-pricing-a-bond.md). That lesson also covers [Accrued interest](accrued-interest.md), [Bond price](bond-price.md) and [Premium bond](premium-bond.md).
