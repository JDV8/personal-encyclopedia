# Guaranteed investment certificate (GIC)

A deposit that pays a fixed or variable rate for a set term; typically CDIC-insured up to limits at member institutions. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Inflation-linked bonds, zero-coupon bonds and GICs](../../03-securities/02-bonds/10-inflation-linked-zero-coupon-and-gics.md). That lesson also covers [GIC ladder](gic-ladder.md), [Inflation-linked bond](inflation-linked-bond.md), [Real Return Bond](real-return-bond.md) and [Breakeven inflation](breakeven-inflation.md).
