# Debenture

An unsecured bond backed only by the issuer's general creditworthiness, not specific collateral. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Corporate bonds and high yield](../../03-securities/02-bonds/08-corporate-bonds-and-high-yield.md). That lesson also covers [Covenant](covenant.md), [Subordinated debt](subordinated-debt.md), [Floating-rate note](floating-rate-note.md) and [Call risk](call-risk.md).
