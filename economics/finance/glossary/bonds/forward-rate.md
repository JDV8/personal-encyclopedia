# Forward rate

The interest rate for a future period implied by today's yield curve. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The yield curve](../../03-securities/02-bonds/09-the-yield-curve.md). That lesson also covers [Spot rate](spot-rate.md), [Term structure of interest rates](term-structure.md), [Yield curve](yield-curve.md) and [Inverted yield curve](inverted-yield-curve.md).
