# National debt

The total amount a government owes from past deficits. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Government bonds](../../03-securities/02-bonds/07-government-bonds.md). That lesson also covers [Canada Savings Bond](canada-savings-bond.md), [Treasury bill](treasury-bill.md), [Government bond](government-bond.md) and [Canada Mortgage Bond](canada-mortgage-bond.md).
