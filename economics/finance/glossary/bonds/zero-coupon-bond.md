# Zero-coupon bond

A bond that pays no coupons and is sold at a discount to face value; all the return comes at maturity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Inflation-linked bonds, zero-coupon bonds and GICs](../../03-securities/02-bonds/10-inflation-linked-zero-coupon-and-gics.md). That lesson also covers [Strip bond](strip-bond.md), [Guaranteed investment certificate (GIC)](gic.md), [GIC ladder](gic-ladder.md) and [Inflation-linked bond](inflation-linked-bond.md).
