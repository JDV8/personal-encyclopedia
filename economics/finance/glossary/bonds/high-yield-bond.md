# High-yield bond

A bond rated below investment grade that pays higher yields to compensate for greater default risk; also called a junk bond. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Corporate bonds and high yield](../../03-securities/02-bonds/08-corporate-bonds-and-high-yield.md). That lesson also covers [Corporate bond](corporate-bond.md), [Indenture](indenture.md), [Debenture](debenture.md) and [Covenant](covenant.md).
