# Subordinated debt

Debt that ranks below senior debt in repayment if the issuer fails, and therefore pays a higher yield. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Corporate bonds and high yield](../../03-securities/02-bonds/08-corporate-bonds-and-high-yield.md). That lesson also covers [Floating-rate note](floating-rate-note.md), [Call risk](call-risk.md), [Leveraged buyout (LBO)](leveraged-buyout.md) and [Junk bond](junk-bond.md).
