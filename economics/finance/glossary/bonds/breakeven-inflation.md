# Breakeven inflation

The inflation rate at which a nominal bond and an inflation-linked bond of the same maturity give the same return. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Inflation-linked bonds, zero-coupon bonds and GICs](../../03-securities/02-bonds/10-inflation-linked-zero-coupon-and-gics.md). That lesson also covers [Zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md), [Strip bond](strip-bond.md), [Guaranteed investment certificate (GIC)](gic.md) and [GIC ladder](gic-ladder.md).
