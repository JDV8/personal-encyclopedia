# Current yield

A bond's annual coupon divided by its price; ignores gains or losses at maturity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Yield to maturity](../../03-securities/02-bonds/03-yield-to-maturity.md). That lesson also covers [Yield to call](yield-to-call.md), [Basis point](basis-point.md), [Reinvestment risk](reinvestment-risk.md) and [Yield to maturity (YTM)](yield-to-maturity.md).
