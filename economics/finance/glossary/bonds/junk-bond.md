# Junk bond

A bond rated below investment grade (below BBB− or Baa3). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Corporate bonds and high yield](../../03-securities/02-bonds/08-corporate-bonds-and-high-yield.md). That lesson also covers [High-yield bond](high-yield-bond.md), [Corporate bond](corporate-bond.md), [Indenture](indenture.md) and [Debenture](debenture.md).
