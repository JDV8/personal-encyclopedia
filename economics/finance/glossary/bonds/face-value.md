# Face value

The amount a bond repays at maturity, also called par value; usually 1,000 for corporate bonds. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What a bond is](../../03-securities/02-bonds/01-what-is-a-bond.md). That lesson also covers [Par](par.md), [Maturity](maturity.md), [Bond](bond.md) and [Coupon](coupon.md).
