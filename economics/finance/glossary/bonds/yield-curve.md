# Yield curve

A graph of yields on bonds of the same quality across maturities. Also called the term structure of interest rates; normally upward sloping. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The yield curve](../../03-securities/02-bonds/09-the-yield-curve.md). That lesson also covers [Inverted yield curve](inverted-yield-curve.md), [Term premium](term-premium.md), [Forward rate](forward-rate.md) and [Spot rate](spot-rate.md).
