# Credit spread

The extra yield a risky bond pays over a comparable government bond; it widens when perceived risk rises. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Credit risk and ratings](../../03-securities/02-bonds/06-credit-risk-and-ratings.md). That lesson also covers [Recovery rate](recovery-rate.md), [Credit risk](credit-risk.md), [Credit rating](credit-rating.md) and [Investment grade](investment-grade.md).
