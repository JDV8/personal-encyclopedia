# Strip bond

A zero-coupon bond created by separating the coupons and principal of a regular bond. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Inflation-linked bonds, zero-coupon bonds and GICs](../../03-securities/02-bonds/10-inflation-linked-zero-coupon-and-gics.md). That lesson also covers [Guaranteed investment certificate (GIC)](gic.md), [GIC ladder](gic-ladder.md), [Inflation-linked bond](inflation-linked-bond.md) and [Real Return Bond](real-return-bond.md).
