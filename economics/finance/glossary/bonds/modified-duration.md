# Modified duration

Macaulay duration divided by one plus the yield per period; the percentage price change for a 1% yield change. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Duration and convexity](../../03-securities/02-bonds/05-duration-and-convexity.md). That lesson also covers [Negative convexity](negative-convexity.md), [Duration](duration.md), [Convexity](convexity.md) and [Immunization](immunization.md).
