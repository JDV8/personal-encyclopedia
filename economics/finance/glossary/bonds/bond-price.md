# Bond price

The present value of a bond's coupons and face value discounted at the market yield. It moves opposite to yields. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Pricing a bond](../../03-securities/02-bonds/02-pricing-a-bond.md). That lesson also covers [Premium bond](premium-bond.md), [Discount bond](discount-bond.md) and [Accrued interest](accrued-interest.md).
