# Government bond

A bond issued by a national government, considered to have very low credit risk when it borrows in its own currency. Also called sovereign debt. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Government bonds](../../03-securities/02-bonds/07-government-bonds.md). That lesson also covers [Canada Mortgage Bond](canada-mortgage-bond.md), [Sovereign debt](sovereign-debt.md), [Sovereign default](sovereign-default.md) and [National debt](national-debt.md).
