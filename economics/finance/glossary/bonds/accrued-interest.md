# Accrued interest

Interest earned on a bond since its last coupon date; a buyer pays it to the seller on top of the quoted price. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Pricing a bond](../../03-securities/02-bonds/02-pricing-a-bond.md). That lesson also covers [Bond price](bond-price.md), [Premium bond](premium-bond.md) and [Discount bond](discount-bond.md).
