# Floating-rate note

A bond whose coupon resets periodically with a benchmark rate, so its price changes little when rates move. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Corporate bonds and high yield](../../03-securities/02-bonds/08-corporate-bonds-and-high-yield.md). That lesson also covers [Call risk](call-risk.md), [Leveraged buyout (LBO)](leveraged-buyout.md), [Junk bond](junk-bond.md) and [High-yield bond](high-yield-bond.md).
