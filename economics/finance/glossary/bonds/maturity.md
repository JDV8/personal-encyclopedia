# Maturity

The date on which the principal of a loan or bond is due to be repaid; also the length of time remaining until that date. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What a bond is](../../03-securities/02-bonds/01-what-is-a-bond.md). That lesson also covers [Bond](bond.md), [Coupon](coupon.md), [Face value](face-value.md) and [Par](par.md).
