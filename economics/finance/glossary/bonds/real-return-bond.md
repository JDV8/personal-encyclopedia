# Real Return Bond

A Government of Canada inflation-indexed bond; the government stopped issuing them in November 2022. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Inflation-linked bonds, zero-coupon bonds and GICs](../../03-securities/02-bonds/10-inflation-linked-zero-coupon-and-gics.md). That lesson also covers [Breakeven inflation](breakeven-inflation.md), [Zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md), [Strip bond](strip-bond.md) and [Guaranteed investment certificate (GIC)](gic.md).
