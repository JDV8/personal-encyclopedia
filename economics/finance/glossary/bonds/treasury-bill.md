# Treasury bill

A short-term government discount security, maturing in a year or less; used as the benchmark risk-free rate. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Government bonds](../../03-securities/02-bonds/07-government-bonds.md). That lesson also covers [Government bond](government-bond.md), [Canada Mortgage Bond](canada-mortgage-bond.md), [Sovereign debt](sovereign-debt.md) and [Sovereign default](sovereign-default.md).
