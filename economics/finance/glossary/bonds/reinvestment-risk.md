# Reinvestment risk

The risk that coupons or proceeds must be reinvested at lower rates than the original yield. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Yield to maturity](../../03-securities/02-bonds/03-yield-to-maturity.md). That lesson also covers [Yield to maturity (YTM)](yield-to-maturity.md), [Current yield](current-yield.md), [Yield to call](yield-to-call.md) and [Basis point](basis-point.md).
