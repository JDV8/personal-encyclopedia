# Inverted yield curve

A yield curve in which short-term yields exceed long-term yields, which has often preceded recessions. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The yield curve](../../03-securities/02-bonds/09-the-yield-curve.md). That lesson also covers [Term premium](term-premium.md), [Forward rate](forward-rate.md), [Spot rate](spot-rate.md) and [Term structure of interest rates](term-structure.md).
