# Macaulay duration

The weighted average time until a bond's cash flows are received. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Duration and convexity](../../03-securities/02-bonds/05-duration-and-convexity.md). That lesson also covers [Modified duration](modified-duration.md), [Negative convexity](negative-convexity.md), [Duration](duration.md) and [Convexity](convexity.md).
