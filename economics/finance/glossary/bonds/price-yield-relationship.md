# Price-yield relationship

Bond prices and yields move in opposite directions in a curved, convex relationship. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The price–yield relationship](../../03-securities/02-bonds/04-the-price-yield-relationship.md). That lesson also covers [Pull to par](pull-to-par.md) and [Interest rate risk](interest-rate-risk.md).
