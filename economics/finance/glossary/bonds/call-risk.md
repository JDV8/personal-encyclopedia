# Call risk

The risk that an issuer redeems a callable bond early, typically when rates have fallen, forcing reinvestment at lower yields. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Corporate bonds and high yield](../../03-securities/02-bonds/08-corporate-bonds-and-high-yield.md). That lesson also covers [Leveraged buyout (LBO)](leveraged-buyout.md), [Junk bond](junk-bond.md), [High-yield bond](high-yield-bond.md) and [Corporate bond](corporate-bond.md).
