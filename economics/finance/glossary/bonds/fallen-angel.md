# Fallen angel

A bond originally issued as investment grade that has been downgraded to high yield. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Credit risk and ratings](../../03-securities/02-bonds/06-credit-risk-and-ratings.md). That lesson also covers [Credit spread](credit-spread.md), [Recovery rate](recovery-rate.md), [Credit risk](credit-risk.md) and [Credit rating](credit-rating.md).
