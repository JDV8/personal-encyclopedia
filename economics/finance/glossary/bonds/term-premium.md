# Term premium

The extra yield investors demand for holding a long-term bond instead of rolling short-term ones. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The yield curve](../../03-securities/02-bonds/09-the-yield-curve.md). That lesson also covers [Forward rate](forward-rate.md), [Spot rate](spot-rate.md), [Term structure of interest rates](term-structure.md) and [Yield curve](yield-curve.md).
