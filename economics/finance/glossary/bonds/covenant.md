# Covenant

A promise in a debt contract limiting what the borrower may do, such as taking on more debt, to protect lenders. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Corporate bonds and high yield](../../03-securities/02-bonds/08-corporate-bonds-and-high-yield.md). That lesson also covers [Subordinated debt](subordinated-debt.md), [Floating-rate note](floating-rate-note.md), [Call risk](call-risk.md) and [Leveraged buyout (LBO)](leveraged-buyout.md).
