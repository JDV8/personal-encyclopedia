# Credit rating

An agency's opinion of a borrower's creditworthiness, such as AAA to D, which strongly influences the yield it must pay. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Credit risk and ratings](../../03-securities/02-bonds/06-credit-risk-and-ratings.md). That lesson also covers [Investment grade](investment-grade.md), [Fallen angel](fallen-angel.md), [Credit spread](credit-spread.md) and [Recovery rate](recovery-rate.md).
