# Indenture

The legal contract between a bond issuer and bondholders, setting out terms and covenants. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Corporate bonds and high yield](../../03-securities/02-bonds/08-corporate-bonds-and-high-yield.md). That lesson also covers [Debenture](debenture.md), [Covenant](covenant.md), [Subordinated debt](subordinated-debt.md) and [Floating-rate note](floating-rate-note.md).
