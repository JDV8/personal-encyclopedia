# Canada Mortgage Bond

A bond issued through Canada Housing Trust and guaranteed by the federal government to fund insured mortgages. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Government bonds](../../03-securities/02-bonds/07-government-bonds.md). That lesson also covers [Sovereign debt](sovereign-debt.md), [Sovereign default](sovereign-default.md), [National debt](national-debt.md) and [Canada Savings Bond](canada-savings-bond.md).
