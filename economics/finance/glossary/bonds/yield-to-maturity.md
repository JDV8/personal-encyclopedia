# Yield to maturity (YTM)

The single discount rate that makes a bond's present value equal its market price: the annualised return if held to maturity with coupons reinvested at the same rate. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Yield to maturity](../../03-securities/02-bonds/03-yield-to-maturity.md). That lesson also covers [Current yield](current-yield.md), [Yield to call](yield-to-call.md), [Basis point](basis-point.md) and [Reinvestment risk](reinvestment-risk.md).
