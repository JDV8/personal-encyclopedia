# Duration risk

The sensitivity of a bond's price to interest-rate changes, measured by duration. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Duration and convexity](../../03-securities/02-bonds/05-duration-and-convexity.md). That lesson also covers [Macaulay duration](macaulay-duration.md), [Modified duration](modified-duration.md), [Negative convexity](negative-convexity.md) and [Duration](duration.md).
