# Basis point

One hundredth of a percentage point (0.01%). A move from 4.00% to 4.25% is 25 basis points. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Yield to maturity](../../03-securities/02-bonds/03-yield-to-maturity.md). That lesson also covers [Reinvestment risk](reinvestment-risk.md), [Yield to maturity (YTM)](yield-to-maturity.md), [Current yield](current-yield.md) and [Yield to call](yield-to-call.md).
