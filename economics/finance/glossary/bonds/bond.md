# Bond

A tradable loan: the issuer promises to pay [coupons](coupon.md) and repay [face value](face-value.md) at [maturity](maturity.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What a bond is](../../03-securities/02-bonds/01-what-is-a-bond.md). That lesson also covers [Coupon](coupon.md), [Face value](face-value.md), [Par](par.md) and [Maturity](maturity.md).
