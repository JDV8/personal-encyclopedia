# Convexity

The curvature of the price-yield relationship; it refines the duration estimate and generally favours bondholders, except for callable bonds with negative convexity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Duration and convexity](../../03-securities/02-bonds/05-duration-and-convexity.md). That lesson also covers [Immunization](immunization.md), [Duration risk](duration-risk.md), [Macaulay duration](macaulay-duration.md) and [Modified duration](modified-duration.md).
