# Par

The face value of a bond, repaid at maturity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [What a bond is](../../03-securities/02-bonds/01-what-is-a-bond.md). That lesson also covers [Maturity](maturity.md), [Bond](bond.md), [Coupon](coupon.md) and [Face value](face-value.md).
