# Duration

A measure of a bond's price sensitivity to interest rates and its weighted-average time to receive cash flows. Macaulay duration is that average time in years; modified duration estimates the percentage price change for a 1% yield change. A bond with duration 7 loses about 7% if yields rise 1 point. Also called interest-rate risk. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Duration and convexity](../../03-securities/02-bonds/05-duration-and-convexity.md). That lesson also covers [Convexity](convexity.md), [Immunization](immunization.md), [Duration risk](duration-risk.md) and [Macaulay duration](macaulay-duration.md).
