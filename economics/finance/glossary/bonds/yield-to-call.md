# Yield to call

The yield calculated assuming a callable bond is redeemed at its first call date rather than at maturity. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Yield to maturity](../../03-securities/02-bonds/03-yield-to-maturity.md). That lesson also covers [Basis point](basis-point.md), [Reinvestment risk](reinvestment-risk.md), [Yield to maturity (YTM)](yield-to-maturity.md) and [Current yield](current-yield.md).
