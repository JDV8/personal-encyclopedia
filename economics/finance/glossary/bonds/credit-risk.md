# Credit risk

The risk that a borrower fails to pay interest or principal as promised. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Credit risk and ratings](../../03-securities/02-bonds/06-credit-risk-and-ratings.md). That lesson also covers [Credit rating](credit-rating.md), [Investment grade](investment-grade.md), [Fallen angel](fallen-angel.md) and [Credit spread](credit-spread.md).
