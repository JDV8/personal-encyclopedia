# Interest rate risk

The risk that rising rates will cut the market value of existing bonds. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The price–yield relationship](../../03-securities/02-bonds/04-the-price-yield-relationship.md). That lesson also covers [Price-yield relationship](price-yield-relationship.md) and [Pull to par](pull-to-par.md).
