# Investment grade

Bonds rated BBB− or higher (S&P scale) and considered low default risk. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Credit risk and ratings](../../03-securities/02-bonds/06-credit-risk-and-ratings.md). That lesson also covers [Fallen angel](fallen-angel.md), [Credit spread](credit-spread.md), [Recovery rate](recovery-rate.md) and [Credit risk](credit-risk.md).
