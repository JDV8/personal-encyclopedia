# Inflation-linked bond

A bond whose principal and coupons adjust with inflation, protecting real returns. Canada's Real Return Bonds were an example, no longer issued since 2022. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Inflation-linked bonds, zero-coupon bonds and GICs](../../03-securities/02-bonds/10-inflation-linked-zero-coupon-and-gics.md). That lesson also covers [Real Return Bond](real-return-bond.md), [Breakeven inflation](breakeven-inflation.md), [Zero-coupon bond](zero-coupon-bond.md) and [Strip bond](strip-bond.md).
