# Pump and dump

Promoting a cheap stock with false hype to raise its price and then selling to the buyers it attracted. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Why markets are regulated](../../05-regulation/01-why-markets-are-regulated.md). That lesson also covers [Information asymmetry](information-asymmetry.md), [Disclosure](disclosure.md), [Moral hazard](moral-hazard.md) and [Systemic risk](systemic-risk.md).
