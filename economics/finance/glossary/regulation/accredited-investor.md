# Accredited investor

An investor meeting income or wealth tests who may buy certain securities without a prospectus. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Securities regulation in Canada](../../05-regulation/03-securities-regulation-in-canada.md). That lesson also covers [Know your client](know-your-client.md), [Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA)](csa.md), [Ontario Securities Commission (OSC)](osc.md) and [National instrument](national-instrument.md).
