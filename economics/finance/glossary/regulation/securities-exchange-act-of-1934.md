# Securities Exchange Act of 1934

The US law that created the SEC and regulates secondary markets, brokers and ongoing reporting. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The SEC and US securities law](../../05-regulation/02-the-sec-and-us-securities-law.md). That lesson also covers [Howey test](howey-test.md), [EDGAR](edgar.md), [Sarbanes–Oxley Act](sarbanes-oxley.md) and [Dodd–Frank Act](dodd-frank.md).
