# Material information

Information a reasonable investor would consider important or that would affect a security's price. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Insider trading, disclosure and fraud](../../05-regulation/05-insider-trading-disclosure-and-fraud.md). That lesson also covers [Tipping](tipping.md), [SEDI](sedi.md), [Accounting fraud](accounting-fraud.md) and [Market manipulation](market-manipulation.md).
