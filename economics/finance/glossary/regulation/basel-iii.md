# Basel III

In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Central banks and monetary policy](../../05-regulation/06-central-banks-and-monetary-policy.md). That lesson also covers [Monetary policy](monetary-policy.md), [Overnight rate](overnight-rate.md), [Quantitative tightening (QT)](quantitative-tightening.md) and [Rate hike](rate-hike.md).
