# Tipping

Passing material non-public information to someone who trades on it. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Insider trading, disclosure and fraud](../../05-regulation/05-insider-trading-disclosure-and-fraud.md). That lesson also covers [SEDI](sedi.md), [Accounting fraud](accounting-fraud.md), [Market manipulation](market-manipulation.md) and [Bre-X](bre-x.md).
