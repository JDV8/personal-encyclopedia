# Rate hike

An increase in the central bank's policy rate, usually to slow inflation, which tends to lower bond prices and raise borrowing costs. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Central banks and monetary policy](../../05-regulation/06-central-banks-and-monetary-policy.md). That lesson also covers [Central bank](central-bank.md), [Policy rate](policy-rate.md), [Lender of last resort](lender-of-last-resort.md) and [Quantitative easing (QE)](quantitative-easing.md).
