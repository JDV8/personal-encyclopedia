# Disclosure

Mandatory publication of material information so investors can judge securities for themselves. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Why markets are regulated](../../05-regulation/01-why-markets-are-regulated.md). That lesson also covers [Moral hazard](moral-hazard.md), [Systemic risk](systemic-risk.md), [Regulatory capture](regulatory-capture.md) and [Pump and dump](pump-and-dump.md).
