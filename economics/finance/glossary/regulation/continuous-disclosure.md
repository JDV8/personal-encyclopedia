# Continuous disclosure

The ongoing obligation of public companies to file financial statements and prompt reports of material changes. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Securities regulation in Canada](../../05-regulation/03-securities-regulation-in-canada.md). That lesson also covers [Registration](registration.md), [Accredited investor](accredited-investor.md), [Know your client](know-your-client.md) and [Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA)](csa.md).
