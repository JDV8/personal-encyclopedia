# Securities Act of 1933

The US law requiring registration and a prospectus for public offerings of securities. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The SEC and US securities law](../../05-regulation/02-the-sec-and-us-securities-law.md). That lesson also covers [Securities Exchange Act of 1934](securities-exchange-act-of-1934.md), [Howey test](howey-test.md), [EDGAR](edgar.md) and [Sarbanes–Oxley Act](sarbanes-oxley.md).
