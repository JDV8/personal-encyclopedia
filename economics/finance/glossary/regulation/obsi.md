# OBSI

The Ombudsman for Banking Services and Investments, a free independent complaint service. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [CIRO and investor protection](../../05-regulation/04-ciro-and-investor-protection.md). That lesson also covers [CIRO](ciro.md), [IIROC](iiroc.md), [Self-regulatory organisation](self-regulatory-organization.md) and [CIPF](cipf.md).
