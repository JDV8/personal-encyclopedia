# SEDI

The System for Electronic Disclosure by Insiders, where Canadian insider trades are reported. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Insider trading, disclosure and fraud](../../05-regulation/05-insider-trading-disclosure-and-fraud.md). That lesson also covers [Accounting fraud](accounting-fraud.md), [Market manipulation](market-manipulation.md), [Bre-X](bre-x.md) and [Affinity fraud](affinity-fraud.md).
