# Howey test

The US test for an investment contract: money invested in a common enterprise with profits expected from others' efforts. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The SEC and US securities law](../../05-regulation/02-the-sec-and-us-securities-law.md). That lesson also covers [EDGAR](edgar.md), [Sarbanes–Oxley Act](sarbanes-oxley.md), [Dodd–Frank Act](dodd-frank.md) and [FINRA](finra.md).
