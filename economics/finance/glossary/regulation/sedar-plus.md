# SEDAR+

Canada's national electronic filing system for prospectuses and disclosure documents. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Securities regulation in Canada](../../05-regulation/03-securities-regulation-in-canada.md). That lesson also covers [Continuous disclosure](continuous-disclosure.md), [Registration](registration.md), [Accredited investor](accredited-investor.md) and [Know your client](know-your-client.md).
