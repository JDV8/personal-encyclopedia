# Lender of last resort

The central bank's role of lending to solvent banks facing a run, ideally freely against good collateral at a penalty rate. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Central banks and monetary policy](../../05-regulation/06-central-banks-and-monetary-policy.md). That lesson also covers [Quantitative easing (QE)](quantitative-easing.md), [Inflation targeting](inflation-targeting.md), [Repo](repo.md) and [Basel III](basel-iii.md).
