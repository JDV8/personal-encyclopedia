# Insider trading

Trading on material non-public information in breach of a duty. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Insider trading, disclosure and fraud](../../05-regulation/05-insider-trading-disclosure-and-fraud.md). That lesson also covers [Material information](material-information.md), [Tipping](tipping.md), [SEDI](sedi.md) and [Accounting fraud](accounting-fraud.md).
