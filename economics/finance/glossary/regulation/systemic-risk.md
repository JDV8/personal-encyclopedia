# Systemic risk

The risk that the failure of one institution or market spreads and threatens the whole financial system. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Why markets are regulated](../../05-regulation/01-why-markets-are-regulated.md). That lesson also covers [Regulatory capture](regulatory-capture.md), [Pump and dump](pump-and-dump.md), [Information asymmetry](information-asymmetry.md) and [Disclosure](disclosure.md).
