# Self-regulatory organisation

An industry body that makes and enforces rules for its members under government oversight. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [CIRO and investor protection](../../05-regulation/04-ciro-and-investor-protection.md). That lesson also covers [CIPF](cipf.md), [CDIC](cdic.md), [OBSI](obsi.md) and [CIRO](ciro.md).
