# EDGAR

The SEC's free public database of company filings. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The SEC and US securities law](../../05-regulation/02-the-sec-and-us-securities-law.md). That lesson also covers [Sarbanes–Oxley Act](sarbanes-oxley.md), [Dodd–Frank Act](dodd-frank.md), [FINRA](finra.md) and [SEC](sec.md).
