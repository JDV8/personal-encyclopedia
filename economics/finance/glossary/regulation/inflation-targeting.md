# Inflation targeting

A policy framework in which the central bank commits to an inflation goal, 2% in Canada. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Central banks and monetary policy](../../05-regulation/06-central-banks-and-monetary-policy.md). That lesson also covers [Repo](repo.md), [Basel III](basel-iii.md), [Monetary policy](monetary-policy.md) and [Overnight rate](overnight-rate.md).
