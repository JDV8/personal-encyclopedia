# Accounting fraud

Deliberately misstating financial results, as at Enron and WorldCom. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Insider trading, disclosure and fraud](../../05-regulation/05-insider-trading-disclosure-and-fraud.md). That lesson also covers [Market manipulation](market-manipulation.md), [Bre-X](bre-x.md), [Affinity fraud](affinity-fraud.md) and [Ponzi scheme](ponzi-scheme.md).
