# CIPF

The Canadian Investor Protection Fund, covering missing client property if a CIRO-member dealer becomes insolvent. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [CIRO and investor protection](../../05-regulation/04-ciro-and-investor-protection.md). That lesson also covers [CDIC](cdic.md), [OBSI](obsi.md), [CIRO](ciro.md) and [IIROC](iiroc.md).
