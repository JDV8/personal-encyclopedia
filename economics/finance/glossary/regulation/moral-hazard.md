# Moral hazard

Taking more risk because someone else bears the cost, as when bailouts are expected. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Why markets are regulated](../../05-regulation/01-why-markets-are-regulated.md). That lesson also covers [Systemic risk](systemic-risk.md), [Regulatory capture](regulatory-capture.md), [Pump and dump](pump-and-dump.md) and [Information asymmetry](information-asymmetry.md).
