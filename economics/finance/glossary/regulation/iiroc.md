# IIROC

The former Investment Industry Regulatory Organization of Canada, amalgamated into CIRO on 1 January 2023. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [CIRO and investor protection](../../05-regulation/04-ciro-and-investor-protection.md). That lesson also covers [Self-regulatory organisation](self-regulatory-organization.md), [CIPF](cipf.md), [CDIC](cdic.md) and [OBSI](obsi.md).
