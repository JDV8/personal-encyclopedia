# Quantitative easing (QE)

A central bank buying bonds with newly created reserves to lower long-term yields; quantitative tightening is the reverse. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Central banks and monetary policy](../../05-regulation/06-central-banks-and-monetary-policy.md). That lesson also covers [Inflation targeting](inflation-targeting.md), [Repo](repo.md), [Basel III](basel-iii.md) and [Monetary policy](monetary-policy.md).
