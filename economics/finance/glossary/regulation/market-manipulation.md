# Market manipulation

Deliberately distorting prices or trading activity to mislead others. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Insider trading, disclosure and fraud](../../05-regulation/05-insider-trading-disclosure-and-fraud.md). That lesson also covers [Bre-X](bre-x.md), [Affinity fraud](affinity-fraud.md), [Ponzi scheme](ponzi-scheme.md) and [Insider trading](insider-trading.md).
