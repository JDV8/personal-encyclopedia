# Registration

The requirement that firms and individuals dealing or advising in securities be registered; checkable at aretheyregistered.ca. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Securities regulation in Canada](../../05-regulation/03-securities-regulation-in-canada.md). That lesson also covers [Accredited investor](accredited-investor.md), [Know your client](know-your-client.md), [Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA)](csa.md) and [Ontario Securities Commission (OSC)](osc.md).
