# Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA)

The umbrella body coordinating Canada's provincial and territorial securities regulators. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Securities regulation in Canada](../../05-regulation/03-securities-regulation-in-canada.md). That lesson also covers [Ontario Securities Commission (OSC)](osc.md), [National instrument](national-instrument.md), [SEDAR+](sedar-plus.md) and [Continuous disclosure](continuous-disclosure.md).
