# Overnight rate

The rate at which major financial institutions lend one another funds for one day; the Bank of Canada's target for it is its [policy rate](policy-rate.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Central banks and monetary policy](../../05-regulation/06-central-banks-and-monetary-policy.md). That lesson also covers [Quantitative tightening (QT)](quantitative-tightening.md), [Rate hike](rate-hike.md), [Central bank](central-bank.md) and [Policy rate](policy-rate.md).
