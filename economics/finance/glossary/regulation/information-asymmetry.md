# Information asymmetry

One party in a transaction knowing more than the other, as company managers know more than outside investors. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Why markets are regulated](../../05-regulation/01-why-markets-are-regulated.md). That lesson also covers [Disclosure](disclosure.md), [Moral hazard](moral-hazard.md), [Systemic risk](systemic-risk.md) and [Regulatory capture](regulatory-capture.md).
