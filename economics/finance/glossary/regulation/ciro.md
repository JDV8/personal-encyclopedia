# CIRO

The Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization, formed in 2023 from IIROC and the MFDA, overseeing investment dealers and mutual fund dealers. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [CIRO and investor protection](../../05-regulation/04-ciro-and-investor-protection.md). That lesson also covers [IIROC](iiroc.md), [Self-regulatory organisation](self-regulatory-organization.md), [CIPF](cipf.md) and [CDIC](cdic.md).
