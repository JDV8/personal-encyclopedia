# National instrument

A rule adopted uniformly by Canadian securities regulators, such as NI 31-103 on registration. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Securities regulation in Canada](../../05-regulation/03-securities-regulation-in-canada.md). That lesson also covers [SEDAR+](sedar-plus.md), [Continuous disclosure](continuous-disclosure.md), [Registration](registration.md) and [Accredited investor](accredited-investor.md).
