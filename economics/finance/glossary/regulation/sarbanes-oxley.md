# Sarbanes–Oxley Act

The 2002 US law strengthening audit and governance after Enron and WorldCom. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The SEC and US securities law](../../05-regulation/02-the-sec-and-us-securities-law.md). That lesson also covers [Dodd–Frank Act](dodd-frank.md), [FINRA](finra.md), [SEC](sec.md) and [Securities Act of 1933](securities-act-of-1933.md).
