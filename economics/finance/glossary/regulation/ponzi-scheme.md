# Ponzi scheme

A fraud that pays earlier investors from later investors' money, collapsing when recruitment slows. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Insider trading, disclosure and fraud](../../05-regulation/05-insider-trading-disclosure-and-fraud.md). That lesson also covers [Insider trading](insider-trading.md), [Material information](material-information.md), [Tipping](tipping.md) and [SEDI](sedi.md).
