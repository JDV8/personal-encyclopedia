# Quantitative tightening (QT)

A central bank shrinking its balance sheet by letting bonds mature without replacement or by selling them, reversing [quantitative easing](quantitative-easing.md). In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Central banks and monetary policy](../../05-regulation/06-central-banks-and-monetary-policy.md). That lesson also covers [Rate hike](rate-hike.md), [Central bank](central-bank.md), [Policy rate](policy-rate.md) and [Lender of last resort](lender-of-last-resort.md).
