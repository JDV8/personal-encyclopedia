# Policy rate

The central bank's key interest rate; in Canada the target for the overnight rate, which anchors short-term market rates. A rate hike raises it. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Central banks and monetary policy](../../05-regulation/06-central-banks-and-monetary-policy.md). That lesson also covers [Lender of last resort](lender-of-last-resort.md), [Quantitative easing (QE)](quantitative-easing.md), [Inflation targeting](inflation-targeting.md) and [Repo](repo.md).
