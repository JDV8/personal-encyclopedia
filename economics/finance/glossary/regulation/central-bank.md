# Central bank

The public institution issuing a country's currency and setting monetary policy, such as the Bank of Canada or the Federal Reserve. Monetary policy is its management of rates and money to meet inflation and employment goals. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Central banks and monetary policy](../../05-regulation/06-central-banks-and-monetary-policy.md). That lesson also covers [Policy rate](policy-rate.md), [Lender of last resort](lender-of-last-resort.md), [Quantitative easing (QE)](quantitative-easing.md) and [Inflation targeting](inflation-targeting.md).
