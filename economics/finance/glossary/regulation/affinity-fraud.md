# Affinity fraud

Fraud that exploits trust within a community or group. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Insider trading, disclosure and fraud](../../05-regulation/05-insider-trading-disclosure-and-fraud.md). That lesson also covers [Ponzi scheme](ponzi-scheme.md), [Insider trading](insider-trading.md), [Material information](material-information.md) and [Tipping](tipping.md).
