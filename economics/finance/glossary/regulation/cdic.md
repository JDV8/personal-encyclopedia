# CDIC

The Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation, insuring eligible deposits up to 100,000 per category at member institutions. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [CIRO and investor protection](../../05-regulation/04-ciro-and-investor-protection.md). That lesson also covers [OBSI](obsi.md), [CIRO](ciro.md), [IIROC](iiroc.md) and [Self-regulatory organisation](self-regulatory-organization.md).
