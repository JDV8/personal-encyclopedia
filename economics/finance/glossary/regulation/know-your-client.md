# Know your client

The duty of registrants to learn a client's finances, goals and risk tolerance and ensure suitability. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Securities regulation in Canada](../../05-regulation/03-securities-regulation-in-canada.md). That lesson also covers [Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA)](csa.md), [Ontario Securities Commission (OSC)](osc.md), [National instrument](national-instrument.md) and [SEDAR+](sedar-plus.md).
