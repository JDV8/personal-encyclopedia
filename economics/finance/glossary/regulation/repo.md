# Repo

A sale of securities with an agreement to repurchase them later at a higher price, effectively a collateralised loan. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Central banks and monetary policy](../../05-regulation/06-central-banks-and-monetary-policy.md). That lesson also covers [Basel III](basel-iii.md), [Monetary policy](monetary-policy.md), [Overnight rate](overnight-rate.md) and [Quantitative tightening (QT)](quantitative-tightening.md).
