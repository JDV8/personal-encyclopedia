# Regulatory capture

A regulator coming to serve the interests of the industry it oversees. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Why markets are regulated](../../05-regulation/01-why-markets-are-regulated.md). That lesson also covers [Pump and dump](pump-and-dump.md), [Information asymmetry](information-asymmetry.md), [Disclosure](disclosure.md) and [Moral hazard](moral-hazard.md).
