# Bre-X

A Calgary-based mining company whose 1997 Busang gold deposit claim proved fraudulent; Canada's best-known market fraud. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Insider trading, disclosure and fraud](../../05-regulation/05-insider-trading-disclosure-and-fraud.md). That lesson also covers [Affinity fraud](affinity-fraud.md), [Ponzi scheme](ponzi-scheme.md), [Insider trading](insider-trading.md) and [Material information](material-information.md).
