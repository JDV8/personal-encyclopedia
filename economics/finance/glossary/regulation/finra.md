# FINRA

The US self-regulatory organisation for broker-dealers. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The SEC and US securities law](../../05-regulation/02-the-sec-and-us-securities-law.md). That lesson also covers [SEC](sec.md), [Securities Act of 1933](securities-act-of-1933.md), [Securities Exchange Act of 1934](securities-exchange-act-of-1934.md) and [Howey test](howey-test.md).
