# Commodity

A standardised, interchangeable raw material such as oil, wheat or copper. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Commodities and gold](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/02-commodities-and-gold.md). That lesson also covers [Roll yield](roll-yield.md) and [Bullion](bullion.md).
