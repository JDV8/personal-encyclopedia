# Exchange rate

The price of one currency in terms of another. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Foreign exchange](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/03-foreign-exchange.md). That lesson also covers [FX spread](fx-spread.md), [Interest-rate parity](interest-rate-parity.md), [Purchasing-power parity](purchasing-power-parity.md) and [Carry trade](carry-trade.md).
