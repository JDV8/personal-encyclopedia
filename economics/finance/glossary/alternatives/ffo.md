# Funds from operations (FFO)

A REIT's net income plus depreciation, excluding property sale gains; the REIT counterpart of earnings. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Real estate and REITs](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/01-real-estate-and-reits.md). That lesson also covers [Capitalisation rate](cap-rate.md), [Net operating income](net-operating-income.md) and [REIT](reit.md).
