# Proof of work

A consensus method in which miners expend computing power to add blocks, as Bitcoin does. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Cryptoassets](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/04-cryptoassets.md). That lesson also covers [Proof of stake](proof-of-stake.md), [Smart contract](smart-contract.md), [Stablecoin](stablecoin.md) and [Private key](private-key.md).
