# Currency hedging

Using forwards or hedged funds to remove the effect of exchange-rate changes on foreign investments. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Foreign exchange](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/03-foreign-exchange.md). That lesson also covers [Exchange rate](exchange-rate.md), [FX spread](fx-spread.md), [Interest-rate parity](interest-rate-parity.md) and [Purchasing-power parity](purchasing-power-parity.md).
