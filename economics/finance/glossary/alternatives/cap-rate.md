# Capitalisation rate

Net operating income divided by property value; a real estate yield measure. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Real estate and REITs](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/01-real-estate-and-reits.md). That lesson also covers [Net operating income](net-operating-income.md), [REIT](reit.md) and [Funds from operations (FFO)](ffo.md).
