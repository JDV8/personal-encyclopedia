# Net operating income

A property's rental income less operating expenses, before financing and taxes. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Real estate and REITs](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/01-real-estate-and-reits.md). That lesson also covers [REIT](reit.md), [Funds from operations (FFO)](ffo.md) and [Capitalisation rate](cap-rate.md).
