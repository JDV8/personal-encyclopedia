# Blockchain

A shared ledger of transactions grouped in linked blocks, maintained by many computers without a central owner. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Cryptoassets](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/04-cryptoassets.md). That lesson also covers [Proof of work](proof-of-work.md), [Proof of stake](proof-of-stake.md), [Smart contract](smart-contract.md) and [Stablecoin](stablecoin.md).
