# Carry trade

Borrowing in a low-interest currency to invest in a higher-interest one. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Foreign exchange](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/03-foreign-exchange.md). That lesson also covers [Currency hedging](currency-hedging.md), [Exchange rate](exchange-rate.md), [FX spread](fx-spread.md) and [Interest-rate parity](interest-rate-parity.md).
