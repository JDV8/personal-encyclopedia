# Roll yield

The gain or loss on replacing expiring futures with later ones; positive in backwardation. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Commodities and gold](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/02-commodities-and-gold.md). That lesson also covers [Bullion](bullion.md) and [Commodity](commodity.md).
