# Smart contract

A program stored on a blockchain that executes automatically when conditions are met. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Cryptoassets](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/04-cryptoassets.md). That lesson also covers [Stablecoin](stablecoin.md), [Private key](private-key.md), [Crypto custody](crypto-custody.md) and [Bitcoin](bitcoin.md).
