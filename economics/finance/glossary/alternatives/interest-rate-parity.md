# Interest-rate parity

The condition that the forward exchange rate offsets the interest-rate difference between two currencies. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Foreign exchange](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/03-foreign-exchange.md). That lesson also covers [Purchasing-power parity](purchasing-power-parity.md), [Carry trade](carry-trade.md), [Currency hedging](currency-hedging.md) and [Exchange rate](exchange-rate.md).
