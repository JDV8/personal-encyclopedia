# Bitcoin

The first major cryptocurrency, launched in 2009 by the pseudonymous Satoshi Nakamoto, with supply capped at 21 million coins. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Cryptoassets](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/04-cryptoassets.md). That lesson also covers [Blockchain](blockchain.md), [Proof of work](proof-of-work.md), [Proof of stake](proof-of-stake.md) and [Smart contract](smart-contract.md).
