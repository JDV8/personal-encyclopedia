# REIT

A real estate investment trust that owns income-producing property and distributes most of its income. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Real estate and REITs](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/01-real-estate-and-reits.md). That lesson also covers [Funds from operations (FFO)](ffo.md), [Capitalisation rate](cap-rate.md) and [Net operating income](net-operating-income.md).
