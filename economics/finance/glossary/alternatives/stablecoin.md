# Stablecoin

A cryptoasset designed to keep a stable value, usually one dollar, via reserves or algorithms. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Cryptoassets](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/04-cryptoassets.md). That lesson also covers [Private key](private-key.md), [Crypto custody](crypto-custody.md), [Bitcoin](bitcoin.md) and [Blockchain](blockchain.md).
