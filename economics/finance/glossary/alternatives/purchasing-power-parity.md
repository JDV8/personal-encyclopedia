# Purchasing-power parity

The idea that exchange rates adjust so identical goods cost the same across countries; it holds loosely over the long run. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Foreign exchange](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/03-foreign-exchange.md). That lesson also covers [Carry trade](carry-trade.md), [Currency hedging](currency-hedging.md), [Exchange rate](exchange-rate.md) and [FX spread](fx-spread.md).
