# Private key

The secret number that proves ownership of crypto and authorises its transfer; lose it and the assets are lost. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Cryptoassets](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/04-cryptoassets.md). That lesson also covers [Crypto custody](crypto-custody.md), [Bitcoin](bitcoin.md), [Blockchain](blockchain.md) and [Proof of work](proof-of-work.md).
