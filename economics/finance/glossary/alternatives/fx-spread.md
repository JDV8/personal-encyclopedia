# FX spread

The gap between the buy and sell exchange rate; a hidden cost of currency conversion. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Foreign exchange](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/03-foreign-exchange.md). That lesson also covers [Interest-rate parity](interest-rate-parity.md), [Purchasing-power parity](purchasing-power-parity.md), [Carry trade](carry-trade.md) and [Currency hedging](currency-hedging.md).
