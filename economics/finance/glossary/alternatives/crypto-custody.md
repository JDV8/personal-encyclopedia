# Crypto custody

Safekeeping crypto assets, either by an exchange or with the owner's own wallet. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Cryptoassets](../../03-securities/05-alternatives/04-cryptoassets.md). That lesson also covers [Bitcoin](bitcoin.md), [Blockchain](blockchain.md), [Proof of work](proof-of-work.md) and [Proof of stake](proof-of-stake.md).
