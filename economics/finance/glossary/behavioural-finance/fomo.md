# FOMO

Fear of missing out; a driver of buying during rallies. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Herding, bubbles and manias](../../07-behavioural-finance/04-herding-bubbles-and-manias.md). That lesson also covers [Meme stock](meme-stock.md), [Herding](herding.md), [Information cascade](information-cascade.md) and [Bubble](bubble.md).
