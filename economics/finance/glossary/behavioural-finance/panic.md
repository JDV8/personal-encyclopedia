# Panic

A sudden, widespread rush to sell or withdraw funds, driven by fear. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Herding, bubbles and manias](../../07-behavioural-finance/04-herding-bubbles-and-manias.md). That lesson also covers [Greater fool theory](greater-fool-theory.md), [Irrational exuberance](irrational-exuberance.md), [Minsky moment](minsky-moment.md) and [FOMO](fomo.md).
