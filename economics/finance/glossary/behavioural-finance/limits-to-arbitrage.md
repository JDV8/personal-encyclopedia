# Limits to arbitrage

Practical barriers, such as funding costs and risk, that stop arbitrageurs eliminating mispricings quickly. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [From rational to behavioural](../../07-behavioural-finance/01-from-rational-to-behavioural.md). That lesson also covers [Behavioural finance](behavioral-finance.md), [Heuristic](heuristic.md) and [Expected utility](expected-utility.md).
