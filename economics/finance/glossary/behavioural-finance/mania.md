# Mania

A phase of extreme investor enthusiasm; the term is used for episodes such as tulip mania. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Herding, bubbles and manias](../../07-behavioural-finance/04-herding-bubbles-and-manias.md). That lesson also covers [Panic](panic.md), [Greater fool theory](greater-fool-theory.md), [Irrational exuberance](irrational-exuberance.md) and [Minsky moment](minsky-moment.md).
