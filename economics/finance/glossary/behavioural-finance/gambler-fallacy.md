# Gambler's fallacy

Believing that a streak must reverse because of "averages", even for independent events. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Overconfidence and other biases](../../07-behavioural-finance/03-overconfidence-and-other-biases.md). That lesson also covers [Base rate](base-rate.md), [Overconfidence](overconfidence.md), [Confirmation bias](confirmation-bias.md) and [Hindsight bias](hindsight-bias.md).
