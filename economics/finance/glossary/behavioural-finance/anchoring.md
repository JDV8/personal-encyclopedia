# Anchoring

Relying too heavily on an initial number, such as a past price. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Mental accounting, anchoring and framing](../../07-behavioural-finance/05-mental-accounting-anchoring-and-framing.md). That lesson also covers [Framing](framing.md), [Narrow framing](narrow-framing.md), [Nudge](nudge.md) and [Default effect](default-effect.md).
