# Myopic loss aversion

Checking results too often and so feeling losses more, which discourages holding stocks. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Prospect theory and loss aversion](../../07-behavioural-finance/02-prospect-theory-and-loss-aversion.md). That lesson also covers [Equity premium puzzle](equity-premium-puzzle.md), [Endowment effect](endowment-effect.md), [Regret aversion](regret-aversion.md) and [Sunk-cost fallacy](sunk-cost-fallacy.md).
