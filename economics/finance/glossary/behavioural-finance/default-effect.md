# Default effect

The tendency to stick with the pre-set option. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Mental accounting, anchoring and framing](../../07-behavioural-finance/05-mental-accounting-anchoring-and-framing.md). That lesson also covers [Fungibility](fungibility.md), [House-money effect](house-money-effect.md), [Mental accounting](mental-accounting.md) and [Anchoring](anchoring.md).
