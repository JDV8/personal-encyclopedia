# Fungibility

The property of being interchangeable, as every dollar is; mental accounting violates it. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Mental accounting, anchoring and framing](../../07-behavioural-finance/05-mental-accounting-anchoring-and-framing.md). That lesson also covers [House-money effect](house-money-effect.md), [Mental accounting](mental-accounting.md), [Anchoring](anchoring.md) and [Framing](framing.md).
