# Overconfidence

Overestimating one's knowledge, skill or the precision of forecasts, leading to excess trading. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Overconfidence and other biases](../../07-behavioural-finance/03-overconfidence-and-other-biases.md). That lesson also covers [Confirmation bias](confirmation-bias.md), [Hindsight bias](hindsight-bias.md), [Recency bias](recency-bias.md) and [Gambler's fallacy](gambler-fallacy.md).
