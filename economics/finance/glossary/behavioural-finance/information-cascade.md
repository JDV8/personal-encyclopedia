# Information cascade

A sequence in which people ignore their own signals and copy those before them. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Herding, bubbles and manias](../../07-behavioural-finance/04-herding-bubbles-and-manias.md). That lesson also covers [Bubble](bubble.md), [Speculative bubble](speculative-bubble.md), [Mania](mania.md) and [Panic](panic.md).
