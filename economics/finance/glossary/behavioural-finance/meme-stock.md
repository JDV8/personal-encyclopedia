# Meme stock

A stock whose price is driven by online communities rather than fundamentals, as GameStop in January 2021. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Herding, bubbles and manias](../../07-behavioural-finance/04-herding-bubbles-and-manias.md). That lesson also covers [Herding](herding.md), [Information cascade](information-cascade.md), [Bubble](bubble.md) and [Speculative bubble](speculative-bubble.md).
