# Hindsight bias

Believing after the fact that an outcome was predictable. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Overconfidence and other biases](../../07-behavioural-finance/03-overconfidence-and-other-biases.md). That lesson also covers [Recency bias](recency-bias.md), [Gambler's fallacy](gambler-fallacy.md), [Base rate](base-rate.md) and [Overconfidence](overconfidence.md).
