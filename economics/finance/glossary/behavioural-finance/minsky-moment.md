# Minsky moment

The sudden collapse of asset prices after a long period of growth fuelled by debt; named for economist Hyman Minsky. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Herding, bubbles and manias](../../07-behavioural-finance/04-herding-bubbles-and-manias.md). That lesson also covers [FOMO](fomo.md), [Meme stock](meme-stock.md), [Herding](herding.md) and [Information cascade](information-cascade.md).
