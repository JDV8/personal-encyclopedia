# Endowment effect

Valuing something more simply because you own it. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Prospect theory and loss aversion](../../07-behavioural-finance/02-prospect-theory-and-loss-aversion.md). That lesson also covers [Regret aversion](regret-aversion.md), [Sunk-cost fallacy](sunk-cost-fallacy.md), [Prospect theory](prospect-theory.md) and [Loss aversion](loss-aversion.md).
