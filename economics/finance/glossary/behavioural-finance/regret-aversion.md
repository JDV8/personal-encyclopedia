# Regret aversion

Avoiding decisions that might later cause regret, such as selling a loser. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Prospect theory and loss aversion](../../07-behavioural-finance/02-prospect-theory-and-loss-aversion.md). That lesson also covers [Sunk-cost fallacy](sunk-cost-fallacy.md), [Prospect theory](prospect-theory.md), [Loss aversion](loss-aversion.md) and [Reference point](reference-point.md).
