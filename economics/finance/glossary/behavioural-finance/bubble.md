# Bubble

A period in which prices rise far above fundamental value, sustained by expectations of further rises. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Herding, bubbles and manias](../../07-behavioural-finance/04-herding-bubbles-and-manias.md). That lesson also covers [Speculative bubble](speculative-bubble.md), [Mania](mania.md), [Panic](panic.md) and [Greater fool theory](greater-fool-theory.md).
