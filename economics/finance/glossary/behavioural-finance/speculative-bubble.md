# Speculative bubble

A bubble driven by buying in the hope of selling higher to others, rather than for the asset's income. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Herding, bubbles and manias](../../07-behavioural-finance/04-herding-bubbles-and-manias.md). That lesson also covers [Mania](mania.md), [Panic](panic.md), [Greater fool theory](greater-fool-theory.md) and [Irrational exuberance](irrational-exuberance.md).
