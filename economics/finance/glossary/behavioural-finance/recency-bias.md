# Recency bias

Overweighting recent events when judging what will happen next. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Overconfidence and other biases](../../07-behavioural-finance/03-overconfidence-and-other-biases.md). That lesson also covers [Gambler's fallacy](gambler-fallacy.md), [Base rate](base-rate.md), [Overconfidence](overconfidence.md) and [Confirmation bias](confirmation-bias.md).
