# Sunk-cost fallacy

Continuing a course because of what has already been spent, not future prospects. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Prospect theory and loss aversion](../../07-behavioural-finance/02-prospect-theory-and-loss-aversion.md). That lesson also covers [Prospect theory](prospect-theory.md), [Loss aversion](loss-aversion.md), [Reference point](reference-point.md) and [Disposition effect](disposition-effect.md).
