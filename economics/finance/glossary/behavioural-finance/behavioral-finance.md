# Behavioural finance

The study of how psychological biases and limits to rationality shape financial decisions and prices. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [From rational to behavioural](../../07-behavioural-finance/01-from-rational-to-behavioural.md). That lesson also covers [Heuristic](heuristic.md), [Expected utility](expected-utility.md) and [Limits to arbitrage](limits-to-arbitrage.md).
