# Base rate

The underlying frequency of an event, often ignored in favour of vivid specifics. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Overconfidence and other biases](../../07-behavioural-finance/03-overconfidence-and-other-biases.md). That lesson also covers [Overconfidence](overconfidence.md), [Confirmation bias](confirmation-bias.md), [Hindsight bias](hindsight-bias.md) and [Recency bias](recency-bias.md).
