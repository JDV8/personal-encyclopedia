# House-money effect

Taking greater risks with recent gains because they feel like windfalls. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Mental accounting, anchoring and framing](../../07-behavioural-finance/05-mental-accounting-anchoring-and-framing.md). That lesson also covers [Mental accounting](mental-accounting.md), [Anchoring](anchoring.md), [Framing](framing.md) and [Narrow framing](narrow-framing.md).
