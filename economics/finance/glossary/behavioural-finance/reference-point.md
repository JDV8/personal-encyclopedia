# Reference point

The baseline, often the purchase price, against which gains and losses are judged. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Prospect theory and loss aversion](../../07-behavioural-finance/02-prospect-theory-and-loss-aversion.md). That lesson also covers [Disposition effect](disposition-effect.md), [Myopic loss aversion](myopic-loss-aversion.md), [Equity premium puzzle](equity-premium-puzzle.md) and [Endowment effect](endowment-effect.md).
