# Framing

The influence of how a choice is presented, such as 90% survival versus 10% mortality. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Mental accounting, anchoring and framing](../../07-behavioural-finance/05-mental-accounting-anchoring-and-framing.md). That lesson also covers [Narrow framing](narrow-framing.md), [Nudge](nudge.md), [Default effect](default-effect.md) and [Fungibility](fungibility.md).
