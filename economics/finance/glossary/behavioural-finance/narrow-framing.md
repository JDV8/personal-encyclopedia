# Narrow framing

Judging each decision in isolation rather than as part of a total portfolio. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Mental accounting, anchoring and framing](../../07-behavioural-finance/05-mental-accounting-anchoring-and-framing.md). That lesson also covers [Nudge](nudge.md), [Default effect](default-effect.md), [Fungibility](fungibility.md) and [House-money effect](house-money-effect.md).
