# Loss aversion

The tendency for a loss to hurt roughly twice as much as an equal gain pleases. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Prospect theory and loss aversion](../../07-behavioural-finance/02-prospect-theory-and-loss-aversion.md). That lesson also covers [Reference point](reference-point.md), [Disposition effect](disposition-effect.md), [Myopic loss aversion](myopic-loss-aversion.md) and [Equity premium puzzle](equity-premium-puzzle.md).
