# Expected utility

The standard theory of choice under risk, in which people maximise the probability-weighted utility of outcomes. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [From rational to behavioural](../../07-behavioural-finance/01-from-rational-to-behavioural.md). That lesson also covers [Limits to arbitrage](limits-to-arbitrage.md), [Behavioural finance](behavioral-finance.md) and [Heuristic](heuristic.md).
