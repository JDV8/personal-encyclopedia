# Prospect theory

Kahneman and Tversky's 1979 theory that people judge outcomes as gains and losses from a reference point and weigh losses more heavily. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Prospect theory and loss aversion](../../07-behavioural-finance/02-prospect-theory-and-loss-aversion.md). That lesson also covers [Loss aversion](loss-aversion.md), [Reference point](reference-point.md), [Disposition effect](disposition-effect.md) and [Myopic loss aversion](myopic-loss-aversion.md).
