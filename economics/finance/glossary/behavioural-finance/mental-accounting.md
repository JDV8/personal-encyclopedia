# Mental accounting

Treating money differently depending on its source or purpose, though it is fungible. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Mental accounting, anchoring and framing](../../07-behavioural-finance/05-mental-accounting-anchoring-and-framing.md). That lesson also covers [Anchoring](anchoring.md), [Framing](framing.md), [Narrow framing](narrow-framing.md) and [Nudge](nudge.md).
