# Confirmation bias

Seeking and favouring information that agrees with existing beliefs. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Overconfidence and other biases](../../07-behavioural-finance/03-overconfidence-and-other-biases.md). That lesson also covers [Hindsight bias](hindsight-bias.md), [Recency bias](recency-bias.md), [Gambler's fallacy](gambler-fallacy.md) and [Base rate](base-rate.md).
