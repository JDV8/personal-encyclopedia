# Nudge

A design of choices, such as automatic enrolment, that steers behaviour without forbidding options. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Mental accounting, anchoring and framing](../../07-behavioural-finance/05-mental-accounting-anchoring-and-framing.md). That lesson also covers [Default effect](default-effect.md), [Fungibility](fungibility.md), [House-money effect](house-money-effect.md) and [Mental accounting](mental-accounting.md).
