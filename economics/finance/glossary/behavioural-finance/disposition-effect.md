# Disposition effect

The tendency to sell winners too early and hold losers too long. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Prospect theory and loss aversion](../../07-behavioural-finance/02-prospect-theory-and-loss-aversion.md). That lesson also covers [Myopic loss aversion](myopic-loss-aversion.md), [Equity premium puzzle](equity-premium-puzzle.md), [Endowment effect](endowment-effect.md) and [Regret aversion](regret-aversion.md).
