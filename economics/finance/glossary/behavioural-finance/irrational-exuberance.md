# Irrational exuberance

Alan Greenspan's 1996 phrase for unfounded optimism pushing up asset prices; later the title of Robert Shiller's book. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Herding, bubbles and manias](../../07-behavioural-finance/04-herding-bubbles-and-manias.md). That lesson also covers [Minsky moment](minsky-moment.md), [FOMO](fomo.md), [Meme stock](meme-stock.md) and [Herding](herding.md).
