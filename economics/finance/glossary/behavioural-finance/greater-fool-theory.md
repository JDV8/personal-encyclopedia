# Greater fool theory

Buying an overpriced asset believing a "greater fool" will pay more later. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Herding, bubbles and manias](../../07-behavioural-finance/04-herding-bubbles-and-manias.md). That lesson also covers [Irrational exuberance](irrational-exuberance.md), [Minsky moment](minsky-moment.md), [FOMO](fomo.md) and [Meme stock](meme-stock.md).
