# Asset location

Deciding which assets to hold in which account type to reduce tax, such as bonds in a registered account. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Taxes on investments](../../09-personal-investing-canada/06-taxes-on-investments.md). That lesson also covers [Marginal tax rate](marginal-tax-rate.md), [Capital gain](capital-gain.md), [Inclusion rate](inclusion-rate.md) and [Adjusted cost base (ACB)](adjusted-cost-base.md).
