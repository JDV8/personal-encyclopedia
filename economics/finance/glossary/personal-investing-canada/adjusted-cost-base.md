# Adjusted cost base (ACB)

The average cost of your holding including commissions and adjusted for items such as return of capital, used to compute gains. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Taxes on investments](../../09-personal-investing-canada/06-taxes-on-investments.md). That lesson also covers [Dividend tax credit](dividend-tax-credit.md), [Superficial loss](superficial-loss.md), [Tax-loss harvesting](tax-loss-harvesting.md) and [Withholding tax](withholding-tax.md).
