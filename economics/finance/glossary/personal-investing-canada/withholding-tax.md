# Withholding tax

Tax deducted at source from payments, notably on dividends from foreign companies. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Taxes on investments](../../09-personal-investing-canada/06-taxes-on-investments.md). That lesson also covers [T5 slip](t5.md), [T1135](t1135.md), [CRA](cra.md) and [Asset location](asset-location.md).
