# Over-contribution

Contributing above your room, which attracts a penalty tax of 1% a month on the excess. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Registered and non-registered accounts](../../09-personal-investing-canada/02-registered-and-non-registered-accounts.md). That lesson also covers [Carry-forward](carry-forward.md), [Registered account](registered-account.md), [Non-registered account](non-registered-account.md) and [RESP](resp.md).
