# Tax-loss harvesting

Selling a loser to realise a capital loss that offsets gains, then reinvesting in a similar but not identical holding. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Taxes on investments](../../09-personal-investing-canada/06-taxes-on-investments.md). That lesson also covers [Withholding tax](withholding-tax.md), [T5 slip](t5.md), [T1135](t1135.md) and [CRA](cra.md).
