# Marginal tax rate

The tax rate on the next dollar of income. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Taxes on investments](../../09-personal-investing-canada/06-taxes-on-investments.md). That lesson also covers [Capital gain](capital-gain.md), [Inclusion rate](inclusion-rate.md), [Adjusted cost base (ACB)](adjusted-cost-base.md) and [Dividend tax credit](dividend-tax-credit.md).
