# RRSP

The Registered Retirement Savings Plan: contributions are deductible, growth is tax-deferred and withdrawals are taxed. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The RRSP](../../09-personal-investing-canada/04-the-rrsp.md). That lesson also covers [RRIF](rrif.md), [Spousal RRSP](spousal-rrsp.md), [Earned income](earned-income.md) and [Pension adjustment](pension-adjustment.md).
