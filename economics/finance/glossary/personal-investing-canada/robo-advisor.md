# Robo-advisor

An online service that builds and rebalances a portfolio for you, for a fee. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Costs, brokers and choosing a platform](../../09-personal-investing-canada/08-costs-brokers-and-choosing-a-platform.md). That lesson also covers [In-kind transfer](in-kind-transfer.md) and [Norbert's gambit](norberts-gambit.md).
