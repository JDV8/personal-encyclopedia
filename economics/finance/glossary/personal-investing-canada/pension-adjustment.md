# Pension adjustment

A reduction in RRSP room for pension benefits earned in a workplace plan. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The RRSP](../../09-personal-investing-canada/04-the-rrsp.md). That lesson also covers [Notice of assessment](notice-of-assessment.md), [Home Buyers' Plan](home-buyers-plan.md), [Tax deduction](tax-deduction.md) and [Tax deferral](tax-deferral.md).
