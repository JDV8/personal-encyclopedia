# Dollar-cost averaging

Investing a fixed amount at regular intervals regardless of price. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Building a simple portfolio](../../09-personal-investing-canada/07-building-a-simple-portfolio.md). That lesson also covers [Three-fund portfolio](three-fund-portfolio.md) and [All-in-one ETF](all-in-one-etf.md).
