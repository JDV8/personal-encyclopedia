# FHSA

The First Home Savings Account, launched in 2023: deductible contributions and tax-free qualifying withdrawals for a first home. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The FHSA](../../09-personal-investing-canada/05-the-fhsa.md). That lesson also covers [First-time home buyer](first-time-home-buyer.md) and [Qualifying withdrawal](qualifying-withdrawal.md).
