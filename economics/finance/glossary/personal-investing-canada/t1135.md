# T1135

A CRA form for reporting foreign property above 100,000 in cost. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Taxes on investments](../../09-personal-investing-canada/06-taxes-on-investments.md). That lesson also covers [CRA](cra.md), [Asset location](asset-location.md), [Marginal tax rate](marginal-tax-rate.md) and [Capital gain](capital-gain.md).
