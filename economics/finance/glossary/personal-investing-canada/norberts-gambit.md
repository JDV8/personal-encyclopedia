# Norbert's gambit

Converting currency cheaply by buying a dual-listed security in one currency and selling it in the other. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Costs, brokers and choosing a platform](../../09-personal-investing-canada/08-costs-brokers-and-choosing-a-platform.md). That lesson also covers [Robo-advisor](robo-advisor.md) and [In-kind transfer](in-kind-transfer.md).
