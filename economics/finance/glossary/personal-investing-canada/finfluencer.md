# Finfluencer

A social-media personality giving finance tips, often without registration or disclosure of conflicts. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Scams and common mistakes](../../09-personal-investing-canada/09-scams-and-common-mistakes.md). That lesson also covers [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](caf-centre.md), [Phishing](phishing.md), [Pig-butchering scam](pig-butchering.md) and [Red flag](red-flag.md).
