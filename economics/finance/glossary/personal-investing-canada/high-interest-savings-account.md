# High-interest savings account (HISA)

A savings account paying a higher rate than regular chequing, often CDIC-insured. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Foundations before investing](../../09-personal-investing-canada/01-foundations-before-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Savings rate](savings-rate.md), [Employer match](employer-match.md) and [Emergency fund](emergency-fund.md).
