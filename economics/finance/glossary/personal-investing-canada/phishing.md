# Phishing

A scam using fake messages or websites to steal passwords and account details. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Scams and common mistakes](../../09-personal-investing-canada/09-scams-and-common-mistakes.md). That lesson also covers [Pig-butchering scam](pig-butchering.md), [Red flag](red-flag.md), [Finfluencer](finfluencer.md) and [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](caf-centre.md).
