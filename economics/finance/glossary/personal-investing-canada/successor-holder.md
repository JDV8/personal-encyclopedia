# Successor holder

A surviving spouse who can take over a deceased spouse's TFSA, keeping its tax-free status. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The TFSA](../../09-personal-investing-canada/03-the-tfsa.md). That lesson also covers [TFSA](tfsa.md).
