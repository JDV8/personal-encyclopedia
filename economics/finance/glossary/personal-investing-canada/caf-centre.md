# Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre

The national agency collecting reports of fraud and scams, run by the RCMP, the Competition Bureau and the OPP. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Scams and common mistakes](../../09-personal-investing-canada/09-scams-and-common-mistakes.md). That lesson also covers [Phishing](phishing.md), [Pig-butchering scam](pig-butchering.md), [Red flag](red-flag.md) and [Finfluencer](finfluencer.md).
