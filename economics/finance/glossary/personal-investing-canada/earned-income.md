# Earned income

Income from employment and certain other sources that creates RRSP room; excludes investment income. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The RRSP](../../09-personal-investing-canada/04-the-rrsp.md). That lesson also covers [Pension adjustment](pension-adjustment.md), [Notice of assessment](notice-of-assessment.md), [Home Buyers' Plan](home-buyers-plan.md) and [Tax deduction](tax-deduction.md).
