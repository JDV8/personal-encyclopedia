# Superficial loss

A loss denied for tax when you or an affiliated person buy the same security within 30 days before or after the sale. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Taxes on investments](../../09-personal-investing-canada/06-taxes-on-investments.md). That lesson also covers [Tax-loss harvesting](tax-loss-harvesting.md), [Withholding tax](withholding-tax.md), [T5 slip](t5.md) and [T1135](t1135.md).
