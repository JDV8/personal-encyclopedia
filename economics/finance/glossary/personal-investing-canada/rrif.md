# RRIF

The Registered Retirement Income Fund, into which an RRSP must be converted by the end of the year you turn 71, with minimum annual withdrawals. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The RRSP](../../09-personal-investing-canada/04-the-rrsp.md). That lesson also covers [Spousal RRSP](spousal-rrsp.md), [Earned income](earned-income.md), [Pension adjustment](pension-adjustment.md) and [Notice of assessment](notice-of-assessment.md).
