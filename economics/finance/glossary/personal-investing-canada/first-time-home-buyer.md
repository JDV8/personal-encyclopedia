# First-time home buyer

Someone who has not lived in a home they or their spouse owned in a recent period set by the programme. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The FHSA](../../09-personal-investing-canada/05-the-fhsa.md). That lesson also covers [Qualifying withdrawal](qualifying-withdrawal.md) and [FHSA](fhsa.md).
