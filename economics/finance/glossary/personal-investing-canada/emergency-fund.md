# Emergency fund

Easily accessible savings, commonly three to six months of expenses, held before investing. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Foundations before investing](../../09-personal-investing-canada/01-foundations-before-investing.md). That lesson also covers [High-interest savings account (HISA)](high-interest-savings-account.md), [Savings rate](savings-rate.md) and [Employer match](employer-match.md).
