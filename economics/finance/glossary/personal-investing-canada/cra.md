# CRA

The Canada Revenue Agency, which administers taxes and registered accounts. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Taxes on investments](../../09-personal-investing-canada/06-taxes-on-investments.md). That lesson also covers [Asset location](asset-location.md), [Marginal tax rate](marginal-tax-rate.md), [Capital gain](capital-gain.md) and [Inclusion rate](inclusion-rate.md).
