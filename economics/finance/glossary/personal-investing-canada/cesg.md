# CESG

The Canada Education Savings Grant, a government top-up of 20% on the first 2,500 a year contributed to an RESP, within lifetime limits. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Registered and non-registered accounts](../../09-personal-investing-canada/02-registered-and-non-registered-accounts.md). That lesson also covers [Contribution room](contribution-room.md), [Over-contribution](over-contribution.md), [Carry-forward](carry-forward.md) and [Registered account](registered-account.md).
