# T5 slip

The Canadian tax slip reporting investment income such as interest and non-eligible dividends. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Taxes on investments](../../09-personal-investing-canada/06-taxes-on-investments.md). That lesson also covers [T1135](t1135.md), [CRA](cra.md), [Asset location](asset-location.md) and [Marginal tax rate](marginal-tax-rate.md).
