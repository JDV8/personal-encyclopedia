# Red flag

A warning sign of fraud, such as guaranteed returns, pressure to act fast, or unregistered sellers. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Scams and common mistakes](../../09-personal-investing-canada/09-scams-and-common-mistakes.md). That lesson also covers [Finfluencer](finfluencer.md), [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](caf-centre.md), [Phishing](phishing.md) and [Pig-butchering scam](pig-butchering.md).
