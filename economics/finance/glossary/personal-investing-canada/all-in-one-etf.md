# All-in-one ETF

A single ETF holding a diversified portfolio of stocks and bonds in fixed proportions and rebalancing itself. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Building a simple portfolio](../../09-personal-investing-canada/07-building-a-simple-portfolio.md). That lesson also covers [Dollar-cost averaging](dollar-cost-averaging.md) and [Three-fund portfolio](three-fund-portfolio.md).
