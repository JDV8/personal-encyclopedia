# Inclusion rate

The share of a capital gain added to taxable income, 50% in Canada. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Taxes on investments](../../09-personal-investing-canada/06-taxes-on-investments.md). That lesson also covers [Adjusted cost base (ACB)](adjusted-cost-base.md), [Dividend tax credit](dividend-tax-credit.md), [Superficial loss](superficial-loss.md) and [Tax-loss harvesting](tax-loss-harvesting.md).
