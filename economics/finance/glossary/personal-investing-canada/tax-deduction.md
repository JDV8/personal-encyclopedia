# Tax deduction

An amount subtracted from taxable income, such as an RRSP contribution. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The RRSP](../../09-personal-investing-canada/04-the-rrsp.md). That lesson also covers [Tax deferral](tax-deferral.md), [RRSP](rrsp.md), [RRIF](rrif.md) and [Spousal RRSP](spousal-rrsp.md).
