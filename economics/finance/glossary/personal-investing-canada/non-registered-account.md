# Non-registered account

A taxable account with no special status and no contribution limits; income and gains are taxed. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Registered and non-registered accounts](../../09-personal-investing-canada/02-registered-and-non-registered-accounts.md). That lesson also covers [RESP](resp.md), [CESG](cesg.md), [Contribution room](contribution-room.md) and [Over-contribution](over-contribution.md).
