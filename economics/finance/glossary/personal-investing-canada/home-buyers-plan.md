# Home Buyers' Plan

A programme allowing first-time buyers to withdraw up to 60,000 from an RRSP, repayable over 15 years. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The RRSP](../../09-personal-investing-canada/04-the-rrsp.md). That lesson also covers [Tax deduction](tax-deduction.md), [Tax deferral](tax-deferral.md), [RRSP](rrsp.md) and [RRIF](rrif.md).
