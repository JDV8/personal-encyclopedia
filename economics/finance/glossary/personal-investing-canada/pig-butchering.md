# Pig-butchering scam

A long con in which scammers build a relationship online and steer the victim into fake investments. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Scams and common mistakes](../../09-personal-investing-canada/09-scams-and-common-mistakes.md). That lesson also covers [Red flag](red-flag.md), [Finfluencer](finfluencer.md), [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](caf-centre.md) and [Phishing](phishing.md).
