# Carry-forward

In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Registered and non-registered accounts](../../09-personal-investing-canada/02-registered-and-non-registered-accounts.md). That lesson also covers [Registered account](registered-account.md), [Non-registered account](non-registered-account.md), [RESP](resp.md) and [CESG](cesg.md).
