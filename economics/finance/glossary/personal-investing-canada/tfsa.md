# TFSA

The Tax-Free Savings Account, started in 2009, in which growth and withdrawals are tax-free. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The TFSA](../../09-personal-investing-canada/03-the-tfsa.md). That lesson also covers [Successor holder](successor-holder.md).
