# RESP

The Registered Education Savings Plan, whose growth is taxed in the student's hands on withdrawal and which can attract government grants. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Registered and non-registered accounts](../../09-personal-investing-canada/02-registered-and-non-registered-accounts.md). That lesson also covers [CESG](cesg.md), [Contribution room](contribution-room.md), [Over-contribution](over-contribution.md) and [Carry-forward](carry-forward.md).
