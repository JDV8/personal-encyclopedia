# Capital gain

The profit from selling an asset for more than its cost. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Taxes on investments](../../09-personal-investing-canada/06-taxes-on-investments.md). That lesson also covers [Inclusion rate](inclusion-rate.md), [Adjusted cost base (ACB)](adjusted-cost-base.md), [Dividend tax credit](dividend-tax-credit.md) and [Superficial loss](superficial-loss.md).
