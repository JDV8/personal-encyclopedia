# Registered account

An account, such as a TFSA or RRSP, registered with the Canada Revenue Agency and given special tax treatment. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Registered and non-registered accounts](../../09-personal-investing-canada/02-registered-and-non-registered-accounts.md). That lesson also covers [Non-registered account](non-registered-account.md), [RESP](resp.md), [CESG](cesg.md) and [Contribution room](contribution-room.md).
