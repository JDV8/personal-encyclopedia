# Spousal RRSP

An RRSP to which one spouse contributes and the other is the annuitant, shifting taxable retirement income. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [The RRSP](../../09-personal-investing-canada/04-the-rrsp.md). That lesson also covers [Earned income](earned-income.md), [Pension adjustment](pension-adjustment.md), [Notice of assessment](notice-of-assessment.md) and [Home Buyers' Plan](home-buyers-plan.md).
