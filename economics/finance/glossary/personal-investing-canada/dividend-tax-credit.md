# Dividend tax credit

A credit that reduces tax on eligible dividends from Canadian corporations. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Taxes on investments](../../09-personal-investing-canada/06-taxes-on-investments.md). That lesson also covers [Superficial loss](superficial-loss.md), [Tax-loss harvesting](tax-loss-harvesting.md), [Withholding tax](withholding-tax.md) and [T5 slip](t5.md).
