# Employer match

Employer contributions to a workplace plan matching an employee's, effectively a guaranteed return. In this course the term is explained in context in the lesson [Foundations before investing](../../09-personal-investing-canada/01-foundations-before-investing.md). That lesson also covers [Emergency fund](emergency-fund.md), [High-interest savings account (HISA)](high-interest-savings-account.md) and [Savings rate](savings-rate.md).
