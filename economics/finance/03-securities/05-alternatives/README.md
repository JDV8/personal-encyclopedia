# Alternatives: real estate, commodities, currencies and crypto

Beyond stocks, bonds, funds and derivatives lie assets that are harder to value or behave differently. This branch covers real estate and REITs, where income and leverage matter; commodities and gold, which produce no cash flow; foreign exchange, which is a price between two monies; and cryptoassets, a new and contested category. Each lesson asks the same questions: what produces the return, how is it valued, what are the risks and who protects the investor.

**Prerequisites:** [Foundations](../../01-foundations/README.md), [Stocks](../01-stocks/README.md), and [Derivatives](../04-derivatives/README.md) for the futures and swap vocabulary.

## Suggested reading order

Lessons are independent; read them in any order. Foreign exchange is the most useful for a Canadian investor holding foreign assets.

## References & further reading

- David Geltner, Norman Miller, Jim Clayton and Piet Eichholtz, *Commercial Real Estate Analysis and Investments* (Advanced). The standard text on property valuation and investment.
- Peter L. Bernstein, *The Power of Gold* (Beginner). A history of gold's place in human affairs.
- Gary Gorton and K. Geert Rouwenhorst, "Facts and Fantasies about Commodity Futures", *Financial Analysts Journal* (2006) (Advanced). A well-known academic study of commodity futures returns.
- Arvind Narayanan, Joseph Bonneau, Edward Felten, Andrew Miller and Steven Goldfeder, *Bitcoin and Cryptocurrency Technologies* (Intermediate). A university textbook on how blockchains work.
- Satoshi Nakamoto, "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System" (2008) (Intermediate). The original nine-page white paper.
- Zeke Faux, *Number Go Up* (Beginner). A journalist's investigation of the crypto boom and its failures.
- Paul Krugman, Maurice Obstfeld and Marc Melitz, *International Economics: Theory and Policy* (Intermediate). The standard text on exchange rates and international finance.
- Barry Eichengreen, *Exorbitant Privilege* (Intermediate). On the dollar's role as the world's reserve currency.
