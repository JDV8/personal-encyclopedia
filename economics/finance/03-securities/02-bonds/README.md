# Bonds

A bond is a tradable loan, and because its cash flows are fixed by contract, bonds are the cleanest place to practise the present-value machinery from the foundations section. This branch explains the parts of a bond, how its price is the present value of its payments, how yield to maturity inverts the pricing problem, and why prices move opposite to yields. It then measures that sensitivity with duration and convexity, turns to credit risk and ratings, surveys government and corporate bonds including high yield, explains the yield curve, and closes with inflation-linked bonds, zero-coupon bonds and Canadian GICs.

**Prerequisites:** [Foundations](../../01-foundations/README.md), especially present value and annuities; [Equity and debt](../../02-firms-and-capital/03-equity-and-debt.md).

## Suggested reading order

Read 01 to 05 in order: they form one argument from the definition of a bond to its interest-rate sensitivity. Lessons 06 to 08 are about who is borrowing and how likely they are to pay. Lesson 09, the yield curve, builds on yield to maturity and government bonds. Lesson 10 collects three specialised instruments. Work the price tables with a spreadsheet to make the pricing relationships concrete.

## References & further reading

- Frank J. Fabozzi, *Bond Markets, Analysis, and Strategies* (Intermediate). The standard university textbook on bond mathematics, markets and strategies.
- Frank J. Fabozzi (ed.), *The Handbook of Fixed Income Securities* (Advanced). A comprehensive professional reference.
- Bruce Tuckman and Angel Serrat, *Fixed Income Securities: Tools for Today's Markets* (Advanced). Rigorous treatment of the yield curve, duration and derivatives.
- Sidney Homer and Richard Sylla, *A History of Interest Rates* (Advanced). Four thousand years of interest rate history.
- Zvi Bodie, Alex Kane and Alan J. Marcus, *Investments* (Intermediate). The bond chapters cover pricing, duration and the term structure.
- Annette Thau, *The Bond Book* (Beginner). A practical guide to buying and understanding bonds.
- Michael Lewis, *Liar's Poker* (Beginner). A memoir of the 1980s Salomon Brothers bond trading desk, entertaining and informative about the culture of the market.
- Connie Bruck, *The Predators' Ball* (Intermediate). The story of Drexel Burnham Lambert and the junk bond market.
