# Regulation and the institutions around markets

Markets rest on trust and trust rests on rules. This section explains why securities markets are regulated, then follows the US model (the SEC and the 1933 and 1934 Acts) and the Canadian one (provincial regulators coordinated by the CSA, CIRO, CIPF and CDIC). It covers the legal concepts every investor should know (disclosure, insider trading and fraud) and ends with the central bank, whose policy rate is the most important single price in finance.

**Prerequisites:** [Markets](../04-markets/README.md) and the [securities](../03-securities/README.md) section.

## Suggested reading order

Read 01 for the economic logic, then 02 and 03 for the US and Canadian systems. Lesson 04 is the most practical for a Canadian investor. Lesson 05 covers misconduct and warning signs; lesson 06 stands alone and connects to the bond and history sections.

## References & further reading

- Zvi Bodie, Alex Kane and Alan J. Marcus, *Investments* (Intermediate). Chapters on the regulation of securities markets.
- George A. Akerlof, "The Market for 'Lemons': Quality Uncertainty and the Market Mechanism", *Quarterly Journal of Economics* (1970) (Advanced). The classic paper on information asymmetry.
- Joel Seligman, *The Transformation of Wall Street* (Advanced). A history of the SEC and federal securities regulation.
- Bethany McLean and Peter Elkind, *The Smartest Guys in the Room* (Beginner). The story of the Enron collapse.
- Kurt Eichenwald, *Conspiracy of Fools* (Beginner). A detailed narrative of Enron.
- Harry Markopolos, *No One Would Listen* (Beginner). An account of the attempt to warn the SEC about Madoff.
- Walter Bagehot, *Lombard Street* (Advanced). The 1873 classic on central banking and the lender of last resort.
- Ben S. Bernanke, *21st Century Monetary Policy* (Intermediate). A former Fed chair on how modern monetary policy works.
- Frederic S. Mishkin, *The Economics of Money, Banking and Financial Markets* (Intermediate). A standard textbook on banks, central banks and financial markets.
