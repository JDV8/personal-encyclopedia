# Body

Body is the living or material organism, as opposed (in some systems) to soul or mind. What a body is — atoms, elements, hylomorphic compound, or qi-configuration — depends on the metaphysics. Ethical attitudes to the body range from the Phaedo's wariness, through Aristotelian and Confucian care of a lived body, to Cynic shamelessness and some Daoist ease in transformation.
