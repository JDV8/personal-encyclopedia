# General terms

English and later Latin terms used to study these traditions: the five branches, the vocabulary of argument, and words such as substance, virtue, and nature. Many point on to a Greek or Chinese entry, or to a concept lesson, where the technical work is done.

**Prerequisites:** the [glossary overview](../README.md), and the relevant tradition's introductory lesson.

## Suggested reading order

Start with the five branches if you are new to the map: metaphysics, epistemology, ethics, logic, political philosophy. Then use the rest as a lookup while reading lessons.

## References & further reading

- Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics* and *Metaphysics*, any standard translation (Intermediate). Many of these English terms were shaped by reading him.
- Julia Annas, *Ancient Philosophy: A Very Short Introduction* (Beginner). A compact guide to how the branches hang together.
