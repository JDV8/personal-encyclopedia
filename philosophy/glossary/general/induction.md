# Induction

Induction, in a broad sense, is inference from particular cases to a general claim, or from what has been observed to what has not. Aristotle discusses epagoge as a route to first principles. It is not deduction, and its conclusions can be doubtful even when the cases are genuine. The modern 'problem of induction' is a later sharpening of an old issue.
