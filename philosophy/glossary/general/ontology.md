# Ontology

Ontology is the study of being, or of what kinds of thing exist and how they exist. It is often used as a near-synonym of metaphysics, or as the part of metaphysics that inventories what there is. Parmenides' 'what-is,' Aristotle's ousia, and Plotinus's One are ontological proposals. The word itself is later than those debates.
