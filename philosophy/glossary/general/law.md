# Law

Law may be a city's written or customary rules, a natural or cosmic order, or (in Legalism) a public standard of reward and punishment used as an instrument of the state. Greek nomos, Confucian and ritual norms, and Legalist fa are not one thing. The liberal idea of a rule of law that binds the rulers is not to be assumed in ancient 'law' talk.
