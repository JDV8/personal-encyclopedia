# Mind

Mind, in a loose modern sense, covers thinking, perceiving, and willing. Ancient terms only partly overlap: nous is intellect or a cosmic mind; psyche is often life-soul; xin is a heart-mind, not a pure intellect cut off from feeling. Anaxagoras and Plotinus give mind (nous) a cosmological role. Do not assume Descartes's mind when you see the English word in a translation.
