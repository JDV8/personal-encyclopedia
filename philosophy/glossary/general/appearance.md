# Appearance

Appearance is how things present themselves, as opposed to how they are. The contrast with reality is one of philosophy's oldest tools, and one of its most dangerous: it can clarify (a stick looks bent in water), and it can license a wholesale distrust of the senses. Pyrrhonists live by appearances without claiming to know the hidden reality behind them.
