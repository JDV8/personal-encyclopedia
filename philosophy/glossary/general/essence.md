# Essence

Essence is what a thing is, the 'what it was to be' (to ti en einai) in Aristotle's phrase, often identified with form and definition. To state an essence is to say what makes this thing the kind of thing it is, as opposed to its accidents. The term has a long medieval afterlife. In this path, start with Aristotle's substance books, not with later schoolbook essences.
