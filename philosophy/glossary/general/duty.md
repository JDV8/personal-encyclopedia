# Duty

Duty is what one is bound to do, as opposed to what one merely wants or what would pay. The word is more at home in later deontological ethics than in Aristotle, but ancient law, piety, Confucian role-obligations (xiao, loyalty), and Stoic 'the fitting' are related. Do not force Kant's categorical imperative onto these earlier vocabularies.
