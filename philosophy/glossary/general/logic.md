# Logic

Logic asks what makes an argument valid, what follows from what, and how language can clarify or mislead. Aristotle's syllogistic is one early systematic logic; the later Mohists developed tools for names, kinds, and inference; the Stoics developed a serious logic of propositions. Logic is a family of techniques for making reasoning inspectable, not a Greek exclusive.
