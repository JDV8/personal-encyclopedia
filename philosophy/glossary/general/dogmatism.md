# Dogmatism

In ordinary English, dogmatism is stubborn belief. In Hellenistic philosophy it is a technical counterpart to skepticism: the dogmatic schools claim to have found the truth about non-evident things. Sextus treats even the claim 'nothing can be known' as a dogma. In that usage the term names a style of philosophy, not only an insult.
