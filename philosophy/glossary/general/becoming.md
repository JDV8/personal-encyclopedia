# Becoming

Becoming is change, coming-to-be, process: the world's not staying put. Heraclitus is the emblem of taking becoming as fundamental; Parmenides is the emblem of denying that it can be thought. Aristotle's potentiality and actuality, and Chinese process vocabularies (Dao, yin-yang, the Changes), are ways of taking becoming seriously without simply announcing flux.
