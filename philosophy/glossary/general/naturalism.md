# Naturalism

Naturalism is a family of views that explain the world, and sometimes ethics, without appeal to a separate supernatural realm — or that treat nature as the inclusive whole. Milesian arche-theories, atomism, and Xunzi's non-intervening Heaven can all be called naturalistic in some sense. The word is modern and slippery.
