# Convention

Convention (nomos, custom, artifice) is what holds by agreement, habit, or law rather than by nature. The fifth-century physis/nomos debate, Zhuangzi's distrust of fixed standards, and Xunzi's defense of ritual as needed artifice all turn on this contrast. Convention is not automatically 'false'; it can be the only way a shared world holds.
