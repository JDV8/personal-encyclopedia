# Vice

A vice is a stable defect of character, often (in Aristotle) an extreme on either side of a mean: cowardice and rashness as vices relative to courage. In Stoic ethics, vice is the only genuine evil. Confucian texts contrast the junzi with the small person, whose settled bent is toward profit rather than rightness.
