# Cosmology

Cosmology, in the philosophical sense, is an account of the world as an ordered whole: its origin or source, its structure, and the place of human beings in it. Early Greek arche-theories, Plato's Timaeus, Stoic fire and logos, and Chinese yin-yang and Five Phase schemes are cosmologies. They are not modern observational cosmology.
