# Rationalism

Rationalism, as a later label, gives reason or the intellect a leading role in knowledge, sometimes including substantial knowledge not derived from the senses. Plato's Forms and recollection are often called rationalist in this loose sense. The term is more precise in early modern philosophy than in antiquity.
