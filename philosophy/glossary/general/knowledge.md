# Knowledge

Knowledge is the success term for a grasp of how things are that is more than lucky true opinion — in Plato, often an account-backed or Form-directed grasp; in Aristotle, often demonstration of causes. What knowledge is, and whether we have any, is the opening problem of epistemology.
