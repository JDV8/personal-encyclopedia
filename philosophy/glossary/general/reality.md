# Reality

Reality, in philosophical use, is what is, as opposed to appearance, illusion, or mere convention. The word is bland until you ask whose test of the real is in force: Parmenidean being, Platonic Forms, Aristotelian substances, atoms and void, or a Dao prior to names. 'Reality' is often a prize term for a metaphysical view.
