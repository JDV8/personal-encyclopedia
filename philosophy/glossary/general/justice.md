# Justice

Justice is the virtue or the structure of giving each their due, of lawful order, or of a harmonious soul and city — the exact filling varies. Plato's Republic, Aristotle's long treatment in Ethics V, Confucian yi (rightness) and government by virtue, and Mohist impartiality are all theories of justice or of its neighbors. 'Justice is the advantage of the stronger' is the challenge those theories have to meet.
