# Being

Being is both the fact that something is and the various ways in which things are. Parmenides makes 'it is' the only path; Aristotle distinguishes senses of being; Plotinus places the One beyond being. 'Being' is not a single obvious object. Much of Greek metaphysics is the work of untangling the word.
