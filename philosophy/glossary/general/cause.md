# Cause

A cause, in the broad Greek sense (aitia), is a 'because': an answer to why a thing is as it is. Aristotle's four causes (material, formal, efficient, final) are four kinds of because, not only 'what pushed it.' Modern 'cause' often means only the efficient cause. When reading ancient texts, keep the broader sense available.
