# Contradiction

A contradiction is the holding, or the asserting, of both a claim and its denial in the same respect. Aristotle treats a principle of non-contradiction as a first principle of being and of speech. Apparent contradictions may dissolve when respects are distinguished — which is part of what dialectic is for. Paradoxes (Zeno, the School of Names) test the edges of this idea.
