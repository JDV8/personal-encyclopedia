# Reason

Reason is the capacity to give and follow accounts, to infer, and (in some theories) to see first principles or to rule the rest of the soul. Logos, nous, and phronesis are more precise Greek tools; 'reason' in English can blur them. Chinese texts have their own vocabularies of patterning, heart-mind, and knowing, which should not be automatically translated as 'Reason' with a capital letter.
