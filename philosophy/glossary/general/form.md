# Form

Form, in this encyclopedia, usually means either Plato's separate intelligible eidos or Aristotle's organizing principle of a substance (the shape of the statue, the soul of the animal). The English word has to cover both, which is why the Greek and the concept lessons matter. It is not, in these texts, a mere shape on a surface.
