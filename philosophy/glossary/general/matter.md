# Matter

Matter is that of which a thing is made, or that which can take on form — bronze of the statue, the body of the living thing, and, at the limit in Aristotle, a prime matter never found bare. Ancient matter is not automatically the matter of modern physics. Atomists' atoms are matter of a different design: already formed bits, not a bare potential.
