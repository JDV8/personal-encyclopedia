# Opinion

Opinion (often Plato's doxa) is a judgment that may be true or false, typically about the changing or the sensible, and that falls short of knowledge even when true — unless, as in the Meno, it is 'tied down' by an account. The word in English can also mean a mere preference; the philosophical sense is stronger.
