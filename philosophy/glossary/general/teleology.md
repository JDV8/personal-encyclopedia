# Teleology

Teleology is explanation by ends or purposes: a thing is understood by what it is for. Aristotle's final cause is the classic Greek form; an acorn is intelligible as on the way to being an oak. Teleology in nature was a main target of early modern science. Not every 'for the sake of' is a conscious intention.
