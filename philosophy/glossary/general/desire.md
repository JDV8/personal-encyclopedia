# Desire

Desire is a directed want — for food, honor, knowledge, or the beloved. Ethics is often a theory of which desires to educate, reduce, or follow. Platonic eros can be climbed; Stoic and Epicurean therapies sort or correct desire; Confucian and Daoist texts worry about desire's excess and its political exploitation. Desire is not only a psychological fact; it is a field of training.
