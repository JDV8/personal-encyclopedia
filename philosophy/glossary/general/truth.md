# Truth

Truth is, at a first pass, the getting it right of a thought or a statement: saying of what is that it is, and of what is not that it is not, in a classical formulation close to Aristotle. Philosophers then disagree about whether truth is correspondence, coherence, disclosure (aletheia), or something else. Ancient skepticism complicates how far we can claim it.
