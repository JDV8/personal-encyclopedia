# Idealism

Idealism, in later usage, is the family of views that treat mind, ideas, or Forms as more fundamental than matter, or that treat the world as somehow mind-dependent. Plato is sometimes called an idealist because of the Forms, though his Forms are not 'ideas in a human mind.' Prefer 'Forms' or 'Intellect' when those are what is meant.
