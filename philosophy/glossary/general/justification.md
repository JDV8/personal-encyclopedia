# Justification

Justification is the standing a belief or claim has when it is well supported by reasons or evidence — a later, especially modern, name for a problem already visible in Plato's 'account' (logos) and in Aristotle's demonstration. Skeptics press how far justification can be completed without circularity or an arbitrary stop.
