# Dualism

Dualism is any view that takes two kinds of thing as fundamental — most often mind and body, or Form and sensible. Plato's soul/body contrast in the Phaedo is a classic (if not always consistent) dualism; Aristotle's hylomorphism is a deliberate alternative. Yin and yang are usually complementary processes, not two independent substances.
