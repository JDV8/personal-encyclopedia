# Will

Will, as a later name, is the faculty of choosing or initiating action. Ancient pieces of the problem include Aristotelian prohairesis (deliberate choice), Stoic assent, and debates about akrasia. A single faculty called 'the will' is more at home in later Latin and Christian philosophy. Prefer the author's own term when you can.
