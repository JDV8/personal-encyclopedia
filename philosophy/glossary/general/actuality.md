# Actuality

Actuality is the fulfillment of a potential: the oak as what the seed was toward, the statue as what the bronze could be, activity as opposed to mere capacity. Aristotle also calls the unmoved mover pure actuality. The pair with potentiality is more precise than the English 'real' versus 'possible.'
