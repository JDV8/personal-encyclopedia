# Identity

Identity is a thing's being itself, and remaining itself through change or over time. The problem of change is in part a problem of identity: is the river the same river? Is the person the same person? 'Identity' also names, in logic, the relation expressed by 'is' when we say that A is A, as opposed to predication ('A is pale').
