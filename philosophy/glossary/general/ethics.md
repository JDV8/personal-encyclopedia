# Ethics

Ethics asks how one should live, what is good or right, and what sort of person is worth becoming. It includes virtue, duty, consequence, ritual, and cultivation. Aristotle's mean, the Stoic claim that virtue is the only good, Confucius's ren and li, and Mencius's sprouts are different ethical programs, not merely different cultures.
