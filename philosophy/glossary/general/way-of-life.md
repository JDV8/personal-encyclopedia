# Way of life

A way of life, as Pierre Hadot and others have stressed, is what many ancient philosophies claimed to be: a trained pattern of perception, desire, and action, not only a theory. Cynic poverty, Epicurean friendship, Stoic exercises, Confucian ritual self-cultivation, and Zhuangzian ease are all ways of life in this sense. Doctrine still matters; it is not replaced by lifestyle.
