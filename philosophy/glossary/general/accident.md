# Accident

An accident, in the Aristotelian sense, is a feature a thing can gain or lose without ceasing to be that thing — a sitting Socrates, a white horse's particular posture. It contrasts with essence or substance. 'Accident' here is not a mishap; it is a technical term of predication and of being.
