# Fatalism

Fatalism is the view that what will happen will happen regardless of what we do, so that deliberation and effort are empty. Mozi attacks a version of this as sapping the people's work. It is close to, but not the same as, determinism: one can hold that events have causes and still hold that our efforts are among the causes.
