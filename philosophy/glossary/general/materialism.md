# Materialism

Materialism is the view that what is fundamentally real is matter, body, or physical stuff — in ancient terms, often atoms and void, or a bodily pneuma, rather than separate immaterial Forms or souls. Democritus and Epicurus can be described this way, with caution about Stoic pneuma, which is bodily but also rational and divine.
