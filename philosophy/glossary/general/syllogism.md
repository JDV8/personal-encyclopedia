# Syllogism

A syllogism is a form of argument in which, Aristotle held, from two premises a conclusion follows of necessity — classically, in the pattern of terms such as 'all As are Bs.' It is the core of the Organon. The word is sometimes used loosely for any tight argument; the technical sense is narrower.
