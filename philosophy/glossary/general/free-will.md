# Free will

Free will, as a later name, is the problem of whether and how agents can be the origin of their actions in a way that supports praise, blame, and responsibility. Aristotle on voluntary action and akrasia, the Epicurean swerve, and the Stoic account of assent are ancient pieces of this problem. Do not assume a modern 'contra-causal' will as the only ancient option.
