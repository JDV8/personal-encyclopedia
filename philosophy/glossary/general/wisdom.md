# Wisdom

Wisdom may be theoretical insight (sophia), practical judgment (phronesis, or Confucian zhi in some uses), or a lived sageliness (the shengren). English 'wisdom' blurs these. Ancient schools often treat wisdom as the point of philosophy, and then disagree about whether it is contemplation, political skill, or a settled mind.
