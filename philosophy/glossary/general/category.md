# Category

A category, in Aristotle's sense, is one of the most general ways of saying that something is: substance, quantity, quality, relation, and the rest. The word later comes to mean any basic class in a scheme. When this encyclopedia points to 'the categories,' the Aristotelian list is usually meant.
