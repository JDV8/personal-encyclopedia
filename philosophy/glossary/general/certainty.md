# Certainty

Certainty is a state or a status of being beyond rational doubt, or of being such that error is impossible. Stoic 'cognitive impressions' and some readings of demonstration aim at it; skeptics deny that we have it about non-evident things. It is a higher bar than knowledge as 'well-justified true belief' in some modern senses, and it should not be casually required of every ancient claim to know.
