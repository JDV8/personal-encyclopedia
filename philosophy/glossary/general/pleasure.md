# Pleasure

Pleasure is a felt enjoyment or satisfaction. Ancient ethics disagrees about its place: a crude hedonism takes it as the good; Epicurus identifies the highest pleasure with absence of pain and disturbance; Aristotle treats pleasure as properly completing activity, not as the good itself; Stoics rank it among indifferents. The first question is always 'which pleasure, and in the service of what?'
