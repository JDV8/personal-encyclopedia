# Contingent

The contingent is what is so but might have been otherwise — most particular facts, most historical events, and, in many systems, the sensible world as a whole. It is the counterpart of the necessary. Aristotle's accidental, and some readings of the Timaeus's 'likely story,' belong near this idea without always using the later word.
