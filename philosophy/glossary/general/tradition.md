# Tradition

A tradition is a handed-on practice of texts, interpretations, and institutions. Philosophical traditions (Platonic, Aristotelian, Confucian, Daoist) are arguments over time, not single doctrines. This encyclopedia treats 'traditional ascription' (Moses wrote the Torah; Confucius wrote the Wings of the Changes) as one kind of historical claim, to be set beside scholarly ones, not as a synonym for 'false' or for 'true.'
