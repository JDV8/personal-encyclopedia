# Universal

A universal is what can be common to many particulars: horse, justice, redness. The metaphysical problem is whether universals are real apart from things (as in some readings of Plato's Forms), only in things (a common Aristotelian pattern), or only in names and thoughts. The School of Names and later Mohists worry a related problem in the vocabulary of names and kinds.
