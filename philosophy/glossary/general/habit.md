# Habit

Habit, in ethics, is a settled disposition formed by repeated action. Aristotle's hexis and the Confucian emphasis on practice, ritual, and teaching make habit central: you become just by doing just acts, under guidance. Habit is not mindless routine in these theories; it is how character is built, and it still needs practical judgment in the particular case.
