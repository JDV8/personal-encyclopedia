# A posteriori

A posteriori knowledge depends on experience. The Latin contrast with a priori is later than the ancient texts in this encyclopedia, but the issue is old: how much of what we know comes through the senses, and what (if anything) does not. Aristotle's 'from things more known to us' is a related, not identical, distinction.
