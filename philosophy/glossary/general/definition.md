# Definition

A definition states what a thing is, ideally by giving its essence or a set of marks that distinguish it. Socratic inquiry often starts by demanding one; Aristotelian science uses definitions as starting-points; the School of Names and later Mohists worry about how names define kinds. A bad definition is one of the main ways thought goes wrong in this encyclopedia's texts.
