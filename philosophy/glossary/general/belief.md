# Belief

Belief, in a broad sense, is taking something to be so, with or without knowledge. In some translations of Plato, doxa covers both opinion and belief. Stoic 'assent' is a close practical cousin: the move by which an impression becomes yours. Not every tradition wants belief as the central cognitive attitude (Pyrrhonists try to do with less).
