# Happiness

Happiness in modern English is often a feeling. Ancient eudaimonia is closer to flourishing over a life, and Confucian success is closer to a completed person in a well-ordered world. When a translation says 'happiness,' check whether the source word is eudaimonia, a pleasure term, or something else. See the concept lesson on eudaimonia.
