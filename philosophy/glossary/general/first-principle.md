# First principle

A first principle is a starting-point that is not argued from something deeper in the same science: an arche, a basic law of thought, a self-evident or indubitable truth, or a Way prior to names. The search for first principles is the method of this encyclopedia as well as a topic inside it. See the concept lesson on arche.
