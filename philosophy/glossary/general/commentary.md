# Commentary

A commentary is a later text written on an earlier one, often becoming the form in which the earlier book is actually read. Guo Xiang on the Zhuangzi, Wang Bi on the Daodejing and the Changes, and Zhu Xi on the Four Books are decisive examples. To read 'the Analects' after Zhu Xi is already to read a commented book. This encyclopedia flags that layering when it matters.
