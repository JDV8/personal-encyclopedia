# Dialectic

Dialectic is argument that proceeds by question and answer, or by testing opposed claims. Socratic elenchus, Platonic dialectic as the highest study, and Aristotelian dialectic from reputable opinions are related practices, not one method. The word later takes on Hegelian and other senses; in this encyclopedia the ancient senses are primary.
