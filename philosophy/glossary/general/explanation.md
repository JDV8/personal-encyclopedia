# Explanation

An explanation answers a why or a how. Aristotle's four causes are four kinds of explanation; correlative cosmology explains by placing things in cycles and correspondences; atomism explains by rearrangement. Much philosophical disagreement is disagreement about what would count as explaining, not only about which explanation is true.
