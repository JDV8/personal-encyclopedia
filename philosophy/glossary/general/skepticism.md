# Skepticism

Skepticism is a family of stances that withhold or challenge claims to knowledge. Ancient Pyrrhonism suspends judgment and seeks tranquility; Academic skeptics attacked Stoic certainty; modern skepticism often targets the foundations of science or the external world. The word is not a synonym for cynicism.
