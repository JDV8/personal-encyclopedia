# Aesthetics

Aesthetics, in the later name, is the philosophy of art and of beauty (and of related values such as the ugly or the sublime). Plato's treatments of beauty, eros, and poetry, and Aristotle's Poetics, belong to this branch before it had the name. Early Chinese thought often folds beauty into ritual, music, and pattern rather than into a separate science of art.
