# A priori

A priori knowledge is knowledge independent of particular sensory experience, as later philosophers use the term (the Latin and Kantian vocabulary is not Aristotle's). Mathematical knowledge and, in some readings, knowledge of Forms or of first principles have been called a priori. Use the label as a comparison, and prefer each tradition's own terms (nous, demonstration, innate knowing) when they do the work.
