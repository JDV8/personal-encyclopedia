# Atomism

Atomism is the view that reality consists of indivisible bodies moving in a void, and that macroscopic change is rearrangement. Leucippus and Democritus framed it as a reply to Parmenides; Epicurus put it to ethical work. It is a species of materialism, but not modern chemistry.
