# Metaphysics

Metaphysics is the branch of philosophy that asks what reality is like at the most general level: what there is, what a thing is, whether change is real, what a cause is, and what a mind, a person, or a god would be if they are real. The name is later and editorial (the books 'after the physics' in Aristotle's corpus), but the questions are already those of the early Greek search for an arche and of Chinese arguments about Dao, qi, and Heaven.
