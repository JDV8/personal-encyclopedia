# Necessary

A necessary truth or being is one that could not have been otherwise. Parmenides' what-is, logical consequence, and, in some theologies, God or the unmoved mover have been called necessary. The contrast is with the contingent: what happens to be so but might have been otherwise. Modal vocabulary is easy to anachronize; watch how each author marks 'must' and 'cannot.'
