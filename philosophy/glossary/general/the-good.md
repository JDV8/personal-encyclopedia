# The good

The good is what is worth aiming at, or what makes a life or an action choiceworthy. Plato's Form of the Good, Aristotle's eudaimonia, Stoic virtue, Epicurean pleasure as lack of disturbance, and Confucian ren are rival fillings of this role. 'The good' is the prize term of ethics; always ask 'good for whom, and as what?'
