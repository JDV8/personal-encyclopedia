# Soul

Soul (psyche, and in other traditions hun/po or the heart-mind xin) is the principle of life, of thought, or of a self that might survive death — or some mix of these. Presocratic and Aristotelian souls can be very close to 'what makes this body alive'; Platonic and some religious souls are more easily separable. Always ask which job the word is doing in the text at hand.
