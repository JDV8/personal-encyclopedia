# Character

Character is the settled shape of a person's ethical dispositions — what you can reliably expect them to care about and to do. It is the main object of virtue ethics, as opposed to an ethics that looks only at isolated acts or at rules. Both Aristotelian and Confucian paths are character-centered, with different accounts of nature and of training.
