# Determinism

Determinism is the view that, given the past and the laws (or the nature) of the world, only one future is possible. Stoic fate is a major ancient form; Epicurus is reported to have introduced a swerve of atoms partly to loosen it. The word is later than these debates. It should not be confused with fatalism, which often adds that effort is pointless.
