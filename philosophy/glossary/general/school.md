# School

A school, in ancient philosophy, is a lineage of teachers, texts, and practices — the Academy, the Lyceum, the Stoa, the Garden, the Ru, the Mohists — not only a set of doctrines. Ways of life, institutional places, and rivalries belong to the idea. 'School of Names' and 'Hundred Schools' are later bibliographic labels as well as real clusters.
