# Virtue

Virtue is a stable excellence of character or of a capacity — in Greek, arete; in Confucian contexts, often de or one of the named virtues such as ren and yi. It is not only a single good action. Ancient ethics is largely a theory of virtues, not only of rules or of outcomes, though outcomes and rules appear inside virtue theories too.
