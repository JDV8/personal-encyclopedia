# Political philosophy

Political philosophy asks what justifies authority, what a good political order is, and what we owe strangers and rulers. Plato's Republic, Aristotle's Politics, Confucian government by virtue, Mozi's impartial care, and Legalist statecraft are all answers to political questions, sometimes sharply opposed.
