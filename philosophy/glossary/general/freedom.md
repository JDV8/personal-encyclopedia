# Freedom

Freedom may mean political liberty, the legal status opposite slavery, or an inner independence of the ruling faculty (Epictetus). These should not be collapsed. A Stoic slave can be called 'free' in the inner sense without any endorsement of legal slavery. Cynic and Daoist freedoms, again, have different enemies: convention, forced usefulness, or the court.
