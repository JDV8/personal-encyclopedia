# Fallacy

A fallacy is a bad argument that can look like a good one: an invalid inference, a tricky ambiguity, or a rhetorical substitution of force or insult for a reason. Aristotle's Sophistical Refutations is an early catalogue. Naming fallacies is useful only if you can also show where the reasoning fails.
