# Pluralism

Pluralism, in metaphysics, is the view that there are many fundamental kinds or many fundamental things. Empedocles' four roots, Anaxagoras's mixtures, and atomism are pluralist replies to Parmenidean monism. In ethics and politics the word has a later, different life. Here the metaphysical sense is usually in view.
