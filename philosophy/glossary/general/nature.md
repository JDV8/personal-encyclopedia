# Nature

Nature (physis, xing, 'the natural') may mean what grows of itself, the world's order, a thing's essence, or a standard opposed to convention (nomos). 'Live according to nature' is a very different slogan in Cynic and in Stoic mouths. Chinese xing (human nature) is a related, not identical, debate. Always ask which nature is being named.
