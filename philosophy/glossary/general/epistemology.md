# Epistemology

Epistemology is the branch that asks what knowledge is, how it is possible, and how it differs from opinion, appearance, or lucky true belief. Plato's split between knowledge and opinion, the later Mohist interest in standards of judgment, and the Greek Skeptics' suspension of judgment all belong here.
