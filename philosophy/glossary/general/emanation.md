# Emanation

Emanation (or procession) is the Neoplatonic account of how reality depends on a first principle: from the One proceeds Intellect, from Intellect Soul, without any lessening of the source. It is not a creation-from-nothing in time. Plotinus is the main author on this path; later Islamic and medieval Latin thinkers inherit versions of the scheme.
