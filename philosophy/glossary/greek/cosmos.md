# *Cosmos*

Cosmos means an ordered world, as opposed to chaos or a mere heap. Later tradition often associates the word's philosophical use with the Pythagoreans. To call the world a cosmos is already to claim that it has a structure that can, in principle, be understood — a claim shared, in different vocabularies, by almost every school on this path.
