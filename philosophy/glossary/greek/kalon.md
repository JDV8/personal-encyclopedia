# *Kalon*

Kalon is the beautiful, the fine, or the noble — an aesthetic and ethical word at once. The Symposium's climb is toward the kalon itself. Aristotle's virtuous person acts for the sake of the kalon. English has to choose among 'beautiful,' 'noble,' and 'fine,' and none is perfect.
