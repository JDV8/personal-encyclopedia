# *Sophrosyne*

Sophrosyne is temperance or moderation: the virtue of being sound-minded about bodily pleasures and, more broadly, of self-restraint and good measure. It is one of the cardinal virtues in the Republic (the agreement of the parts of the soul and city about who should rule). English 'temperance' can sound narrower than the Greek.
