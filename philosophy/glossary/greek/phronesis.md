# *Phronesis*

Phronesis is practical wisdom: the capacity to deliberate well about what is good for a human life and to see the mean in the particular case. In Aristotle it is indispensable to ethical virtue; the two require each other. It is not the same as sophia (theoretical wisdom) or as cleverness about means to any end whatever.
