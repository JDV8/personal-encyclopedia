# *Psyche*

Psyche is soul: in early uses, a principle of life and of breath; in Plato, a subject that can be immortal, tripartite, and educated; in Aristotle, the first actuality of a living body. 'Soul' in English often sounds more religious than psyche does in a biological chapter of Aristotle. Check the text.
