# *Askesis*

Askesis is training or exercise: the disciplined practice by which a Cynic or Stoic (and, later, a monk) shapes desire and perception. English 'asceticism' comes from the word but often sounds narrower, as mere denial. In these schools askesis is a method, not only a refusal.
