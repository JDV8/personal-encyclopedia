# *Eros*

Eros is love as desire, often (in Plato) a lack that seeks the beautiful, able to climb from bodies to the Beautiful itself. The Symposium and Phaedrus are the main texts. It is distinguished from philia and from later Christian agape, and it is not only 'romantic love' in a modern sense.
