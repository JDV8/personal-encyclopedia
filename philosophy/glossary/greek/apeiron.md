# *Apeiron*

The apeiron is the indefinite, unbounded, or unlimited — Anaximander's arche, a source that is not already one of the familiar elements. The word later also names the infinite in Aristotle's physics (mostly potential). In the Milesian setting, the point is that the source of definite things may itself lack a determining quality.
