# *Techne*

Techne is a craft or art: a teachable, reasoned capacity to produce or to act (medicine, shipbuilding, rhetoric if it is a craft). Plato's Gorgias asks whether rhetoric is a techne or only a knack. Techne is distinguished from, and sometimes ranked below, episteme or phronesis, depending on the author.
