# *Episteme*

Episteme is knowledge or scientific understanding, as opposed to doxa. In Plato it is typically of Forms; in Aristotle it is often demonstrative knowledge of causes and of what cannot be otherwise. English 'science' and 'knowledge' each capture part of the word.
