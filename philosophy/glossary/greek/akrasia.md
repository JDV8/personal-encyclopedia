# *Akrasia*

Akrasia is lack of self-control, acting against one's better judgment. Socrates is often read as denying it (if you really knew, you would do the good). Aristotle analyzes it at length in Ethics VII and treats it as a real phenomenon. The word is the classic complication of 'virtue is knowledge.'
