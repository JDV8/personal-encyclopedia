# *Sophia*

Sophia is wisdom, especially theoretical or expert wisdom — the word inside 'philosophy.' In Aristotle it is the highest intellectual virtue, knowledge of first principles and causes, distinguished from phronesis. In ordinary Greek it can also mean skill or cleverness, as in the 'Sophists,' the paid experts of wisdom.
