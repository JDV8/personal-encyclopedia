# *Mimesis*

Mimesis is imitation or representation, the key term of the Poetics (and of Republic X's critique of poetry). Tragedy is a mimesis of a serious action. The word is broader than 'copying' and includes the way a plot represents a possible human action, not only a likeness of a face.
