# *Prohairesis*

Prohairesis is deliberate choice in Aristotle: a desire formed by deliberation, central to responsible action. In Epictetus it becomes the ruling faculty, the 'moral purpose' that is up to us, almost the true self. The same word therefore lives in two related but different psychologies.
