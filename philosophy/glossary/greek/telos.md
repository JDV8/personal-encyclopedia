# *Telos*

Telos is an end, goal, or completion: that for the sake of which a thing is or acts. It is the keyword of Aristotelian teleology and of the final cause. A life, a city, and an organ can have a telos. The word is also behind 'teleology' and, more loosely, behind talk of a complete life in ethics.
