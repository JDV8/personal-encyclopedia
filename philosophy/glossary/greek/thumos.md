# *Thumos*

Thumos is spirit, spiritedness, or the seat of anger, pride, and the love of honor. In the Republic it is the middle part of the soul, allied (when educated) with reason against appetite. It is not 'the will' and not mere rage. English translations wander among 'spirit,' 'anger,' and 'heart.'
