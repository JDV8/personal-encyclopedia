# Hylomorphism

Hylomorphism is the Aristotelian view that natural substances are compounds of form (morphe, eidos) and matter (hyle). A living body is not a machine plus a ghost, and not a mere heap of stuff. Soul is the form of a living body. The term itself is scholarly and later; the doctrine is in the Physics, Metaphysics, and De Anima.
