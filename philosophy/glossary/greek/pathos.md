# *Pathos*

Pathos (plural pathe) is a passion, emotion, or being-affected: anger, fear, pity, and the rest. Aristotle analyzes emotions in the Rhetoric; Stoics treat the passions as faulty judgments; tragedy, in the Poetics, works through pity and fear. The word also means 'suffering' and, in rhetoric, the emotional proof.
