# *Aporia*

Aporia is puzzlement, an impasse, a path (poros) that has run out. It is the typical ending of early Socratic dialogues, and a productive one: you no longer think you know what you did not know. Later Aristotle also starts inquiries from aporiai, the knots that have to be untied.
