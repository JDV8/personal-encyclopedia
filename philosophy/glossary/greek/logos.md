# *Logos*

Logos can mean word, speech, story, proportion, reason, or a defendable account. Heraclitus uses it for a common measure of the world; Aristotle's logic inspects logoi; the Stoics identify a cosmic logos with a rational fire or with Zeus. The New Testament's 'In the beginning was the Logos' inherits a thick word. It is not automatically identical with Dao.
