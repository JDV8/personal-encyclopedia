# *Metempsychosis*

Metempsychosis is the transmigration of souls through different bodies, human and animal — a teaching associated with Pythagoras and, in a related form, with Empedocles' wandering daimon, and used by Plato in myths of recollection and of Er. It is a claim about the soul's career, not a metaphor only, in the sources that assert it.
