# *Physis*

Physis is nature: what grows or holds of itself, a thing's constitution, or the world's order. It is famously contrasted with nomos (law, convention) in fifth-century debate. 'Live according to nature' is a Cynic and Stoic slogan whose meaning depends on which physis is meant. It is related to, but not the same as, Chinese xing.
