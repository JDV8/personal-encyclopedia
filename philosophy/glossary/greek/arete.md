# *Arete*

Arete is excellence or virtue: the distinctive goodness of a thing at being what it is, and, in ethics, of a human life. A knife, a horse, and a person can have arete. It is the central term of Greek virtue ethics, later specified as habit, knowledge, or the only good, depending on the school.
