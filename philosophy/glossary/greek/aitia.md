# *Aitia*

Aitia (or aition) is a cause or a 'because' — an answer to why. Aristotle's four aitiai are four kinds of explanation, not only efficient pushes. In legal Greek the word also means blame or responsibility, a reminder that 'cause' and 'charge' are neighbors.
