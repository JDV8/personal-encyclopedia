# *Arche*

Arche means a beginning, source, or ruling principle. In early Greek philosophy it names whatever is taken to be fundamental: water, the apeiron, air, number, or what-is. The particular answers matter less, at first, than the form of the question. See the concept lesson for the full path from the Milesians onward.
