# *Doxa*

Doxa is opinion, belief, or seeming — in Parmenides the 'way of appearance,' in Plato the cognitive state that tracks sensibles and falls short of episteme, even when true. It is not always an insult; the Meno takes true doxa seriously as a guide to action, if it can be tied down.
