# *Hedone*

Hedone is pleasure. Epicurus makes a carefully understood hedone (as lack of pain and disturbance) the good; Aristotle denies that pleasure is the good but treats it as completing activity; Plato's Philebus is a late, careful inquiry into the mix of pleasure and intelligence in the good life. 'Hedonism' as a slur is usually coarser than these texts.
