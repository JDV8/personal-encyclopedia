# *Aletheia*

Aletheia is truth, sometimes heard as un-concealment. Parmenides' way of truth is a path of what-is; later Heideggerian readings should not be assumed in every occurrence. In ordinary philosophical Greek it is often simply the contrast term to falsehood or to doxa.
