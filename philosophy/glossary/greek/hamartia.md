# *Hamartia*

Hamartia in the Poetics is the tragic error or missing of the mark from which the hero falls — often not a Victorian 'tragic flaw' of character, and not automatically a sin. The word's later Christian sense of sin should not be read back automatically into Aristotle's account of plot.
