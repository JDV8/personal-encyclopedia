# *Theoria*

Theoria is contemplation or spectatorship: in Aristotle, the highest activity of the intellect, the core of the contemplative life ranked first in Ethics X. It is not 'a theory' in the modern sense, though 'theory' comes from the word. It is an activity, not a set of sentences.
