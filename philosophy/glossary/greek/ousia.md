# *Ousia*

Ousia, usually translated substance or essence, is Aristotle's name for what is primarily real: this horse, this person, or, in a harder register, form and essence. The word is from the verb 'to be.' Latin substantia and essentia split what Greek holds in one term, which is why translations wander.
