# *Oikeiosis*

Oikeiosis is appropriation or familiarization: the Stoic account of how a living thing is oriented to its own constitution and how, in rational beings, concern can extend from self to family, city, and humanity. It is a bridge from psychology to cosmopolitan ethics, not a modern 'instinct' slogan only.
