# *Entelecheia*

Entelecheia is a term closely related to energeia, often rendered 'actuality' or 'completeness' — a having-its-end-in-itself. Soul, in De Anima, is the first entelechy of a natural body that has life potentially. The exact shade relative to energeia is a scholarly issue; both oppose mere potentiality.
