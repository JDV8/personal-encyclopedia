# Academic skepticism

Academic skepticism is the skeptical phase of Plato's Academy, associated with Arcesilaus and Carneades, which argued especially against Stoic claims to certainty and sometimes allowed 'probable' impressions as a basis for action. Sextus distinguishes it from Pyrrhonism, not always fairly. It is skepticism from inside Plato's school, not a denial of Plato's existence.
