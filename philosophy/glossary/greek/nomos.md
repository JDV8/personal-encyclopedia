# *Nomos*

Nomos is law, custom, or convention — what holds by human institution rather than by physis. The physis/nomos debate is a central fifth-century argument about justice, the gods, and whether shame is natural. Sophists, tragedians, and Plato's Callicles all work this contrast. It is not a synonym for 'fake.'
