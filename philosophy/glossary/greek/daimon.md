# *Daimon*

A daimon is a divine or semi-divine power, not necessarily 'a demon' in the later Christian sense. Socrates' daimonion is an inner sign that turns him away from actions. Empedocles' fallen daimon wanders through bodies. In the Symposium, eros is a daimonic intermediary, not a full god. Translation as 'spirit' or 'demon' needs a warning label.
