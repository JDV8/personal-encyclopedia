# *Pneuma*

Pneuma is breath or spirit: in Stoic physics, a tensed, bodily, rational stuff that structures the cosmos and the soul, often identified with the active logos. It is not an immaterial ghost. Medical and earlier uses of the word (vital breath) sit in the background. Translations as 'spirit' can mislead toward a later, non-bodily sense.
