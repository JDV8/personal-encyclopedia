# *Clinamen*

The clinamen is the slight swerve of atoms, reported by Lucretius as an Epicurean addition that loosens strict determinism and makes room for freedom. We know it more from Lucretius than from Epicurus's own surviving letters, and its exact role is contested. It should not be treated as a cartoon of randomness.
