# *Anamnesis*

Anamnesis is recollection: Plato's claim, in the Meno and Phaedo, that learning is the soul's recovery of knowledge it already had, often knowledge of Forms. It is an answer to the paradox of inquiry. Whether to take it literally or as a picture of midwifed insight is a standing question.
