# *Catharsis*

Catharsis in the Poetics is the 'purification' or 'purgation' of pity and fear that tragedy is said to effect. The exact medical or ritual sense is still argued. Do not reduce the whole of the Poetics to this one obscure, famous word — but do not skip it either.
