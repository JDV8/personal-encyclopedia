# *Philia*

Philia is friendship or friendly love, a central topic of Nicomachean Ethics VIII–IX. Aristotle treats it as essential to a flourishing life and distinguishes friendships of utility, pleasure, and virtue. It is not eros, and not every modern 'friendship' is philia in this rich sense.
