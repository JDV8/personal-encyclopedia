# *Nous*

Nous is intellect, mind, or (in Anaxagoras and in Neoplatonism) a cosmic ordering mind. In Aristotle it is the capacity that grasps first principles, and, controversially, a possibly separable intellect in De Anima III. Plotinus makes Nous the second hypostasis, the living place of the Forms. Do not flatten all these into one 'reason.'
