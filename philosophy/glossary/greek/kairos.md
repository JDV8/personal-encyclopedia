# *Kairos*

Kairos is the timely, the opportune moment — a qualitative sense of time, as opposed to mere succession (chronos). It matters in rhetoric (when to speak), in ethics (the particular case), and in some medical and political writing. A mean, for Aristotle, is also a hitting of the timely mark.
