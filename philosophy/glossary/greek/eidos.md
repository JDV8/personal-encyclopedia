# *Eidos*

Eidos (plural eide) is form, look, or kind — the word behind Plato's Forms (also idea). In Aristotle it is the form of a substance, not a separate entity. The same Greek word therefore names the central dispute between the two. See the concept lessons on the Forms and on form and matter.
