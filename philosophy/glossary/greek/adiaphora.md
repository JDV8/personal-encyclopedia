# *Adiaphora*

Adiaphora are indifferents: in Stoic ethics, everything other than virtue and vice — health, wealth, even life. Some indifferents are 'preferred,' some 'dispreferred,' but they are not truly good or evil. The doctrine is the most counterintuitive, and most distinctive, piece of Stoic value theory.
