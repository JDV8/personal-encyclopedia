# *Autarkeia*

Autarkeia is self-sufficiency, a Cynic and also Aristotelian ideal (the complete life, the complete city). For the Cynics it means reducing wants until fortune has little to take. For Aristotle, a self-sufficient life still needs friends; autarkeia is not isolation.
