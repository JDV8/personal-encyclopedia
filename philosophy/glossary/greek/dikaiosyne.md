# *Dikaiosyne*

Dikaiosyne is justice, the virtue around which the Republic is built (each part doing its work) and which Aristotle divides into kinds in Ethics V (lawfulness, fairness, particular justice). It is the political and ethical keyword for 'right order among persons and in the soul.'
