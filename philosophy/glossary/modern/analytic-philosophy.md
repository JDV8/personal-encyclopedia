# Analytic philosophy

The tradition, dominant in English-speaking universities since the mid-twentieth century, that grew from Frege, Russell, Moore and Wittgenstein. It emphasizes clarity, argument, logical analysis and the study of language and meaning, often in dialogue with science. It includes logical positivism, ordinary-language philosophy, and later naturalistic metaphysics and philosophy of mind. Its contrast with "continental" philosophy is partly a matter of style and canon, and many philosophers now cross the divide.
