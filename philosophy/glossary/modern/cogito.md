# Cogito

Short for *cogito, ergo sum*, "I think, therefore I am", the first certainty Descartes reaches in his search for knowledge immune to doubt. The French form *je pense, donc je suis* appears in the *Discourse on Method* (1637); in the *Meditations* (1641) he puts it as "I am, I exist" being necessarily true whenever conceived. However radically I doubt, my doubting shows that I exist as a thinking thing. Critics asked whether "I" is warranted (Lichtenberg suggested "it thinks"), and whether it is an inference or an intuition.
