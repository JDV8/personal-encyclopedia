# Oikophilia

Roger Scruton's name (from Greek *oikos*, "home", and *philia*, "love") for the love of home: the attachment to a particular place, people and inherited way of life that, he argued, is the ordinary motive of conservation. Oikophilia is not, in his account, mere private comfort, and not the same as xenophobia. It is what makes people mind a landscape, a town, a legal tradition or a language, and pass them on. It is central to his conservatism and to his environmental writing (*Green Philosophy*, also published as *How to Think Seriously About the Planet*). He contrasted it with [oikophobia](oikophobia.md), the educated repudiation of home.
