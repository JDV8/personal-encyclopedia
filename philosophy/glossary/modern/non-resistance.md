# Non-resistance

Tolstoy's interpretation of Jesus's command "resist not evil" (Matthew 5:39) as an absolute rule: never use violence, even against evil, even in self-defence or in the service of the state. In *The Kingdom of God Is Within You* (1894) he argued that this condemns armies, courts, police and patriotism. He drew on American non-resistants such as William Lloyd Garrison and Adin Ballou. Gandhi, who corresponded with Tolstoy, developed it into *satyagraha*, active non-violent resistance.
