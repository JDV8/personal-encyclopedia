# The absurd

In Camus's *The Myth of Sisyphus* (1942), the confrontation between the human need for meaning, unity and clarity and "the unreasonable silence of the world". The absurd lies in neither side alone but in their relation. Camus rejects suicide and the "leap" of faith as escapes and argues for living with the absurd in revolt, freedom and passion. Kierkegaard used "the absurd" differently, for the paradox that faith embraces. The "theatre of the absurd" (Beckett, Ionesco) took the word to the stage.
