# Providence

God's foresight and governance of the world, by which all things are ordered to their ends. Boethius, in *The Consolation of Philosophy*, distinguishes providence (the plan as it exists in God's mind) from fate (its unfolding in time), and argues that God's eternal knowledge does not destroy human freedom. Christian, Jewish and Islamic thinkers debated how providence relates to chance, evil and free will. Secular echoes appear in Hegel's "cunning of reason" and in Adam Smith's "invisible hand".
