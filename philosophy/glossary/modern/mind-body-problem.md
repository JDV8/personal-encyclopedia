# Mind–body problem

The question of how mental states (thoughts, sensations, feelings) relate to the physical body and brain. Descartes framed its modern form: mind is a thinking, unextended substance and body an extended, unthinking one, yet they interact. Princess Elisabeth of Bohemia asked how an immaterial mind could move a body. Responses include Spinoza's dual-aspect monism, Leibniz's pre-established harmony, Malebranche's occasionalism, idealism, materialism, behaviourism, identity theory, functionalism and contemporary debates about consciousness.
