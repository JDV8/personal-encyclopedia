# Scapegoat mechanism

In René Girard's theory, the process by which a community in the grip of escalating mimetic rivalry restores peace by uniting against a single victim, held responsible for the crisis and expelled or killed. The unanimity of the accusation conceals the victim's arbitrariness; the peace that follows seems to confirm the victim's guilt and power, and the victim is later sacralized. Girard held that myth, ritual sacrifice and prohibition derive from it, and that the Bible exposes it. The name comes from the goat of Leviticus 16.
