# Collective unconscious

Jung's term for a layer of the unconscious psyche that is not acquired through personal experience but shared by all humans, inherited and containing archetypes. It contrasts with the personal unconscious, made up mainly of forgotten or repressed material and "complexes". Jung argued for it from parallels between patients' fantasies and ancient myths they could not have known. Critics hold that such parallels can be explained by shared experience and culture.
