# Repression

In Freud's psychoanalysis, the defence mechanism by which thoughts, memories or wishes unacceptable to the ego are kept out of consciousness. Repressed contents remain active and return in disguised forms: dreams, slips, jokes and neurotic symptoms. Freud called the theory of repression "the corner-stone on which the whole structure of psychoanalysis rests". Experimental psychologists have debated whether repression, especially of traumatic memories, exists in the form Freud described.
