# Essence and existence

The distinction between *what* a thing is (its essence or nature) and the fact *that* it is (its existence). Avicenna argued that in all created things essence does not include existence: I can understand what a phoenix is without knowing whether any exists. Only in God, the Necessary Existent, are essence and existence identical. Aquinas developed this into a central thesis of his metaphysics. The pair returns in existentialism, where Sartre claims that for human beings "existence precedes essence".
