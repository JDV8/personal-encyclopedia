# Utilitarianism

The ethical theory that the right action is the one that produces the greatest overall happiness or well-being. Jeremy Bentham founded it as a reforming doctrine, with his felicific calculus of pleasures and pains; John Stuart Mill refined it, distinguishing higher and lower pleasures; Henry Sidgwick gave it systematic form. It is consequentialist (judging acts by outcomes), welfarist and impartial. Critics, from Kant's followers to Rawls, object that it can sacrifice individuals for aggregate gains and ignores the separateness of persons.
