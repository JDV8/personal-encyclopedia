# Intentionality

The "aboutness" or directedness of mental states toward objects: a belief is about something, a fear is of something, a perception is of something. Franz Brentano made it the mark of the mental in *Psychology from an Empirical Standpoint* (1874), reviving a scholastic term. Husserl made the structure of intentional consciousness the subject of phenomenology. It is unrelated to "intention" in the sense of purpose. Analytic philosophers (Searle, Dennett, Fodor) debate how a physical system can have intentional states.
