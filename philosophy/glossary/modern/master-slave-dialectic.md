# Master–slave dialectic

A famous section of Hegel's *Phenomenology of Spirit* (often translated "lordship and bondage"). Two self-consciousnesses each seek recognition and fight; the one who fears death submits and becomes the slave (bondsman). The master gains recognition, but only from someone he does not recognize, and depends on the slave's labour; the slave, through fear and work on things, gains self-consciousness and independence. It influenced Marx, Alexandre Kojève's lectures, Sartre, de Beauvoir and Frantz Fanon.
