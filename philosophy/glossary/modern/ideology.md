# Ideology

In Marx and Engels's sense, a system of ideas that expresses and legitimizes the interests of a dominant class while presenting them as universal or natural: "the ideas of the ruling class are in every epoch the ruling ideas" (*The German Ideology*). Later thinkers (Gramsci on hegemony, Althusser, the Frankfurt School) developed theories of how ideology reproduces social order. Arendt used the word for totalizing systems that explain everything from one premise. In ordinary usage it means any set of political beliefs.
