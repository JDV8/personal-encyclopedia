# Theodicy

A justification of God's goodness and justice in view of evil, a word coined by Leibniz in his *Theodicy* (1710), from Greek *theos*, god, and *dike*, justice. Leibniz argued that God created the best of all possible worlds, which contains evil only because a world without it would be worse overall. Voltaire mocked this in *Candide* after the Lisbon earthquake of 1755; Kant argued all theodicies fail. Augustine's free-will defence and John Hick's "soul-making" theodicy are other classic types.
