# Übermensch

Nietzsche's term, translated "overman" or "superman", introduced in *Thus Spoke Zarathustra* (1883–85): "I teach you the overman. Man is something that shall be overcome." The overman is an ideal of a human being who creates new values after the death of God, affirming life and the earth rather than another world. It is not a biological race; the Nazi use of it relied on distortion. Its meaning (a goal for humanity or a rare individual?) remains debated.
