# Family resemblance

Wittgenstein's term in the *Philosophical Investigations* (§66–67) for the way the things that fall under a concept (like "game") may share no single common feature but rather "a complicated network of similarities overlapping and criss-crossing", like the resemblances among members of a family (build, features, eye colour, gait). It challenged the assumption that definitions must give necessary and sufficient conditions, and influenced prototype theory in psychology.
