# Master and slave morality

Two types of valuation Nietzsche distinguished in *Beyond Good and Evil* (§260) and the *Genealogy*. Master morality begins from self-affirmation: the noble call themselves "good" and the low "bad". Slave morality begins with reaction: from ressentiment it calls the powerful "evil" and its own humility, patience and pity "good". Nietzsche traced the triumph of slave morality to Judaism and Christianity. Critics object to his history and his contempt for compassion; it should not be confused with Hegel's master–slave dialectic.
