# Pessimism

As a philosophical doctrine, the claim that life contains more suffering than happiness and that existence is not worth having, or that the world is the worst rather than the best possible. Schopenhauer is its classic modern exponent: willing is endless striving rooted in lack, satisfaction is brief, and boredom follows. Its ancestors include Ecclesiastes and Buddhism; its successors include Eduard von Hartmann, Philipp Mainländer, Emil Cioran and contemporary antinatalism (David Benatar). Nietzsche began as a Schopenhauerian and ended by calling pessimism a symptom of decline.
