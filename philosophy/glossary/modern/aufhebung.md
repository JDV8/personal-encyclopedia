# Aufhebung

A German word Hegel prized because it means at once to cancel, to preserve and to lift up. In dialectical development a stage is *aufgehoben* ("sublated"): its one-sidedness is negated, its truth preserved, and it is raised into a richer whole. The often-cited triad "thesis–antithesis–synthesis" comes from Fichte and later popularizers rather than Hegel. Marx took over the idea of history developing through contradictions resolved at a higher level.
