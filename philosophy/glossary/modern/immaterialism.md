# Immaterialism

Berkeley's thesis that there is no material substance: physical objects are collections of ideas, and their being consists in being perceived (*esse est percipi*). Only minds and their ideas exist. The regularity and continued existence of things are explained by God, who perceives everything and produces our ideas in order. Berkeley thought this refuted scepticism and atheism; Samuel Johnson famously "refuted" it by kicking a stone, which misses the point, since Berkeley did not deny that stones are perceived as solid.
