# Existence precedes essence

Sartre's formula, in *Existentialism Is a Humanism* (1945), for the core of atheistic existentialism. Artefacts are made according to a prior concept (essence); if there is no God to conceive human nature, then human beings first exist and only afterwards define themselves by their choices. There is no fixed human nature to determine what we should be. Critics reply that biology, culture and situation shape us more than Sartre allowed; de Beauvoir applied the idea to the claim that one is not born a woman.
