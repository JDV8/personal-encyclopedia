# Authenticity

In existentialism, owning one's existence: choosing and acting from one's own understanding rather than conforming to "what one does". For Heidegger (*Eigentlichkeit*, from *eigen*, own), authenticity arises from facing one's death and taking over one's thrownness resolutely; everyday Dasein is lost in "the they". For Sartre, it means recognizing one's freedom rather than fleeing it in bad faith. Critics such as Adorno (*The Jargon of Authenticity*, 1964) attacked the concept as empty or ideological.
