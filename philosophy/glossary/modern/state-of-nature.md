# State of nature

The condition of human beings imagined without political authority, used to justify and limit government. For Hobbes it is a war of all against all in which life is "solitary, poor, nasty, brutish, and short". For Locke it is governed by natural law and natural rights, but insecure. For Rousseau, natural humans were solitary, peaceful and free, and society corrupted them. Few of these thinkers claimed it was a literal historical stage; it is a thought experiment about what government is for.
