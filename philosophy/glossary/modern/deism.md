# Deism

Belief in a God known by reason and nature rather than revelation: a creator who designed the world and its laws but does not intervene through miracles or special revelation. Deists such as John Toland, Matthew Tindal and many Enlightenment figures (Voltaire, Thomas Paine, several American founders) criticized clerical authority, dogma and religious persecution, and often reduced religion to morality. Opponents saw deism as a halfway house to atheism. Hume's *Dialogues Concerning Natural Religion* attacked its key argument from design.
