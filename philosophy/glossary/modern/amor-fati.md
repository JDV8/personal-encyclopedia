# Amor fati

Latin for "love of fate", Nietzsche's formula for the highest affirmation of life: "that one wants nothing to be different, not forward, not backward, not in all eternity. Not merely bear what is necessary … but love it" (*Ecce Homo*). He first uses it in *The Gay Science* (§276). It connects with eternal recurrence and contrasts with resignation. The Stoics, especially Epictetus and Marcus Aurelius, taught acceptance of fate, though without the same exuberance.
