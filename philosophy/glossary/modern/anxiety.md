# Anxiety (Angst)

In existentialist thought, a mood different from fear: fear has a definite object, while anxiety concerns nothing in particular, or one's own freedom and existence as a whole. Kierkegaard's *The Concept of Anxiety* (1844) called it "the dizziness of freedom". Heidegger made it the mood that discloses being-in-the-world and individualizes Dasein by tearing it from everyday absorption. Sartre called anguish the reflective consciousness of freedom. The term is also used, more broadly, in psychology.
