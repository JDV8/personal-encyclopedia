# Psychoanalysis

Both a theory of the mind and a therapeutic method, founded by Sigmund Freud in the 1890s. The theory emphasizes unconscious wishes, repression, childhood sexuality, conflict between id, ego and super-ego, and the meaning of dreams and symptoms. The method uses free association and the interpretation of transference in the relationship with the analyst. It split into many schools (Adler, Jung's analytical psychology, Klein, Lacan, ego psychology, object relations). Its scientific status was challenged by Popper and Adolf Grünbaum.
