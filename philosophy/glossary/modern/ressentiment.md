# Ressentiment

A French word Nietzsche used in *On the Genealogy of Morality* (1887) for the festering resentment of the weak against the strong, which, unable to act out its hostility, becomes creative and produces values. The "slave revolt in morality" begins when ressentiment calls the strong "evil" and the weak "good". Max Scheler wrote a study of it (1912) defending Christian love against Nietzsche's charge. Girard argued that ressentiment is a form of mimetic rivalry.
