# Logical atomism

The view developed by Russell (in lectures of 1918) and in a different form by Wittgenstein's *Tractatus* that the world consists of simple "atomic facts" and that language, properly analysed, consists of atomic propositions corresponding to them, combined by logical connectives. Analysis reveals the true logical form beneath ordinary grammar. Both thinkers later abandoned it, and the search for ultimate simples was criticized in the *Philosophical Investigations*.
