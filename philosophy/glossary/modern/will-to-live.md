# Will to live

Schopenhauer's name (*Wille zum Leben*) for the blind, insatiable striving that he held to be the thing in itself, the inner nature of everything, manifest as gravity in stones, growth in plants, instinct in animals and desire in humans. It is the source of suffering, since it can never be satisfied. Liberation comes through aesthetic contemplation, compassion and, finally, the denial of the will in asceticism. Freud's account of drives and Nietzsche's will to power developed, and transformed, the idea.
