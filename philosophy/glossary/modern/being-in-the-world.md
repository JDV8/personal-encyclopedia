# Being-in-the-world

Heidegger's hyphenated phrase for Dasein's basic state: not a mind inside a skull looking out at objects, but an engaged existence always already absorbed in a world of meaningful practices and equipment. The hammer is encountered first as "ready-to-hand" in hammering, not as an object with properties ("present-at-hand"). The idea challenges Descartes's subject–object split and influenced Merleau-Ponty's phenomenology of the body and Hubert Dreyfus's critique of classical artificial intelligence.
