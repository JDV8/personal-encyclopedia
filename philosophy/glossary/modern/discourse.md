# Discourse

In Foucault and later theory, a historically specific system of statements, concepts and practices that defines what can be said, by whom and with what authority, within a field (medicine, psychiatry, law, sexuality). Discourses do not simply describe objects; they help constitute them. In *The Archaeology of Knowledge* (1969) Foucault analysed the rules governing discursive formations. In linguistics and everyday English, "discourse" more simply means extended speech or writing.
