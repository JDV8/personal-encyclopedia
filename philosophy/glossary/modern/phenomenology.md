# Phenomenology

Literally the study of "appearances" (Greek *phainomena*). In Hegel's *Phenomenology of Spirit* it is the story of consciousness's development. As a twentieth-century movement founded by Husserl, it is the descriptive study of the structures of experience as lived from the first-person point of view, "back to the things themselves", setting aside (bracketing) assumptions about the external world. Heidegger, Sartre, Merleau-Ponty, Levinas and others transformed it. It influenced psychology, psychiatry and cognitive science.
