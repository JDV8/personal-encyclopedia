# Lifeworld

Husserl's term (*Lebenswelt*) in his late *Crisis of European Sciences* (1936) for the pre-scientific world of everyday lived experience, taken for granted as the background of all meaning and action. The mathematical sciences, starting with Galileo, abstracted from it and then forgot that their idealizations rest on it, causing a "crisis" of meaning. Schutz, Merleau-Ponty and Habermas (who contrasts lifeworld with "system") developed the idea.
