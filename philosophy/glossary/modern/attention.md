# Attention

In Simone Weil's thought, a waiting, receptive and desireless openness of mind to reality, to truth, to another person's suffering and to God. "Attention, taken to its highest degree, is the same thing as prayer"; the love of neighbour consists in being able to ask the afflicted, "What are you going through?" Even school exercises, done for the sake of attention, can train it. Iris Murdoch made "attention" central to her moral philosophy in *The Sovereignty of Good* (1970).
