# Decreation

Simone Weil's term for consenting to undo the self's claim to be the centre of the world, "to make something created pass into the uncreated". God, she thought, withdrew in creating the world so that something other than God could exist; we imitate this renunciation so that God can love the world through us. Decreation differs from destruction, which is violent and imposed. Readers have related it to mysticism and to the Kabbalistic idea of *tsimtsum*; critics worry about its self-negation.
