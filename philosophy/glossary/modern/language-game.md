# Language-game

A term from Wittgenstein's *Philosophical Investigations* for language understood as woven into activities: giving and obeying orders, describing objects, reporting events, telling jokes, thanking, praying. Language-games are embedded in "forms of life". The term stresses that words get their meaning from their use in such practices, and that there is no single essence of language. It also names simple invented languages (like the builders' game of §2) that Wittgenstein uses to illuminate ours.
