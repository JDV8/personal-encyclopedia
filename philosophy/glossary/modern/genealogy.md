# Genealogy

A method of historical critique that traces the contingent, often unflattering origins of values, concepts and institutions we take as natural or eternal, in order to question their authority. Nietzsche's *On the Genealogy of Morality* (1887) traced Christian morality to ressentiment and guilt to debt. Foucault adopted the method in "Nietzsche, Genealogy, History" (1971) and *Discipline and Punish*. Genealogies need not debunk: Bernard Williams's *Truth and Truthfulness* offers a "vindicatory" one.
