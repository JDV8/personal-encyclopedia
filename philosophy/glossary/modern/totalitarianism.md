# Totalitarianism

A form of government that seeks total control over all aspects of public and private life, beyond older tyrannies and dictatorships. The term arose among Italian anti-fascists in the 1920s and was then embraced by Mussolini. Hannah Arendt's *Origins of Totalitarianism* (1951) analysed Nazi Germany and Stalin's Soviet Union as using ideology and terror to dominate human beings totally. Carl Friedrich and Zbigniew Brzezinski offered a list of defining features. Critics argue the concept lumps together very different regimes.
