# Autonomy

Literally "self-legislation" (Greek *autos*, self, and *nomos*, law). For Kant, the will is autonomous when it gives itself the moral law through reason, rather than being determined by desires, authority or external ends (heteronomy); autonomy is the ground of human dignity. Rousseau's idea that obedience to a law one prescribes oneself is liberty anticipated this. In contemporary ethics and medicine, autonomy usually means a person's right to make decisions about their own life, a looser sense than Kant's.
