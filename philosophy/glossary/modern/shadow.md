# Shadow

In Jung's psychology, the archetype representing the parts of the personality that the conscious ego does not want to recognize: inferior, primitive or socially unacceptable traits, but also undeveloped potentials. The shadow is often projected onto others, enemies or scapegoats. Becoming conscious of one's shadow is a first step in individuation, a moral effort requiring considerable courage. The idea has entered popular psychology and literary criticism.
