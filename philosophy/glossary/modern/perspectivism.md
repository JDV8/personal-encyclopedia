# Perspectivism

Nietzsche's view that there are no facts independent of interpretation and no view from nowhere: all knowing is from a perspective shaped by drives, needs and history. "There is *only* a perspective seeing, *only* a perspective 'knowing'" (*Genealogy*, III.12); the more perspectives we bring to bear, the more complete our "objectivity". Scholars debate whether this is a form of relativism or a sophisticated account of knowledge. Ortega y Gasset developed a related perspectivism.
