# Individuation

In Jung's psychology, the lifelong process by which a person becomes a psychologically integrated whole, "an in-dividual", by bringing unconscious contents (the shadow, anima or animus, other archetypes) into relation with consciousness, centred on the Self rather than the ego. Jung thought this was the task especially of the second half of life. In scholastic philosophy, "the principle of individuation" names what makes an individual this one rather than another (matter, for Aquinas).
