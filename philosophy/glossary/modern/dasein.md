# Dasein

German for "existence" or literally "being-there", Heidegger's term in *Being and Time* for the kind of being that human beings are: the entity for which its own being is an issue, which always already has some understanding of being. Heidegger avoided words like "subject", "consciousness" or "person" because they carry assumptions from Descartes. Dasein's being is being-in-the-world, care and temporality. Translators usually leave the word in German.
