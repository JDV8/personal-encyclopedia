# Nihilism

From Latin *nihil*, nothing. In Russia in the 1860s, popularized by Turgenev's *Fathers and Sons*, nihilism named a radical rejection of traditional authority, religion and art in favour of science and utility. For Nietzsche, nihilism is the situation in which "the highest values devalue themselves": with the death of God, the foundations of Western morality and meaning collapse. He saw it as a danger and a passage to be overcome by a revaluation of values. More loosely, nihilism means the belief that nothing has meaning or value.
