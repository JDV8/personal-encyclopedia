# Alienation

A condition in which something that is one's own appears as foreign and hostile. Hegel and Feuerbach used the idea (Feuerbach: God is humanity's own essence projected outward). In his *Economic and Philosophic Manuscripts of 1844*, Marx argued that under capitalism workers are alienated from the product of their labour, from the activity of labour, from their "species-being" (their human capacity for free, creative production) and from other people. The concept was revived in twentieth-century Marxism, existentialism and sociology.
