# The Other

In phenomenology and existentialism, other persons as experienced by a subject: centres of consciousness I can never fully access, who can see me as an object. Hegel's struggle for recognition, Sartre's "look" and Levinas's ethics of the face are key treatments. De Beauvoir argued in *The Second Sex* that woman has been constituted as man's Other, defined in relation to him as the inessential to the essential. Postcolonial thinkers such as Frantz Fanon and Edward Said analysed "othering" of colonized peoples.
