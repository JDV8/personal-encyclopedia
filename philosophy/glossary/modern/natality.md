# Natality

Hannah Arendt's term, central to *The Human Condition* (1958), for the fact that human beings are born, each one a new beginning, and so capable of action: of starting something new and unpredictable. Philosophy, she thought, had focused on mortality; politics rests on natality. She quoted Augustine: "that a beginning be made, man was created" (*City of God* XII.21). The idea underlies her hope at the end of *The Origins of Totalitarianism*.
