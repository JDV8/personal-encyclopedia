# Bundle theory

The view that a self, or an object, is nothing over and above a collection of perceptions or properties, with no underlying substance holding them together. Hume, looking inward, found only particular perceptions and never a self, and concluded that the mind is "a bundle or collection of different perceptions". In an appendix he admitted he could not explain what unites them. The view resembles the Buddhist doctrine of no-self and is defended in modified form by Derek Parfit.
