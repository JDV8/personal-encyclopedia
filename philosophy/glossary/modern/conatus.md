# Conatus

Latin for "striving" or "endeavour". In Spinoza's *Ethics* (III, proposition 6), each thing, as far as it is in itself, strives to persevere in its being; this striving is the thing's very essence. In human beings conscious conatus is desire; joy is an increase of our power of acting, sadness a decrease. Hobbes used *conatus* for small beginnings of motion. Schopenhauer's will to live and Nietzsche's will to power have been compared to it.
