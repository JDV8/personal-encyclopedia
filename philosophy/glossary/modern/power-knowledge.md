# Power/knowledge

Foucault's term for the inseparability of power and knowledge in modern societies: knowledge (psychiatry, criminology, medicine, economics) is produced within relations of power and in turn makes new forms of power possible, defining what is normal and creating categories of persons (the delinquent, the homosexual, the madman) to be managed. Power is not merely repressive or held by a sovereign; it is productive and dispersed. A collection of Foucault's interviews was published in English as *Power/Knowledge* (1980).
