# Problem of evil

The challenge of reconciling the existence of evil and suffering with a God who is all-powerful, all-knowing and wholly good. The logical version claims these are inconsistent; the evidential version claims evil makes God's existence improbable. Epicurus is credited (by Lactantius) with an early form. Responses include Augustine's view of evil as privation of good and the result of free will, Leibniz's best possible world, and arguments that God has reasons beyond our grasp. Dostoevsky's Ivan Karamazov refuses any harmony bought with children's suffering.
