# Will to power

Nietzsche's idea (*Wille zur Macht*) that the fundamental drive of living things is not self-preservation or pleasure but the expansion and expression of power: growing, mastering, overcoming resistance, including self-overcoming. It appears in *Thus Spoke Zarathustra* and *Beyond Good and Evil*. A book titled *The Will to Power* was compiled after his death by his sister Elisabeth Förster-Nietzsche and Heinrich Köselitz from notebooks, and is not a work he completed. Scholars debate whether it is a metaphysics, a psychology, or both.
