# Picture theory of meaning

The account of language in Wittgenstein's *Tractatus*: a proposition is a logical picture of a possible state of affairs, whose elements (names) stand for objects and whose structure mirrors the arrangement of those objects. A proposition is true if the state it pictures obtains. Wittgenstein reportedly got the idea from a report of a Paris court case in which a traffic accident was reconstructed with model cars. The theory implies that ethics and logical form cannot be pictured, and so cannot be said.
