# Unconscious

Mental processes and contents of which a person is unaware but which influence experience and behaviour. Philosophers such as Leibniz ("petites perceptions"), Schopenhauer and Eduard von Hartmann (*Philosophy of the Unconscious*, 1869) discussed it before Freud, who made the dynamic unconscious, held out of awareness by repression, the foundation of psychoanalysis. Jung added a collective unconscious. Contemporary cognitive science studies a "cognitive unconscious" of automatic processes, without Freud's theory of repressed wishes.
