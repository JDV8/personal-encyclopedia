# Bad faith

Sartre's term (*mauvaise foi*) in *Being and Nothingness* for self-deception about one's freedom: pretending to be a fixed thing determined by role, character or circumstance, or, conversely, pretending to be pure freedom without facticity. His examples are a café waiter whose movements are a little too precise, as if he were playing at being a waiter, and a woman on a date who lets her hand be taken while acting as if she had not noticed. Bad faith is possible because consciousness is never simply what it is.
