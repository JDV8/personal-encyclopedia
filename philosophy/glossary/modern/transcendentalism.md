# Transcendentalism

A New England movement of the 1830s–1850s, centred on Ralph Waldo Emerson, Henry David Thoreau, Margaret Fuller and Bronson Alcott. Drawing on Romanticism, Kant (as filtered through Coleridge), Platonism and Hindu scriptures, it held that divinity pervades nature and humanity, that intuition can grasp truths beyond the senses, and that individuals should trust themselves over institutions and tradition. It fed into abolitionism, women's rights, educational reform and environmentalism. It should not be confused with Kant's "transcendental" philosophy.
