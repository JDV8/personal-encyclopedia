# Humanism

In the Renaissance, humanism was an educational and literary movement centred on the *studia humanitatis*: grammar, rhetoric, poetry, history and moral philosophy, studied through classical Latin and Greek texts. Humanists such as Petrarch, Erasmus and Montaigne sought eloquence, practical wisdom and a recovery of ancient learning, usually within Christianity. In a later sense, humanism names any outlook placing human dignity and reason at the centre, often secular. Sartre's *Existentialism Is a Humanism* and Heidegger's reply show that the meaning of the word remained contested.
