# Pantheism

The view that God and the universe (or nature) are identical, rather than God being a transcendent creator. Spinoza's formula *Deus sive Natura*, "God or Nature", made him the paradigmatic modern pantheist, though some read him as a naturalist or atheist. The word was coined in the early eighteenth century; the "pantheism controversy" of the 1780s, over whether Lessing had been a Spinozist, made Spinoza central to German thought. Panentheism, by contrast, holds that the world is in God but God exceeds it.
