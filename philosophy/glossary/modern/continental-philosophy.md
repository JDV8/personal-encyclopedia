# Continental philosophy

A loose label, coined largely by English-speaking philosophers, for traditions rooted mainly in Germany and France since Kant and Hegel: phenomenology, existentialism, hermeneutics, critical theory, structuralism, post-structuralism and deconstruction. Its themes often include history, culture, the critique of modernity, and lived experience. The name is geographically odd (analytic philosophy also has continental roots in Frege and Vienna) and describes a family of styles rather than a doctrine.
