# Eternal recurrence

Nietzsche's thought that everything that has happened will recur infinitely, exactly the same. In *The Gay Science* (§341) a demon proposes it: would you curse him, or call it divine? In *Thus Spoke Zarathustra* it is the "most abysmal thought". Whether Nietzsche meant it as a cosmological hypothesis or as a test of one's affirmation of life is debated; most readers stress the second: live so that you could will your life again. Ancient cyclical cosmologies, including the Stoics', held versions of it.
