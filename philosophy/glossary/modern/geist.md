# Geist

German for "spirit" or "mind". For Hegel, *Geist* is reason becoming conscious of itself through history and human communities: in individuals (subjective spirit), in law, morality, family, civil society and state (objective spirit), and in art, religion and philosophy (absolute spirit). The *Phenomenology of Spirit* traces its development. The *Zeitgeist*, "spirit of the age", is a related idea. Critics charged that Hegel's *Geist* turned history into a theodicy.
