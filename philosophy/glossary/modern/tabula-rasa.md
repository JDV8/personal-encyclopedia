# Tabula rasa

Latin for "scraped tablet", the image of the mind at birth as blank, with all ideas coming from experience. The image goes back to Aristotle's comparison of the mind with an unwritten writing tablet, but is associated above all with Locke, who in the *Essay Concerning Human Understanding* rejected innate ideas and described the mind as "white paper, void of all characters". Leibniz replied that the mind is more like veined marble, with predispositions. The debate continues in disputes about innate cognitive structure.
