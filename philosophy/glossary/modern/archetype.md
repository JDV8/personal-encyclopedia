# Archetype

In Jung's analytical psychology, an inherited, universal pattern or form in the collective unconscious that structures experience and appears in images in myths, religions, dreams and fantasies across cultures: the mother, the child, the hero, the wise old man, the trickster, the shadow, the anima and animus, the Self. Jung distinguished the archetype itself, unknowable, from archetypal images. The Greek *archetypon* (original pattern) was used earlier by Platonists and Augustine.
