# Principle of sufficient reason

The principle that nothing is without a reason why it is so rather than otherwise. Leibniz made it one of the two great principles of reasoning, alongside the principle of contradiction, and used it to argue for God as the sufficient reason of the series of contingent things, and against absolute space. Spinoza relied on a similar principle. Schopenhauer's doctoral dissertation, *On the Fourfold Root of the Principle of Sufficient Reason* (1813), distinguished four kinds of reason (becoming, knowing, being and acting).
