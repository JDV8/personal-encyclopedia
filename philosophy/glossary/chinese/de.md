# *De*

De is virtue, potency, or an efficacy one 'obtains': the power of a cultivated person (in Confucian uses) or of what stays close to the Dao (in the Daodejing), sometimes almost magnetic. 'Virtue' is a fair start; 'inner power' sometimes helps. Government by de is the Confucian political ideal.
