# *Mingjia* (School of Names)

Mingjia, the School of Names, is a later label for Warring States disputers about names, kinds, and paradox, especially Hui Shi and Gongsun Long. They are specialists of language, often dismissed as sophists, and they belong next to the later Mohist canons and to Zhuangzi's satire of disputation.
