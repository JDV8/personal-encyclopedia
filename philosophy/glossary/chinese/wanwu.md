# *Wanwu*

Wanwu, the 'ten thousand things' (or 'myriad things'), is a standard name for the world's multiplicity — everything that arises from the Dao, everything yin and yang process through. It is not a literal count. Hui Shi and Zhuangzi both play with the thought that the ten thousand things may also be one, depending on standpoint.
