# *Tianming*

Tianming is the Mandate of Heaven: the early Zhou idea that Heaven grants rule to the virtuous and can withdraw it from the corrupt. Mencius uses it to justify removing a destroyer of ren. It ties cosmology to politics, and it is not a modern electoral theory. 'Mandate' here is a moral-cosmic charge, not a parliamentary brief.
