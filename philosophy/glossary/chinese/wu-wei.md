# *Wu wei*

Wu wei is non-forcing or non-contrived action, often mistranslated as simple inaction. In the Daodejing it is a way of ruling and living that does not fight the grain; in the Zhuangzi it is also skillful spontaneity. Legalism can borrow the word for a very different stillness of the ruler. See the concept lesson.
