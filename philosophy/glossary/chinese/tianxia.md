# *Tianxia*

Tianxia, 'All-under-Heaven,' is the world as a political-cosmological whole, the field of order that a true king would bring to peace. The Great Learning's chain ends in ping tianxia. It is not simply 'the state' (guo), and not simply 'the planet.' It is the widest Confucian political horizon.
