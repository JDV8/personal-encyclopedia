# *Li* (principle)

This li (a different character from ritual li) is pattern or principle, the intelligible order in things, made central by Zhu Xi: one li variously present, instantiated in qi. It is a Song metaphysical term, not a Warring States keyword in this sense. It is easy to confuse with ritual li in English romanization.
