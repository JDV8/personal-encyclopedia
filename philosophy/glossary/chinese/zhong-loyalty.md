# *Zhong* (loyalty / doing one's best)

Zhong can mean loyalty, especially to a ruler, and also doing one's utmost, conscientiousness. In the Analects it is often paired with shu as a 'thread' of the teaching. Later political uses can narrow it to official loyalty; the earlier range is wider.
