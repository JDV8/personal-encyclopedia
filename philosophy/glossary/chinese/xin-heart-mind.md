# *Xin* (heart-mind)

Xin is the heart-mind: the seat of both feeling and thought, not a pure intellect cut off from emotion. Mencius locates the sprouts in the xin. Later 'Learning of the Mind' (Wang Yangming) makes xin the place where pattern is immediately present. Translating it only as 'heart' or only as 'mind' always loses something.
