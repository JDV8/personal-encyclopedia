# Chinese terms

Romanized keywords from the Chinese path — ren, li, dao, de, wu wei, xing, and their neighbors. Romanization can hide different characters (two lis, two xins, two shus); the entries flag those traps. Prefer the term, when you can, to a single English stand-in.

**Prerequisites:** the [glossary overview](../README.md), and the relevant tradition's introductory lesson.

## Suggested reading order

A good first handful: dao, de, ren, li (ritual), xing, tian, wu wei, fa. Then follow links into the thinker and text overviews.
