# *Baijia* (Hundred Schools)

Baijia, the 'Hundred Schools,' is a later shorthand for the intellectual ferment of the Warring States, not a literal count of a hundred departments. Confucians, Mohists, Daoist texts, Legalists, and the School of Names are among the families. The shared problem is often how to restore order after the Zhou breakdown.
