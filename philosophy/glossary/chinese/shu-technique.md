# *Shu* (technique)

This shu (a different word from reciprocity shu) is administrative technique: the methods a ruler uses to appoint, monitor, and assess officials, associated with Shen Buhai and synthesized by Han Feizi. It is the least 'public' of the three Legalist tools, and the most easily turned into a manual of suspicion.
