# *He* (harmony)

He is harmony: the blending of differences into a working order, often contrasted with mere sameness. It is a political and musical as well as a cosmological ideal in early China. Ritual and music (li-yue) are supposed to train it. Harmony is not automatically 'everyone already agrees.'
