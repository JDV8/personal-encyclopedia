# *Ru*

Ru is the name for the Confucian tradition as a scholarly and ritual lineage (the ru school), older as a word than the Latin 'Confucian.' It is the tradition of the Analects, Mencius, Xunzi, and later imperial curriculum, not only the person of Confucius. English still usually says 'Confucian.'
