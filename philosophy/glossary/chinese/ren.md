# *Ren*

Ren is the central Confucian virtue of humaneness or full human-heartedness: a cultivated concern for others, grown from family feeling and extended outward. Confucius is stingy with the word; it is an achievement, not a mood. It is paired with li (ritual). Mencius finds a sprout of it in compassion. English 'benevolence' is often too thin.
