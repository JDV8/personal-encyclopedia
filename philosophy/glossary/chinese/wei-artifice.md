# *Wei* (artifice)

Wei, in Xunzi, is artifice or deliberate effort: the teaching, ritual, and shaping by which raw nature becomes good, as a board is straightened against a frame. It is a positive technical term here, not a synonym for 'fake.' Zhuangzi's worries about the artificial run in a different direction.
