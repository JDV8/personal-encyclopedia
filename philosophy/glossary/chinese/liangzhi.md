# *Liangzhi*

Liangzhi is innate or pure knowing, Wang Yangming's name for the mind's native moral awareness, always already able to recognize the good. It is a radicalization of a Mencian theme, and the starting point of his critique of Zhu Xi's outward-leaning investigation of things.
