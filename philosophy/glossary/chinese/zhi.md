# *Zhi* (wisdom)

Zhi is wisdom or knowing, one of the Confucian virtues (and, in Mencius, a sprout in the sense of right and wrong). Later it is also the 'knowledge' in Wang Yangming's unity of knowledge and action. Like English 'wisdom,' it can lean toward moral discernment or toward information; context decides.
