# *Shu* (reciprocity)

Shu is reciprocity or sympathetic consideration, close to the Analects' 'do not impose on others what you do not want for yourself.' It is often paired with zhong (doing one's best, or loyalty). Together they are sometimes called the 'one thread' of Confucius's teaching, in a famous passage.
