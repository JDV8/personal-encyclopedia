# *Fa*

Fa is law, standard, or a publicly promulgated rule. In Legalism it is one of the three tools of statecraft: clear rewards and punishments, actually applied. In Mohist and other uses it can also mean a model or standard of judgment more generally. It is not liberal 'rule of law.'
