# *Xiaoren*

The xiaoren is the 'small person,' the Analects' foil to the junzi: not necessarily evil, but narrow, profit-minded, and unreliable in adversity. The contrast is ethical, not a claim about body size or, in the mature Confucian use, a mere class insult, even though the words have social histories.
