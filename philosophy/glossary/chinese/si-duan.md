# *Si duan* (four sprouts)

The four sprouts (siduan) are Mencius's native beginnings of virtue: compassion (ren), shame (yi), courtesy (li), and the sense of right and wrong (zhi). They need nourishing, not pulling. The child about to fall into a well is the most famous illustration.
