# *Yin*

Yin is the complementary counterpart of yang: originally the shady side of a hill, then a name for rest, winter, interior, the yielding, and related phases of process. It is not simply 'bad' or 'female' as a moral insult, though later popular uses sometimes slide that way. The point is timely interplay, not the extermination of one pole.
