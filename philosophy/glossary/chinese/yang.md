# *Yang*

Yang is the complementary counterpart of yin: originally the sunny side of a hill, then a name for activity, summer, the firm, and related phases. Midday yang gives way to yin; midwinter yin turns toward yang. The pattern is cyclical and relational, not a war of two substances.
