# You and *wu* (being and nonbeing)

You and wu are 'there is' and 'there is not,' or being and nonbeing, a pair the Daodejing famously entangles: being and nonbeing give rise to each other, and the useful emptiness of a hub or a room is a wu that does work. This is not automatically Parmenides' what-is-not, and should not be forced into that mold without care.
