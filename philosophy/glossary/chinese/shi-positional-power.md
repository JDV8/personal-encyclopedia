# *Shi* (positional power)

Shi is positional power, the authority that belongs to the throne as a throne, stressed by Shen Dao and absorbed by Han Feizi. Influence should come from the seat, not from the occupant's charisma or virtue. It is one of the three Legalist tools, next to fa and shu.
