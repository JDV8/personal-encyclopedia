# *Xiao*

Xiao is filial piety: the love, service, and ritual care owed to parents and ancestors, and the first school of ren in Confucian ethics. Mohists worry that a filial starting point never becomes impartial; Confucians reply that love must be grown from where it actually starts. The word is more than 'being nice to your parents.'
