# *Wuxing*

Wuxing, the Five Phases, are Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water as phases of activity standing in generating and overcoming cycles — not five lumps of matter. They organize correlative cosmology, medicine, and, politically, stories of dynastic succession. 'Five elements' is a misleading translation if it suggests static stuffs.
