# *Taiji*

Taiji, the 'Great Ultimate,' is a later cosmological name for the source or totality of yin-yang process, important in Song thought (including Zhu Xi) and in later diagrams. It is not a Warring States keyword in this developed sense. Wuji ('without ultimate') is sometimes paired with it as a prior or complementary term.
