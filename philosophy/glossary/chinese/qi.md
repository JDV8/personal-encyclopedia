# *Qi*

Qi is vital stuff, breath, or psycho-physical energy — the active medium of later Chinese cosmology and medicine, and, in Zhu Xi, the stuff in which pattern (li) is instantiated, sometimes clearly and sometimes turbidly. Early uses vary; do not read a single modern 'energy' into every occurrence. It is not Aristotle's hyle, and not a ghost.
