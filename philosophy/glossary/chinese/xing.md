# *Xing*

Xing is nature, especially human nature: the inborn direction or raw material of a person. Mencius says it is good (sprouts of virtue); Xunzi says it is raw or bad and needs ritual artifice. The debate is the most important intra-Confucian argument on this path. It is related to, not identical with, Greek physis.
