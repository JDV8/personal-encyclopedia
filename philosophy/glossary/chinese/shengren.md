# *Shengren*

A shengren is a sage, the highest ethical and often political exemplar — more than a junzi, a completed person who can transform others or even form a triad with Heaven and Earth (in the Doctrine of the Mean). Sage-kings of antiquity (Yao, Shun, Yu, the early Zhou) are the standard models. The term can be moral, political, or both.
