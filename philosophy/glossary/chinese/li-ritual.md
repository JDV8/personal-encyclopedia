# *Li* (ritual)

Li is ritual, ceremony, and the inherited forms of appropriate conduct: how one mourns, greets, rules, and serves parents. For Confucius, form trains feeling; without ren, li is empty, and without li, ren has no shape. It is a different word from later Confucian li meaning principle or pattern. Do not collapse the two.
