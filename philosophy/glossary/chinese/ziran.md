# *Ziran*

Ziran, 'self-so' or 'naturally so,' is the quality of what is not forced or artificially imposed — a close companion of wu wei in Daoist texts. It is not 'nature' as a wilderness aesthetic only, and not physis in the Greek sense, though the comparison is sometimes useful. A ziran action looks as if it happened of itself.
