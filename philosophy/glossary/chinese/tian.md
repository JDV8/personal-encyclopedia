# *Tian*

Tian is Heaven: the sky, the order of the world, a moral horizon, or, in some texts, a will that can endorse a standard (Mozi) or grant and withdraw a mandate. It is usually not 'God' as in Genesis, and it is not mere weather. Xunzi naturalizes it as a regular order that does not intervene.
