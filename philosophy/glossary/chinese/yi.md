# *Yi*

Yi is rightness or appropriateness: the Confucian virtue of doing what the situation and one's role require, as opposed to acting from profit (a different li). Mencius pairs it with ren and finds a sprout of it in the sense of shame. It is one of the nearest Chinese neighbors to 'justice,' without being a translation of dikaiosyne.
