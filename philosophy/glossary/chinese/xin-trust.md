# *Xin* (trustworthiness)

This xin (a different character from the heart-mind) is trustworthiness, good faith, the keeping of one's word — one of the commonly listed Confucian virtues or social bonds. It is easy to confuse with heart-mind xin in romanization. In the Analects it is a basic demand of the junzi and of friendship.
