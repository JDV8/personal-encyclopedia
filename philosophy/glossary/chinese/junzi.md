# *Junzi*

The junzi, once a noble by birth, becomes in the Analects an ethical title: the exemplary person, as opposed to the xiaoren (small person) who calculates advantage. Education, ritual, and ren make a junzi. The translation 'gentleman' is dated and a bit narrow; 'exemplary person' is plainer.
