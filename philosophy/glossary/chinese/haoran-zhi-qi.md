# *Haoran zhi qi*

Haoran zhi qi, often 'flood-like qi,' is Mencius's name for a vast, rightly nourished vital energy that fills the space between Heaven and Earth when one is accumulated in rightness. It is a cultivated inner power, not a brute force, and it belongs to his picture of an unmoved mind. The phrase is easy to mystify; keep it next to the ethical argument about sprouts.
