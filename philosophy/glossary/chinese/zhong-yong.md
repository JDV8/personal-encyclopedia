# *Zhongyong*

Zhongyong, the title usually translated Doctrine of the Mean, names both a short classic and a pair of ideas: the central (zhong) and the ordinary or constant practice (yong). It is a cousin of, not a translation of, Aristotle's mean. After Zhu Xi it is one of the Four Books.
