# *Dao*

Dao means a road or path, and by extension a way of doing something, a teaching, or the way the world itself operates. Every early school claims a dao. In the Daodejing the word also names a source prior to names. It is not automatically 'Daoism.' Compare logos carefully; do not treat them as one term.
