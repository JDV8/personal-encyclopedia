# *Zhixing heyi*

Zhixing heyi is the unity of knowledge and action: Wang Yangming's claim that genuine knowing is already a doing — to 'know' one should be filial and not act is not really to know. It is paired with liangzhi, and it is a thesis about moral knowledge, not a claim that every skill is automatic.
