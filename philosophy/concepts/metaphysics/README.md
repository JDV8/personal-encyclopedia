# Metaphysics concepts

Granular lessons on what there is and how change is possible: first principles, logos, Forms, substance, atoms and void, Dao, yin-yang, and the One. Read these alongside the historical surveys, not instead of them.

**Prerequisites:** [Philosophy](../../README.md) and the [concepts overview](../README.md).

## Suggested reading order

1. [Arche and first principles](arche-and-first-principles.md)
2. [Logos as account and order](logos-as-account-and-order.md)
3. [The problem of change](the-problem-of-change.md)
4. [Being and not-being](being-and-not-being.md)
5. Then either the Platonic-Aristotelian cluster (Forms, form and matter, four causes, potentiality, substance) or the Chinese and later cluster (Dao, yin-yang, the One).

## References & further reading

- Aristotle, *Physics* and *Metaphysics* (Intermediate). The main ancient source for cause, substance, and potentiality.
- G. S. Kirk, J. E. Raven, and M. Schofield, *The Presocratic Philosophers* (Advanced). For arche, logos, and the problem of change.
