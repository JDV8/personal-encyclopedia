# Ethics concepts

Granular lessons on virtue, flourishing, habit, and the major Greek and Chinese first principles of a good life — including wu wei and impartial care.

**Prerequisites:** [Philosophy](../../README.md) and the [concepts overview](../README.md).

## Suggested reading order

Start with [virtue and arete](virtue-and-arete.md) and [*eudaimonia*](eudaimonia.md), then the mean, habit, and virtue as knowledge. For Hellenistic ethics, read [ataraxia and apatheia](ataraxia-and-apatheia.md) and [living according to nature](living-according-to-nature.md). For the Chinese path, read [ren and li](ren-and-li.md), [human nature](human-nature-xing.md), [wu wei](wu-wei.md), and [impartial care](impartial-care.md).

- Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics*, trans. Terence Irwin (Beginner). The home of eudaimonia, the mean, and habit.
- Edward Slingerland, trans., *Analects* (Beginner). The home of ren and li.
- A. A. Long, *Hellenistic Philosophy* (Intermediate). For ataraxia, apatheia, and living according to nature.
