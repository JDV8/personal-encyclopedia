# Nineteenth-century thinkers

One overview page per thinker, from Hegel to Nietzsche. Dostoevsky and Tolstoy were novelists rather than philosophers; their pages say so, and explain why philosophers keep returning to them.

**Prerequisites:** [Nineteenth-century philosophy](../README.md)

## Suggested reading order

Hegel, then Schopenhauer (his great enemy); Mill and Marx; Emerson and Thoreau; Kierkegaard; Dostoevsky and Tolstoy; Nietzsche last, since he answers almost all of them.

## References & further reading

- R. J. Hollingdale, *Nietzsche: The Man and His Philosophy* (Beginner). A sympathetic intellectual biography.
- Walter Kaufmann, *Nietzsche: Philosopher, Psychologist, Antichrist* (Intermediate). The book that rescued Nietzsche's reputation in English after the war.
- David E. Cartwright, *Schopenhauer: A Biography* (Intermediate).
- Joseph Frank, *Dostoevsky: A Writer in His Time* (Intermediate). One-volume abridgement of the great five-volume biography.
- Rosamund Bartlett, *Tolstoy: A Russian Life* (Beginner).
- Isaiah Berlin, *The Hedgehog and the Fox* (Beginner). A famous essay on Tolstoy's view of history.
