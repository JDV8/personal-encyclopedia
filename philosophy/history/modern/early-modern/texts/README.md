# Early modern texts

Overviews of sixteen early modern books, from *The Prince* to the *Groundwork*: dating and form, outline, key ideas, reception and recommended editions. Hume's *Treatise* page also covers his two *Enquiries*.

**Prerequisites:** [Early modern thinkers](../thinkers/README.md)

## Suggested reading order

If you read only four: Descartes's *Meditations*, Hume's *Enquiry concerning Human Understanding* (see the *Treatise* page), Rousseau's *Social Contract*, and Kant's *Groundwork*. Otherwise read in date order as listed.

## References & further reading

- René Descartes, *Meditations on First Philosophy*, trans. John Cottingham (Cambridge) (Beginner).
- Michel de Montaigne, *The Complete Essays*, trans. M. A. Screech (Penguin) (Beginner).
- Sebastian Gardner, *Routledge Philosophy Guidebook to Kant and the Critique of Pure Reason* (Advanced).
- Jonathan Bennett's modernized versions at earlymoderntexts.com (Beginner). Free, careful paraphrases of many of these books.
