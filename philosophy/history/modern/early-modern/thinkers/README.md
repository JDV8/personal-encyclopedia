# Early modern thinkers

One overview page per major thinker from Machiavelli to Kant: life and context, core ideas (with a diagram), influence and criticism, and links to their key texts. Adam Smith is here as a moral philosopher; his economics is covered in the [economics history-of-thought path](../../../../../economics/microeconomics/10-history-of-thought/thinkers/01-adam-smith.md).

**Prerequisites:** [Early modern philosophy](../README.md)

## Suggested reading order

Read chronologically: Machiavelli and Montaigne; Bacon, Descartes and Hobbes; Pascal, Spinoza, Locke and Leibniz; Berkeley and Hume; Voltaire, Rousseau and Smith; Kant.

## References & further reading

- John Cottingham, *Descartes* (Blackwell) (Beginner). A short, clear introduction to the whole philosophy.
- Steven Nadler, *Spinoza: A Life* (Intermediate). The standard biography, with the philosophy in context.
- A. J. Ayer, *Hume: A Very Short Introduction* (Beginner).
- Manfred Kuehn, *Kant: A Biography* (Intermediate). The best life of Kant in English.
- Leo Damrosch, *Jean-Jacques Rousseau: Restless Genius* (Beginner). A vivid biography.
