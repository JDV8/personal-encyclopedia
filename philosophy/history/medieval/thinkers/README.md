# Medieval thinkers

One overview file for each major medieval thinker in this encyclopedia, Christian, Muslim and Jewish. Each covers life and context, core ideas, influence and criticism, and points to the texts in [texts/](../texts/README.md). The numbered lessons in the parent folder remain the narrative path.

**Prerequisites:** [Medieval philosophy](../README.md), especially [Faith and reason](../01-faith-and-reason.md)

## Suggested reading order

Read in historical order: the late-antique founders (Augustine, Boethius); the first scholastic (Anselm); the great Islamic and Jewish Aristotelians (Avicenna, Averroes, Maimonides); the high scholastic synthesis (Aquinas); and its late-medieval critic (Ockham).

## References & further reading

- Anthony Kenny, *Medieval Philosophy* (Beginner). Short, reliable portraits of each of these thinkers in context.
- Peter Brown, *Augustine of Hippo: A Biography* (Intermediate). The classic life of Augustine.
- Edward Feser, *Aquinas: A Beginner's Guide* (Beginner). A clear, if partisan, introduction to Aquinas's metaphysics and arguments.
- Oliver Leaman, *An Introduction to Classical Islamic Philosophy* (Intermediate). Avicenna, Averroes and their world.
