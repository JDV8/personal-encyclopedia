# Medieval texts

Overviews of six medieval books: what each is, when and how it was written, its outline, its key ideas and its reception. Read them before or alongside the books themselves; recommended translations are named in each file.

**Prerequisites:** [Medieval thinkers](../thinkers/README.md)

## Suggested reading order

Read Augustine's *Confessions* first, then *The City of God*; then Boethius, Anselm's *Proslogion*, Maimonides' *Guide* and finally the *Summa Theologiae*.

## References & further reading

- Augustine, *Confessions*, trans. Henry Chadwick (Oxford World's Classics) (Beginner). The most widely recommended modern translation.
- Boethius, *The Consolation of Philosophy*, trans. Victor Watts (Penguin) (Beginner).
- Thomas Aquinas, *Selected Writings*, ed. Ralph McInerny (Penguin) (Intermediate). A manageable way into the *Summa*.
- Maimonides, *The Guide of the Perplexed*, trans. Shlomo Pines (Chicago) (Advanced).
