# Chinese thinkers

This folder gives one overview file for each major Warring States (and immediately related) figure on the Chinese path. Use it for a person and their sources; use the numbered lessons in the parent folder for the narrative, and [texts/](../texts/README.md) for the books themselves.

**Prerequisites:** [Ancient Chinese philosophy](../README.md) and [foundational concepts](../01-foundational-concepts.md).

## Suggested reading order

1. Confucian line: [Confucius](confucius.md), [Mencius](mencius.md), [Xunzi](xunzi.md)
2. Daoist voices: [Laozi](laozi.md), [Zhuangzi](zhuangzi.md)
3. Rivals on standards and the state: [Mozi](mozi.md), [Gongsun Long and Hui Shi](gongsun-long-and-hui-shi.md), [Shang Yang](shang-yang.md), [Han Feizi](han-feizi.md)
4. Cosmology: [Zou Yan](zou-yan.md)

Then the later bridges in [imperial Chinese thinkers](../../../imperial/chinese/thinkers/README.md): [Zhu Xi](../../../imperial/chinese/thinkers/zhu-xi.md) and [Wang Yangming](../../../imperial/chinese/thinkers/wang-yangming.md).

## References & further reading

- Bryan W. Van Norden, *Introduction to Classical Chinese Philosophy* (Beginner). A clear first map of these figures and their disagreements.
- Philip J. Ivanhoe and Bryan W. Van Norden, eds., *Readings in Classical Chinese Philosophy* (Beginner). Well-chosen translations of the main texts, useful beside these overviews.
- A. C. Graham, *Disputers of the Tao* (Advanced). A classic, argument-rich history of the Hundred Schools.
- Benjamin I. Schwartz, *The World of Thought in Ancient China* (Advanced). Broader intellectual history around the same thinkers.
- Wing-tsit Chan, *A Source Book in Chinese Philosophy* (Intermediate). Translations that also reach the later figures in the imperial folder.
