# *Metaphysics*

**Prerequisites:** [Aristotle](../thinkers/aristotle.md), [*Physics*](physics.md); helpful: the [*Organon*](organon.md)

## Name, dating, and authorship

*Ta meta ta physika* ("the things after the physics") is almost certainly a later editorial name for a set of related treatises, not Aristotle's own title. He speaks of **first philosophy**, or of a science of being as being, and of theology. The work as we have it (fourteen books, numbered with Greek letters) is not a single smooth composition; Book Alpha and Book Lambda are often read first, the central "substance books" (Zeta, Eta, Theta) are the hardest.

## Outline and key ideas

The opening: all humans desire to know; wisdom is knowledge of first causes and principles. Aristotle reviews his predecessors (including the [Milesians](../thinkers/thales.md), [Plato](../thinkers/plato.md)'s Forms, and the Pythagoreans) as incomplete accounts of the [four causes](../../../../concepts/metaphysics/the-four-causes.md). The science he wants is not a special science of a special genus; it is the science of **being as being**, and of the primary being, [substance](../../../../concepts/metaphysics/substance.md). The central books ask what substance is: form, matter, the composite, essence (*to ti en einai*). [Potentiality and actuality](../../../../concepts/metaphysics/potentiality-and-actuality.md) (Book Theta) deepen the *Physics*. Book Lambda argues to a divine **unmoved mover**, a thought thinking itself, as the final cause that the heavens and nature "hang upon." Whether first philosophy is mainly ontology (being as being) or mainly theology (the highest being) is a tension *inside* the work, not only in the later tradition.

## Reception

This is the book that named a branch of philosophy. Islamic and Christian medieval metaphysics is unimaginable without it (and without the arguments about the mover, the intellect, and the eternity of the world). Heidegger and a long modern literature still argue with "the question of being" as Aristotle posed it. For this path, it is the fullest Greek attempt to answer [Parmenides](../thinkers/parmenides.md) and [Plato](../thinkers/plato.md) in one science.

## Key ideas

- First philosophy: being as being, substance, and first causes.
- A composite, sometimes rough, set of treatises, not a polished single book.
- The unmoved mover of Book Lambda is a thinking god, a final cause, not a craftsman of the *Timaeus* type.
