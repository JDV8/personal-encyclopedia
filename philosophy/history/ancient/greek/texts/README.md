# Greek texts

One overview file for each major primary work on the Greek path. These are not substitutes for reading the works. They are maps: dating and authorship, outline, key ideas, and how later readers used the book.

**Prerequisites:** [Ancient Greek philosophy](../README.md) and, for any given text, the matching file in [thinkers/](../thinkers/README.md).

## Suggested reading order

**Plato, first sequence:** [Euthyphro](euthyphro.md), [Apology](apology.md), [Crito](crito.md), [Meno](meno.md), [Phaedo](phaedo.md).

**Plato, ethics and politics:** [Protagoras](protagoras.md), [Gorgias](gorgias.md), [Symposium](symposium.md), [Phaedrus](phaedrus.md), [Republic](republic.md), [Laws](laws.md).

**Plato, later technical and cosmological:** [Parmenides](parmenides.md), [Theaetetus](theaetetus.md), [Sophist](sophist.md), [Timaeus](timaeus.md).

**Aristotle:** [Organon](organon.md), [Physics](physics.md), [On the Soul](de-anima.md), [Nicomachean Ethics](nicomachean-ethics.md), [Politics](politics.md), [Metaphysics](metaphysics.md), then [Poetics](poetics.md) and [Rhetoric](rhetoric.md).

**Hellenistic and late antique:** [Letter to Menoeceus](letter-to-menoeceus.md), [Letters to Lucilius](letters-to-lucilius.md), [Enchiridion](enchiridion.md), [Discourses](discourses.md), [Meditations](meditations.md), [Enneads](enneads.md).

## References & further reading

- John M. Cooper, ed., *Plato: Complete Works* (Intermediate). The standard English set for all the Platonic texts listed here.
- Jonathan Barnes, ed., *The Complete Works of Aristotle* (Intermediate). Two volumes, the usual complete English Aristotle.
- Terence Irwin, trans., *Nicomachean Ethics* (Beginner). A widely used, carefully annotated translation to start the ethics.
- Robin Waterfield or A. A. Long and D. N. Sedley, *The Hellenistic Philosophers* (Intermediate). Long and Sedley (Advanced as a set) is the best sourcebook for Epicurus, the Stoa, and the skeptics.
- Pierre Hadot, *The Inner Citadel* (Intermediate). A philosophical reading of the *Meditations*.
- A. H. Armstrong, trans., *Plotinus* (Loeb Classical Library) (Advanced). The standard bilingual *Enneads*.
