## Name and dates

Plato (c. 427–347 BCE) was an Athenian of a distinguished family, a student of Socrates, and the founder of the Academy, a community of research and teaching that lasted, with interruptions and changes, for centuries. He lived through the Peloponnesian War, the Thirty, the restoration of democracy, and Socrates' death. Those events are not background color; they are the political wound of the [*Republic*](../texts/republic.md) and the *Apology*.

## Sources

He is the first Greek philosopher whose works survive complete, and they survive as **dialogues**, not treatises. A handful of letters also circulate under his name; the Seventh Letter, if authentic or close to authentic, would matter for his political biography, but its authorship is disputed. Ancient reports of "unwritten doctrines" (especially a lecture on the Good) suggest that the dialogues are not the whole of his teaching. For us, the dialogues are the body of work.

A common teaching division — early "Socratic" dialogues, middle constructive ones, late critical and official ones — is a scholarly tool, not Plato's own table of contents. It is useful if held loosely.

## Thought

The most famous metaphysical claim is the Theory of [Forms](../../../../concepts/metaphysics/the-forms.md) (*eide*). Particular beautiful things are mixed, changing, and perishable; Beauty itself is a stable, intelligible object that particulars "participate in." Knowledge (*episteme*) grasps Forms; opinion (*doxa*) tracks sensibles. The Form of the Good, in *Republic* VI–VII, plays a role for intelligibles analogous to the sun's role for visibles. The [Cave](../texts/republic.md) and the divided line are pictures of education as a turning of the soul, and of the political danger that faces anyone who returns to the prisoners.

This is a reply to [the problem of change](../../../../concepts/metaphysics/the-problem-of-change.md) and to [Parmenides](parmenides.md): give knowledge an unchanging object without simply deleting the sensible world. Later dialogues (*Parmenides*, *Sophist*, *Theaetetus*) press difficulties — the "third man," the being of difference and not-being, the definition of knowledge — hard enough that "the Theory of Forms" is better treated as a family of attempts than as one dogma.

Ethics and politics are not detachable. In the *Republic*, city and soul share a three-part structure: reason, spirit (*[thumos](../../../../glossary/greek/thumos.md)*), appetite. Justice is each part doing its work, with reason ruling. The constructive city includes philosopher-rulers, a severe education, and, for the guardians, common property and family — a thought-experiment and a critique of existing politics, whose status as a blueprint is still argued. The *Symposium* and *Phaedrus* treat [*eros*](../../../../glossary/greek/eros.md) as a force that can be educated toward Beauty itself. The *Laws*, probably late, designs a second-best city more thick with institutions and divine law.

Plato also inherits [Pythagorean](pythagoras.md) mathematics and a story of the soul's immortality and [recollection](../../../../concepts/epistemology/anamnesis.md) (*Meno*, *Phaedo*). The *Timaeus* offers a cosmological myth: a craftsman god orders a living world on an intelligible model. Myth, in Plato, is not a failure of argument. It is a form he uses when argument reaches a certain edge.

## Reception

The Academy, Aristotle's twenty years there, Middle Platonism, and [Plotinus](plotinus.md) make him a founder of a tradition, not only an author. Christian, Islamic, and Jewish philosophers would later reread the Forms, the soul, and the *Timaeus*'s craftsman. Modern philosophy still argues with the *Republic* and the *Theaetetus*. To study him well is to read dialogues as dramas — who is speaking, to whom, after what political disaster — and not only as a store of doctrines.

## Key ideas

- Dialogues are the form, not a wrapper; characters and occasion matter.
- Forms are a reply to flux and to Eleatic being; later dialogues test the theory.
- Soul, city, *eros*, and education are one project, not four side topics.
