# Greek thinkers

This folder gives one overview file for each major thinker on the Greek path, from the Milesians through late antique [Plotinus](plotinus.md). Use it when you want a person, not a school survey or a single book. The numbered lessons in the parent folder remain the narrative path; these files go deeper on lives, sources, and reception.

**Prerequisites:** [Ancient Greek philosophy](../README.md), especially [foundational concepts](../01-foundational-concepts.md).

## Suggested reading order

Read in historical clusters, not only A to Z.

1. Milesians: [Thales](thales.md), [Anaximander](anaximander.md), [Anaximenes](anaximenes.md)
2. Structure, flux, and being: [Pythagoras](pythagoras.md), [Heraclitus](heraclitus.md), [Parmenides](parmenides.md), [Zeno of Elea](zeno-of-elea.md)
3. Pluralist and atomic replies: [Empedocles](empedocles.md), [Anaxagoras](anaxagoras.md), [Democritus and Leucippus](democritus-and-leucippus.md)
4. City and method: [Protagoras and the Sophists](protagoras-and-the-sophists.md), [Socrates](socrates.md), [Plato](plato.md), [Aristotle](aristotle.md)
5. Ways of life: [Diogenes and the Cynics](diogenes-and-the-cynics.md), [Epicurus](epicurus.md), [Zeno of Citium](zeno-of-citium.md), [Seneca](seneca.md), [Epictetus](epictetus.md), [Marcus Aurelius](marcus-aurelius.md), [Pyrrho](pyrrho.md), [Sextus Empiricus](sextus-empiricus.md)
6. Late antique close: [Plotinus](plotinus.md)

## References & further reading

- Julia Annas, *Ancient Philosophy: A Very Short Introduction* (Beginner). A short, reliable map of the questions these thinkers share.
- Jonathan Barnes, *Early Greek Philosophy* (Beginner). A readable collection of Presocratic fragments in translation, with brief framing.
- John M. Cooper, ed., *Plato: Complete Works* (Intermediate). The standard single-volume English Plato, the right place to read the dialogues cited here.
- A. A. Long, *Hellenistic Philosophy* (Intermediate). Clear on Stoics, Epicureans, and Skeptics after Aristotle.
- G. S. Kirk, J. E. Raven, and M. Schofield, *The Presocratic Philosophers* (Advanced). The classic presentation of the early fragments and the arguments about them.
- Pierre Hadot, *What Is Ancient Philosophy?* (Intermediate). Argues that these figures taught ways of life, not only doctrines.
