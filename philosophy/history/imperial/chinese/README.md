# Imperial Chinese philosophy

A small, later branch for Confucian thought under empire, beginning with the Song–Ming "Learning of the Way." It exists so that [Zhu Xi](thinkers/zhu-xi.md) and [Wang Yangming](thinkers/wang-yangming.md) are not forced into the ancient folder.

**Prerequisites:** [Ancient Chinese philosophy](../../ancient/chinese/README.md).

## Suggested reading order

1. [Thinkers](thinkers/README.md): [Zhu Xi](thinkers/zhu-xi.md), then [Wang Yangming](thinkers/wang-yangming.md)

## Contents

- [thinkers/](thinkers/README.md) — Zhu Xi, Wang Yangming
- [texts/](texts/README.md) — reserved for later primary works (Zhu Xi's collected conversations, Wang's *Instructions for Practical Living*)

## References & further reading

- Wing-tsit Chan, *A Source Book in Chinese Philosophy* (Intermediate). Standard translated selections for this period.
- Daniel K. Gardner, trans., *The Four Books: The Basic Teachings of the Later Confucian Tradition* (Beginner). Shows how Zhu Xi framed the ancient books as a curriculum.
