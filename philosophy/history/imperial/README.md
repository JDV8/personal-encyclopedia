# Later and imperial philosophy

This branch is for philosophy after the ancient window: not a full history yet, only the later Chinese Confucian bridges needed to understand how the Warring States books were later read and taught.

**Prerequisites:** [Ancient philosophy](../ancient/README.md), especially the [Chinese path](../ancient/chinese/README.md).

## Suggested reading order

1. [Imperial Chinese thought](chinese/README.md)
2. [Zhu Xi](chinese/thinkers/zhu-xi.md)
3. [Wang Yangming](chinese/thinkers/wang-yangming.md)

## References & further reading

- Wing-tsit Chan, *A Source Book in Chinese Philosophy* (Intermediate). The most convenient English path from the ancient Confucians into the Song and Ming.
- Peter K. Bol, *Neo-Confucianism in History* (Advanced). Places the Song Learning of the Way in its institutional and intellectual setting.
