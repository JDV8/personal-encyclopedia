# Medicine

This branch collects courses on human health and disease. It is organised as foundations first: [microbiology](microbiology/README.md) (what causes infections), [immunology](immunology/README.md) (how the body defends itself) and [epidemiology](epidemiology/README.md) (how we know what causes or prevents disease in populations). The first full course built on them is [vaccines](immunology/vaccines/README.md), nested under immunology because vaccination is applied immunology. A shared [glossary](glossary/README.md) defines terms across all four.

**Prerequisites:** none.

## Suggested reading order

1. [Microbiology](microbiology/README.md), lessons 01–06.
2. [Immunology](immunology/README.md), lessons 01–09.
3. [Epidemiology](epidemiology/README.md), lessons 01–14.
4. [Vaccines](immunology/vaccines/README.md): how they work, how they are tested and regulated, the diseases they prevent, history, schedules, adverse events, controversies, litigation and compensation, public health and ethics, people and key documents.

Use the [glossary](glossary/README.md) whenever a term is unfamiliar.

## Key studies & sources

Every source below was checked to exist (DOI, court record or official page). Notes summarise what each found and its main strengths or limitations.

### Official reports and guidance

- [CDC, *Epidemiology and Prevention of Vaccine-Preventable Diseases* (the "Pink Book")](https://www.cdc.gov/pinkbook/hcp/table-of-contents/index.html): Free US reference textbook on vaccine-preventable diseases and vaccines (14th ed., 2021, chapters updated since).
- [Public Health Agency of Canada, *Canadian Immunization Guide*](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/canadian-immunization-guide.html): Canada's national clinical guide, maintained with NACI.
- [UK Health Security Agency, *Immunisation against infectious disease* (the "Green Book")](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/immunisation-against-infectious-disease-the-green-book): The UK's official vaccine reference.

## References & further reading

- **Beginner.** Philipp Dettmer, *Immune* (2021). The best popular entry point to the immune system.
- **Beginner.** Adam Kucharski, *The Rules of Contagion* (2020). How epidemics spread.
- **Intermediate.** Kenneth J. Rothman, *Epidemiology: An Introduction* (2nd ed., 2012).
- **Advanced.** Stanley A. Plotkin, Walter A. Orenstein, Paul A. Offit and Kathryn M. Edwards (eds), *Plotkin's Vaccines* (8th ed., 2023). The standard reference on every vaccine.
