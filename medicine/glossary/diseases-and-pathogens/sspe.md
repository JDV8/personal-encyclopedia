# SSPE (subacute sclerosing panencephalitis)

A rare, invariably fatal brain disease that develops years after measles infection, usually when infection occurred in infancy. Recent estimates suggest it affects roughly one in 1,000–1,400 children infected before age five. It has not been shown to be caused by measles vaccine, and it has nearly disappeared where vaccination is high.
