# Tuberculosis

A bacterial infection, mostly of the lungs, caused by Mycobacterium tuberculosis, which kills more than a million people a year, the most of any single infectious agent. The only licensed vaccine, BCG, protects young children against severe forms but offers variable protection against adult lung disease. New vaccines such as M72 are in trials.
