# HPV (human papillomavirus)

A family of more than 200 viruses, some of which cause warts and others, especially types 16 and 18, cause cervical, anal, throat and other cancers. HPV vaccines, first licensed in 2006, use virus-like particles. Scotland and Sweden have reported dramatic falls in cervical pre-cancer and cancer in vaccinated cohorts.
