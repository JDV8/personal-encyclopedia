# Antigenic shift

An abrupt, major change in influenza's surface proteins, usually when genes from different strains, including animal strains, reassort in one host. Shift can produce a new subtype to which few people are immune, causing pandemics, as in 1957, 1968 and 2009. It is distinct from gradual drift.
