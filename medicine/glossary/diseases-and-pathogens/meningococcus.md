# Meningococcus (Neisseria meningitidis)

A bacterium carried harmlessly in the throat by many people that can cause rapid, fatal meningitis and sepsis, mainly in infants and adolescents. Serogroups A, B, C, W and Y cause most disease. Conjugate vaccines and newer protein-based MenB vaccines are available; MenAfriVac nearly eliminated group A epidemics in Africa.
