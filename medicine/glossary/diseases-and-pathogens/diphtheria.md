# Diphtheria

A bacterial infection in which a toxin forms a grey membrane in the throat that can block breathing and damages heart and nerves. Before vaccination it was a leading killer of children. It resurged in former Soviet states in the 1990s when vaccination faltered, with over 150,000 cases, and in Yemen and Rohingya camps more recently.
