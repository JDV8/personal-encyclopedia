# Smallpox

A disease caused by variola virus, with a characteristic rash and a fatality rate of about 30% for the major form. It killed an estimated 300 million people in the 20th century. Vaccination with vaccinia virus, then surveillance and containment, led to eradication, certified in 1980; samples remain in two laboratories.
