# Variolation

The deliberate infection of a person with material from smallpox pustules, usually through a scratch, to produce a milder infection and lifelong immunity. Practised for centuries in Asia and Africa and introduced to Britain in 1721, it killed about 1–2% of recipients compared with about 30% for natural smallpox, and could spread the disease.
