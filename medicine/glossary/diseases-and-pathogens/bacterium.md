# Bacterium

A single-celled organism without a nucleus. Most bacteria are harmless, but some cause disease directly, such as tuberculosis, or through toxins, such as tetanus and diphtheria. Antibiotics treat many bacterial infections, but vaccines remain important because of rapid disease, toxin-mediated damage and growing antibiotic resistance.
