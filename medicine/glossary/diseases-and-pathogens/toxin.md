# Toxin

A poison produced by a living organism. Bacterial exotoxins, such as those of tetanus, diphtheria and pertussis, cause the main symptoms of these diseases even when the bacteria stay localised. Antibodies against the toxin, induced by toxoid vaccines or given as antitoxin, can prevent or treat disease.
