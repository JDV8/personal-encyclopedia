# Measles

A highly contagious viral disease with fever, cough, rash and complications including pneumonia, encephalitis and death, mainly in young children and malnourished populations. It also causes immune amnesia, erasing existing immunity. Two doses of MMR vaccine are about 97% effective. Measles requires about 95% coverage to prevent outbreaks.
