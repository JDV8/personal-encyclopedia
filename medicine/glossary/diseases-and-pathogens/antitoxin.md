# Antitoxin

Antibodies that neutralise a toxin, originally obtained from immunised horses. Emil von Behring's diphtheria antitoxin, developed with Shibasaburo Kitasato around 1890, earned the first Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine in 1901. Contaminated antitoxin, as in St Louis in 1901, killed children and led to the first US biologics law in 1902.
