# Shingles (herpes zoster)

A painful, blistering rash caused by reactivation of varicella-zoster virus, often in older adults or immunocompromised people. Pain can persist for months as post-herpetic neuralgia. The recombinant adjuvanted vaccine Shingrix is over 90% effective in trials. Some studies have linked shingles vaccination to lower dementia risk, a finding still being investigated.
