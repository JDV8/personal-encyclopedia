# Poliomyelitis

Infection by poliovirus, spread mainly by the faecal-oral route. Most infections are asymptomatic, but in fewer than one in 100 the virus attacks motor neurons, causing paralysis. Epidemics paralysed hundreds of thousands of children each year before vaccines. Wild poliovirus types 2 and 3 have been eradicated; type 1 persists in Pakistan and Afghanistan.
