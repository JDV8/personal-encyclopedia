# Tetanus

A disease caused by a toxin from Clostridium tetani spores found in soil, which enter wounds and cause painful muscle spasms, including lockjaw. Neonatal tetanus, often from unclean cutting of the umbilical cord, killed hundreds of thousands of babies a year. Tetanus cannot be eradicated because spores persist in the environment.
