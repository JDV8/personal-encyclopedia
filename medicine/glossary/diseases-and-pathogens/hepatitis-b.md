# Hepatitis B

A liver infection caused by a virus spread by blood, sex and from mother to baby. Infants infected at birth have about a 90% chance of chronic infection, which can lead to cirrhosis and liver cancer. Hepatitis B vaccine was called the first anti-cancer vaccine; birth-dose policy became a 2025 controversy in the US.
