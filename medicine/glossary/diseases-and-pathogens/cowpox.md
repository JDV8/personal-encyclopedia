# Cowpox

A mild poxvirus infection of cows and humans, transmitted from cattle to milkmaids. Edward Jenner's 1796 experiment used cowpox from a milkmaid, Sarah Nelmes, to protect James Phipps against smallpox. The virus later used in smallpox vaccine, vaccinia, is related to but genetically distinct from cowpox; its precise origin is uncertain.
