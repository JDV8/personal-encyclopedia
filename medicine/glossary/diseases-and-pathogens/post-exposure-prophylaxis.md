# Post-exposure prophylaxis

Treatment given after exposure to prevent disease, often by vaccination with or without immune globulin. It works when the incubation period is long enough for immunity to develop, as with rabies, hepatitis B, measles, varicella and smallpox. Rabies post-exposure prophylaxis is nearly always effective if given promptly.
