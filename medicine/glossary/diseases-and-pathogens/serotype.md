# Serotype

A variant of a microbe distinguished by the antibodies that recognise its surface antigens. Pneumococcus has about 100 serotypes, defined by its capsule; dengue has four. Immunity to one serotype may not protect against others, so vaccines must include several, and serotype replacement can occur when vaccines remove some types.
