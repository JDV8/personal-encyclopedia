# RSV (respiratory syncytial virus)

A common respiratory virus that is the leading cause of hospitalisation in infants and causes significant illness in older adults. A 1960s formalin-inactivated vaccine worsened disease and caused two deaths, setting back the field for decades. Vaccines for older adults and pregnant women and long-acting monoclonal antibodies for infants were licensed from 2023.
