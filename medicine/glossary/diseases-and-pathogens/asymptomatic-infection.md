# Asymptomatic infection

Infection without symptoms. Most poliovirus infections are asymptomatic, with paralysis in fewer than one in 100, which allowed silent spread. Asymptomatic infections complicate surveillance and mean that reported cases underestimate total infections, which affects estimates of case fatality. Serological surveys, which test blood for antibodies, are used to estimate how many people have been infected silently.
