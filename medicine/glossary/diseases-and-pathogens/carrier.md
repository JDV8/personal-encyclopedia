# Carrier

A person who harbours and can transmit a pathogen without symptoms. 'Typhoid Mary' Mallon, a cook in New York in the early 1900s, is the classic example. Carriage matters for vaccines: those that reduce carriage, such as conjugate vaccines, produce herd effects, while toxoid vaccines protect against disease without preventing carriage.
