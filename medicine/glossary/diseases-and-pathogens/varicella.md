# Varicella (chickenpox)

A highly contagious infection by varicella-zoster virus causing an itchy blistering rash, usually mild in children but more serious in adults, pregnant women and immunocompromised people. The virus stays dormant in nerves and can reactivate as shingles. Live varicella vaccine was licensed in the US in 1995; the UK added it to its schedule only in 2026.
