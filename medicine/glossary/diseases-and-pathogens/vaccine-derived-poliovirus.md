# Vaccine-derived poliovirus

A strain descended from the oral polio vaccine that has mutated and regained the ability to cause paralysis and spread. Circulating vaccine-derived poliovirus outbreaks occur where immunisation coverage is low. They now cause more paralysis cases than wild virus in some years, which led to the novel OPV2 vaccine and plans to stop oral vaccine use.
