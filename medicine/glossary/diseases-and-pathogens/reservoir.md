# Reservoir

The place where a pathogen normally lives and multiplies, from which it spreads: humans, animals or the environment. Tetanus spores live in soil, so vaccination protects individuals but cannot eliminate the disease. Pathogens restricted to humans, such as measles and polio, are candidates for eradication.
