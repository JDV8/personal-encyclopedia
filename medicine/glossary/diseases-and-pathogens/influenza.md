# Influenza

An acute respiratory infection by influenza viruses that causes seasonal epidemics and occasional pandemics. Vaccines are updated yearly because of antigenic drift, and their effectiveness typically ranges from 40% to 60% in well-matched years. Influenza kills hundreds of thousands worldwide each year, mostly older adults.
