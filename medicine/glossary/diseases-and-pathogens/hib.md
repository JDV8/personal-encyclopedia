# Hib (Haemophilus influenzae type b)

A bacterium that, despite its name, does not cause influenza. Before conjugate vaccines it was the leading cause of bacterial meningitis in US children under five, causing deafness, brain damage and death. Hib conjugate vaccines, introduced from 1987, reduced disease by over 99% in many countries.
