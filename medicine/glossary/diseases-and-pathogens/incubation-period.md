# Incubation period

The time between infection and the first symptoms: about 10–14 days for measles, one to three days for influenza and months to years for some rabies cases. Knowing it helps trace contacts and decide whether post-exposure vaccination can still help, as with rabies and measles vaccine within 72 hours of exposure.
