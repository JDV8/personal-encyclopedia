# Antigenic drift

Gradual accumulation of small mutations in a virus's surface proteins, such as influenza's haemagglutinin, that let it partly escape existing immunity. Drift is why influenza vaccines must be updated each year and why their effectiveness varies. SARS-CoV-2 variants show a similar process, prompting updated COVID-19 vaccines.
