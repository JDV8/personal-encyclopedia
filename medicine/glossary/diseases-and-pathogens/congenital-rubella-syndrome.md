# Congenital rubella syndrome

Birth defects, including deafness, cataracts, heart defects and intellectual disability, caused by rubella infection during early pregnancy. The 1964–65 US rubella epidemic caused an estimated 20,000 cases. Rubella vaccination aims mainly to protect babies, by preventing infection in women and stopping its circulation. The Americas were declared free of endemic rubella in 2015.
