# Vaccine-enhanced disease

Worse disease in vaccinated people who later become infected than in unvaccinated people. It occurred with a formalin-inactivated RSV vaccine in the 1960s, when 80% of vaccinated infants who caught RSV were hospitalised and two died, and with Dengvaxia in seronegative children. Regulators required careful monitoring for it in COVID-19 vaccine trials.
