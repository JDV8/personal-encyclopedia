# Rotavirus

The leading cause of severe diarrhoea in young children worldwide before vaccines, causing hundreds of thousands of deaths a year, mostly in poorer countries. Oral live vaccines introduced in 2006 cut hospitalisations sharply. The first vaccine, RotaShield, was withdrawn in 1999 because of intussusception; current vaccines carry a much smaller risk.
