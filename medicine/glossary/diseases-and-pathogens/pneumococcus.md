# Pneumococcus (Streptococcus pneumoniae)

A bacterium that causes pneumonia, meningitis, bloodstream infections and ear infections, especially in young children and older adults. It has about 100 capsular serotypes. Conjugate vaccines covering 10–21 serotypes protect children and adults and have produced strong herd effects, partly offset by serotype replacement.
