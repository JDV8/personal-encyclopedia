# Antimicrobial resistance

The ability of bacteria and other microbes to survive drugs designed to kill them, driven by overuse of antibiotics. It threatens treatment of common infections. Vaccines help by preventing infections that would otherwise be treated with antibiotics, and pneumococcal and typhoid conjugate vaccines have reduced resistant infections.
