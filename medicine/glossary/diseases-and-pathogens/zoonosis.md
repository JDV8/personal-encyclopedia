# Zoonosis

An infection that passes from animals to humans, such as rabies, Ebola, mpox and avian influenza; many emerging diseases are zoonoses. Diseases with animal reservoirs cannot be eradicated by human vaccination alone, which is one reason smallpox, which had no animal reservoir, could be eradicated.
