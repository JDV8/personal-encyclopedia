# Virus

A tiny infectious agent consisting of genetic material, DNA or RNA, inside a protein coat and sometimes a fatty envelope, that can only replicate inside living cells. Viruses cause measles, polio, influenza, hepatitis B, HPV and COVID-19. Antibiotics do not work against them, which makes vaccines and antivirals the main tools.
