# VAPP (vaccine-associated paralytic polio)

Paralytic polio caused by oral polio vaccine in a vaccinated person or close contact, occurring roughly once per 2.4 million doses in the US, more often after the first dose. Because wild polio had been eliminated, the US switched to inactivated polio vaccine in 2000, which cannot cause VAPP.
