# Serotype replacement

The increase in disease from serotypes not included in a vaccine after vaccine serotypes have been suppressed. It has been seen after pneumococcal conjugate vaccines, partly eroding their benefit and driving the development of vaccines covering more serotypes. Overall invasive pneumococcal disease has still fallen substantially in vaccinated populations.
