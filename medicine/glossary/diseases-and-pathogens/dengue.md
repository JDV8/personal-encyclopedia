# Dengue

A mosquito-borne viral disease in tropical and subtropical regions, with four serotypes. A first infection is usually mild, but a second infection with a different serotype carries higher risk of severe dengue through antibody-dependent enhancement. The first vaccine, Dengvaxia, increased risk in people never previously infected; newer vaccines such as Qdenga are in use.
