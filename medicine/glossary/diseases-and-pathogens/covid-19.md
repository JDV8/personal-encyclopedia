# COVID-19

The disease caused by the coronavirus SARS-CoV-2, first identified in Wuhan, China, in late 2019, which caused a pandemic declared in March 2020. Vaccines were authorised within a year. WHO's estimate of excess deaths in 2020–21 was about 14.9 million. The virus's origin, whether from an animal market or a laboratory, remains disputed.
