# Pertussis (whooping cough)

A bacterial respiratory infection that causes prolonged coughing fits, most dangerous for young infants, who may stop breathing. Whole-cell vaccines were highly effective but caused frequent reactions; acellular vaccines are better tolerated but immunity wanes faster. Vaccinating pregnant women protects newborns; a UK study estimated about 91% effectiveness against disease in babies under three months.
