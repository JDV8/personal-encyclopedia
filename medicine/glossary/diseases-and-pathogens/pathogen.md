# Pathogen

Any organism or agent that causes disease: viruses, bacteria, fungi, parasites and prions. Not every microbe is a pathogen; most bacteria in and on the body are harmless or useful. How much disease a pathogen causes depends on its virulence and on the host's immunity, which vaccines aim to strengthen before exposure.
