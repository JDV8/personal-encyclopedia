# Immune amnesia

The loss of pre-existing immune memory caused by measles, which infects and destroys memory lymphocytes. Studies published in 2019 showed that measles erased 11–73% of children's antibody repertoire, leaving them vulnerable to other infections for years. It helps explain why measles vaccination reduced overall child mortality more than expected.
