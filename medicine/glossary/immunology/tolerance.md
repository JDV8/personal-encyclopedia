# Immune tolerance

The immune system's learned non-response to the body's own tissues and to harmless substances, established largely by deleting or suppressing self-reactive lymphocytes in the thymus and bone marrow and by regulatory T cells. When tolerance fails, autoimmune disease can result. Claims that vaccines break tolerance are the basis of many alleged links between vaccines and autoimmune disease.
