# Antigen

Any molecule that the adaptive immune system can recognise, usually a protein or polysaccharide on the surface of a microbe or toxin. Antibodies and T-cell receptors bind to small parts of an antigen called epitopes. Vaccines work by presenting the immune system with antigens, whole or in part, without the full danger of infection, so that it is ready when the real pathogen appears.
