# Active immunity

Immunity produced by a person's own immune system after infection or vaccination, as opposed to borrowed antibodies. It takes days to weeks to develop but can last years or a lifetime because it creates memory cells. All vaccines aim at active immunity, except passive products such as immune globulins and monoclonal antibodies, which are sometimes grouped with them.
