# Complement

A set of blood proteins that, once triggered by antibodies or microbial surfaces, form a cascade that coats microbes for phagocytosis, attracts immune cells and can punch holes in bacterial membranes. People with deficiencies in late complement components are highly susceptible to meningococcal disease, which is why meningococcal vaccines are recommended for them.
