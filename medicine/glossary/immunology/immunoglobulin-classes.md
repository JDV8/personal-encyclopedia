# Immunoglobulin classes (IgG, IgM, IgA, IgE, IgD)

The five types of antibody, distinguished by their constant regions. IgM appears first in a new response; IgG dominates later responses, crosses the placenta and lasts longest; IgA protects mucosal surfaces such as the gut and airways; IgE drives allergic reactions and anaphylaxis; IgD's role is less clear. Class switching from IgM to other classes requires T-cell help.
