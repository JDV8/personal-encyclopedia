# Inflammation

The body's local response to infection or injury, marked by redness, heat, swelling, pain and loss of function. Blood vessels widen and leak, and immune cells flood in. Inflammation at the injection site is expected after many vaccines and is part of how they work. Chronic or misdirected inflammation, by contrast, underlies many diseases.
