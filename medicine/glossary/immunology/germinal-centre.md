# Germinal centre

A structure that forms in lymph nodes and spleen during an immune response, where B cells multiply, mutate their antibody genes and are selected for better binding with help from T follicular helper cells. This produces high-affinity antibodies, class switching and long-lived plasma and memory cells. Vaccines that drive strong germinal centres tend to give durable protection.
