# Maternal antibodies

IgG antibodies that cross the placenta from mother to baby, especially in late pregnancy, and give the newborn temporary passive protection for weeks to months. Vaccinating pregnant women against pertussis, influenza, RSV and COVID-19 boosts this transfer. Maternal antibodies can also blunt responses to some infant vaccines, which is one reason measles vaccine is given after about 12 months.
