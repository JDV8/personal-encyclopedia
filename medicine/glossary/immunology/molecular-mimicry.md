# Molecular mimicry

The idea that a microbial or vaccine antigen resembling a human protein can trigger antibodies or T cells that cross-react with the body's own tissue. It is well established for some infections: rheumatic fever after streptococcal throat infection, and Guillain-Barré after Campylobacter. It is often proposed, but rarely demonstrated, as a mechanism for vaccine-related autoimmunity.
