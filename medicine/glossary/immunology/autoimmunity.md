# Autoimmunity

An immune response against the body's own tissues, causing diseases such as type 1 diabetes, multiple sclerosis and Guillain-Barré syndrome. Infections can trigger autoimmune disease, and the question of whether vaccines do is central to many safety debates. Large studies have mostly been reassuring, with a few confirmed exceptions such as GBS after the 1976 swine flu vaccine.
