# Antibody

A Y-shaped protein, also called an immunoglobulin, made by B cells and plasma cells, that binds a specific epitope. Antibodies neutralise viruses and toxins, coat microbes for destruction by phagocytes, and activate complement. There are five main classes: IgG, IgA, IgM, IgE and IgD. Antibody levels after vaccination are often used as a measurable stand-in for protection.
