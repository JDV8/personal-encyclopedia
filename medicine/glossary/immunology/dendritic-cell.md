# Dendritic cell

A branched immune cell that patrols tissues, captures antigen and, once activated by danger signals, migrates to lymph nodes to start adaptive responses. Ralph Steinman's discovery of dendritic cells earned a share of the 2011 Nobel Prize. They link innate and adaptive immunity, and are the main target of adjuvants and of the lipid nanoparticles in mRNA vaccines.
