# Pattern-recognition receptor

A receptor of innate immune cells that detects molecular patterns common to microbes, such as bacterial cell-wall components or foreign RNA, or signs of tissue damage. Toll-like receptors are the best-known family. Many adjuvants act through these receptors, and Karikó and Weissman's discovery that modified nucleosides avoid Toll-like receptor activation made mRNA vaccines tolerable.
