# Passive immunity

Protection from ready-made antibodies rather than the body's own response: from mother to baby, or by injection of immune globulin or monoclonal antibodies. It acts immediately but fades within weeks to months and leaves no memory. Examples include rabies and tetanus immune globulin and nirsevimab for infant RSV. Active immunity, from infection or vaccination, is the alternative.
