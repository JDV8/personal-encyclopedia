# Antigen-presenting cell

A cell that takes up antigen, processes it, and displays fragments on MHC class II molecules to helper T cells. Dendritic cells are the most important, carrying antigen from tissues such as the injection site to lymph nodes; macrophages and B cells also present antigen. Adjuvants work largely by activating these cells.
