# HLA (human leukocyte antigen)

The human version of the MHC, encoded by the most variable genes in the genome. HLA types matter for organ transplantation and are associated with many autoimmune diseases. In vaccine safety, the HLA-DQB1*06:02 type is strongly linked to narcolepsy, which helped researchers investigate the narcolepsy cases after Pandemrix vaccination in 2009–10.
