# MHC (major histocompatibility complex)

A family of cell-surface proteins that display peptide fragments to T cells; in humans they are called HLA. Class I molecules, on almost all cells, show peptides from inside the cell to CD8 cells. Class II molecules, on antigen-presenting cells, show peptides from outside to CD4 cells. HLA variation between people partly explains why responses to vaccines and some adverse events differ.
