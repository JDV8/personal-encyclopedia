# Helper T cell (CD4)

A T cell carrying the CD4 marker that recognises antigen on MHC class II molecules and releases cytokines that direct other immune cells. Helper T cells license B cells to class switch and form memory, and help CD8 cells. Their loss in untreated HIV infection shows their importance: patients become vulnerable to many infections, and responses to vaccines become weak.
