# Phagocyte

A cell that engulfs and digests microbes and debris, chiefly neutrophils and macrophages. Elie Metchnikoff described phagocytosis in the 1880s, sharing the 1908 Nobel Prize with Paul Ehrlich. Phagocytes are central to innate immunity and work together with antibodies, which mark targets through opsonisation. Some aluminium adjuvant particles are taken up by macrophages at the injection site.
