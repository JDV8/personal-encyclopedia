# Immunogen

A substance that not only binds immune receptors but actually provokes an immune response. All immunogens are antigens, but not all antigens are immunogenic: small molecules and some polysaccharides bind antibodies yet trigger little response on their own. Vaccine design is largely about making antigens more immunogenic, for example by linking them to carrier proteins or adding adjuvants.
