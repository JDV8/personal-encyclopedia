# Anaphylaxis

A rapid, potentially fatal allergic reaction affecting several body systems, typically within minutes to an hour of exposure, with hives, swelling, breathing difficulty and falling blood pressure. It is usually driven by IgE antibodies. After vaccination it is rare, at roughly one case per million doses for most vaccines, and is treated with adrenaline, which is why vaccinees are observed briefly.
