# Cytotoxic T cell (CD8)

A T cell carrying the CD8 marker that recognises peptides on MHC class I molecules, which nearly all cells display, and kills cells showing foreign peptides, such as virus-infected cells. Live attenuated, viral-vector and mRNA vaccines, which produce antigen inside cells, are particularly good at inducing CD8 responses; inactivated and subunit vaccines usually induce fewer.
