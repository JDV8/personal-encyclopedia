# Plasma cell

A fully differentiated B cell that acts as an antibody factory, secreting thousands of antibody molecules per second. Most plasma cells die within days or weeks, but some migrate to the bone marrow and survive for years or decades, continuously producing antibody. These long-lived plasma cells explain why antibody levels after measles infection or vaccination can persist for most of a lifetime.
