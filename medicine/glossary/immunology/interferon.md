# Interferon

A group of cytokines, first described in 1957, that cells release when infected by viruses, warning neighbouring cells to resist infection and activating immune cells. Type I interferons are a key early antiviral defence. Some viruses evolved ways to block them, and people with rare genetic defects in interferon pathways can suffer severe illness from live vaccines or infections such as COVID-19.
