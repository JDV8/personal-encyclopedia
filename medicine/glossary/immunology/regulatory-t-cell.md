# Regulatory T cell

A subset of CD4 T cells, usually marked by the FOXP3 protein, that suppresses other immune responses and maintains tolerance to self. Babies born with FOXP3 mutations develop severe autoimmune disease. The 2025 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine was awarded to Mary Brunkow, Fred Ramsdell and Shimon Sakaguchi for discoveries about peripheral immune tolerance, including these cells.
