# Monoclonal antibody

An antibody made in the laboratory from a single clone of cells, so that every molecule is identical and binds the same epitope. Developed by César Milstein and Georges Köhler in 1975, monoclonals are now used as drugs, diagnostics and passive immunisations, such as nirsevimab against RSV in infants. They give immediate but temporary protection.
