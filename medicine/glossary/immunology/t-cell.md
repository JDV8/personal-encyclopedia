# T cell

A lymphocyte that matures in the thymus and recognises antigen fragments displayed on MHC molecules. The two main kinds are CD4 helper T cells, which coordinate responses, and CD8 cytotoxic T cells, which kill infected cells. T cells are important against viruses and intracellular bacteria, and help explain why vaccines can protect against severe disease even when antibodies wane.
