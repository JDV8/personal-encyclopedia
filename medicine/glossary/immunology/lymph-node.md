# Lymph node

A small bean-shaped organ where lymph from tissues is filtered and where antigen-presenting cells meet B and T cells. Adaptive responses begin here, and germinal centres form inside. Swollen lymph nodes in the armpit are a common, harmless reaction after vaccination, notably after mRNA COVID-19 vaccines, and can show up on mammograms taken soon afterwards.
