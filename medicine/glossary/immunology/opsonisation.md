# Opsonisation

The coating of a microbe with antibodies or complement proteins so that phagocytes can grab and destroy it more easily; the coating molecules are called opsonins. It is the main way antibodies protect against encapsulated bacteria such as pneumococcus, Hib and meningococcus, whose sugar capsules otherwise make them slippery to phagocytes, and is measured in assays used to license pneumococcal vaccines.
