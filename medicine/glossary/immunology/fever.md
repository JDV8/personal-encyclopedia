# Fever

A rise in the body's set-point temperature, driven by cytokines acting on the brain's hypothalamus. Fever is a common reaction to infections and, less often, to vaccines. In young children a rapid rise can trigger febrile seizures, which are frightening but usually harmless; the risk after measles-containing vaccines is small and higher with combined MMRV in the second year.
