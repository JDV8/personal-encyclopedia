# Neutralising antibody

An antibody that blocks a virus or toxin from working, for example by binding the part of a virus that attaches to cells. Neutralisation is measured in laboratory assays and is often the best available correlate of protection, as for measles, polio and COVID-19. Not all antibodies that bind a pathogen neutralise it.
