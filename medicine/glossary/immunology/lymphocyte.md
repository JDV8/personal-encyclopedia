# Lymphocyte

A type of white blood cell that includes B cells, T cells and natural killer cells. B and T lymphocytes carry receptors that are each specific for one antigen, generated by random rearrangement of gene segments, so the body has millions of different clones. The clonal selection theory explains how vaccination expands the few clones that match a vaccine's antigens.
