# Immunodeficiency

A weakened immune system, either inherited, as in severe combined immunodeficiency, or acquired through HIV, cancer treatment, organ transplant drugs or other medicines. Immunodeficient people are at greater risk from infections, may respond poorly to vaccines, and can be harmed by live vaccines, which is a key contraindication. They depend heavily on vaccinated people around them.
