# Affinity maturation

The gradual improvement in how tightly antibodies bind their target during and after an immune response, through mutation of antibody genes in germinal centres and selection of the best-binding B cells. It is one reason why booster doses spaced months apart can produce better antibodies than doses given close together, and why intervals in vaccine schedules matter.
