# Epitope

The specific part of an antigen that an antibody or T-cell receptor actually binds, often only a handful of amino acids or sugar units. One protein can carry many epitopes. B-cell epitopes are usually on the surface of the folded molecule, while T-cell epitopes are short peptides displayed on MHC molecules. Mutations in key epitopes let viruses such as influenza escape existing immunity.
