# Toll-like receptor

A family of pattern-recognition receptors, named after the fruit-fly gene Toll, that recognise microbial products such as lipopolysaccharide, flagellin and viral RNA or DNA. Their discovery earned Bruce Beutler and Jules Hoffmann a share of the 2011 Nobel Prize. Newer adjuvants such as MPL in some hepatitis B, HPV and shingles vaccines and CpG in Heplisav-B are designed to activate specific Toll-like receptors.
