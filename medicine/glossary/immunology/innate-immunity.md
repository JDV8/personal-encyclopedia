# Innate immunity

The fast, non-specific first line of defence present from birth: skin and mucous membranes, phagocytes, natural killer cells, complement, and inflammatory signals. It responds within minutes to hours to general features of microbes and damaged tissue. Innate responses cause most of the short-lived side effects of vaccination, such as sore arms and fever, and are needed to start adaptive immunity.
