# Cytokine

A small signalling protein that immune cells use to communicate, including interleukins, interferons and tumour necrosis factor. Cytokines coordinate inflammation, fever and the type of adaptive response. Short-lived cytokine release explains common reactions after vaccination such as fever, fatigue and muscle aches; a dangerous excess, a cytokine storm, can occur in some severe infections.
