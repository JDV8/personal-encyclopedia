# Antibody-dependent enhancement

A phenomenon in which non-neutralising or low levels of antibody make a later infection worse, for example by helping a virus enter immune cells. It is well documented for dengue: a second infection with a different serotype can be more severe. This explained the safety problem with Dengvaxia in children never previously infected; it was a feared but unconfirmed risk for COVID-19 vaccines.
