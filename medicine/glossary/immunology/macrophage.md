# Macrophage

A large, long-lived phagocyte resident in tissues that engulfs microbes, presents antigen and releases cytokines. Macrophages help clear infections and repair tissue. Some bacteria, such as the tuberculosis bacterium, survive inside them. In vaccine controversies, macrophagic myofasciitis, a lesion of aluminium-containing macrophages at the injection site, has been proposed as a disease, a claim most reviews have not accepted.
