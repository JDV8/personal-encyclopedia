# Pre-registration

Publicly recording a study's hypotheses, outcomes and analysis plan before collecting data, for example on ClinicalTrials.gov. It makes it harder to change outcomes after seeing results. Since 2005, major medical journals have required trial registration as a condition of publication. Observational studies are registered less often, which leaves more room for flexible analysis.
