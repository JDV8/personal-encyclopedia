# Elimination

Ending continuous transmission of a disease in a defined region, usually for at least 12 months, while imported cases may still occur. The Americas eliminated measles in 2016, but Venezuela, Brazil, Canada in 2025 and the US in 2026 lost the status. Elimination is distinct from eradication, which is global and permanent.
