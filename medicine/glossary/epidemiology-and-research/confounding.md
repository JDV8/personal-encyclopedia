# Confounding

Distortion of an association by a third factor linked to both exposure and outcome. Vaccinated people may differ in health, wealth and behaviour from unvaccinated people, so differences in outcomes may not be due to vaccination. Randomisation, matching and statistical adjustment are used to control it.
