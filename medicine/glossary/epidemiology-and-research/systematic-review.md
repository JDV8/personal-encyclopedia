# Systematic review

A review that uses explicit, reproducible methods to find, appraise and combine all relevant studies on a question, unlike narrative reviews. Cochrane reviews are well-known examples. Systematic reviews sit near the top of evidence hierarchies but are only as good as the studies they include.
