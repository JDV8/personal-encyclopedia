# Causation

The relationship in which one event brings about another. Association does not prove causation. Criteria such as those proposed by Austin Bradford Hill in 1965 (strength, consistency, temporality, dose-response, plausibility and others) help judge whether an association is causal. WHO uses a structured algorithm for individual vaccine adverse events.
