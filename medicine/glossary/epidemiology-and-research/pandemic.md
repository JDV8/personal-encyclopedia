# Pandemic

An epidemic spreading across several continents, affecting large numbers of people. Recent pandemics include the 1918, 1957, 1968 and 2009 influenza pandemics, HIV and COVID-19. WHO's declarations have legal consequences, including activation of liability protections for vaccines in the US under the PREP Act.
