# Number needed to vaccinate

The number of people who must be vaccinated to prevent one case, hospitalisation or death, the inverse of the absolute risk reduction. It depends on how common the disease is: when a disease is rare, the number is large even for an effective vaccine. It is useful for weighing benefits against rare harms.
