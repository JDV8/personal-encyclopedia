# Case fatality rate

The proportion of people diagnosed with a disease who die from it. It depends on how many mild cases are detected: if only severe cases are counted, the rate looks high. Measles has a case fatality rate of about one to three per 1,000 in rich countries but much higher in malnourished populations.
