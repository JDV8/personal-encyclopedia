# Basic reproduction number (R0)

The average number of people one infected person would infect in a fully susceptible population. It depends on the pathogen and on social contact patterns. Measles has an R0 commonly quoted as 12–18, among the highest known; seasonal influenza about 1–2. R0 determines the herd immunity threshold.
