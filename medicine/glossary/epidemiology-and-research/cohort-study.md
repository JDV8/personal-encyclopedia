# Cohort study

An observational study following groups defined by an exposure, such as vaccinated and unvaccinated people, over time to compare outcomes. Nationwide Danish cohorts of hundreds of thousands of children found no link between MMR and autism. Cohort studies can study rare outcomes but may be biased by confounding.
