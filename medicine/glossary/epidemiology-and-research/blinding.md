# Blinding

Keeping participants, investigators or outcome assessors unaware of who received which intervention, to prevent expectations from biasing results. Double-blind trials hide allocation from both participants and investigators. Vaccine trials can be partially unblinded by noticeable side effects such as sore arms, which may affect reporting. Outcomes measured objectively, such as laboratory-confirmed infection, are less vulnerable.
