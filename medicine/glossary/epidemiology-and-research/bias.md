# Bias

Systematic error that distorts study results away from the truth, unlike random error. Types include selection bias, information bias such as recall bias, publication bias and confounding. Bias can arise from study design, analysis or funding pressures, and both supporters and critics of vaccines accuse each other's studies of it.
