# Mortality rate

The number of deaths in a population over a period, usually per 1,000 or 100,000 people per year. Disease-specific mortality fell for many infections before vaccines, owing to nutrition, sanitation and medical care; vaccine critics use this to argue vaccines mattered little, while epidemiologists point to sharp falls in cases after vaccine introduction.
