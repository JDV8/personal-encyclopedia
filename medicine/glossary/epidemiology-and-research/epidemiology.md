# Epidemiology

The study of how diseases are distributed in populations and what determines them, using counting, comparison and statistics. Epidemiology identified smoking as a cause of lung cancer, tracks outbreaks, and provides most of the evidence about vaccine effectiveness and safety, since rare effects can only be seen across very large populations.
