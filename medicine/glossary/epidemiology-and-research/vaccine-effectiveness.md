# Vaccine effectiveness

The reduction in disease among vaccinated people in real-world use, estimated from observational studies such as test-negative designs. Effectiveness may be lower than trial efficacy because of storage, waning, different populations or new variants, and observational estimates can be biased by differences between vaccinated and unvaccinated people.
