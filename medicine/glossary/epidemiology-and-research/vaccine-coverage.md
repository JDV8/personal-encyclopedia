# Vaccine coverage

The proportion of a target population that has received a vaccine, often reported for DTP3 (three doses of DTP) as a marker of system performance. WHO and UNICEF publish annual estimates. Coverage figures may be inflated by administrative data, and household surveys sometimes give lower estimates.
