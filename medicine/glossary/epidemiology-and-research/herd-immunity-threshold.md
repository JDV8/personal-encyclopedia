# Herd immunity threshold

The proportion of a population that must be immune to stop sustained spread, approximately 1 − 1/R0 in a simple model. For measles this is about 92–95%; for polio about 80–85%. Real thresholds are affected by clustering of unvaccinated people, waning immunity and imperfect vaccines.
