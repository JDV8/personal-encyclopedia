# Eradication

Permanent reduction of a disease to zero worldwide, so that control measures are no longer needed. Only smallpox among human diseases, and rinderpest in cattle, have been eradicated. Polio and Guinea worm are close. Eradication requires no animal reservoir, effective tools and sustained political commitment.
