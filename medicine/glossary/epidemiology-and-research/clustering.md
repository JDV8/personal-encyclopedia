# Clustering of non-vaccination

The tendency of unvaccinated people to live, worship and attend school together, so that local coverage can fall far below national averages. Outbreaks start in these clusters, as in the Amish in Ohio, Orthodox Jewish communities in New York, Mennonite communities in Texas and Ontario, and Samoa. National coverage figures can hide them.
