# Base-rate fallacy

Ignoring how common something is in the population when interpreting data. For example, if 95% of people are vaccinated, most hospital patients with a disease may be vaccinated even when the vaccine is effective, simply because there are so many more vaccinated people. Misreading such figures is common in vaccine debates.
