# Multiple comparisons

The problem that testing many outcomes or subgroups produces some statistically significant results by chance alone. With 20 independent tests at p < 0.05, one false positive is expected. Re-analyses of vaccine studies that find a significant subgroup, such as the Hooker re-analysis of the CDC MMR data, need to be weighed against this.
