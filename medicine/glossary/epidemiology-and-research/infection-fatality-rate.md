# Infection fatality rate

The proportion of all infected people, including undiagnosed and asymptomatic ones, who die. It is lower than the case fatality rate and requires serological surveys to estimate. Disputes over the infection fatality rate of COVID-19 shaped early policy debates and arguments about vaccine benefit in young people.
