# Odds ratio

The ratio of the odds of an outcome in one group to the odds in another, commonly used in case-control studies. For rare outcomes it approximates the relative risk. An odds ratio of 1 means no association; confidence intervals indicate uncertainty. When the outcome is common, the odds ratio overstates the relative risk, a frequent source of misleading headlines.
