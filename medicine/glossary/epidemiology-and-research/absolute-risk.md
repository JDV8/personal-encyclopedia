# Absolute risk

The actual probability of an outcome in a group over a period, such as 3 in 100,000. Absolute risk differences, and their inverse, the number needed to vaccinate, show the practical size of benefits and harms. Both sides of vaccine debates sometimes use relative figures where absolute ones would give a clearer picture.
