# Excess mortality

The number of deaths above what would be expected based on previous years. It captures deaths missed by cause-of-death coding, as in the COVID-19 pandemic, but depends heavily on the baseline chosen. Excess-mortality claims have been made both to show pandemic deaths and, contested by most analyses, to allege vaccine harms.
