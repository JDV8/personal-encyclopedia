# Ring vaccination

Vaccinating the contacts of a case, and contacts of those contacts, to contain spread, rather than the whole population. It was central to smallpox eradication and was used in the Guinea Ebola trial of 2015, which found the rVSV-ZEBOV vaccine highly effective. It requires good surveillance and quick action.
