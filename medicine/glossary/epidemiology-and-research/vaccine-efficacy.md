# Vaccine efficacy

The percentage reduction in disease among vaccinated people compared with unvaccinated people under the controlled conditions of a randomised trial, calculated as one minus the relative risk. A 95% efficacy does not mean 5% of vaccinated people get sick; it means their risk is 95% lower. Efficacy can differ from real-world effectiveness.
