# Passive surveillance

Surveillance that relies on voluntary reports from doctors, patients or manufacturers, such as the US VAERS, Canada's CAEFISS and the UK Yellow Card scheme. It can detect unexpected signals quickly and cheaply, but it suffers from under-reporting and stimulated reporting and lacks a denominator, so it cannot by itself show causation or rates.
