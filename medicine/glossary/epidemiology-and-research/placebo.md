# Placebo

An inactive substance given to a control group to compare with the active treatment. In vaccine trials, placebos may be saline or may contain the adjuvant or another vaccine; critics argue that active comparators can hide side effects, while trialists say saline placebos are sometimes unethical when an effective vaccine exists.
