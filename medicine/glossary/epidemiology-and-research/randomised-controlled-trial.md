# Randomised controlled trial

A study in which participants are randomly assigned to receive an intervention or a control, such as placebo or another vaccine. Randomisation balances known and unknown factors between groups, making it the strongest design for establishing cause. Vaccines are licensed mainly on RCT evidence, but trials are too small for rare harms.
