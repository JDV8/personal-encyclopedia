# Healthy vaccinee effect

The tendency of people who get vaccinated, especially older adults offered influenza vaccines, to be healthier than those who do not, because frail people may skip vaccination. It can make vaccines look more protective in observational studies than they are; the opposite bias can occur when sicker people are prioritised.
