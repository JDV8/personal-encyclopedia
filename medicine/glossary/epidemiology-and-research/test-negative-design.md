# Test-negative design

A study design for vaccine effectiveness in which people tested for a disease are compared: those who test positive are cases and those who test negative are controls. It reduces bias from differences in healthcare-seeking. It is widely used for influenza and COVID-19 vaccine effectiveness, though it assumes the vaccine does not affect the chance of illness from other causes that lead to testing.
