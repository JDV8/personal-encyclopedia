# Surveillance

Ongoing, systematic collection, analysis and reporting of health data to guide action. Disease surveillance detects outbreaks; vaccine safety surveillance detects adverse events. It can be passive, relying on voluntary reports such as VAERS, or active, systematically checking records, as in the Vaccine Safety Datalink.
