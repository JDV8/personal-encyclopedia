# Disproportionality analysis

A statistical method that looks for adverse events reported more often for one vaccine than for others in a passive surveillance database, using measures such as proportional reporting ratios. It identifies signals but cannot measure risk, because report counts are affected by publicity, reporting habits and the number of doses given.
