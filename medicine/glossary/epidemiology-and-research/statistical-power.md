# Statistical power

The probability that a study will detect a true effect of a given size. Small studies have low power, so they can miss real effects. Pre-licensure vaccine trials of a few thousand to tens of thousands of people can detect common side effects but not very rare ones, which is why post-marketing surveillance is needed.
