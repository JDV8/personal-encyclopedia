# Active surveillance

Surveillance that systematically seeks out cases, for example by analysing electronic health records of millions of patients, as in the US Vaccine Safety Datalink, or by contacting hospitals. It has a denominator, allowing comparison of rates in vaccinated and unvaccinated people or periods, and is used to confirm or reject signals.
