# Diagnostic substitution

When a condition is increasingly diagnosed in place of another, so recorded rates shift without a real change in disease. Part of the rise in autism diagnoses reflects children previously labelled with intellectual disability or other conditions now being diagnosed with autism, along with broader criteria since 1994.
