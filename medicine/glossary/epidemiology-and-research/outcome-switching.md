# Outcome switching

Changing a study's primary outcome after the data are in, without disclosure, so that a favourable result can be reported. Comparisons of trial registrations with publications have found it to be common. It is one of the questionable research practices covered in the reading-research course.

**See also:** [pre registration](pre-registration.md), [publication bias](publication-bias.md), [conflict of interest](../safety-law-and-policy/conflict-of-interest.md)
