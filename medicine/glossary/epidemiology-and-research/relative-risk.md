# Relative risk

The ratio of the risk of an outcome in one group to the risk in another; a relative risk of 2 means twice the risk. Relative risks sound dramatic for rare events, so absolute risks, the actual chances, are also needed. A doubling of a one-in-a-million risk is still two in a million.
