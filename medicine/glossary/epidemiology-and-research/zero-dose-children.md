# Zero-dose children

Children who have not received even the first dose of DTP vaccine, used as a marker of those missed entirely by health systems. WHO and UNICEF estimated 14.3 million in 2024, concentrated in conflict zones and poor, remote communities. Reducing them is a central goal of Immunization Agenda 2030 and Gavi.
