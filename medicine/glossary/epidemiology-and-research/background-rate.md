# Background rate

The expected frequency of a condition in a population regardless of vaccination. With millions vaccinated, many illnesses and deaths will happen soon after vaccination by coincidence. Comparing observed cases to the background rate, in observed-to-expected analyses, is a first step in assessing safety signals.
