# Endemic

Describes a disease constantly present in a population at a relatively stable level, such as malaria in parts of Africa. Endemic does not mean mild: endemic diseases can kill many people. Measles elimination means ending endemic transmission for at least 12 months. When an eliminated disease becomes endemic again, re-establishment is defined by 12 months of continuous spread of one chain of transmission.
