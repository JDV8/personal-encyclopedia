# Recall bias

Systematic differences in how accurately people remember past events. Parents of children with autism or other conditions may search their memories more intensely for possible causes such as vaccination, creating spurious associations in case-control studies. Using medical records, recorded before the outcome was known, instead of memories reduces it, which is why record-linkage studies are preferred for vaccine safety.
