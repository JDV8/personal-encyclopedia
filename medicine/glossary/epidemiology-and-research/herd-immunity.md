# Herd immunity

The indirect protection of unvaccinated or non-immune people when enough of the population is immune that a pathogen cannot spread effectively. It protects babies too young to be vaccinated and people who cannot be. It only applies to diseases spread from person to person, not tetanus, and depends on vaccines that block transmission.
