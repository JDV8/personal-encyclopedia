# Safety signal

Information suggesting a possible new causal link between a vaccine and an adverse event, or a new aspect of a known link, that warrants further study. Signals often arise from passive reports and are checked with active surveillance and controlled studies. Most signals are not confirmed, but some, such as myocarditis after mRNA vaccines, are.
