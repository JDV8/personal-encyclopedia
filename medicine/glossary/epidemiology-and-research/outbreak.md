# Outbreak

More cases of a disease than expected in a particular place and time; a large outbreak is an epidemic. For eliminated diseases such as measles, a single case may trigger a public-health response. Outbreak investigations reveal who was infected and their vaccination status, which provides data on vaccine effectiveness.
