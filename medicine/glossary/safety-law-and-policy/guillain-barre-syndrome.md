# Guillain-Barré syndrome

A rare autoimmune disorder in which the immune system attacks peripheral nerves, causing weakness and sometimes paralysis, usually followed by recovery. It is often triggered by infections such as Campylobacter. The 1976 swine flu vaccine increased risk by about one case per 100,000 doses; later influenza vaccines have been associated with about one to two extra cases per million doses.
