# Vaccine hesitancy

Delay in acceptance or refusal of vaccines despite availability, as defined by a WHO expert group in 2014. It exists on a continuum from accepting all vaccines with doubts to refusing all. Causes include complacency, lack of confidence and inconvenience. WHO listed it among ten threats to global health in 2019.
