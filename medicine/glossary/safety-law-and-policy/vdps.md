# Vaccine Damage Payment Scheme (UK)

The UK scheme, created in 1979, that makes a one-off tax-free payment, currently £120,000, to people severely disabled by certain vaccines, defined as at least 60% disability. It is not compensation for loss and does not prevent lawsuits. Critics say the threshold is too high and payments too low.
