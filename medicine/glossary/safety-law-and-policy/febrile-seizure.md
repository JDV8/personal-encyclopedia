# Febrile seizure

A seizure in a young child, usually between six months and five years, triggered by fever. Febrile seizures affect about 2–5% of children, are frightening but generally harmless, and rarely lead to epilepsy. MMR and especially MMRV vaccines slightly increase risk about one to two weeks after vaccination, when the vaccine virus causes fever.
