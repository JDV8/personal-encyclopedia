# Package insert

The official product information supplied with a vaccine, also called the prescribing information or label. It lists ingredients, indications, contraindications and adverse events reported in trials and post-marketing surveillance, whether or not caused by the vaccine, partly for legal reasons. Lists of reported events are often misread as proven side effects.
