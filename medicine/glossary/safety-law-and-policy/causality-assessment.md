# Causality assessment

The structured process of deciding whether an adverse event in an individual was caused by a vaccine. The WHO algorithm asks whether there is another cause, whether there is a known causal link, and whether timing is consistent, sorting cases into consistent, indeterminate, inconsistent or unclassifiable. Population studies are needed to establish general causation.
