# JCVI (Joint Committee on Vaccination and Immunisation)

The independent expert committee advising UK health departments on vaccines, set up in 1963. It considers cost-effectiveness formally, and the government is legally obliged to implement its recommendations for England where they are cost-effective. Its slower adoption of varicella vaccine is an example of its cost-effectiveness approach.
