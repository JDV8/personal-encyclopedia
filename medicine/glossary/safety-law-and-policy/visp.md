# Vaccine Injury Support Program (Canada)

Canada's federal no-fault compensation program for serious and permanent injuries from Health Canada-authorised vaccines given on or after 8 December 2020, launched in 2021. Quebec has had its own program since 1985. Reporting in 2025 criticised delays and the program's administration by a private contractor, and the government moved to change administrators. Claimants must show the injury is probably linked to the vaccine.
