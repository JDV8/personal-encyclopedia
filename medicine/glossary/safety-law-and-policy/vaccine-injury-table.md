# Vaccine Injury Table

A legal list in US regulations of injuries presumed to be caused by specific vaccines if they occur within set time windows, such as anaphylaxis within four hours. Table claims do not need to prove causation; off-table claims must. The table has been revised several times, including removing some injuries as evidence changed.
