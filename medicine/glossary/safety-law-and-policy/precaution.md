# Precaution

A condition in which vaccination might increase the risk of a serious reaction or reduce effectiveness, requiring clinical judgement rather than an absolute ban, such as moderate or severe acute illness or a history of GBS within six weeks of a previous influenza vaccine. Precautions are often temporary, and the vaccine is usually given once the condition has passed.
