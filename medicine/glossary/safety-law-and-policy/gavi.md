# Gavi, the Vaccine Alliance

A public-private partnership founded in 2000 that helps lower-income countries buy vaccines, funded by governments and the Gates Foundation. Gavi says it has helped vaccinate over a billion children. Critics question its influence on national priorities and its links with industry. The US ended its funding in 2025.
