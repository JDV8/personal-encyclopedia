# Misinformation

False or misleading information, whether shared in good faith or not; disinformation is deliberately false. Vaccine misinformation spreads widely online. Research suggests that warning people about manipulation techniques, called prebunking, can reduce susceptibility. Defining misinformation is itself contested when evidence is evolving, as with early COVID-19 debates about transmission and origins, and content moderation has prompted lawsuits over free speech.
