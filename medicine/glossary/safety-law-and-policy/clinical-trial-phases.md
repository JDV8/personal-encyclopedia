# Clinical trial phases

The stages of vaccine testing in humans: phase I tests safety and dose in tens of people; phase II expands to hundreds to refine dose and check responses; phase III tests efficacy and safety in thousands to tens of thousands; phase IV is post-marketing surveillance. Phases can overlap, as in COVID-19 vaccine development.
