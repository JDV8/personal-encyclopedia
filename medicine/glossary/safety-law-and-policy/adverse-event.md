# Adverse event

Any harmful or unwanted medical occurrence after vaccination, whether or not caused by the vaccine. The distinction matters: an adverse event following immunisation is a temporal association, while an adverse reaction implies the vaccine caused it. Surveillance systems collect adverse events and causality assessment decides which are reactions.
