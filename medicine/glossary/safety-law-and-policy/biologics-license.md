# Biologics licence

The US approval, granted by FDA's Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research through a Biologics License Application, required to market a vaccine. It reviews safety, efficacy, manufacturing and labelling. Other regulators include the EMA in Europe, Health Canada and the UK's MHRA; WHO prequalification is used for UN purchasing.
