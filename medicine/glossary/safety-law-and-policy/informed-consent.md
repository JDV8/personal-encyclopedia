# Informed consent

Voluntary agreement to a medical procedure after being told its purpose, benefits, risks and alternatives. It is a core principle of medical ethics and research, established after Nazi experiments and abuses such as the Tuskegee study. Vaccine critics argue mandates undermine it; supporters argue information statements and exemption systems preserve it.
