# Non-medical exemption

Permission to skip required vaccinations for religious, philosophical or personal reasons. US states vary: some, like California since 2016, allow only medical exemptions. Higher exemption rates are linked to outbreaks, and several states tightened rules after the 2014–15 Disneyland measles outbreak, while others loosened them after COVID-19. In 2024–25 US kindergarten exemptions reached a record 3.6%.
