# Vaccine schedule

The recommended timetable of vaccines by age, set nationally by bodies such as ACIP, NACI and JCVI. Schedules balance disease risk at each age, immune responses, interference between vaccines and practicality. They differ between countries, which vaccine critics cite as evidence of arbitrariness and experts explain by differences in disease, health systems and costs.
