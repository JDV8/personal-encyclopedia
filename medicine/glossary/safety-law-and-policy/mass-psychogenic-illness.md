# Mass psychogenic illness

The spread of real physical symptoms, such as fainting, headaches and tremors, through a group without an organic cause, often triggered by anxiety. Several clusters after HPV vaccination in schools, for example in Colombia in 2014, have been attributed to it. The diagnosis is controversial because it can seem dismissive to patients.
