# Special master

A judicial officer of the US Court of Federal Claims who hears vaccine injury claims under the VICP. Special masters decide on causation and compensation using a less formal process than ordinary courts, and their decisions can be appealed. In the Omnibus Autism Proceeding, three special masters rejected the test cases in 2009 and 2010.
