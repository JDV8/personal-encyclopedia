# NACI (National Advisory Committee on Immunization)

Canada's expert committee, established in 1964, that advises the Public Health Agency of Canada on vaccine use. Its recommendations inform provincial and territorial schedules, which differ because health care is provincially run. Unlike ACIP, it does not determine insurance coverage, and provinces decide which vaccines to fund. It publishes statements and the Canadian Immunization Guide chapters.
