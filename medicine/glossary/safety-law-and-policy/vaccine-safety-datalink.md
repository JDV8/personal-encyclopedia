# Vaccine Safety Datalink

A collaboration since 1990 between CDC and several US health-care organisations that uses the electronic health records of millions of people to monitor vaccine safety actively. It allows rapid comparisons of vaccinated and unvaccinated groups or risk windows. Critics have sought wider independent access to its data.
