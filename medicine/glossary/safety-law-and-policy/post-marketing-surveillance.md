# Post-marketing surveillance

Monitoring of a vaccine's safety and effectiveness after it is licensed and in wide use, sometimes called phase IV. Rare adverse events, such as intussusception after RotaShield or VITT after adenovirus vector COVID-19 vaccines, are usually detected only at this stage because they are too rare for pre-licensure trials.
