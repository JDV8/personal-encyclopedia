# Expanded Programme on Immunization

WHO's programme, launched in 1974, to make basic childhood vaccines available to all children, starting with BCG, DTP, polio and measles. Global DTP3 coverage rose from under 5% to around 85%. A 2024 study estimated that vaccination programmes saved about 154 million lives over its first 50 years.
