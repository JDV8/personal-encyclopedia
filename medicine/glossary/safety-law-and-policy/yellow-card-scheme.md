# Yellow Card scheme

The UK's system, run by the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency since 1964, for reporting suspected side effects of medicines and vaccines. Created after the thalidomide disaster, it is a passive system like VAERS. Canada's equivalent for vaccines is CAEFISS, and the European Union uses EudraVigilance.
