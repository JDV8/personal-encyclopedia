# SIRVA (shoulder injury related to vaccine administration)

Shoulder pain and restricted movement caused by injecting a vaccine too high into the shoulder joint or bursa rather than the deltoid muscle. It is a recognised injury on the US Vaccine Injury Table since 2017 and accounts for a large share of compensated claims, mostly after influenza vaccines in adults.
