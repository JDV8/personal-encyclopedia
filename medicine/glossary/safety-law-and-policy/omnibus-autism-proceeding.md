# Omnibus Autism Proceeding

A process in the US Court of Federal Claims that consolidated about 5,000 claims that vaccines caused autism. Test cases on MMR plus thimerosal and on thimerosal alone were rejected in 2009 and 2010, and appeals failed. A separate case, Hannah Poling, was conceded by the government in 2007, and became public in 2008, on the basis that vaccines aggravated an underlying mitochondrial disorder.
