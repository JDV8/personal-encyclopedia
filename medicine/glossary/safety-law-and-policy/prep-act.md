# PREP Act

The US Public Readiness and Emergency Preparedness Act of 2005, which lets the health secretary grant manufacturers and providers broad immunity from liability for countermeasures such as COVID-19 vaccines during a declared emergency, except for wilful misconduct. Injured people can apply to the Countermeasures Injury Compensation Program instead, which pays far fewer claims.
