# Human challenge study

A trial in which volunteers are deliberately infected with a pathogen under controlled conditions, often after receiving a vaccine, to test protection quickly. They are used for malaria, influenza, cholera and typhoid and were run for COVID-19 in the UK in 2021. They raise ethical questions about consent and risk.
