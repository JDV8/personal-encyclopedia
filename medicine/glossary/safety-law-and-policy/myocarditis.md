# Myocarditis

Inflammation of the heart muscle, often caused by viral infections including COVID-19. mRNA COVID-19 vaccines cause myocarditis rarely, mainly in adolescent and young adult males after the second dose. Most vaccine cases are mild and resolve, though long-term outcomes are still studied. COVID-19 infection itself also causes myocarditis.
