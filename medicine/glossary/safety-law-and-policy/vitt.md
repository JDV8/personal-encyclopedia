# VITT (vaccine-induced immune thrombotic thrombocytopenia)

A rare, sometimes fatal syndrome of blood clots, often in the brain or abdomen, with low platelet counts, caused by antibodies against platelet factor 4 after adenovirus vector COVID-19 vaccines (AstraZeneca and Johnson & Johnson). Recognised in March 2021, it led many countries to restrict these vaccines to older age groups.
