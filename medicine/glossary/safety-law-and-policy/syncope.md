# Syncope

Fainting, a brief loss of consciousness from a fall in blood flow to the brain, often triggered by pain, anxiety or the sight of needles. It is common after injections in adolescents and can cause injuries from falling, which is why people are advised to sit during and after vaccination. Clusters of fainting are sometimes misread as vaccine toxicity.
