# Serious adverse event

A regulatory term for an adverse event that results in death, is life-threatening, requires hospitalisation or prolongs it, causes persistent disability or a birth defect, or is otherwise medically important. Serious does not mean caused by the vaccine; serious events are tracked in trials and surveillance for both vaccine and control groups.
