# Intussusception

A condition in which one part of the bowel slides into another, causing blockage, which is most common in infants. The first rotavirus vaccine, RotaShield, caused about one excess case per 10,000 infants and was withdrawn in 1999. Current rotavirus vaccines carry a smaller risk, estimated at one to six per 100,000, outweighed by their benefits.
