# Countermeasures Injury Compensation Program

The US program compensating injuries from countermeasures covered by the PREP Act, including COVID-19 vaccines. It has a one-year filing deadline, no judicial hearing and limited benefits, and by 2025 had compensated only a tiny share of over 13,000 COVID-19 claims. Both vaccine critics and many legal scholars have called it inadequate.
