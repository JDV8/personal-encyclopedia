# Vaccine Injury Compensation Program

The US no-fault scheme created by the National Childhood Vaccine Injury Act of 1986 to compensate people injured by covered vaccines, funded by a 75-cent excise tax per disease prevented in each dose. Claims are heard by special masters at the Court of Federal Claims; claimants must usually go through it before suing manufacturers.
