# ACIP (Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices)

The committee that advises CDC on which vaccines to recommend and for whom, created in 1964. Its recommendations drive US schedules and insurance coverage. In June 2025 Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. removed all 17 members and appointed new ones; a federal court blocked parts of its work in March 2026.
