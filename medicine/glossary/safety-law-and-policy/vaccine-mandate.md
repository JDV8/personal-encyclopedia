# Vaccine mandate

A legal requirement to be vaccinated, usually as a condition of school attendance, employment or travel rather than an absolute compulsion. Mandates raise coverage but can harden opposition. In the US, states set school mandates, upheld in principle in Jacobson v. Massachusetts (1905) and Zucht v. King (1922).
