# WHO prequalification

WHO's assessment of vaccines' quality, safety and efficacy for countries that rely on UN agencies such as UNICEF and Gavi to buy them. Prequalification often relies on approval by a stringent national regulator. It made possible vaccines such as MenAfriVac, made in India for African use, and typhoid conjugate vaccines.
