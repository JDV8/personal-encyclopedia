# Jacobson v. Massachusetts

A 1905 US Supreme Court case upholding Cambridge, Massachusetts' power to fine people who refused smallpox vaccination during an outbreak. The Court held that individual liberty can be limited for public health, within limits. It has been cited in many later cases, including COVID-19 mandate challenges, where its scope was debated.
