# Emergency Use Authorization

A US FDA mechanism allowing unapproved medical products, or unapproved uses, during a declared emergency when benefits outweigh known risks and there are no adequate alternatives. COVID-19 vaccines were first used under EUAs in December 2020. Full approval, through a biologics licence, followed for some vaccines in 2021 and later.
