# Contraindication

A condition that makes vaccination inadvisable because the risk of harm outweighs the benefit, such as severe allergy to a previous dose or a vaccine component, or severe immunodeficiency for live vaccines. True contraindications are few. Precautions are conditions requiring caution, such as moderate illness, where vaccination may be delayed.
