# Conflict of interest

A situation in which a researcher's or official's financial or personal interests could influence their judgement, such as industry funding, patents, consultancy or litigation work. Conflicts do not make findings wrong, but they warrant scrutiny and disclosure. They exist on both sides of vaccine debates, from manufacturer funding to paid expert witnesses.
