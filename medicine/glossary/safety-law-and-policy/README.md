# Safety, law and policy

Terms for vaccine safety monitoring, specific adverse events, compensation programmes, mandates, advisory committees and global programmes. Each entry is a short definition of at least 50 words with links to related entries and to the lessons where the term is explained in depth.

**Prerequisites:** the [glossary overview](../README.md).

## Suggested reading order

Start with adverse event, VAERS, causality assessment and contraindication, then VICP, PREP Act and vaccine mandate.

## Key studies & sources

Every source below was checked to exist (DOI, court record or official page). Notes summarise what each found and its main strengths or limitations.

### Reference works used for definitions

- [CDC, *Epidemiology and Prevention of Vaccine-Preventable Diseases* (the "Pink Book")](https://www.cdc.gov/pinkbook/hcp/table-of-contents/index.html): Free US reference; government source.
- [Public Health Agency of Canada, *Canadian Immunization Guide*](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/canadian-immunization-guide.html): Canada's national clinical guide.
- [UK Health Security Agency, *Immunisation against infectious disease* (the "Green Book")](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/immunisation-against-infectious-disease-the-green-book): The UK's official vaccine reference.

## References & further reading

- **Beginner.** Philipp Dettmer, *Immune* (2021). Readable introduction to immune terms.
- **Intermediate.** Kenneth J. Rothman, *Epidemiology: An Introduction* (2nd ed., 2012). Defines the epidemiological terms clearly.
- **Advanced.** Stanley A. Plotkin, Walter A. Orenstein, Paul A. Offit and Kathryn M. Edwards (eds), *Plotkin's Vaccines* (8th ed., 2023). The standard reference; note that several editors developed vaccines.
