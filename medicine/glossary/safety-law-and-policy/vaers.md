# VAERS (Vaccine Adverse Event Reporting System)

The US passive surveillance system, run jointly by CDC and FDA since 1990, where anyone can report a health problem after vaccination. It is designed to detect signals quickly, not to prove causation. Reports are unverified, under- and over-reporting both occur, and raw counts are frequently misused to claim vaccine harms.
