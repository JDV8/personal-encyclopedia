# Virus-like particle

A particle made of viral structural proteins that self-assemble into a shell resembling the virus but containing no genetic material, so it cannot replicate. Because it looks like a virus to the immune system, it is highly immunogenic. HPV and hepatitis B vaccines use virus-like particles, which helps explain their very high and durable antibody responses.
