# Lot release

The testing of each batch, or lot, of vaccine by the manufacturer and often independently by a national regulator before it can be distributed. Tests cover potency, sterility, purity and safety. The 1955 Cutter incident, in which live poliovirus remained in an inactivated vaccine, led to much stricter batch testing in the United States.
