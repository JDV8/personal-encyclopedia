# Aluminium adjuvant

Aluminium hydroxide, aluminium phosphate or related salts used as adjuvants in many vaccines, including DTaP, hepatitis B, HPV and pneumococcal conjugate vaccines. Infants receive a few milligrams over the first six months, more than they absorb from diet, though modelling puts the body burden below regulatory safety thresholds. Critics argue that injected aluminium behaves differently from dietary aluminium and needs better study; a large 2025 Danish study found no associations with chronic disease.
