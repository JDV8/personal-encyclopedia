# PEG (polyethylene glycol)

A synthetic polymer widely used in medicines, cosmetics and laxatives, and a component of the lipid nanoparticles in mRNA COVID-19 vaccines. PEG allergy is rare but was considered a possible cause of the small number of anaphylaxis cases after these vaccines, and known PEG allergy was listed as a contraindication.
