# Toxoid

A bacterial toxin chemically inactivated, usually with formaldehyde, so it is harmless but still provokes antibodies that neutralise the real toxin. Diphtheria and tetanus vaccines are toxoids. They protect against the disease, which is caused by the toxin, but not necessarily against carriage of the bacterium, and need boosters to maintain protection.
