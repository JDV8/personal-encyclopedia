# Adjuvant

An ingredient added to a vaccine to strengthen and shape the immune response, usually by activating innate immunity at the injection site. Aluminium salts have been used since the 1920s; newer adjuvants include oil-in-water emulsions such as MF59 and AS03, and combinations such as AS01. Adjuvants allow less antigen per dose but can increase local reactions.
