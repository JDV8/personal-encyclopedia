# Lipid nanoparticle

A tiny fat droplet, roughly 100 nanometres across, that encloses fragile mRNA, protects it from enzymes and helps it enter cells. Lipid nanoparticles contain ionisable lipids, cholesterol, helper lipids and PEG-lipids. They also act as an adjuvant. Allergy to PEG has been suggested as a cause of rare anaphylaxis after mRNA COVID-19 vaccines.
