# Reactogenicity

The tendency of a vaccine to cause short-lived, expected reactions, such as pain, redness and swelling at the injection site, fever, fatigue and headache. Reactogenicity reflects innate immune activation and is measured in trials using diaries. It is distinct from serious adverse events, which are rare. Adjuvanted and mRNA vaccines are relatively reactogenic.
