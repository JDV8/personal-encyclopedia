# Breakthrough infection

An infection in a fully vaccinated person. Breakthroughs are expected because no vaccine is 100% effective. When most of a population is vaccinated, most cases may be in vaccinated people, even though their individual risk is lower; this base-rate effect is often misread as evidence that vaccines do not work.
