# Polysaccharide capsule

A slippery coat of sugars around some bacteria, including pneumococcus, Hib, meningococcus and typhoid bacteria, that helps them evade phagocytes. Antibodies against the capsule protect, and it defines serotypes. Plain polysaccharide vaccines work poorly in children under two and do not create memory, which conjugate vaccines overcome.
