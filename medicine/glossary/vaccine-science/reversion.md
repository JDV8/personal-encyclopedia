# Reversion

The process by which a live attenuated vaccine strain regains virulence through mutation as it replicates, in the vaccinated person or as it spreads. The best-known case is oral polio vaccine, which can revert and, in under-immunised communities, circulate to cause vaccine-derived polio outbreaks. Newer strains such as novel OPV2 were engineered to be more genetically stable.
