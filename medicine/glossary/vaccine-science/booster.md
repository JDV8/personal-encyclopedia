# Booster dose

An additional dose given after the primary series to restore or increase immunity that has waned or to lengthen protection. Tetanus and diphtheria boosters are recommended every 10 years in many countries; COVID-19 boosters were offered as immunity and variants changed. Boosters exploit immune memory to produce a fast, strong response.
