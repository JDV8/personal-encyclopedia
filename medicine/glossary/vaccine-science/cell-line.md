# Cell line

A population of cells that can be grown in culture for many generations, used to grow viruses for vaccines. Examples include Vero monkey kidney cells and the human diploid lines WI-38 and MRC-5, derived from two elective abortions in the 1960s and used for rubella, varicella and hepatitis A vaccines. Their origin raises ethical questions for some religious groups.
