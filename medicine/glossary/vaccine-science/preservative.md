# Preservative

A substance added to multi-dose vaccine vials to prevent contamination by bacteria and fungi once the vial is opened. Thimerosal was the commonest; 2-phenoxyethanol is another. Preservatives became standard after fatal contamination incidents, such as the 1928 Bundaberg disaster in Australia, where 12 children died after receiving contaminated diphtheria toxin-antitoxin.
