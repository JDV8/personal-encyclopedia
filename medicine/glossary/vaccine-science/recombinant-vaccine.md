# Recombinant vaccine

A vaccine whose antigen is produced by genetic engineering: the gene for the antigen is inserted into yeast, bacteria, insect or mammalian cells, which then manufacture the protein. The first was the hepatitis B vaccine licensed in 1986, made in yeast. Recombinant technology avoids handling dangerous pathogens or human plasma in production.
