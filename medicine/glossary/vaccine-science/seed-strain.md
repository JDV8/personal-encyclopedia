# Seed strain

A characterised stock of virus or bacterium used as the starting material for every batch of a vaccine, to ensure consistency. For influenza, WHO recommends new strains twice a year and candidate vaccine viruses are distributed to manufacturers. Seed lots are tested for identity, purity and absence of contaminating agents.
