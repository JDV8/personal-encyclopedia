# Primary vaccine failure

When a vaccinated person fails to develop protective immunity in the first place, for example because of maternal antibodies, a broken cold chain or individual biology. About 7% of children do not respond to one dose of measles vaccine, which is why a second dose is given. It differs from secondary failure, where immunity wanes.
