# Cold chain

The system of refrigerators, freezers, cool boxes and monitoring that keeps vaccines at the correct temperature from factory to patient, usually 2–8 °C. Breaks in the cold chain can destroy potency without visible change, leading to vaccine failure. Vaccine vial monitors, heat-sensitive labels that change colour, help health workers detect exposure.
