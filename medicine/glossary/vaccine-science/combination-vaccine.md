# Combination vaccine

A single product protecting against several diseases, such as MMR or the six-in-one hexavalent vaccines for diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis, polio, Hib and hepatitis B. Combinations reduce injections and improve coverage. They must be shown not to reduce responses to individual components or increase side effects, which sometimes happens, as with MMRV and febrile seizures.
