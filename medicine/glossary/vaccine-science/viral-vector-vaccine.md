# Viral vector vaccine

A vaccine that uses a harmless or crippled virus, often an adenovirus, to carry a gene for the target antigen into cells, which then make the antigen. Examples include the Oxford-AstraZeneca and Johnson & Johnson COVID-19 vaccines and the Ervebo Ebola vaccine. Pre-existing immunity to the vector can reduce effectiveness; adenovirus vectors were linked to rare VITT.
