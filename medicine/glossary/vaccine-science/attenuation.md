# Attenuation

Weakening a pathogen so it can no longer cause serious disease while remaining able to stimulate immunity. Classic methods include repeated passage through animals or cell cultures at low temperatures, as with Sabin's polio strains and Hilleman's Jeryl Lynn mumps strain, or chemical treatment, as in some of Pasteur's work. Modern methods delete or alter specific genes.
