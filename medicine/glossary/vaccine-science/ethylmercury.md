# Ethylmercury

The form of mercury released when thimerosal breaks down in the body. It is cleared from blood much faster than methylmercury, the form found in fish and responsible for the Minamata poisoning, with a half-life of about a week rather than about seven weeks. Early safety guidance was based on methylmercury because data on ethylmercury were scarce.
