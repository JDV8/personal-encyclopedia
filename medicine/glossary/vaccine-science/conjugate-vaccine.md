# Conjugate vaccine

A vaccine in which a bacterial capsule sugar is chemically linked to a carrier protein, turning a poorly immunogenic, T-independent antigen into one that recruits T-cell help. This makes it work in infants and induces memory and herd effects. Hib, pneumococcal and meningococcal conjugate vaccines transformed child health from the late 1980s onwards.
