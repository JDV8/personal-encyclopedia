# Good manufacturing practice

Regulations and guidelines, enforced by bodies such as the FDA and EMA, that set standards for the facilities, processes, documentation and quality control used to make medicines and vaccines. GMP aims to ensure that every batch is consistent and safe. Failures in GMP have led to product recalls and supply shortages.
