# Vaccine

A biological product that trains the immune system to recognise a pathogen or toxin without causing the disease, by presenting antigens in a weakened, killed, purified or genetically encoded form. The word comes from the Latin *vacca*, cow, after Jenner's use of cowpox against smallpox; Pasteur extended it to all such preparations in honour of Jenner.
