# Excipient

Any ingredient in a vaccine other than the antigen: adjuvants, preservatives, stabilisers such as sugars and gelatin, buffers, and residues of manufacturing such as traces of egg protein, antibiotics or formaldehyde. Excipients are listed in the product's package insert. Allergy to certain excipients, such as gelatin, is one of the few real contraindications.
