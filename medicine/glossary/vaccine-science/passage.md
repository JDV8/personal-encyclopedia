# Passage

Growing a virus or bacterium repeatedly in a series of cultures or animals, each time transferring it to fresh hosts. Serial passage in unusual conditions selects variants adapted to them, which are often weakened in humans. BCG was produced by 230 passages of a cattle tuberculosis bacterium over 13 years. Passage is also used to grow vaccine seed stocks.
