# Subunit vaccine

A vaccine containing only selected parts of a pathogen, such as purified proteins or sugars, rather than the whole organism. Acellular pertussis, hepatitis B, HPV and shingles vaccines are subunit vaccines. They cannot cause infection and tend to have fewer side effects, but usually need adjuvants and repeated doses, and some, like acellular pertussis, give shorter-lasting protection.
