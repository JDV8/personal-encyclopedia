# Immunisation

The process of making someone immune, whether actively by vaccination or passively by giving antibodies. In everyday use immunisation and vaccination are interchangeable, but strictly vaccination refers to giving a vaccine, while immunisation refers to the resulting immunity, which not every vaccinated person achieves. Public-health agencies often prefer immunisation in programme names.
