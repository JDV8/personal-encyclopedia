# Waning immunity

The decline in protection over time after infection or vaccination, sometimes called secondary vaccine failure. It is marked for acellular pertussis, mumps and protection against COVID-19 infection, but much slower for measles. Waning protection against infection often precedes waning protection against severe disease, which T cells and memory help maintain.
