# Vaccine vial monitor

A small heat-sensitive label on a vaccine vial whose inner square darkens with cumulative heat exposure. If the inner square matches or is darker than the outer circle, the vaccine should not be used. Introduced for oral polio vaccine in 1996, vial monitors allow some vaccines to be used outside the cold chain under controlled conditions.
