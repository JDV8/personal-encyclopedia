# Immunobridging

Inferring that a vaccine will protect by showing it produces immune responses similar to a vaccine already proven effective, rather than by running a new efficacy trial. It is used for new formulations, age groups and updated COVID-19 and influenza strains. Critics argue it can be overused when correlates of protection are uncertain.
