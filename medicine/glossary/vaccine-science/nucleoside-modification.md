# Nucleoside modification

Replacing the normal RNA building block uridine with a chemically modified version such as pseudouridine or N1-methylpseudouridine. Karikó and Weissman showed in 2005 that this stops synthetic mRNA from triggering strong innate immune alarms and increases protein production, which made mRNA vaccines practical and won them the 2023 Nobel Prize.
