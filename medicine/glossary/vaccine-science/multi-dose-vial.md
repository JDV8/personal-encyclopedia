# Multi-dose vial

A vial containing several doses of vaccine, which lowers cost and storage space and is important for global programmes. Because the vial is punctured repeatedly, it needs a preservative or must be discarded within hours. The WHO open-vial policy sets how long opened vials may be used. Single-dose vials and prefilled syringes avoid preservatives but cost more.
