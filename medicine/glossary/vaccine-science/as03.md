# AS03

An oil-in-water emulsion adjuvant made by GlaxoSmithKline containing squalene and vitamin E (alpha-tocopherol), used in pandemic influenza vaccines including Pandemrix in 2009–10. Pandemrix was later linked to narcolepsy, especially in Scandinavia; whether the adjuvant, the antigen preparation or both were responsible is still debated, since a similar AS03 vaccine in Canada showed a much smaller signal.
