# Squalene

A natural oil made by plants and animals, including in the human liver, and traditionally obtained from shark liver. It is a component of the adjuvants MF59 and AS03. Claims that squalene in vaccines caused Gulf War syndrome were investigated and not supported. Concerns about shark harvesting have prompted research into plant or yeast sources.
