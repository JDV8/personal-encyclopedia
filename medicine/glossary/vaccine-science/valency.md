# Valency

The number of strains or types a vaccine covers: a monovalent vaccine targets one, a quadrivalent four, and so on. The 9-valent HPV vaccine covers nine HPV types; pneumococcal conjugate vaccines have grown from 7 to 20 or more serotypes. Higher valency broadens protection but makes manufacturing more complex.
