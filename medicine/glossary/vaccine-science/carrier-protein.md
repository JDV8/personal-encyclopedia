# Carrier protein

The protein to which a polysaccharide or hapten is attached in a conjugate vaccine, so that helper T cells can recognise it and help the B cells that bind the sugar. Common carriers include tetanus toxoid, a non-toxic diphtheria toxin mutant called CRM197, and meningococcal outer membrane protein. Interference between carriers is considered when designing schedules.
