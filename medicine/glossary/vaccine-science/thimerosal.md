# Thimerosal (thiomersal)

A mercury-containing preservative, about half ethylmercury by weight, used in multi-dose vaccine vials since the 1930s. In 1999 US agencies asked for its removal from childhood vaccines as a precaution, which fuelled fears of a link to autism. Studies have not found such a link. It remains in some multi-dose influenza vaccines worldwide.
