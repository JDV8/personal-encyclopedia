# mRNA vaccine

A vaccine that delivers messenger RNA, wrapped in lipid nanoparticles, instructing cells to make an antigen such as the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein. The mRNA does not enter the nucleus or alter DNA and is broken down within days. Pfizer-BioNTech and Moderna COVID-19 vaccines were the first licensed. They are linked to rare myocarditis, mainly in young males.
