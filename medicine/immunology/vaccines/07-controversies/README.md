# Controversies

The disputed questions, presented with the strongest evidence and arguments on each side. The section covers the autism claims (MMR, thimerosal, the epidemiology of diagnosis, the CDC whistleblower story and the 2025 US government claims), aluminium adjuvants, "too many, too soon", whole-cell pertussis vaccine, HPV vaccine safety scares, COVID-19 vaccines and mandates, natural versus vaccine immunity, the historical mortality argument and SIDS. It then turns to conflicts of interest on all sides, exemptions, and episodes where trust was damaged by real events: Dengvaxia, the Trovan trial and Nigerian polio boycott, and the CIA's fake vaccination campaign. It ends with trust and misinformation. Throughout, funding and conflicts of interest are noted for key studies and for critics, and the reading-research course is linked for the methods behind each argument.

**Prerequisites:** [Adverse events](../06-adverse-events/), especially causality assessment, and the reading-research course on [study designs](../../../../science/research-methods/reading-research/03-study-designs/), [causation](../../../../science/research-methods/reading-research/04-causation/) and [funding and conflicts](../../../../science/research-methods/reading-research/08-funding-and-conflicts/).

## Key studies & sources

Every source below was checked to exist (DOI, court record or official page). Notes summarise what each found and its main strengths or limitations.

### Peer-reviewed studies

- [Wakefield et al., *Lancet* 1998 (retracted)](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(97)11096-0): The retracted 1998 *Lancet* paper. Author paid by litigation lawyers via Legal Aid, undisclosed.
- [Madsen et al., *NEJM* 2002](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa021134): Danish cohort of 537,303 children: no MMR–autism association. Danish government funding; some authors at the State Serum Institute.
- [Hviid et al., *Annals of Internal Medicine* 2019](https://doi.org/10.7326/M18-2101): Danish cohort of 657,461: MMR hazard ratio 0.93. Funded by the Novo Nordisk Foundation and Danish Ministry of Health.
- [Taylor, Swerdfeger & Eslick, *Vaccine* 2014 (meta-analysis)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2014.04.085): Meta-analysis of 1.2 million children: no link between vaccines, thimerosal or mercury and autism.
- [Jain et al., *JAMA* 2015](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2015.3077): US insurance claims, siblings of autistic children: no MMR–autism link. Funded by HHS; authors at a consultancy.
- [Price et al., *Pediatrics* 2010](https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2010-0309): Thimerosal exposure and autism, VSD case-control. CDC-funded VSD study.
- [DeStefano, Price & Weintraub, *J Pediatrics* 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpeds.2013.02.001): Cumulative antigen exposure not associated with autism.
- [Andersson & Hviid, *Annals of Internal Medicine* 2025 (aluminium adjuvants, Denmark)](https://doi.org/10.7326/ANNALS-25-00997): Danish cohort of 1.2 million: aluminium not associated with 50 chronic disorders. The HHS secretary sought retraction; the journal declined.
- [Daley et al., *Academic Pediatrics* 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.acap.2022.08.006): VSD: cumulative aluminium modestly associated with persistent asthma (aHR 1.26/1.19). CDC-funded, and reported a possible harm.
- [Mold et al., *Journal of Trace Elements in Medicine and Biology* 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtemb.2017.11.012): Aluminium in brain tissue of five autistic donors; no controls. Part-funded by an organisation that challenges vaccine safety.
- [Mawson et al., *Journal of Translational Science* 2017](https://doi.org/10.15761/JTS.1000186): Survey of homeschooling mothers reporting more illness in vaccinated children; self-selected sample, parental report.
- [Schmitz et al., *Deutsches Ärzteblatt International* 2011 (KiGGS)](https://doi.org/10.3238/arztebl.2011.0099): German national survey: unvaccinated children had no fewer allergies.
- [Kulenkampff, Schwartzman & Wilson, *Archives of Disease in Childhood* 1974](https://doi.org/10.1136/adc.49.1.46): 36 children with neurological illness after DTP; started the UK pertussis scare.
- [Gangarosa et al., *Lancet* 1998](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(97)04334-1): Countries where anti-vaccine movements disrupted pertussis vaccination had 10–100 times higher incidence.
- [Berkovic et al., *Lancet Neurology* 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(06)70446-X): Many 'vaccine encephalopathy' cases were Dravet syndrome.
- [Simms et al., *Lancet Public Health* 2020 (HPV vaccine hesitancy in Japan)](https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-2667(20)30010-4): Modelling: Japan's HPV crisis may cause 5,000–5,700 extra cervical-cancer deaths.
- [Arnheim-Dahlström et al., *BMJ* 2013](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.f5906): Nearly one million Nordic girls: no consistent link between HPV vaccine and 53 conditions.
- [Brinth et al., *Vaccine* 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2015.03.098): Danish clinic case series of POTS after HPV vaccination; uncontrolled.
- [Lei et al., *NEJM* 2020](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1917338): Sweden: 88% lower invasive cervical cancer in girls vaccinated before 17.
- [Fraiman et al., *Vaccine* 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2022.08.036): Reanalysis of mRNA trials: 12.5 excess serious adverse events of special interest per 10,000. Authors declared no competing interests.
- [Watson et al., *Lancet Infectious Diseases* 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1473-3099(22)00320-6): Modelling: 14.4–19.8 million deaths averted in the first year of COVID-19 vaccination. Some authors had consultancy fees from WHO and industry.
- [Gazit et al., *Clinical Infectious Diseases* 2022](https://doi.org/10.1093/cid/ciac262): Israel: prior infection protected more than two vaccine doses against Delta.
- [Mina et al., *Science* 2019](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aay6485): Measles infection erases 11–73% of existing antibodies.
- [van Panhuis et al., *NEJM* 2013 (Project Tycho)](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMms1215400): Project Tycho: vaccines prevented about 100 million US cases since 1924.
- [Vennemann et al., *Vaccine* 2007 (meta-analysis)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2007.02.077): Meta-analysis: vaccination associated with halved SIDS risk; healthy vaccinee bias possible.
- [Sridhar et al., *NEJM* 2018](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1800820): Dengvaxia increases severe dengue in seronegative recipients. Sanofi-funded, and reported the harm.
- [Larson et al., *Human Vaccines & Immunotherapeutics* 2018 (Philippines confidence)](https://doi.org/10.1080/21645515.2018.1522468): Philippine vaccine confidence fell from 93% to 32% after Dengvaxia.
- [Martinez-Bravo & Stegmann, *Journal of the European Economic Association* 2022](https://doi.org/10.1093/jeea/jvab018): CIA vaccine ruse: vaccination fell 23–39% in districts with most Islamist support.
- [Loomba et al., *Nature Human Behaviour* 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-021-01056-1): Randomised experiment: misinformation cut definite intent to vaccinate by about 6 points.
- [Lundh et al., Cochrane methodology review, 2017](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.MR000033.pub3): Cochrane: industry-sponsored studies report more favourable results.
- [Jefferson et al., *BMJ* 2009](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.b354): Influenza vaccine studies: industry funding linked to prestigious publication, not quality. Lead author disclosed past GSK shares.

### Systematic and expert reviews

- [Institute of Medicine, *Immunization Safety Review: Vaccines and Autism* (2004)](https://doi.org/10.17226/10997): IOM rejected causal links between MMR or thimerosal and autism.
- [Institute of Medicine, *The Childhood Immunization Schedule and Safety* (2013)](https://doi.org/10.17226/13563): IOM review of the whole childhood schedule: no evidence of harm, but few studies of the schedule as a whole.
- [Institute of Medicine, *Adverse Effects of Pertussis and Rubella Vaccines* (1991)](https://doi.org/10.17226/1815): IOM on pertussis and rubella vaccines.
- [Institute of Medicine, *Vaccinations and Sudden Unexpected Death in Infancy* (2003)](https://doi.org/10.17226/10649): IOM: evidence favours rejection of a causal link between vaccines and SIDS.
- [Arbyn et al., Cochrane review of HPV vaccines, 2018](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD009069.pub3): Cochrane review of HPV vaccine trials (2018).
- [Jørgensen, Gøtzsche & Jefferson, *BMJ Evidence-Based Medicine* 2018](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjebm-2018-111012): Nordic Cochrane Centre critique of the Cochrane HPV review.
- [Munblit et al., *Clinical Infectious Diseases* 2026 (critique of the Henry Ford analysis)](https://doi.org/10.1093/cid/ciag403): Review of the unpublished Henry Ford vaccinated–unvaccinated analysis.

### Official reports and court rulings

- [*Lancet* retraction notice, 2010](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(10)60175-4): *Lancet* retraction of the Wakefield paper, 2010.
- [European Medicines Agency, "Review concludes evidence does not support that HPV vaccines cause CRPS or POTS" (November 2015)](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/review-concludes-evidence-does-not-support-hpv-vaccines-cause-crps-or-pots): EMA: evidence does not support HPV vaccines causing CRPS or POTS.
- [HHS, "President Trump, Secretary Kennedy announce bold actions to tackle autism epidemic" (22 September 2025)](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/hhs-trump-kennedy-autism-initiatives-leucovorin-tylenol-research-2025.html): HHS announcement on autism causes, September 2025.
- [FDA, Vinay Prasad email to FDA staff (28 November 2025)](https://www.fda.gov/media/191442/download): FDA official's memo claiming child deaths from COVID-19 vaccines.
- [*Abdullahi v. Pfizer, Inc.*, 562 F.3d 163 (2d Cir. 2009)](https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/2531/abdullahi-v-pfizer-inc/): US appeals court on Pfizer's Trovan trial and non-consensual experimentation.
- [Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health, "White House responds to public health deans" (May 2014)](https://publichealth.jhu.edu/2014/white-house-responds-to-public-health-deans-the-central-intelligence-agency-makes-no-use-of-operational-vaccination-programs): White House pledge that the CIA will not use vaccination programmes.
- [*Murthy v. Missouri*, 603 U.S. 43 (2024)](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/23pdf/23-411_3dq3.pdf): Supreme Court on government contacts with social-media platforms.
- [UNICEF press release on *The State of the World's Children 2023* (April 2023)](https://www.unicef.org/press-releases/sowc_2023_immunization): Childhood vaccine confidence fell in 52 of 55 countries during the pandemic.

### Notable critical sources

- [Tomljenovic & Shaw, *Journal of Inorganic Biochemistry* 2011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jinorgbio.2011.08.008): Argues aluminium adjuvants are a neglected risk; authors' work funded by vaccine-critical foundations.
- [Shoenfeld & Agmon-Levin, *Journal of Autoimmunity* 2011 (ASIA)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jaut.2010.07.003): Proposes autoimmune syndrome induced by adjuvants (ASIA).
- [Hooker, *Translational Neurodegeneration* 2014 (retracted)](https://doi.org/10.1186/2047-9158-3-16): Retracted reanalysis of CDC MMR data.
- [William W. Thompson, statement regarding the 2004 MMR–autism article (27 August 2014; archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20150221101545/http:/www.morganverkamp.com/august-27-2014-press-release-statement-of-william-w-thompson-ph-d-regarding-the-2004-article-examining-the-possibility-of-a-relationship-between-mmr-vaccine-and-autism): William Thompson's statement through his lawyer.
- [Miller & Goldman, *Human & Experimental Toxicology* 2011](https://doi.org/10.1177/0960327111407644): Ecological study linking vaccine doses to infant mortality.
- [Center for Countering Digital Hate, *The Disinformation Dozen* (2021)](https://www.counterharm.com/research/the-disinformation-dozen): Report on the 'Disinformation Dozen'; methods disputed by Facebook.

## References & further reading

- **Beginner.** Seth Mnookin, *The Panic Virus: A True Story of Medicine, Science, and Fear* (2011). Narrative history of the autism controversies.
- **Beginner.** Heidi J. Larson, *Stuck: How Vaccine Rumors Start and Why They Don't Go Away* (2020). Trust and rumour from the Vaccine Confidence Project's founder.

- **Intermediate.** Brian Deer, *The Doctor Who Fooled the World* (2020). The full Wakefield investigation.
- **Intermediate.** Paul A. Offit, *Deadly Choices: How the Anti-Vaccine Movement Threatens Us All* (2011). A defender's account; Offit co-invented RotaTeq.
- **Intermediate.** Elena Conis, *Vaccine Nation: America's Changing Relationship with Immunization* (2015). Historian's account of how US vaccine politics developed.
- **Intermediate.** Jonathan M. Berman, *Anti-vaxxers: How to Challenge a Misunderstood Movement* (2020). History and psychology of vaccine opposition.
- **Intermediate.** Andrew J. Wakefield, *Callous Disregard: Autism and Vaccines – The Truth Behind a Tragedy* (2010). Wakefield's own defence; read alongside Deer.
- **Intermediate.** Suzanne Humphries and Roman Bystrianyk, *Dissolving Illusions: Disease, Vaccines, and the Forgotten History* (2013). The historical-mortality argument; see lesson 12 for its limits.
- **Intermediate.** Peter C. Gøtzsche, *Vaccines: Truth, Lies, and Controversy* (2020). A critical evidence-based-medicine view from the former Nordic Cochrane Centre director, broadly supportive of some vaccines and critical of others.
- **Advanced.** Mark Navin, *Values and Vaccine Refusal: Hard Questions in Ethics, Epistemology, and Health Care* (2015). Philosophical treatment of refusal and exemptions.
- **Advanced.** Institute of Medicine, *The Childhood Immunization Schedule and Safety* (2013). Free; the key review on cumulative schedule safety.
