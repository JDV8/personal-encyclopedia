# Public health and ethics

Vaccination as a population-level enterprise. The section explains the difference between control, elimination and eradication, and why smallpox and rinderpest were eradicated while measles elimination has been lost in the UK, Canada and the United States. It looks at how outbreaks follow clusters of low coverage, using Samoa, Canada, West Texas and the Philippines as case studies, and at the global programmes (the Expanded Programme on Immunization, Gavi, COVAX and Immunization Agenda 2030) that brought vaccines to most of the world's children, with their critics. It ends with the ethics of mandates: the harm principle, free-riding, autonomy, proportionality and the intervention ladder.

**Prerequisites:** [Herd immunity](../../../epidemiology/04-herd-immunity.md) and [outbreaks](../../../epidemiology/05-outbreaks-epidemics-and-endemicity.md) in the epidemiology section; [controversies](../07-controversies/) and [litigation](../08-litigation-and-compensation/) for the trust and legal background.

## Key studies & sources

Every source below was checked to exist (DOI, court record or official page). Notes summarise what each found and its main strengths or limitations.

### Peer-reviewed studies and reviews

- [Dowdle, *Bulletin of the WHO* 1998, "The principles of disease elimination and eradication"](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2305684/): Classic definitions of control, elimination and eradication.
- [Salmon et al., *Lancet* 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(12)62189-8): Review linking exemption policy, clusters and outbreaks.
- [Trotter et al., *Lancet Infect Dis* 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1473-3099(17)30301-8): MenAfriVac: confirmed meningitis A down more than 99% in vaccinated populations.
- [Fine, Eames & Heymann, *Clinical Infectious Diseases* 2011](https://doi.org/10.1093/cid/cir007): Herd immunity concepts and thresholds.
