# The diseases

One overview for each of 29 diseases that vaccines prevent or reduce: what the disease does, how common it was before vaccines and is now, how the vaccines work and how well, their known side effects, and where relevant the controversies and policy changes attached to them (including the January 2026 US schedule changes and the 2026 court block). Pages are listed alphabetically; each can be read on its own.

**Prerequisites:** [Microbiology](../../../microbiology/README.md) for the pathogens; [Types of vaccines](../01-how-vaccines-work/02-types-of-vaccines.md) for the platforms.

## Suggested reading order

Childhood schedule diseases: measles, mumps, rubella, polio, diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis, Hib, pneumococcal, meningococcal, hepatitis A and B, rotavirus, chickenpox, HPV, influenza, COVID-19 and RSV. Historical: smallpox. Travel and global: rabies, yellow fever, typhoid, cholera, Japanese encephalitis, TB, dengue, mpox, Ebola and malaria.

## Key studies & sources

Every source below was checked to exist (DOI, court record or official page). Notes summarise what each found and its main strengths or limitations.

### Peer-reviewed studies

- [Hviid et al., *Annals of Internal Medicine* 2019](https://doi.org/10.7326/M18-2101): Danish cohort of 657,461 children; no increased autism risk after MMR. Nationwide registry, government (Novo Nordisk Foundation and Danish Ministry of Health) funded.
- [Mina et al., *Science* 2015](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaa3662): Measles causes immune amnesia, increasing deaths from other infections for two to three years. Ecological plus mechanistic data.
- [Whitney et al., *NEJM* 2003](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa022823): Herd effects of pneumococcal conjugate vaccine on adults. Large active surveillance; observational.
- [Klein et al., *NEJM* 2012](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1200850): Pertussis protection after acellular DTaP waned about 42% per year. Kaiser Permanente case-control study; partly funded by Sanofi Pasteur.
- [Warfel, Zimmerman & Merkel, *PNAS* 2014](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1314688110): Baboon study suggesting acellular pertussis vaccines do not prevent infection or transmission. Animal model, small numbers.
- [Chang et al., *NEJM* 1997](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM199706263362602): Taiwan's hepatitis B programme reduced childhood liver cancer; first evidence of a vaccine preventing cancer. Ecological, before–after.
- [Lei et al., *NEJM* 2020](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1917338): Swedish registry: HPV vaccination before 17 cut invasive cervical cancer by 88%. Observational; strong dose–response by age.
- [Falcaro et al., *Lancet* 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(21)02178-4): England: 87% lower cervical cancer in women offered HPV vaccine at 12–13. Observational; national cancer registry.
- [Murphy et al., *NEJM* 2001](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM200102223440804): RotaShield and intussusception: about 1 per 10,000. Case-control; led to withdrawal.
- [Kim et al., *American Journal of Epidemiology* 1969](https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordjournals.aje.a120955): 1960s RSV vaccine trial in which vaccinated children had more severe disease. A documented vaccine harm.
- [Dieussaert et al., *NEJM* 2024](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2305478): GSK maternal RSV trial stopped because of excess preterm births. Industry-funded; transparent publication of a negative result.
- [Sridhar et al., *NEJM* 2018](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1800820): Dengvaxia increased severe dengue in seronegative people. Sanofi-funded reanalysis of trial data.
- [Henao-Restrepo et al., *Lancet* 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(16)32621-6): Ebola ring vaccination trial; very high efficacy. WHO-led; design compromises noted.
- [RTS,S Clinical Trials Partnership, *Lancet* 2015](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(15)60721-8): RTS,S malaria vaccine phase 3: about 36% efficacy over four years in young children. GSK and PATH funded.
- [Datoo et al., *Lancet* 2024 (R21 trial)](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(23)02511-4): R21 malaria vaccine phase 3: about 75% efficacy in seasonal sites. Oxford and Serum Institute.
- [Deputy et al., *NEJM* 2023](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2215201): MVA-BN effectiveness against mpox: 75% (one dose), 86% (two). Case-control; CDC.
- [Eyting et al., *Nature* 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08800-x): Wales natural experiment: shingles vaccination reduced new dementia diagnoses by about a fifth. Strong design; needs replication.

### Systematic reviews and burden estimates

- [Di Pietrantonj et al., Cochrane review of MMR/MMRV vaccines, 2020](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD004407.pub4): MMR: no link with autism; increased febrile seizures; many studies at high risk of bias.
- [Demicheli et al., Cochrane review of influenza vaccines in healthy adults, 2018](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD001269.pub6): Influenza vaccines in healthy adults: modest absolute benefit.
- [Arbyn et al., Cochrane review of HPV vaccines, 2018](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD009069.pub3): HPV vaccines: high-certainty protection against precancer; criticised by Jørgensen et al.
- [Wahl et al., *Lancet Global Health* 2018](https://doi.org/10.1016/s2214-109x(18)30247-x): Global pneumococcal and Hib deaths in children, 2000–15.
- [Troeger et al., *JAMA Pediatrics* 2018](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapediatrics.2018.1960): Global rotavirus deaths in 2016 and deaths averted by vaccination.
- [Mangtani et al., *Clinical Infectious Diseases* 2014](https://doi.org/10.1093/cid/cit790): BCG protection against TB varies widely by latitude and prior exposure.

### Official sources

- [CDC, *Epidemiology and Prevention of Vaccine-Preventable Diseases* ("Pink Book")](https://www.cdc.gov/pinkbook/hcp/table-of-contents/index.html): CDC chapters on each vaccine-preventable disease, with pre-vaccine US figures.
- [WHO measles fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/measles): WHO measles fact sheet (deaths, coverage).
- [WHO poliomyelitis fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/poliomyelitis): WHO polio fact sheet (eradication status).
- [WHO hepatitis B fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hepatitis-b): WHO hepatitis B fact sheet.
- [WHO malaria fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/malaria): WHO malaria fact sheet, including vaccine recommendations.
- [HHS fact sheet, "CDC Childhood Immunization Recommendations" (January 2026)](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/fact-sheet-cdc-childhood-immunization-recommendations.html): HHS January 2026 childhood schedule changes, affecting many diseases on this list.
- [Brazil Ministry of Health, temporary discontinuation of Butantan-DV vaccination (8 June 2026)](https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2026/junho/ministerio-da-saude-descontinua-temporariamente-estrategia-atual-de-vacinacao-do-butantan-contra-dengue): Brazil's June 2026 pause of the Butantan-DV dengue vaccine after a safety signal.

### Court rulings

- [*United States ex rel. Krahling v. Merck & Co.*, No. 23-2553 (3d Cir. 6 August 2024)](https://www2.ca3.uscourts.gov/opinarch/232553np.pdf): Third Circuit affirms dismissal of the Merck mumps whistleblower case on materiality grounds (2024).
- [*American Academy of Pediatrics v. Kennedy*, First Circuit docket 26-1503 (CourtListener)](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/73286021/american-academy-of-pediatrics-v-kennedy/): *AAP v. Kennedy*: 2026 schedule changes preliminarily blocked.

## References & further reading

- **Beginner.** David M. Oshinsky, *Polio: An American Story* (2005). Pulitzer Prize-winning history of polio, the March of Dimes, Salk and Sabin.
- **Beginner.** John M. Barry, *The Great Influenza: The Story of the Deadliest Pandemic in History* (2004). The 1918 pandemic.
- **Beginner.** Steven Johnson, *The Ghost Map* (2006). John Snow and the 1854 London cholera outbreak.
- **Beginner.** David Quammen, *Spillover: Animal Infections and the Next Human Pandemic* (2012). Ebola, zoonoses and emerging diseases.
- **Intermediate.** D. A. Henderson, *Smallpox: The Death of a Disease* (2009). The eradication campaign by the man who led it.
- **Intermediate.** Michael Willrich, *Pox: An American History* (2011). Smallpox epidemics, compulsory vaccination and resistance around 1900.
- **Advanced.** David L. Heymann (ed.), *Control of Communicable Diseases Manual* (21st ed., APHA, 2022). Concise reference on every disease here.
- **Advanced.** Stanley A. Plotkin et al. (eds), *Plotkin's Vaccines* (8th ed., 2023). A chapter per disease and vaccine.
