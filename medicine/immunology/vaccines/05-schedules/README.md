# Schedules

How immunisation schedules are built, and how and why they differ. The section covers the US schedule before and after the 2025–26 changes (including the court block), Canada's provincial schedules with Ontario as the example, the UK's cost-effectiveness-driven programme, Denmark and the other Nordic countries (the model for the 2026 US cuts), a side-by-side comparison, how schedules have grown and shrunk over time, vaccination in pregnancy, adulthood and travel, contraindications and precautions, and alternative or delayed schedules. Schedules change often; tables here describe late 2026 and should be checked against official sources.

**Prerequisites:** [How vaccines work](../01-how-vaccines-work/) and [Advisory committees](../02-development-and-regulation/06-advisory-committees-acip-naci-and-jcvi.md).

## Key studies & sources

Every source below was checked to exist (DOI, court record or official page). Notes summarise what each found and its main strengths or limitations.

### Peer-reviewed studies

- [Offit et al., *Pediatrics* 2002](https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.109.1.124): Estimates antigen exposure fell from >3,000 (1980) to about 125 (2000) despite more vaccines. Lead author co-invented RotaTeq.
- [Glanz et al., *Pediatrics* 2009](https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2008-2150): Kaiser Colorado case-control: children of pertussis-vaccine refusers had much higher pertussis risk. Small number of refusers; observational.
- [Glanz et al., *JAMA Pediatrics* 2013](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapediatrics.2013.502): Vaccine Safety Datalink cohort of 323,247: undervaccination rising; undervaccinated children had fewer outpatient but more inpatient visits. Confounded by care-seeking; CDC-funded.
- [Hambidge et al., *Pediatrics* 2014](https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2013-3429): Same cohort: delaying first MMR past 15 months doubled the relative risk of post-vaccine febrile seizures; infant timing made no difference. CDC-funded.
- [Smith & Woods, *Pediatrics* 2010](https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2009-2489): Reanalysis of public VSD data: no neuropsychological advantage from delayed vaccination.
- [Klein et al., *Pediatrics* 2010](https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2010-0665): MMRV as first dose roughly doubles febrile-seizure risk versus separate MMR + varicella. Several authors had manufacturer research funding.
- [Amirthalingam et al., *Lancet* 2014](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(14)60686-3): England: maternal pertussis vaccination about 91% effective in protecting young infants. Government (Public Health England) study.
- [Baxter et al., *Pediatrics* 2017](https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2016-4091): Kaiser Northern California, ~149,000 newborns: maternal Tdap 91% effective in first 2 months (wide CI). Two authors disclosed manufacturer-related conflicts.
- [Miller & Goldman, *Human & Experimental Toxicology* 2011](https://doi.org/10.1177/0960327111407644): Critical: among 34 rich nations, more infant doses correlated with higher infant mortality. Ecological; authors are vaccine critics.
- [Nysetvold et al., "Infant vaccination does not predict increased infant mortality rate: correcting past misinformation", medRxiv preprint 2021](https://doi.org/10.1101/2021.09.03.21263082): Reanalysis of the same data for 185 countries: doses explain <3% of variance; HDI predicts mortality. Preprint.
- [Goldman & Miller, *Cureus* 2023 (response to critics)](https://doi.org/10.7759/cureus.34566): Miller and Goldman's response defending their selection of countries.

### Systematic and expert reviews

- [Institute of Medicine, *The Childhood Immunization Schedule and Safety* (2013)](https://doi.org/10.17226/13563): IOM review of the childhood schedule: no evidence of harm, few whole-schedule studies, recommended VSD-based research instead of trials. Federally commissioned.

### Official schedules and guidance

- [CDC newsroom, "CDC Acts on Presidential Memorandum to Update Childhood Immunization Schedule" (5 January 2026)](https://www.cdc.gov/media/releases/2026/2026-cdc-acts-on-presidential-memorandum-to-update-childhood-immunization-schedule.html): CDC's January 2026 revised childhood schedule (11 routine diseases).
- [HHS fact sheet, "CDC Childhood Immunization Recommendations" (January 2026)](https://www.hhs.gov/press-room/fact-sheet-cdc-childhood-immunization-recommendations.html): HHS announcement of the 2026 schedule, citing Denmark.
- [KFF, "The New Federal Vaccine Schedule for Children: What Changed and What Are the Implications?"](https://www.kff.org/other-health/the-new-federal-vaccine-schedule-what-changed/): KFF's independent explainer of what changed and its implications.
- [Ontario Ministry of Health, "Ontario's routine immunization schedule"](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontarios-routine-immunization-schedule): Ontario's publicly funded schedule.
- [Public Health Agency of Canada, *Canadian Immunization Guide*: recommended immunization schedules](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/publications/healthy-living/canadian-immunization-guide-part-1-key-immunization-information/page-13-recommended-immunization-schedules.html): Canadian Immunization Guide schedules (NACI).
- [UKHSA, "Complete routine immunisation schedule from 1 September 2026"](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-complete-routine-immunisation-schedule/complete-routine-immunisation-schedule-from-1-july-2026): UKHSA complete routine immunisation schedule from September 2026.
- [Danish Health Authority, *The Danish Childhood Immunisation Programme* (English, 2022)](https://www.sst.dk/media/ymhpf0cq/boernevaccinationsprogrammet-i-danmark-2022-engelsk.pdf): Danish Health Authority childhood vaccination programme.
- [CDC, *General Best Practice Guidelines for Immunization*: contraindications and precautions](https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-best-practices/contraindications-precautions.html): CDC contraindications and precautions, including conditions wrongly treated as contraindications.
- [Public Health Agency of Canada, *Canadian Immunization Guide*: contraindications and precautions](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/publications/healthy-living/canadian-immunization-guide-part-2-vaccine-safety/page-3-contraindications-precautions-concerns.html): Canadian equivalent.
- [CDC, "Notice to readers: thimerosal in vaccines: a joint statement of the American Academy of Pediatrics and the Public Health Service", *MMWR* 1999;48(26):563–565](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/mm4826a3.htm): AAP/PHS statement asking for thimerosal removal as a precaution, while noting no evidence of harm.

### Court rulings

- [*American Academy of Pediatrics v. Kennedy*, First Circuit docket 26-1503 (CourtListener)](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/73286021/american-academy-of-pediatrics-v-kennedy/): *AAP v. Kennedy*: the March 2026 preliminary injunction against the schedule overhaul and the pending First Circuit appeal.
- [AP News, "Judge blocks changes to US government vaccine recommendations" (March 2026)](https://apnews.com/article/kennedy-acip-vaccines-cdc-fc758951019f41d2f5e81e4e2faa22d3): News report of the injunction.

### Notable critical sources

- [Offit & Moser, *Pediatrics* 2009](https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2008-2189): Offit and Moser's critique of Bob Sears's alternative schedule (Offit co-invented RotaTeq).
- [Los Angeles Times, "California doctor critical of vaccines is punished for exempting 2-year-old boy from all childhood immunizations" (29 June 2018)](https://www.latimes.com/local/california/la-me-ln-sears-license-20180629-story.html): Report of the California medical board's 2018 probation of Sears over an exemption letter.

## References & further reading

- **Beginner.** Paul A. Offit and Charlotte A. Moser, *Vaccines and Your Child: Separating Fact from Fiction* (2011). Vaccine-by-vaccine guide by CHOP's Vaccine Education Center; Offit co-invented RotaTeq.
- **Beginner.** Robert W. Sears, *The Vaccine Book: Making the Right Decision for Your Child* (2007; revised 2011). The best-known alternative schedule, read to understand the hesitant-parent position; its schedule is untested and widely criticised.
- **Intermediate.** Elena Conis, *Vaccine Nation: America's Changing Relationship with Immunization* (2015). How the US schedule grew and why that provoked unease.
- **Intermediate.** Mark A. Largent, *Vaccine: The Debate in Modern America* (2012). Sympathetic analysis of parental concerns about the schedule.
- **Advanced.** CDC, *Epidemiology and Prevention of Vaccine-Preventable Diseases* (the "Pink Book"). Clinical detail behind each recommendation.
- **Advanced.** Stanley A. Plotkin, Walter A. Orenstein, Paul A. Offit and Kathryn M. Edwards (eds), *Plotkin's Vaccines* (8th ed., 2023). The standard reference.
