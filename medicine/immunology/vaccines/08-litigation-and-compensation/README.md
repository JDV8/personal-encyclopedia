# Litigation and compensation

How the law deals with vaccine injuries and vaccine mandates. The section starts with the 1980s DTP lawsuits that nearly drove manufacturers from the US market, and the 1986 law that replaced most lawsuits with a no-fault compensation program. It explains how the Vaccine Injury Compensation Program and its injury table work, what happened to more than 5,000 autism claims in the omnibus proceeding, and why COVID-19 vaccines fall under a much weaker program. It covers Canada's and Quebec's programs, litigation that still reaches the courts (Gardasil, the UK scheme and AstraZeneca, European courts), the line of mandate cases from *Jacobson* to COVID-19, and COVID-19 litigation. Arguments from injured claimants and from public-health and industry perspectives are presented side by side.

**Prerequisites:** [Adverse events](../06-adverse-events/), especially causality assessment, and [controversies](../07-controversies/) for the scientific background to the autism and DTP claims.

## Suggested reading order

Read 01–05 in order for the US system, then 06 for COVID-19 compensation. 07 and 08 cover other countries and remaining litigation; 09 and 10 cover mandates and COVID-19 cases.

### Court rulings

- [*Jacobson v. Massachusetts*, 197 U.S. 11 (1905)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/197/11): States may require vaccination; limits for arbitrary or cruel applications.
- [*Zucht v. King*, 260 U.S. 174 (1922)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/260/174): School vaccination requirements upheld.
- [*Buck v. Bell*, 274 U.S. 200 (1927)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/274/200): Forced sterilisation upheld citing *Jacobson*; now condemned.
- [*Prince v. Massachusetts*, 321 U.S. 158 (1944)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/321/158): Religious liberty does not include exposing children to disease.
- [*Lapierre v. Attorney General of Quebec*, [1985] 1 SCR 241](https://decisions.scc-csc.ca/scc-csc/scc-csc/en/40/1/document.do): Supreme Court of Canada: no compensation without fault; led to Quebec's program.
- [*Althen v. Secretary of HHS*, 418 F.3d 1274 (Fed. Cir. 2005)](https://www.cafc.uscourts.gov/opinions-orders/04-5146.pdf): Three-part test for off-table causation.
- [*Bruesewitz v. Wyeth LLC*, 562 U.S. 223 (2011)](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/562/223/): Design-defect claims against vaccine makers pre-empted.
- [Court of Justice of the EU, *N. W and Others v Sanofi Pasteur MSD*, Case C-621/15 (21 June 2017)](https://curia.europa.eu/juris/liste.jsf?num=C-621/15): EU court lets national courts infer causation without scientific consensus.
- [*National Federation of Independent Business v. OSHA*, 595 U.S. 109 (2022)](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/21pdf/21a244_hgci.pdf): OSHA employer mandate blocked.
- [*Biden v. Missouri*, 595 U.S. 87 (2022)](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/21pdf/21a240_d18e.pdf): Health-worker mandate allowed.
- [*Groff v. DeJoy*, 600 U.S. 447 (2023)](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/22pdf/22-174_k536.pdf): Higher bar for denying religious accommodation.
- [*United States ex rel. Krahling v. Merck & Co.*, No. 23-2553 (3d Cir. 6 August 2024)](https://www2.ca3.uscourts.gov/opinarch/232553np.pdf): Mumps vaccine False Claims Act case: judgment for Merck upheld.
- [*In re Gardasil Products Liability Litigation*, No. 3:22-md-03036 (W.D.N.C. 11 March 2025), order on summary judgment](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-ncwd-3_22-md-03036/pdf/USCOURTS-ncwd-3_22-md-03036-25.pdf): Gardasil MDL: failure-to-warn claims pre-empted.
- [*Texas v. Pfizer Inc.*, No. 5:23-cv-00312-C (N.D. Tex. 30 December 2024), order granting dismissal](https://www.courthousenews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/texas-v-pfizer-order-granting-motion-to-dismiss.pdf): Texas suit dismissed on PREP Act grounds.
- [*State of Kansas v. Pfizer*, No. 25-3085 (10th Cir.), docket](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/70287684/state-of-kansas-v-pfizer/): Kansas suit docket.
- [*Jackson v. Ventavia Research Group*, No. 24-40564 (5th Cir. 2026)](https://www.ca5.uscourts.gov/opinions/pub/24/24-40564-CV0.pdf): Ventavia whistleblower suit dismissal upheld.
- [Supreme Court order in *Miller v. McDonald*, No. 25-133 (8 December 2025), vacating and remanding in light of *Mahmoud v. Taylor*](https://www.supremecourt.gov/orders/courtorders/120825zor_i4ek.pdf): Supreme Court remand of New York Amish exemption case.
- [*Miller v. McDonald*, No. 24-681 (2d Cir. 17 November 2026), order denying rehearing en banc](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca2/24-681/24-681-2026-11-17.html): Second Circuit denies rehearing, with dissents.

## References & further reading

- **Beginner.** Elena Conis, *Vaccine Nation: America's Changing Relationship with Immunization* (2015). Includes the DTP crisis and the 1986 Act.
- **Beginner.** Paul A. Offit, *The Cutter Incident: How America's First Polio Vaccine Led to the Growing Vaccine Crisis* (2005). How liability law shaped the vaccine industry; Offit co-invented RotaTeq.
- **Intermediate.** Anna Kirkland, *Vaccine Court: The Law and Politics of Injury* (2016). A sociolegal study of the VICP, sympathetic to neither side.
- **Intermediate.** Harris L. Coulter and Barbara Loe Fisher, *DPT: A Shot in the Dark* (1985). The parents' case against whole-cell DTP that shaped the 1986 Act; Fisher co-founded the National Vaccine Information Center.
- **Advanced.** Lawrence O. Gostin and Lindsay F. Wiley, *Public Health Law: Power, Duty, Restraint* (3rd ed., 2016). The standard text on public-health powers, including *Jacobson*.
- **Advanced.** Louise Kuo Habakus and Mary Holland (eds), *Vaccine Epidemic: How Corporate Greed, Biased Science, and Coercive Government Threaten Our Human Rights, Our Health, and Our Children* (2nd ed., 2012). A critical legal and ethical case against mandates; Holland later led Children's Health Defense.
