# People in vaccine history

Short profiles of twelve people who shaped vaccination and the arguments about it. The pioneers are Edward Jenner, Louis Pasteur, Jonas Salk, Albert Sabin, Maurice Hilleman, Stanley Plotkin, D. A. Henderson and Katalin Karikó. Paul Offit stands for vaccine science's public defenders. Andrew Wakefield, Barbara Loe Fisher and Robert F. Kennedy Jr. stand for its critics, from fraud, through safety advocacy, to government policy. Each profile tries to state fairly how supporters and critics see the person, notes conflicts of interest where they matter (Offit's RotaTeq royalties, Wakefield's legal funding, Kennedy's Gardasil litigation interest), and links back to the lessons where their work is discussed in depth. The profiles can be read in any order.

**Prerequisites:** None; the profiles are self-contained but link to the [history](../04-history/) and [controversies](../07-controversies/) sections.

## Suggested reading order

Read alphabetically or by interest. Pair Jenner and Henderson with the smallpox lessons, Salk and Sabin with polio, and Wakefield, Fisher and Kennedy with the controversies section.

## Key studies & sources

Every source below was checked to exist (DOI, court record or official page). Notes summarise what each found and its main strengths or limitations.

### Primary scientific papers

- [Francis et al., *JAMA* 1955 (1954 polio field trial)](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.1955.02960140028004): Thomas Francis's evaluation of the 1954 Salk vaccine field trial; funded by the National Foundation for Infantile Paralysis.
- [Karikó et al., *Immunity* 2005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.immuni.2005.06.008): Karikó and Weissman's nucleoside-modification paper, the foundation of mRNA vaccines; academic NIH-funded work.
- [Wakefield et al., *Lancet* 1998 (retracted)](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(97)11096-0): The retracted MMR case series; undisclosed legal-aid funding and patent interests.

### Official findings and records

- [Fenner et al., *Smallpox and Its Eradication* (WHO, 1988)](https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/39485): WHO's history of smallpox eradication, co-written by Henderson; official account by participants.
- [WHO smallpox health topic](https://www.who.int/health-topics/smallpox): WHO on smallpox and its eradication.
- [Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 2023, press release](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/2023/press-release/): Nobel Prize announcement for Karikó and Weissman.
- [General Medical Council, Fitness to Practise Panel findings on Wakefield, Walker-Smith and Murch (January 2010), as reported by *The BMJ*](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.c593): The GMC's findings against Wakefield, reported in The BMJ.
- [*Lancet* retraction notice, 2010](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(10)60175-4): The Lancet's 2010 retraction.
- [FactCheck.org, "RFK Jr.'s flawed justifications for 'clean sweep' of CDC vaccine advisory panel" (June 2025)](https://www.factcheck.org/2025/06/rfk-jr-s-flawed-justifications-for-clean-sweep-of-cdc-vaccine-advisory-panel/): Kennedy's replacement of ACIP and what was said about it.

### Investigations and journalism

- [Deer, *BMJ* 2011, "How the case against the MMR vaccine was fixed"](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.c5347): Brian Deer on how the Wakefield case series was altered; BMJ-commissioned investigation.
- [Deer, *BMJ* 2011, "How the vaccine crisis was meant to make money"](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.c5258): Deer on Wakefield's legal and commercial interests.
- [Godlee, Smith & Marcovitch, *BMJ* 2011 editorial](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.c7452): BMJ editorial calling the paper fraudulent.
- [Salon, "Correcting our record" (16 January 2011)](https://www.salon.com/2011/01/16/dangerous_immunity/): Salon's removal of Kennedy's 'Deadly Immunity'.
- [New York Times, "RFK Jr. says he won't keep financial stake in HPV vaccine lawsuits" (30 January 2025)](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/01/30/health/rfk-jr-hpv-vaccine-lawsuits-fees.html): Kennedy's financial interest in Gardasil litigation.
- [BioSpace, "Royalty Pharma AG acquires royalty interest in RotaTeq for $182 million" (2008)](https://www.biospace.com/royalty-pharma-ag-acquires-royalty-interest-in-rotateq-r-for-182-million): RotaTeq royalty sale; context for criticism of Offit's COI.

## References & further reading

- **Beginner.** Paul A. Offit, *Vaccinated: One Man's Quest to Defeat the World's Deadliest Diseases* (2007). Hilleman's life; the author is a vaccine co-inventor.
- **Beginner.** Katalin Karikó, *Breaking Through: My Life in Science* (2023). Memoir.
- **Beginner.** D. A. Henderson, *Smallpox: The Death of a Disease* (2009). Eradication by its leader.
- **Beginner.** Seth Mnookin, *The Panic Virus* (2011). The modern vaccine-autism controversy and its main figures.
- **Intermediate.** David M. Oshinsky, *Polio: An American Story* (2005). Salk, Sabin and the polio crusade; Pulitzer Prize.
- **Intermediate.** Brian Deer, *The Doctor Who Fooled the World* (2020). The Wakefield investigation by the journalist who conducted it.
- **Intermediate.** Harris L. Coulter and Barbara Loe Fisher, *DPT: A Shot in the Dark* (1985). A founding text of the US vaccine-safety movement; read critically.
- **Intermediate.** Andrew J. Wakefield, *Callous Disregard* (2010). Wakefield's own account; read alongside Deer.
- **Advanced.** Gerald L. Geison, *The Private Science of Louis Pasteur* (1995). Revisionist history from Pasteur's notebooks.
- **Advanced.** Robert F. Kennedy Jr., *The Real Anthony Fauci* (2021). Primary source for Kennedy's views; widely criticised for inaccuracies.
